A playbook is what kind of hero you are: it suggests your stats and gives you its own moves. The game's staff may add more; `+sheet/playbook` lists the ones your game has.

## Warden

Suggested stats: Con +2, Str +1, Wis +1, Dex +0, Cha +0, Int -1.

Starting moves: Hold the Line. A new Warden picks 1 of: Stand Fast or Watchful Eye.

### Hold the Line

When you stand your ground so that others can get away, roll+Con. On a 10+, everyone you cover gets clear and nothing gets past you. On a 7–9, they get clear, but you take a blow or are driven back; the GM says which.

*Rolls with Con.*

### Stand Fast

When something tries to move you from where you stand, roll+Con. On a 10+, you do not budge, and you take +1 forward against it. On a 7–9, you hold your place, but it costs you; the GM says what.

*Rolls with Con.*

### Watchful Eye

When you keep watch over a place you guard, roll+Wis. On a 10+, nothing comes near without your knowing, and you hold 1 to act first when it does. On a 7–9, you see it coming, but only just.

*Rolls with Wis.*

## Wayfarer

Suggested stats: Wis +2, Dex +1, Int +1, Cha +0, Con +0, Str -1.

Starting moves: Know the Way. A new Wayfarer picks 1 of: Read the Sky or Forage.

### Know the Way

When you lead the party through country you have travelled before, roll+Wis. On a 10+, you arrive where and when you meant to. On a 7–9, you arrive, but late, tired or somewhere a little off; the GM says which.

*Rolls with Wis.*

### Read the Sky

When you study the sky before setting out, roll+Wis. On a 10+, you know the coming day's weather, and the party takes +1 forward on the road. On a 7–9, you know the weather, but not when it will turn.

*Rolls with Wis.*

### Forage

When you spend a few hours living off the land, roll+Wis. On a 10+, you find food and water for the whole party for a day. On a 7–9, you find enough for yourself and one other.

*Rolls with Wis.*


---

*The Warden and Wayfarer playbooks and their moves are the sheet package's own.*
