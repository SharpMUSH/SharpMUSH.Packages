Staff give XP, and Duties can give it as a reward. Once your character is approved, an advance costs 5 XP:

- `+sheet/advance <stat>` adds +1 to a stat, up to +3.
- `+sheet/advance <move>` takes another move from your playbook.

Your game may change the cost and the highest stat. Each advance is an entry on your sheet's ledger, so
`+sheet/ledger` shows how your character grew. Staff can take one back with `+sheet/revoke`, or clear a sheet
so character creation starts again with `+sheet/clear`.

`+sheet/log` lists every change to your XP: how much, what you had after, why, and who gave or spent it.
