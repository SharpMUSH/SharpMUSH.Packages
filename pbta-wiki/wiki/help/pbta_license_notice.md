## What is from Dungeon World

The stats (Str, Dex, Con, Int, Wis, Cha), and the text of the moves on the [[Basic moves|main:pbta_basic_moves]] and [[Special moves|main:pbta_special_moves]] pages: Hack and Slash, Volley, Defy Danger, Defend, Spout Lore, Discern Realities, Parley, Aid or Interfere, Last Breath, Take Watch, Undertake a Perilous Journey, Carouse, Supply, Recruit and Outstanding Warrants.

## Changes

Bold type is dropped, and list items are written as lines beginning with a dash, set apart from the text around them. No words of the moves are changed. A line under each move, saying what it rolls with and how to make it, is added.
