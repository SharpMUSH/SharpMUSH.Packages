# Atomic Robo RPG SRD and Fate System Toolkit Notice

The atomic-robo package's modes and their skills, mega-stunts and the GM's fate point reserve, invention with its catches and flaws, and brainstorms are based on the Atomic Robo RPG system reference document. Scale, and what each step of it does to a roll or to harm, is based on the Fate System Toolkit. The wording of the help and of every message is this package's own. It uses none of the Atomic Robo comic's characters, setting or art.
