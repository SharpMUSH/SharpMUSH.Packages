Action scientists build things in the middle of the story. An **invention** is a gadget with a function aspect, a number of benefits, a catch for each benefit, and a flaw.

## Building one

Roll a science skill against twice the benefits you want. What the invention does becomes its function aspect. The roll decides who chooses the catches, the conditions you must meet before it works:

- **Fail**: the GM chooses them all.
- **Tie**: the GM chooses first, then you take turns.
- **Success**: the GM chooses one, and you choose the rest.
- **Success with style**: you choose them all.

Every invention adds its benefits to the GM's fate point reserve, one fewer for each Bug among its catches, and the GM names its **flaw**, an aspect the GM may compel.

## Commands

- `+invent <skill>=<benefits> <what it does>` rolls where the room sees it: `+invent mechanics=1 Spring Heels`.
- `+invent` lists your inventions; `+invent/list <name>` someone else's.
- `+invent/catch <invention>=<catch>` and `+invent/flaw <invention>=<flaw>` write them down.
- `+invent/drop <invention>` lets one go.

For example:

`+invent mechanics=1 Spring Heels`

Output: `[SCIENCE] Bob invents Spring Heels: Mechanics: 4dF[0, -1, 0, 1] + 2 = 2 (Fair) against Fair (+2): a tie, no shifts (roll 26). The GM chooses its catch. It adds 1 to the GM's fate point reserve, and the GM names its flaw.`
