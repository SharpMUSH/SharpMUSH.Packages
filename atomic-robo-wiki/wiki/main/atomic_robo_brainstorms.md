A **brainstorm** is the group working out what is going on: three facts, and then a hypothesis that becomes true.

## How it runs

1. Someone asks the question: `+brainstorm/start Why are the ants giant?`
2. Everyone taking part compels one of their own aspects to join, and gets a fate point: `+brainstorm/join <aspect>`.
3. Each round, everyone rolls a skill: `+brainstorm <skill>`. The first fact is against Good (+3), and each fact after it against the last winning total. The winner states the fact: `+brainstorm/fact <fact>`.
4. After the third fact, the group counts its **victories**. A win is one victory, two with three shifts or more. A tie between winners is one between them, and a lone tie one at a cost.
5. With three victories or more, everyone rolls once more, and the highest names the hypothesis: `+brainstorm/hypothesis <aspect>`. It is true, with a free invocation at four victories and two at six. With fewer than three, the answer stays a mystery.

Whoever started it closes each round with `+brainstorm/next`. `+brainstorm` shows where it stands, and `+brainstorm/stop` ends it.

For example:

`+brainstorm/start Why are the ants giant?`

Output: `[SCIENCE] Alice starts a brainstorm: Why are the ants giant? Everyone taking part compels one of their aspects to join: +brainstorm/join <aspect>.`
