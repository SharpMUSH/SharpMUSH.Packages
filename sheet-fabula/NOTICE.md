## AI-generated content

This package contains AI-generated content: its softcode, help and messages were written with an AI model and play-tested on a running server.

## What it uses

The four attributes, their die sizes and starting sets, checks, Difficulty Levels, critical successes and fumbles, HP, MP, IP and Fabula Points, status effects, bonds and their emotions, levels, and the Core Rulebook's class names with what each class's first level gives. The help states these rules in its own words. It copies no text, art, logos or trade dress from the books, and holds no skill, spell or item descriptions: a player records a skill's name and level, and reads its rules in the Core Rulebook.
