A **gadget** joins an aspect and a stunt into one piece of equipment. It works just as well for a magic item.

## Function and flaw

Every gadget has two aspects of its own, which do not take up the character's:

- its **function**, what it is for, like a high concept;
- its **flaw**, what is wrong with it, like a trouble that never goes away.

`+gadget/make <gadget>=<function>/<flaw>` makes one.

## Stunts and cost

Each stunt costs 1 refresh: `+gadget/stunt <gadget>=<stunt>: <what it does>`. A gadget with no stunt costs nothing.

Each flaw past the first takes 1 refresh off (`+gadget/flaw`), but a gadget with any stunt costs at least 1. A flaw has to bite: if it never will, the GM turns it down.

## Two examples

**Magnetic Grapnel Gun**, 2 refresh

- Function: *High-Powered Electromagnetic Swingline*
- Flaw: *Still Working Out the Bugs*
- CLANG!: spend a fate point to secure the grapnel to a metallic object in a dramatic way.
- Trick Shots: +2 to create an advantage with Shoot when you swing around, disarm an opponent, or create a barrier.

**The Ring of Truth**, 1 refresh

- Function: *Magical Lie Detector*
- Flaw: *Wearer Is Cursed to Tell the Truth*
- Pierce the Veil of Lies: spend a fate point, and three times this scene ask the GM whether someone is lying.

One more flaw, such as *Heavy and Unwieldy*, would bring the grapnel gun to 1 refresh.
