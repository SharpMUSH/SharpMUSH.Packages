**Cyberware** makes a character more than human, at a cost in money, recovery and refresh. Every piece has a downside.

## Prostheses and augs

A **prosthesis** replaces something lost and does no more than it did. It costs no refresh and needs no aspect.

An **aug** improves on the human body. Augs run on a **cyber-heart**, a power source that charges from your own body. The heart comes first:

- tie one of your aspects to being a cyborg;
- pay 1 refresh.

The heart also backs up your own heart and filters your blood. Spend a fate point to ignore a poison, or to concede instead of being taken out by something it could save you from.

## What augs cost

- A **major aug**, such as a limb, an organ, or anything wired into your brain, costs 1 refresh.
- **Minor augs**, such as an eye, a hand, or an upgrade to an aug you have, cost 1 refresh for every three.

Before approval, `+aug/install <aug>` puts one in. After it, `+aug/surgery <aug>` rolls Resources:

- Average (+1) for a minor aug or a prosthesis, with a moderate consequence while you recover;
- Good (+3) for a major aug, with a severe one.

## Example augs

- **Cyber-Eye** (minor): +1 to sight-based Notice, and one of Image Filtering, Low-Light Vision, Sonar Imaging, Targeting Interface, Thermal Imaging, Visual Net Interface or Zoom Magnification as an aspect. Each more is another minor aug.
- **Cyber-Legs** (major): move two zones as a free action, and +2 to Athletics to run or jump. One add-on comes with them, and each more is a minor aug: a Hidden Compartment, a Magnetic Grip, or a Pneumatic Kick at Weapon:2.
- **Neural Interface** (major): reach the Net with a thought. Low-security systems do what you want, and you get +2 against high-security ones.
- **Razor Nails** (minor): retractable blades, Weapon:1.
- **Subdermal Plating** (major): oppose most physical attacks with Physique, and once per scene ignore one mild or moderate physical consequence from one.
- **Thermoptic Camouflage** (minor): spend a fate point to vanish from sight and thermal imaging while you stay still.

## The downside

A neural interface can be hacked. Camouflage can short out in the rain. The GM enforces an aug's downside as a compel on the aspect tied to being a cyborg, which the player can refuse, or take a fate point for.
