A **condition** is a hurt with a name, set out ahead of time: Angry, Exhausted, Wounded. It soaks stress the way a consequence does, and while you suffer it, it is an aspect on your sheet. You can invoke it, others can invoke or compel it against you, and the first time someone else invokes it, it is free.

## Kinds

| Kind | Soaks | Clears |
|---|---|---|
| Fleeting | 1 shift | once you catch your breath and calm down |
| Sticky | 2 shifts | after a set event: Exhausted until you sleep, Hungry until you eat |
| Lasting | 4 shifts | after treatment, then one more full session |

The usual six are Angry and Frightened (fleeting), Exhausted and Hungry (sticky), and Broken and Wounded (lasting): 14 shifts in all. A game can change them, keeping the same total.

## Taking them

When you take a hit, check off as many clear conditions as you like to soak it. `+condition/take angry hungry` soaks 3 shifts. The GM can also say you suffer one because of what happened: `+condition/give <name>=<condition>`.

In an encounter, conditions change its copy of your sheet, as stress does, and stay only if the GM keeps the encounter.

## Lasting conditions

A lasting condition has two boxes. You take it only while both are clear, and you check both. To start recovering, someone overcomes Great (+4) for you: `+condition/treat <name>/wounded=lore`. A tie or better clears one box. After one more full session, `+condition/recover wounded` clears the other.
