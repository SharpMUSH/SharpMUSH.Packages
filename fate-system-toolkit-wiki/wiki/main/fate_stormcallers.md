A **Stormcaller** draws magic from one of five great storms that rage at the heart of creation: Earthquake, Flood, Glacier, Inferno and Thunder. The power is always outside you: you can hit something very hard with a rock, but you cannot give yourself the strength of the earth.

## Becoming one

- Attuning costs 2 refresh, or 1 on a game that uses Weapon and Armor ratings: `+storm/attune <storm>`.
- Take an aspect that names your storm, such as *Earthcaller* or *Attuned to the Inferno*.
- Rate the Stormcaller skill. It attacks, defends and creates advantages, so long as your storm is in what you describe.

You may attune to more than one storm, each at its cost.

## The storm's aspect

Each storm's aspect does something all the time, can be invoked for one kind of thing, and compelled for another. Earthquake keeps your footing, helps you endure, and holds you back when you need to be quick. `+storm <storm>` shows each one.

## Barriers

A barrier stands between two zones. Raise one with `+storm/barrier`: you roll against 0, and the result is its rating. Getting past it takes that much, and a defender behind it may use the rating in place of a defence roll. It takes Average (+1) or better to raise one. Earthquake and Glacier get +1. Earthquake can surround a zone at -4, and seal it over at -1 more. Glacier's barrier can run several lengths, at 1 off for each past the first. A Flood barrier loses 1 each exchange, an Inferno barrier 2, and a Thunder barrier is gone after one, unless you hold it at -1 to everything else.

## Attacks

Any storm attacks in your zone without penalty, and at -1 for each zone away (`far`). Earthquake, Flood and Inferno can strike a whole zone; Earthquake can reach further zones at -2 each; Thunder chains to more targets at -1 each. Flood's damage ignores armor, Glacier can freeze a target in place, and a thunderbolt that earns a boost adds a boost of Stunned.

For example, `+storm/attack zone far 1=fair` calls the Inferno on everyone in the next zone at -2.
