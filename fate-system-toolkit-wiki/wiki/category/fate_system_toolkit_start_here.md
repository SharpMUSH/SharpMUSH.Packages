Six rules from the Fate System Toolkit for Fate games. New to Fate itself? Read [[Learn Fate|main:learn_fate]] first.

1. [[Conditions|main:fate_conditions]]: set hurts such as Angry or Wounded that soak stress, as consequences do.
2. [[Wealth|main:fate_wealth]]: money as a stress track you check for a bonus on a roll.
3. [[Stormcallers|main:fate_stormcallers]]: magic drawn from five great storms, at a cost in refresh.
4. [[Supers|main:fate_supers]]: super skills that double for a fate point, and powers that bid against each other.
5. [[Gadgets|main:fate_gadgets]]: equipment and magic items with a function, flaws and stunts.
6. [[Cyberware|main:fate_cyberware]]: augs on a cyber-heart, paid for in refresh and surgery.

Each is on the Fate sheet: `+sheet` shows your conditions, Wealth, storms, super skills, gadgets and cyberware.
