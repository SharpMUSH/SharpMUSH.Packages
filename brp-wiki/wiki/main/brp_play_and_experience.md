## Hit points and power points

Hit points are CON and SIZ added and halved, rounded up, unless your game adds them whole. Power points equal
POW.

- `+sheet/hp -4` loses 4 hit points; `+4` gets them back, and `full` fills them. At 0 or below a character is
  dying.
- `+sheet/pp -2` spends 2 power points, the same way.

## Coin

`+sheet/coin -20 Train ticket` spends 20 coin and notes why. Staff, or the GM of an encounter you are in, give
coin.

## Experience

A successful roll on a skill marks it for an experience check: `+check` says so. Once you are approved,
`+sheet/improve` rolls 1d100 for each marked skill. Over the skill's percentage, it rises by 1d6,
unless your game changed the die. The marks then clear, whatever the rolls.

Staff can mark a skill by hand, or take a mark off.

## The log

`+sheet/log` lists every change to your coin and every skill gain: how much, what you had after, why, and who
made it. Changes in an encounter appear when its GM keeps them.

## The ledger

Your sheet is built from its ledger: every characteristic, skill and gain is an entry. `+sheet/ledger` lists
them. Staff can take one back, so a mistake is fixed without losing the rest.
