Type a key, or the start of a name, in commands. `+sheet/traits` lists the ones your game uses, which may
differ from these.

## Characteristics

A characteristic roll is five times the characteristic. SIZ has no roll of its own. An unassigned
characteristic counts as 10.

| Characteristic | Key | Roll |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | `str` | Effort |
| Constitution | `con` | Stamina |
| Size | `siz` | - |
| Intelligence | `int` | Idea |
| Power | `pow` | Luck |
| Dexterity | `dex` | Agility |
| Charisma | `cha` | Charm |

A skill's base chance is the chance everyone has: a number, or worked out from characteristics, so `DEX x 2`
