Making a character takes two steps. `+sheet/chargen` shows each, what is done, and what comes next.

## 1. Characteristics

Share out 75 points among the seven characteristics, each from 3 to 18, unless your
game changed those numbers.

`+sheet/set str=12` gives Strength 12. `+sheet/set str=` takes its points back.

Set these first: skills start from base chances that follow your characteristics.

## 2. Skills

Spend 300 skill points raising skills above their base chances, at most 75% in any one
skill.

`+sheet/set climb=55` raises Climb to 55%; if its base is 40%, that spends 15 points. `+sheet/set climb=` puts it
back to its base.

## After approval

Once staff approve your character, they change your sheet, and your skills grow with experience. See
[[Hit points, coin and experience|main:brp_play_and_experience]].
