Say what your character does. When the outcome is in doubt, the GM asks for a roll on a skill or a
characteristic.

## Rolling

`+check climb` rolls 1d100 against your Climb percentage. Equal to or under it succeeds. The room sees the roll,
and the game keeps it.

`+check dex` makes a characteristic roll: five times the characteristic. Each has a name of its own, so
`+check agility` is the same roll.

## Success levels

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| A twentieth of the chance or less | A critical success |
| A fifth of the chance or less | A special success |
| The chance or less | A success |
| Over the chance | A failure |
| 96 to 100, narrowing as the chance grows | A fumble |

A skill whose base chance is 5% or more always succeeds on 01 to 05.

## Difficulty

`+check climb=difficult` halves the chance; `easy` doubles it. `automatic` needs no roll and `impossible` fails.
The GM may give a difficulty word instead, which stands for one of these, unless your game changed them:

| Difficulty | Means |
|---|---|
| trivial | automatic, no roll |
| easy | easy, doubled |
| standard | average |
| hard | difficult, halved |
| extreme | difficult, halved |

`+check spot-20` takes 20 from the chance for this roll, and `+20` adds 20. `/private` rolls where only you see
it.

## Example

`+check climb=difficult`

Output: `[SHEET] Alice rolls Climb (55%, difficult, halved: 28%): 1d100[23] = 23: a success (roll 9)`
