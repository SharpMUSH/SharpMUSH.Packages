You keep your own meters in play. Each takes `+<n>`, `-<n>`, a number, or `reset` to go back to where it starts.

| Meter | Runs | Starts | Command |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health | 0 to 5 | 5 | `+sheet/health -2` |
| Spirit | 0 to 5 | 5 | `+sheet/spirit -1` |
| Supply | 0 to 5 | 5 | `+sheet/supply -1` |
| Momentum | -6 to +10 | +2 | `+sheet/momentum +2` |

## Companions

A companion asset has health of its own, up to the most the asset gives. `+sheet/companion hound=-1` hurts it,
`+1` heals it and `reset` puts it back to its most. At 0 it is out of action.

## In an encounter

While you play in an encounter, your meters and companions change on your copy there, and reach your sheet only
when its GM keeps them. See [[Encounters|main:ironsworn_encounters]].
