An iron vow is a promise your character swears, with a progress track of ten boxes of four ticks each.

- `+vow/swear Find my brother=dangerous` swears one.
- `+vow/mark Find my brother` marks progress, by the vow's rank. `=2` marks twice, and a number below 0 takes
  progress off.
- `+vow/fulfil Find my brother` makes the progress roll: the full boxes against two ten-sided dice, with no
  action die.
- `+vow/forsake Find my brother` gives it up.

| Rank | Ticks a mark fills | Experience when fulfilled |
|---|---|---|
| troublesome | 12 | 1 |
| dangerous | 8 | 2 |
| formidable | 4 | 3 |
| extreme | 2 | 4 |
| epic | 1 | 5 |

A strong hit on the progress roll fulfils the vow and marks its experience; a weak hit fulfils it for one less.
On a miss the vow stands. Fulfilled experience goes in your log.

Vows stay on your sheet, whatever encounter you are in.
