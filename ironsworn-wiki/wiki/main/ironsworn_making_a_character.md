Making a character takes three steps. `+sheet/chargen` shows each, what is done, and what comes next.

## 1. Stats

Give your five stats the values 3, 2, 2, 1 and 1, one each, unless your game changed them. The highest is what your
character does best.

`+sheet/set iron=3` gives Iron 3. `+sheet/set iron=` frees the value to give another stat.

## 2. Assets

Take 3 assets, unless your game changed that: companions, paths, combat talents or rituals. Each comes
with its first ability marked.

`+sheet/assets` lists them and `+sheet/assets hound` shows one's three abilities. `+sheet/asset hound` takes it,
and `+sheet/asset hound=remove` gives it back.

## 3. A background vow

Swear the vow that drives your character, extreme or epic: `+vow/swear Avenge my kin=epic`. See
[[Iron vows|main:ironsworn_vows]].

## After approval

Once staff approve your character, they change your stats, and experience buys new assets and abilities. See
[[Assets and experience|main:ironsworn_assets_and_experience]].
