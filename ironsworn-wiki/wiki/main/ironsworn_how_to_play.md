Say what your character does. When the outcome is in doubt, the GM asks for an action roll on one of your stats.

## The action roll

`+check iron` rolls a six-sided die and adds your Iron: that is the action score, no more than 10. Two ten-sided
challenge dice roll against it. The room sees the roll, and the game keeps it.

| You beat | Result |
|---|---|
| Both challenge dice | A strong hit: you do it |
| One challenge die | A weak hit: you do it, at a cost |
| Neither | A miss: it goes wrong |

A tie goes to the challenge die. When the two challenge dice show the same number, it is a match: a strong hit
with a match is a triumph, a miss with a match a disaster.

## Adds and difficulty

`+check iron+1` adds 1 to the action score, for an asset or a bond that helps. `+check wits=hard` names a
difficulty, which adds or takes from the score, unless your game changed them:

| Difficulty | Adds |
|---|---|
| trivial | +2 |
| easy | +1 |
| standard | 0 |
| hard | -1 |
| extreme | -2 |

`/private` rolls where only you see it.

## Example

`+check iron+1`

Output: `[SHEET] Alice rolls Iron: 1d6[4] + 4 = 8 vs 5 and 9: a weak hit (roll 42)`
