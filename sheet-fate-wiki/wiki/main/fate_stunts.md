A stunt is something your character does better than anyone else, or a rule they get to bend. Two common shapes:

- **A bonus**: "Because I *am a trained duellist*, I get +2 when I *attack with Fight* while *facing a single foe*."
- **A rule exception**: "Because I *know everyone on the docks*, once per session I can *find someone who owes me a favour*."

Keep a stunt narrow: a +2 applies to one skill, one action and one kind of situation.

`+sheet/stunt <slot>=<text>` writes one: `+sheet/stunt 1=Duellist: +2 to attack with Fight against a single foe.` The game sets how many stunt slots a sheet has, and you may leave some empty.
