Every rating, roll and difficulty in Fate sits on the ladder.

| Rating | Word |
|---|---|
| +8 | Legendary |
| +7 | Epic |
| +6 | Fantastic |
| +5 | Superb |
| +4 | Great |
| +3 | Good |
| +2 | Fair |
| +1 | Average |
| +0 | Mediocre |
| -1 | Poor |
| -2 | Terrible |

## Rolling

A roll is four Fate dice plus a rating. Each die is -1, 0 or +1. The total is compared with the opposition: a difficulty the GM sets, or another character's roll.

`+check <skill>=<opposition>` rolls against a number, a ladder word such as `fair`, or a difficulty word: trivial, easy, standard, hard or extreme (+0 to +4 unless the game changed them). `+check <skill>+2` adds a bonus, such as for an invoked aspect.

## Outcomes

Each step the total beats the opposition by is a **shift**.

- **Fail**: below the opposition. You don't get what you wanted, or you get it at a serious cost.
- **Tie**: level with it. You get what you wanted at a minor cost, or a lesser version of it.
- **Succeed**: one or two shifts above. You get what you wanted.
- **Succeed with style**: three shifts or more. You get what you wanted and a little extra.

For example, `+check fight=fair` rolls Fight against Fair (+2). With Fight at Good (+3) and the dice at +1, the total is Superb (+5): three shifts, a success with style.
