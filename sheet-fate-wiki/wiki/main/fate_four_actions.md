Every roll is one of four actions.

## Overcome

Get past an obstacle: pick a lock, climb a wall, talk your way past a guard. On a fail you don't, or you do at a serious cost. On a tie you do at a minor cost. On a success you do. With style you also get a boost.

## Create an advantage

Change the situation in your favour: find a weakness, set a trap, take the high ground. A success creates a situation aspect, or finds one that was hidden, with a free invocation on it; with style, two. On a tie you get a boost instead. Using it on an aspect that already exists gives you a free invocation on it.

## Attack

Try to harm someone in a conflict. Each shift is a shift of harm, which the target takes as stress or consequences. With style you may reduce the harm by one to gain a boost.

## Defend

Stop an attack or an advantage someone is creating against you. Defending is a roll against their roll: if you beat it, their action fails, and with style you also get a boost. If you tie, they get a boost instead of what they wanted.

On this game every action is a `+check`: `+check fight=good` to attack against a Good defence, `+check notice=fair` to overcome a Fair obstacle.
