Fate is a game about proactive, capable characters who lead dramatic lives. You describe what your character does, and when the outcome is in doubt you roll four Fate dice and add a rating. Words matter as much as numbers: anything true about a character or a scene can be written down as an aspect and used in play.

## A character in brief

Every character has:

- **Aspects**: short phrases that say who they are, such as a high concept and a trouble.
- **Skills** (or six **approaches**, if the game uses them), rated on the ladder from Mediocre (+0) up.
- **Stunts**: things they do better than anyone else.
- **Stress** and **consequences**: how much harm they can take and what it leaves behind.
- **Fate points**, spent to invoke aspects and earned when aspects cause them trouble.

`+sheet` shows yours. `+chargen` walks you through making one, step by step: see [[Making a Fate Character|main:fate_character_creation]].

## Rolling

`+check <skill>` rolls four Fate dice and adds the skill's rating. Each die shows -1, 0 or +1, so the dice add -4 to +4, usually close to 0. Add an opposition to roll against it: `+check fight=fair`. [[The Ladder and the Dice|main:fate_ladder_and_dice]] explains the result.
