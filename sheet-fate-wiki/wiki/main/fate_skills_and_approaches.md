A game rates either **skills** or **approaches**. `+sheet/traits` lists the ones this game uses.

## Skills

Skills say what your character knows how to do: Fight, Notice, Burglary, Rapport and so on. Each is rated on the ladder; a skill you don't rate is Mediocre (+0). A usual start is one skill at Great (+4), two at Good (+3), three at Fair (+2) and four at Average (+1), a pyramid with each rating holding at least as many skills as the one above it. The sheet warns when a rating holds more skills than the one below it.

## Approaches

Approaches say *how* your character does things instead of *what* they can do. There are six: Careful, Clever, Flashy, Forceful, Quick and Sneaky. A usual start is one at Good (+3), two at Fair (+2), two at Average (+1) and one at Mediocre (+0).

## Rating them

`+sheet/set <skill>=<rating>` rates one, with a number or a ladder word: `+sheet/set fight=good`. You rate them until your character is approved, up to the game's highest starting rating. After that, staff change them.

Each rating comes from a ledger of grants: your own choice, a staff bonus, or something a package builds, such as a mode. `+sheet/ledger` lists them. The highest grant that sets a value wins, and grants that add go on top.
