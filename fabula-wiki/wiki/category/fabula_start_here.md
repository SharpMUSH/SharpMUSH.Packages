This game plays a JRPG-style tabletop system that is compatible with Fabula Ultima. Heroes have four attributes, each rated as a die from d6 to d12. They pick up classes and skills, and a check rolls the dice of two attributes against a Difficulty Level. These pages sum up the rules this game's sheet uses, in our own words. The full rules, the skills and the spells are in the Fabula Ultima Core Rulebook.

## How to play

- [[Character Creation|main:fabula_character_creation]]: attributes, classes, skills, traits and bonds.
- [[Checks|main:fabula_checks]]: rolling two dice, Difficulty Levels, critical successes and fumbles, group checks and initiative.
- [[Fabula Points and Bonds|main:fabula_points_and_bonds]]: invoking your traits, and drawing on a bond.
- [[Resources and Levels|main:fabula_resources_and_levels]]: HP, MP, IP, crisis, defences, experience, levelling up and zenit.
- [[Status Effects|main:fabula_status_effects]]: dazed, enraged, poisoned, shaken, slow and weak.
- [[Classes|main:fabula_classes]]: the fifteen Core Rulebook classes and what their first level gives.
