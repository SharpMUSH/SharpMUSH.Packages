A conflict is a fight, or any contest the heroes and their foes settle in turns. On this game it runs inside an encounter, so the damage and status effects land on the encounter's copies and leave the sheets alone until the GM keeps them.

## Who goes first

Before the first round one hero rolls Initiative, Dexterity plus Insight with their Initiative modifier, against the best Initiative among the foes. If it succeeds, the heroes act first; if not, the foes do. The GM can also simply pick.

## Turns and rounds

The two sides take turns one creature at a time: a hero acts, then a foe, then a hero. When one side runs out of turns, the other takes the rest, and when everyone has acted the round ends and a new one begins. Most foes are soldiers with one turn a round. An elite foe has two turns, and a champion has as many as its champion number, from 2 to 6.

On their turn a hero can attack, guard, use a skill, cast a spell, use an item or do anything else that fits the scene. Guarding makes them resistant to every damage type, and gives them +2 to opposed checks, until their next turn.

## Attacks and damage

An attack is a check against the target's Defense, or its Magic Defense for magic. A hit deals the high roll plus the attack's damage, of one type: physical, air, bolt, dark, earth, fire, ice, light or poison. A critical hit also gives an opportunity.

A creature can have an affinity to a damage type:

| Affinity | Damage |
|---|---|
| Vulnerable | doubled |
| Resistant | halved, rounded down |
| Immune | none |
| Absorbs | heals it instead |

A resistance and a vulnerability to the same type cancel out.

## Down, surrender and sacrifice

A hero at 0 HP is down. They can surrender: they leave the conflict, the heroes lose what the conflict was about or pay a price the GM names, and the player gains Fabula Points. A hero can also make a sacrifice, a last heroic act the GM and the player agree on.

## Villains

A villain is a foe that matters to the story. A minor villain has 5 Ultima Points, a major one 10 and a supreme one 15. A villain spends one to escape the scene, to shake off every status effect and recover 50 MP, or to invoke as heroes do with Fabula Points. When a villain makes an entrance, each hero gains a Fabula Point, and every Ultima Point a villain spends is a point of experience for each player at the end of the session.

## The bestiary

This game's bestiary holds foes of its own, from knee-high Bramble Imps at level 5 to the Ash Wyrm at level 55. A foe's level, rank and four dice give its HP, MP, Initiative and defences, and higher levels make its attacks hit more often and harder. Staff can add foes of their own.

## On this game

`+help encounter conflict` and `+help encounter attack` cover the players' commands, `+help encounter foes` and `+help encounter villains` the GM's. In short:

- `+encounter/initiative` rolls for the heroes before the conflict starts.
- `+attack <target>=<attribute>+<attribute>` attacks, and `+attack/damage <n> <type>` deals a hit's damage. `+check/invoke` works on an attack like any other check.
- `+encounter/guard`, `+encounter/turn` and `+encounter/surrender` guard, end a turn and surrender.
- `+foe` lists the bestiary; the GM brings foes in with `+encounter/foe`, attacks with `+foe/attack` and spends a villain's Ultima Points with `+foe/ultima`.
