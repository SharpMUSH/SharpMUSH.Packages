## Fabula Points

Every hero starts with 3 Fabula Points. A fumble gives one.

After a check, spend a Fabula Point to **invoke** your identity, theme or origin: reroll one die or both. You can keep invoking as long as you have points. Invoking can't turn a fumble around.

## Bonds

A bond's strength is the number of emotions in it, 1 to 3. After a check, a Fabula Point lets you add the strength of a bond to the total, once per check, when that bond matters to what you are doing. A fumble stays a fumble.

Bonds also help in group checks: a supporter who succeeds adds the strength of their bond with the leader, and the strongest such bond counts.
