Each status effect makes the dice of its attributes one size smaller, never below d6. They stack: a slow and enraged hero has Dexterity two sizes smaller.

| Status | Attributes |
|---|---|
| Dazed | Insight |
| Enraged | Dexterity and Insight |
| Poisoned | Might and Willpower |
| Shaken | Willpower |
| Slow | Dexterity |
| Weak | Might |

Status effects change the dice of checks, Defense and Magic Defense. Martial armour sets its own Defense, so a lower Dexterity does not change it. They don't change the maximum HP or MP. Resting ends them all.
