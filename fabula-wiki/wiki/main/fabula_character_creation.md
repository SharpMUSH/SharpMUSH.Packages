A new hero starts at level 5, unless staff set another starting level. Making one takes five steps; `+sheet` shows your progress the whole way.

## 1. Attributes

There are four attributes: **Dexterity**, **Insight**, **Might** and **Willpower**. Each is a die size. A new hero takes one of these sets and assigns it in any order:

| Set | Dice |
|---|---|
| Jack of all trades | d8 d8 d8 d8 |
| Average | d10 d8 d8 d6 |
| Specialised | d10 d10 d6 d6 |

## 2. Classes

Your starting levels are spread across one to three classes. Each class level gives one skill level to spend in that class. A class's first level also gives a benefit: more HP, MP or IP, and for some classes the use of martial weapons, armour or shields, or rituals. See [[Classes|main:fabula_classes]].

A class reaches its highest level at 10, and is then mastered. Each mastered class gives one heroic skill. A hero has at most three classes below level 10 at once.

## 3. Skills

Spend each class's skill levels on that class's skills. A skill can take several levels when the rulebook allows it. The sheet records each skill's name and level; its rules are in the Core Rulebook.

## 4. Identity, theme and origin

- **Identity**: a short phrase for who your hero is, such as "Wandering knight".
- **Theme**: what drives them, such as Duty, Anger or Hope.
- **Origin**: where they come from.

These three are what you invoke with Fabula Points. See [[Fabula Points and Bonds|main:fabula_points_and_bonds]].

## 5. Bonds

A bond ties your hero to a person, a group or an ideal. It holds one to three emotions, at most one from each pair: admiration or inferiority, loyalty or mistrust, affection or hatred. A hero has at most six bonds.

## Equipment

Armour, shields and accessories can add to Defense, Magic Defense and Initiative. Martial armour sets Defense to a number instead of using your Dexterity die. See [[Resources and Levels|main:fabula_resources_and_levels]].

## Approval

When the sheet is done, send it to staff with your application. Until staff approve the character you can change anything; after that, staff change attributes and classes, and you grow through experience.
