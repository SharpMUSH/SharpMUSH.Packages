A check rolls the dice of two attributes and adds them up. The two can be the same attribute, such as Might + Might.

## Difficulty Level

The total succeeds when it reaches the Difficulty Level (DL).

| Difficulty | DL |
|---|---|
| Easy | 7 |
| Normal | 10 |
| Hard | 13 |
| Very hard | 16 |

This game also has **trivial** at 5. Staff can change the numbers.

## High roll

The higher of the two dice is the **high roll**. Some skills and weapons use it, for example to work out damage.

## Critical success and fumble

- When both dice show the same number, and it is 6 or more, the check is a **critical success**: it succeeds whatever the DL.
- When both dice show 1, the check is a **fumble**: it fails, and the roller gains a Fabula Point. A fumble can't be changed by invoking.

## Status effects

A status effect makes an attribute's die one size smaller, never below d6. See [[Status Effects|main:fabula_status_effects]].

## Group checks

Several heroes can act together. Each supporter makes a check against DL 10 first. The leader then rolls and adds 1 for each supporter who succeeded, plus the strongest bond any of those supporters has with the leader.

## Initiative

At the start of a conflict, each side's leader rolls Dexterity + Insight, adding Initiative bonuses from equipment.
