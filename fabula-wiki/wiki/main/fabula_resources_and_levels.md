## HP, MP and IP

| Resource | Most |
|---|---|
| HP | five times the Might die, plus the level |
| MP | five times the Willpower die, plus the level |
| IP | 6 |

Each class whose first level gives HP or MP adds 5 to that resource, and each that gives IP adds 2. Some skills add more. The dice here are the attribute's own size, before status effects.

A hero with HP at half the most or less is in **crisis**. At 0 HP they are down.

Resting brings HP and MP back to full and ends status effects. IP stays where it is.

## Defences

- **Defense** is the current Dexterity die, plus what armour and shields add. Martial armour replaces the Dexterity die with its own Defense.
- **Magic Defense** is the current Insight die, plus what equipment adds.
- **Initiative** is what equipment adds; it goes on the Initiative check.

## Experience and levels

A level costs 10 experience and goes into one class. A hero's level is the sum of their class levels, up to 50. At levels 20 and 40 one attribute grows one die size, up to d12.

Heroes gain experience at the end of a session, from the Game Master. On this game staff give it, and a duty can give it as its reward.

## Zenit

Zenit is money. Heroes spend it on equipment, services and rare items, and earn it from rewards and from what they sell. On this game staff give zenit, for a reward or for something sold, and a duty can give it as its reward; you spend it with `+sheet/buy <item>=<cost>`.
