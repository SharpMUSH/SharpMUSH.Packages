## What it uses

The four attributes, their die sizes and starting sets, checks, Difficulty Levels, critical successes and fumbles, HP, MP, IP and Fabula Points, status effects, bonds and their emotions, levels, and the class names of the Core Rulebook, the High, Techno and Natural Fantasy Atlases and the two bonus classes, with what each class's first level gives. Section 3 of the licence lets a third-party work use class rules from any official publication. The help states these rules in its own words. It copies no text, art, logos or trade dress from the books, and holds no skill, spell or item descriptions: a player records a skill's name and level, and reads its rules in the book its class comes from.
