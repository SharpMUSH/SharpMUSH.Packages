Charge is a game about a group of characters facing obstacles and trying to overcome them. You decide how the characters meet those challenges, and how the world reacts. It uses a pool of six-sided dice and reads the single highest die; with no dice, you roll two and read the lower.

## How to play

- [[The Game|main:charge_the_game]]: the GM, the players, sessions and scenes, and staying safe at the table.
- [[Character Creation|main:charge_character_creation]]: details, the twelve actions, and talents.
- [[Action Rolls|main:charge_action_rolls]]: reading the dice, risk, effect, and the five steps of a roll.
- [[Momentum|main:charge_momentum]]: gaining it, and spending it to push yourself or add tension.
- [[Teamwork|main:charge_teamwork]]: assist, protect and set up.
- [[Consequences|main:charge_consequences]]: what a partial success or a bad outcome costs, and the determination roll to resist it.
- [[Stress and Recovery|main:charge_stress_and_recovery]]: the stress clock, condition, and getting better.
- [[Clocks|main:charge_clocks]]: progress and danger clocks.
- [[Progression|main:charge_progression]]: milestones and talents.
- [[Fortune Rolls and Flashbacks|main:charge_fortune_rolls_and_flashbacks]].
