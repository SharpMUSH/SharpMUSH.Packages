# charge-wiki

The Charge rules as wiki pages, for players to read in the portal's wiki and with `+wiki`: the
game, character creation, action rolls, momentum, teamwork, consequences and the determination roll,
stress and recovery, clocks, progression, and fortune rolls and flashbacks, with a page on the
commands that play them on this game.

This is a **content pack**: wiki pages only, with no objects or softcode. Softcode never reads these
pages. The sheet that plays the rules is [`sheet-charge`](../sheet-charge/); neither package needs the
other.

Based on the Charge SRD under CC BY 4.0, which is based on Blades in the Dark under CC BY 3.0: see
[NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md). The pages keep the SRD's rules text, shortened, without the designer notes.

## What it installs

The wiki collection is named **Charge**, and its landing page is **Start here — Charge**, pinned to the
wiki home.

| Namespace | Pages |
|---|---|
| Category | Start here — Charge |
| Main | The Game, Character Creation, Action Rolls, Momentum, Teamwork, Consequences, Stress and Recovery, Clocks, Progression, Fortune Rolls and Flashbacks, Charge on this game |
| Help | Charge License Notice (wiki admins only may edit or delete it) |

Every page ends with a line linking the Charge License Notice page. Page names start with `charge_`,
so the rules of other systems can share the wiki.
