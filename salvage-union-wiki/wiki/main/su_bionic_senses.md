**Augmentation Tree**
Passive

You gain your choice of integrated [[Thermal Optics|main:su_thermal_optics]], [[Zoom Optics|main:su_zoom_optics]], or [[IR Night Vision Optics|main:su_ir_night_vision_optics]]. This is installed in your cranium via surgical procedure, and grants your Pilot all of the Abilities of the chosen Module. You must spend AP instead of EP to use any of these Abilities. During Downtime you may undergo a further surgical procedure in a Med Bay to remove your current integrated optics and install another one of your choice from the list above.
