**Tech 2 System**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 1 · Passive

A simple, scrap-built, extendable antenna that helps pierce through the atomic background noise. This increases the Range band of any of your installed Modules or Pilot Abilities with the Communicator, Hacking, and Scanner Trait by 1. For example, increasing the range of a Comms Module from Long Range to Far Range.
