**Tech 3 Module**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 3 · 3 EP · Long Range · Turn Action

This Electronic Countermeasure device developed by Stefanus nullifies electronics within a radius, preventing them from being used temporarily, jamming comms, shields, and nullifying Targeters and hacking attempts.

When activated, all enemy Pilots, Mechs, and Vehicles within Range cannot activate or use any Pilot Equipment, Systems, Modules, or Abilities that have the Targeter, Hacking, Shield or Communicator Trait. This effect lasts for 10 minutes.
