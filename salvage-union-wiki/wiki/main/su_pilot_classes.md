## Hybrid Pilot Classes

Hybrid Pilot Classes allow you to advance your Core Class into a new Class, accessing a gamut of new Abilities and other Class trees. When you choose a Hybrid Class you gain access to all Abilities within the Hybrid Class Tree. You may also take Abilities from two other adjacent trees as noted in each respective entry.
