**Tech 3 System**
7 Slots · Salvage Value 4 · Far Range · Damage 5 SP
Traits: Ballistic, Hot (1)

Developed by Sakura Futures for use in wetwork against heavily protected targets such as Consul Mechs, this lightweight railgun was reverse engineered from an original Evantis design. It fires an electro- magnetic ballistic charge at a phenomenal Range.
