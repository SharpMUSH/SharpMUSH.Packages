Bio-Titans are hideous amalgamations of flesh and bio-engineering that roam the wastelands. Many rumours abound as to their origin. Some speculate that they came from outer space or are the result of failed corpo-experimentation. The Cult of Ascension claims they are a punishment for the material hubris of humankind. Whatever the reason, we do know that they are here and they are hungry.

## Bio-Titan Salvage

When any Bio-Titan is reduced to 0 SP, it can be [[salvaged|main:su_salvaging_a_non_mech]] as though it was Scrap. A Bio-Titan will can be salvaged for Bio-Salvage equivalent to its SP. Bio-Salvage can be traded for the equivalent of 1 Tech 1 Scrap. It cannot be used for Crafting or Repairing. It takes up 1 Cargo Slot and can be transported safely. While there are no common uses for Bio-Salvage, some say that there are corpo labs where such Bio-Salvage is in high demand; used in high tech medical applications and as seed material to create new abominations.
