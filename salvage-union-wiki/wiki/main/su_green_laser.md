**Tech 2 System**
4 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Medium Range · Damage 4 SP
Traits: Energy, Hot (2)

The Green Laser fires a solid beam of superheated energy at a target. This was one of the first lasers developed for military use and was used extensively on all sides of the First Corpo War. It remains ubiquitous to this day.
