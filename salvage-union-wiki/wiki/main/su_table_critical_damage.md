This table determines what happens to your Mech when it reaches 0 SP.

**Critical Damage Table**

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Catastrophic Damage** - Your Mech is entirely Destroyed. If you have an Escape Hatch your Pilot must roll on the Critical Injury Table. If you have no Escape Hatch or equivalent your Pilot is dead. |
| 2-5 | **System Destruction** - A System mounted on your Mech chosen by The Mediator is Destroyed. This can be chosen randomly or based on narrative circumstance. In addition your Mech is Inoperational and cannot move or take actions until it regains at least 1 SP. |
| 6-10 | **Module Destruction** - A Module mounted on your Mech chosen by The Mediator is Destroyed. This can be chosen randomly or based on narrative circumstance. In addition your Mech is Inoperational and cannot move or take actions until it regains at least 1 SP. |
| 11-19 | **Core Damage** - Your Mech is stable at 0 SP but Inoperational and cannot move or take actions until it regains at least 1 SP. |
| 20 | **Miraculous Survival** - Your Mech is somehow intact. You have 1 SP and are fully operational. |
