**Legendary Union Rep Tree**
Turn Action

You can place a distress beacon down that will be answered within one hour. You can choose from the following options, and they come to your aid. The Mediator decides what and who the individual Mechs and Pilots are.

**Corpo Mercenary Lance:** 4 Corpo Mechs of up to Tech Level 3 armed for combat.

**Salvage Crew:** 4 Salvager Mechs of up to Tech Level 3 armed with salvaging and rigging tools.

**Corpo Mercenary Squad:** 4 Corpo Squads of up to Tech 3 armed for combat.

**Pilot Salvage Crew:** 4 Salvager Squads of up to Tech 3 armed with salvaging and rigging tools.

**Wasteland Mech Mob:** 10 Wasteland Mechs of up to Tech 1.

**Wasteland Mob:** 10 Wasteland Squads of up to Tech 1
