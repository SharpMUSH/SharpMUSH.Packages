When you or a target in Close Range is hit by a weapon with the Ballistic or Energy Trait you may activate your Reflective Shielding to prevent the hit.

**Reflective Shielding Table**

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | The shield malfunctions. You take no damage from the attack, but it is deflected to a random target within Medium Range, which takes the full damage of the weapon. In addition, your Reflective Shielding System becomes damaged. |
| 2-5 | The shielding fails and you are hit by the attack as normal. |
| 6-10 | The attack deals full damage and is reflected back at the attacker dealing half damage to them of the weapon they attacked with. |
| 11-19 | The attack deals half damage and is reflected back at the attacker dealing the full damage of the weapon they attacked with to them. |
| 20 | The attack deals no damage and is reflected devastatingly back at the attacker, hitting them for 2x the damage of the weapon they attacked with. |
