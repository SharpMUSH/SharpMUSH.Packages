**Tech 4 Module**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 1 · Free Action
Traits: Heat Spike, Hot (2)

This salvager-designed Module reroutes your Mech's power to vastly increase the potential of its locomotive Systems.

When activated, your Mech may move an additional Range Band this turn. Your Mech gains 2 Heat and must make a Heat Check. You may only activate this Module once per turn.
