**Tech 2 Pilot Equipment**
Reaction
Traits: Heavy, Shield

Typically used in breaching and boarding actions, as well as for riot control, this large shield protects its wearer from harm. When your Pilot is hit by an attack you may destroy the Reinforced Polycarbonate Shield to take half damage from the attack and suffer none of its effects.
