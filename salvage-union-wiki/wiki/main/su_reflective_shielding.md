**Tech 5 System**
4 Slots · Salvage Value 3 · 2 EP · Close Range · Reaction
Traits: Shield

This shielding array of Stefanus design is specifically designed to reflect attacks back at the target.

When you or a target in Close Range is hit by a weapon with the Ballistic or Energy Trait you may activate your Reflective Shielding to prevent the hit.

Rolls on the [[Reflective Shielding Table|main:su_table_reflective_shielding]].
