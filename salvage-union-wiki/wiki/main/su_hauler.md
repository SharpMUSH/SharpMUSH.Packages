If you need a job done in the Union, no matter how dirty, you call a Hauler. There is never a job too big, or a deal too messy, that they won't shake your hand with a smile to do, whether it is hauling a leaky reactor through a hot zone or salvaging from a buried arco in a toxic waste dump. This can-do attitude makes them a shoo-in for leadership roles, as other salvagers look up to them for their sheer tenacity.

Play a Hauler if you want to play a hardy character who can haul large amounts of cargo, make tough deals, and protect and inspire their allies.

The Hauler can advance into the [[Union Rep|main:su_union_rep]] or [[Smuggler|main:su_smuggler]] Classes or focus their skills and take Abilities from the Advanced Hauler Tree.

## Core Abilities

### Salvaging Tree

1. [[Squeeze it in|main:su_squeeze_it_in]] - Temporariliy increase the cargo capacity of a Mech.
2. [[Expert Salvager|main:su_expert_salvager]] - Gain additional salvage when Area Salvaging. Bypass the die roll when salvaging a Mech
3. [[Emergency Salvage Drop|main:su_emergency_salvage_drop]] - Retrieve Chassis, Systems, Modules, Scrap, and Equipment from your Crawler.

### Trading Tree

1. [[Read a Person|main:su_read_a_person]] - Learn information about an NPC you're conversing with.
2. [[Let's Make a Deal|main:su_let_s_make_a_deal]] - Attempt to force a deal through on your terms using leverage.
3. [[No Job Too Big|main:su_no_job_too_big]] - When reduced to 0 HP or your Mech is reduced to 0 SP, take a Turn Action or Reaction.

### Leadership Tree

1. [[Folk Song|main:su_folk_song]] - Sing a song to set a mood for an audience of NPCs.
2. [[Behemoth|main:su_behemoth]] - Intimidate opponents and force them to accept your demands or flee.
3. [[Valiant Speech|main:su_valiant_speech]] - Inspire you allies with a rousing speech which heals and provides them with bonuses.

## Advanced Hauler Tree

1. [[Beefcake|main:su_beefcake]] - Increase your size and strength, as well as the size and strength of your Mech.
2. [[Mechapult Master|main:su_mechapult_master]] - Improve your ability with the Mechapult System.
3. [[Can't Stop, Won't Stop|main:su_can_t_stop_won_t_stop]] - Gain a special charge attack. Damage is based on Cargo carried.

## Legendary Tree

- [[Master Salvager|main:su_master_salvager]] - Restore destroyed Systems Modules, and Mechs to Damaged Condition.
- [[Hauling All Day|main:su_hauling_all_day]] - Any Mech that you Pilot doubles its Structure Points and Cargo Capacity.
