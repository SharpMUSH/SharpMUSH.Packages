**Tech 4 System**
9 Slots · Salvage Value 4 · Long Range · Damage 6 SP
Traits: Ballistic, Explosive (1)

A Herrsch 360° RealSteel Turret Enclosure controls this 120mm Cannon.

The turret understands and follows basic commands you give it such as attacking a target or defending an area. The turret acts independently of you in the turn order, and may attack once per turn. This does not take up your Turn Action. The turret may not Push.
