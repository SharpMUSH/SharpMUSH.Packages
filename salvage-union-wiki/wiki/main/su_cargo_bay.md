**Tech 2 System**
4 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Passive

The true staple System of the Union, used as much by our comrades who founded the Salvage Unions as we do today to haul loads across the wastes and back to the crawler. A Cargo Bay increases a Mech's Cargo Capacity by 10.
