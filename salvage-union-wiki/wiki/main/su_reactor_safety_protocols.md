**Tech 2 Module**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Passive

This Module increases the efficiency and cooling capability of your Mech's reactor, mitigating damage in the event of an overload.

When your Mech rolls on the Reactor Overload Table you may roll on this table instead.

Rolls on the [[Reactor Safety Protocols Table|main:su_table_reactor_safety_protocols]].
