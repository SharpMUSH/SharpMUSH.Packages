You have chosen the path of the Union Rep. You are the prime representative of your Union Crawler on any diplomatic mission, able to rub shoulders with corpos or share drinks with Union Reps from other Union Crawlers as you negotiate for the best terms possible for you and your crew. What will your leadership bring to the table?

The Union Rep has access to theUnion Rep Tree , [[Leadership Tree|main:su_hauler]], and [[Mechanical Knowledge Tree|main:su_engineer]]. You can take Abilities from any of these trees. You can also choose one Ability from the Legendary Union Rep Tree once you have learned all Union Rep Tree Abilities.

## Core Abilities

### Leadership Tree

See [[Hauler Class|main:su_hauler]]

### Mechanical Knowledge Tree

See [[Engineer Class|main:su_engineer]]

## Union Rep Tree

1. [[Union Representative|main:su_union_representative]] - Gain access to and ask questions about settlements and arcos.
2. [[Union Call|main:su_union_call]] - Direct other Pilots to carry out missions in exchange for a Scrap reward.
3. [[Recruit|main:su_recruit]] - Command an NPC follower who will assist you in your missions.

## Legendary Tree

- [[VIP Beacon|main:su_vip_beacon]] - Place a distress beacon to call for aid, which arrives quickly and heavily armed.
- [[Inspirational Union Leader|main:su_inspirational_union_leader]] - Inspire your allies and refresh their HP, AP, and heal their minor injuries.
