You have chosen the path of the Ranger. You have become one with the wastes, able to survive on vast treks, shrugging off danger, and picking off targets with ease. What challenges will you overcome?

The Ranger has access to the Ranger Tree, [[Survivalist Tree|main:su_soldier]], and [[Sniper Tree|main:su_scout]]. They can take Abilities from any of these trees. You can also choose one Ability from the Legendary Ranger Tree once you have learned all Ranger Tree Abilities.

## Core Abilities

### Survivalist Tree

See [[Soldier Class|main:su_soldier]]

### Sniper Tree

See [[Scout Class|main:su_scout]]

## Ranger Tree

1. [[Mecha Companion|main:su_mecha_companion]] - Gain a robot familiar. It can be equipped with Systems and Modules.
2. [[Snipe|main:su_snipe]] - Deal double damage on your next ranged weapon attack if it hits.
3. [[Infiltration|main:su_infiltration]] - Sneak into compounds, settlements and arcos to perform acts of espionage.

## Legendary Tree

- [[Mecha Packmaster|main:su_mecha_packmaster]] - Gain a second Mecha Companion in addition to your first.
- [[One with the Wastelands|main:su_one_with_the_wastelands]] - Ask questions, gain favours and easily navigate the wastes.
