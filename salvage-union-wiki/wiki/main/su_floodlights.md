**Tech 1 System**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 1 · Medium Range · Free Action

Flood Lights, light up a wide area in up to Medium Range of a Mech as a Free Action. This allows you to see more clearly in dark or foggy conditions. The beam can be focussed for tight or wide beam light.
