The Union runs on the backbone of Salvagers who come from all walks of life. This gives them a wide breadth of knowledge which they use to support the Salvage Union. An average day for a Salvager may see them out in the wastes, salvaging scrap in the morning, welding together broken mechs in the afternoon, and hacking into the corpo-net in the evening.

Play a Salvager if you want to be a jack of all trades who can draw from multiple different Abilities as suits your play style.

Salvagers can take a maximum of 12 Pilot Abilities, and can choose Abilities from any of the Core Ability trees from any Class. However they cannot advance into any of the Hybrid Classes, nor do they have any Advanced or Legendary Abilities.

## Core Abilities

### Mech-Tech Tree

1. [[Mass Field Maintenance|main:su_mass_field_maintenance]] - Repair 4 SP on target Mechs or Vehicles in the field.
2. [[If I cut this wire...|main:su_if_i_cut_this_wire]] - Damage Systems and modules on target Mech.
3. [[Mass Field Repair|main:su_mass_field_repair]] - Repair target Systems and Modules in the field.

### Forging Tree

1. [[Jury Rig|main:su_jury_rig]] - Craft improvised equipment from everyday items.
2. [[Mech-Guyver|main:su_mech_guyver]] - Craft Mech Systems and Modules from Scrap in the field.
3. [[Auto-Turret|main:su_auto_turret]] - Construct an Immobile Auto-Turret that can be fitted with Systems and Modules.

### Mechanical Knowledge Tree

1. [[Engineering Expertise|main:su_engineering_expertise]] - Ask questions pertaining to mechanical and engineering topics.
2. [[Talk Shop|main:su_talk_shop]] - Build rapport, and ask favours of other mechanics, salvagers, and workers.
3. [[Mech Acquisition|main:su_mech_acquisition]] - Track down and acquire specific Mech parts and Chassis.

### Hacking Tree

1. [[Hacking Kit|main:su_hacking_kit]] - Versatile tool for allowing Mech Module hacks on foot and vice-versa.
2. [[System and Software Hacker|main:su_system_and_software_hacker]] - Attempt to destroy Systems and Modules on target Mech.
3. [[Denial of Service Attack|main:su_denial_of_service_attack]] - Attempt to shut down target Mech in Range.

### Electronics Tree

1. [[Well actually...|main:su_well_actually]] - Ask questions about Systems, Modules, or Technology.
2. [[Techno Babble|main:su_techno_babble]] - Build rapport and ask favours of other Hackers and technophiles.
3. [[Holo Companion|main:su_holo_companion]] - Construct an intelligent, holographic AI companion, projected from a device on your person.

### Augmentation Tree

1. [[Bionic Senses|main:su_bionic_senses]] - Gain integrated Thermal Optics, Zoom Optics, or IR Night Vision Optics.
2. [[Bionic Arms|main:su_bionic_arms]] - Gain a set of integrated Bionic Arms. Gives an improved base attack.
3. [[Bionic Legs|main:su_bionic_legs]] - Gain a set of integrated Bionic Legs. Gives improved base movement.

### Salvaging Tree

1. [[Squeeze it in|main:su_squeeze_it_in]] - Temporariliy increase the cargo capacity of a Mech.
2. [[Expert Salvager|main:su_expert_salvager]] - Gain additional salvage when Area Salvaging. Bypass the die roll when salvaging a Mech
3. [[Emergency Salvage Drop|main:su_emergency_salvage_drop]] - Retrieve Chassis, Systems, Modules, Scrap, and Equipment from your Crawler.

### Trading Tree

1. [[Read a Person|main:su_read_a_person]] - Learn information about an NPC you're conversing with.
2. [[Let's Make a Deal|main:su_let_s_make_a_deal]] - Attempt to force a deal through on your terms using leverage.
3. [[No Job Too Big|main:su_no_job_too_big]] - When reduced to 0 HP or your Mech is reduced to 0 SP, take a Turn Action or Reaction.

### Leadership Tree

1. [[Folk Song|main:su_folk_song]] - Sing a song to set a mood for an audience of NPCs.
2. [[Behemoth|main:su_behemoth]] - Intimidate opponents and force them to accept your demands or flee.
3. [[Valiant Speech|main:su_valiant_speech]] - Inspire you allies with a rousing speech which heals and provides them with bonuses.

### Recon Tree

1. [[Gather Intelligence|main:su_gather_intelligence]] - Ask two questions about an area and recieve truthful answers.
2. [[Tail|main:su_tail]] - Track a target without being seen.
3. [[Survey Drone|main:su_survey_drone]] - Gain a Survey Drone that can be equipped with Systems and Modules.

### Sleuth Tree

1. [[Silver Tongue|main:su_silver_tongue]] - Glean what motivates an NPC and use it to convince them to do as you wish.
2. [[Forked Tongue|main:su_forked_tongue]] - Lie with confidence and great skill to fool even the most insightful.
3. [[Persona|main:su_persona]] - Disguise yourself and create a false identity.

### Sniper Tree

1. [[You Shot First|main:su_you_shot_first]] - Activate this to act first whenever initiative would be rolled.
2. [[Spotter|main:su_spotter]] - Chooose a target in range for an ally to make a free attack against.
3. [[Custom Sniper Rifle|main:su_custom_sniper_rifle]] - Gain a custom sniper rifle that only you can use.

### Gladitorial Combat Tree

1. [[Charge|main:su_charge]] - Charge a target within Range and make a Melee attack with bonus damage.
2. [[Overpower|main:su_overpower]] - Put and hold a target in a compromised position so that they gain the Vulnerable Trait.
3. [[Duel|main:su_duel]] - Compel a target in Range to fight you in single combat.

### Survivalist Tree

1. [[Wastelander Rapport|main:su_wastelander_rapport]] - Ask questions of locals and gain assistance when required.
2. [[Resourceful|main:su_resourceful]] - Find resources in the wastes and limit the effects of environmental conditions.
3. [[Custom Missile Launcher|main:su_custom_missile_launcher]] - Gain a Custom Missile Launcher that only you can use.

### Tactical Warfare Tree

1. [[Provoke|main:su_provoke]] - Taunt a target in Range to focus its attacks on you.
2. [[Tactical Retreat|main:su_tactical_retreat]] - Allow you and your allies to retreat safely to an adjacent Area.
3. [[Counterattack|main:su_counterattack]] - Use as a reaction to make a single attack against the target that attacked you.
