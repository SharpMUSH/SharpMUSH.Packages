**Salvaging Tree**
3 AP · Short Action

You can call in an emergency salvage drop that deploys from your Union Crawler.

When you activate this Ability, choose one of the following options and it will be delivered to you from your Union Crawler in 10 minutes. You must be in the same region as your Union Crawler.

- Any Mech Chassis you currently have stored in your Union Crawler.
- Any System or Module you currently have stored in your Union Crawler.
- Up to 10 Scrap of any type you have stored in your Union Crawler.
- Up to 5 of any Pilot Equipment you currently have stored in your Union Crawler.
