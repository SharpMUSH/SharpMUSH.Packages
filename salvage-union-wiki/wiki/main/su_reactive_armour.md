**Tech 2 Pilot Equipment**
Traits: Armour, Heavy

Layers of reactive heavy carbonate protect a Pilot against various types of damage, from ballistic and laser damage to explosive and shrapnel damage. Reduce all damage your Pilot takes by 1 to a minimum of 1 when this Armour is equipped. Apply this after other modifiers.
