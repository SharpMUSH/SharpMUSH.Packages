**Tech 5 Module**
2 Slots · Salvage Value 2

This Module combines [[Zoom Optics|main:su_zoom_optics]], [[Thermal Optics|main:su_thermal_optics]], and [[IR Night Vision Optics|main:su_ir_night_vision_optics]], into one. You gain the use of all three and may activate each separately.
