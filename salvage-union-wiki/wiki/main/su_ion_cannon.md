**Tech 5 System**
5 Slots · Salvage Value 4 · Long Range · Damage 3 SP
Traits: Anti-Shielding, Energy, Ion

This Weapons System fires a disabling bolt of ionised energy at the target. A target hit by this weapon cannot activate any Systems, Modules, or Abilities that have an Energy Cost on their next turn.

Stefanus forced Ascension PLC, one of their subsidiaries, to develop this as a countermeasure to their own Neura-Phage Mech, as Neura-Phage Pilots are known to sometimes be driven insane after spending too long wired into the cockpit.
