**Legendary Ranger Tree**
Pilot Equipment

You have trained to be able to construct and control an additional Mecha Companion. This follows all of the normal rules for the [[Mecha Companion Ability|main:su_mecha_companion]], but allows you to have up to two Mecha Companions active in the field at any one time.
