**Tech 5 System**
6 Slots · Salvage Value 5 · Long Range · Damage 7 SP · Uses 6
Traits: Anti-Shielding, Guided, Missile, Targeter

This Weapons System fires a bunker busting torpedo with an explosive charge that burrows into the earth and travels underneath a target, causing a controlled explosion beneath it making it effective against shields.

This weapon was used to bust open the underground home arco of the Spyre Corpo in the Central Wastes by TDA rebels. The ensuing massacre following the siege saw the corpo's oligarchs, executives, and elites summarily tried and executed, and its holdings permanently liquidated.
