The molecular structure of a target hit by Phantom's Morph Scythes begins to unravel with bizarre results.

**[Phantom] Morph Scythes Table**

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | The target is turned into another random entity of its type. For example, a Mech would morph into another random Mech, a Vehicle into a random Vehicle, a creature into another random creature. |
| 2-5 | The target's form discombobulates. A Mech replaces a random System and a random Module with another System or Module of its Tech Level. A creature hit has its flesh hideously rearranged, dying a painful death. |
| 6-10 | A random Module or sense organ on the target is phased from reality and enters the Nether Zone. |
| 11-19 | A random System or appendage on the target is phased from reality and enters the Nether Zone. |
| 20 | The target is phased permanently from reality entering the Nether Zone, from whence there is no known escape. |
