**Tech 2 Pilot Equipment**
Far Range · Uses 3 · Turn Action

A launcher for a flare intended to be used as a distress signal, beacon, or in a pinch, to light up an area. When fired, the flare can be seen from anywhere up to Far Range. It lights up an area within Close Range of its target for 1 hour.
