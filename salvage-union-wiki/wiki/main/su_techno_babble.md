**Electronics Tree**
2 AP · Turn Action

You can effortlessly enunciate a stream of esoteric and mostly incomprehensible thoughts about technology. When you talk to luddites and other such regular folk whilst using this Ability, you distract them for at least 10 minutes as they try to comprehend or at least politely nod and smile.

When you talk to any other Hacker or technophile, this Ability can be used to build rapport with them making them friendly to you. During this conversation you can request a single favour from them and they will do it as long as they have the ability to and it does not put them in significant danger or go strongly against their own morals or personal code
