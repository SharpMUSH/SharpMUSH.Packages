You have chosen the path of the Fabricator. Technology is an extension of yourself which you can toy and tinker with to your steel heart's content. What beautiful creations will you form?

The Fabricator has access to the Fabricator Tree, [[Forging Tree|main:su_engineer]], and [[Electronics Tree|main:su_hacker]]. You can take Abilities from any of these trees. You can also choose one Ability from the Legendary Fabricator Tree once you have learned all Fabricator Tree Abilities.

## Core Abilities

### Electronics tree

### Forging Tree

See [[Engineer Class|main:su_engineer]]

## Fabricator Tree

1. [[Field Fabrication|main:su_field_fabrication]] - Create Systems, Modules and Mech Chassis in the field.
2. [[Miniaturised EMP|main:su_miniaturised_emp]] - Gain a mini-EMP that when activated shuts down all electronics in Close Range for 10 mins.
3. [[Chassis Modder|main:su_chassis_modder]] - Modify your Mech Chassis to gain the Chassis Ability of another Mech in addition to its own.

## Legendary Tree

- [[System Miniaturisation|main:su_system_miniaturisation]] - Make Systems smaller to take up fewer slots and be usable as Pilot Equipment.
- [[Droned Mech Conversion|main:su_droned_mech_conversion]] - Your Mech can be piloted remotely up to Far range.
