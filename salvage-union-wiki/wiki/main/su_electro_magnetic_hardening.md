**Tech 4 System**
3 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Passive

This module hardens your Mech against Electro-Magnetic Pulse effects which typically would be able to shutdown your Mech and disable its systems. Anything with the EMP Trait or Ion Trait does not affect your Mech or any of its attached Systems or Modules.
