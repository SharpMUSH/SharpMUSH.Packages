**Tech 2 System**
3 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Close Range · Damage 3 SP
Traits: Ballistic

Fires a blast of grapeshot which rips through multiple targets at close quarters. The dirt cheap salvage cost of these made them a favourite of salvagers during the Reclamation of the Wastes, when the remnants of corpo-owned industry were swept away under a hail of shrapnel.

When you hit with this weapon one other target of your choice in Close Range takes 2 SP damage.
