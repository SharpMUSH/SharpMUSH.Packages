**Tech 5 System**
3 Slots · Salvage Value 3 · Reaction
Traits: Escape

Developed by Sakura Futures as a means for their pilots to eject from their Mech in safety and comfort. This high-tech ejection System allows you to escape your Mech in the comfort of a well armoured Ejector Pod. The Ejector Pod rocket is propelled from your Mech and lands safely via the aid of a graviton stabilisation system.

The Ejector Pod has 5 SP when deployed.

Rolls on the [[Ejector Pod Table|main:su_table_ejector_pod]].
