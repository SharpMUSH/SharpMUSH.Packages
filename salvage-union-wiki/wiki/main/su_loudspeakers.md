**Tech 1 System**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 1 · Long Range · Free Action

These externally mounted speakers can be clearly heard by anything up to Long Range from you. They can be used to communicate externally with those outside of your Mech or play some beats from your cassette deck.

The Immortan Raiders made effective use of Loudspeakers as a crude shock and awe tool during their devastating raids in the Ferrous Wars.
