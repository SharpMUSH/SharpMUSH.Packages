**Tech 5 Module**
2 Slots · Salvage Value 3 · Long Range · Free Action
Traits: Communicator

Developed by Ascension PLC, this Module connects directly to your Pilot's brain whilst in the Cockpit, and allows telepathic communication with anything that has the Communicator Trait within Range. In addition, you may choose what the output of your brain frequencies sounds like, altering the pitch and tone to your liking. This works as a [[Voice Modulator|main:su_voice_modulator]]. These communications are also encrypted, working as an [[Encrypted Comms|main:su_encrypted_comms]]. The Neuralink Communicator may also be detached and used by your Pilot in the field as a [[Portable Communications Unit|main:su_portable_communications_unit]].
