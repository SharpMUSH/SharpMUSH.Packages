You have chosen the path of the Cyborg. Augmenting your body with mechanical and electronic parts to give you powers beyond the frailty of flesh. Some Cyborgs find themselves losing grip of their humanity, others find a duality between their flesh and mechanical forms. All are welcomed by the Salvage Unions who find Cyborgs an invaluable part of the crew.

How will you adapt to your cybernetic form?

The Cyborg has access to the,Cyborg Tree [[Gladiatorial Combat Tree|main:su_soldier]], and [[Augmentation Tree|main:su_hacker]]. You can take Abilities from any of these trees. You can also choose one Ability from the Legendary Cyborg Tree once you have learned all Cyborg Tree Abilities.

## Core Abilities

### Augmentation Tree

See [[Hacker Class|main:su_hacker]]

### Gladitorial Combat Tree

See [[Soldier Class|main:su_soldier]]

## Cyborg Tree

1. [[Glanded Stims|main:su_glanded_stims]] - Gain additional actions on your turn, with the risk that you could cause yourself harm.
2. [[Modular Face Implant|main:su_modular_face_implant]] - Install a Module interfaced directly to your brain.
3. [[Bionic Endoskeleton|main:su_bionic_endoskeleton]] - You are now fully bionic, your HP is now SP.

## Legendary Tree

- [[Meld Form|main:su_meld_form]] - You are now Meld. You can replicate Equipment and make Meld Tendril attacks.
- [[Ascension|main:su_ascension]] - You are now a virtual being. You control your Mech by wire, and can project your image holographically.
