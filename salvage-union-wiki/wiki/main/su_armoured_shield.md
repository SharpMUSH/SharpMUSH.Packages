**Tech 2 System**
2 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Reaction
Traits: Shield, Wield

This metallic shield is designed to protect the wielder from melee attacks, handy when a Bio-Titan is chomping down on you.

When your Mech or a target in Close Range is hit by a Melee Attack you may use your Armoured Shield to block the attack as a Reaction.

Rolls on the [[Armoured Shield Table|main:su_table_armoured_shield]].
