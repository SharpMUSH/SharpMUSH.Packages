**Tech 3 System**
3 Slots · Salvage Value 1 · Passive

Layered plates of highly resilient metals, plastics, and ceramics that provide additional protection to your Mech.

This System increases your Mech's Max SP by 5 for each Composite Armour Module you have installed.

If this System is Damaged or Destroyed reduce your Max SP by 5 and reduce your current SP to match your new Max SP.
