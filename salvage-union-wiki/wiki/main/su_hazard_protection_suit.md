**Tech 3 Pilot Equipment**
Traits: Armour, Heavy

This all-purpose bio-hazard suit can protect you from the ravages of the wastes once you are zipped up all comfy inside.

The environmental suit protects you from dangerous environmental hazards such as radiation storms, cold snaps, or acid rain. In addition, you take half damage from any severe environmental hazards such as tornadoes, firestorms, or bubbling pools of toxic waste.
