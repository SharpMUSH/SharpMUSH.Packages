This allows you to flood any number of Mechs or Vehicles in Close Range with numerous false signals, overwhelming them with so much information that they temporarily shut down.

**Denial of Service Attack Table**

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | All Mechs within Close Range of your Mech shut down for 10 minutes. This includes your Mech and those of any allies. |
| 2-5 | The hacking attempt fails. |
| 6-10 | All targets must choose to either move or take an action during its next turn. |
| 11-19 | All targets shut down for one round. They are active again at the end of their next turn. |
| 20 | All targets shut down for 10 minutes. |
