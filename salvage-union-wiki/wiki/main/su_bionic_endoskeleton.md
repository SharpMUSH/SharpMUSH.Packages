**Cyborg (Hybrid Class) Tree**
Passive

You are now a cybernetic organism, living tissue over metal endoskeleton.

You undergo a complex and painful mechanical surgery that fully replaces all of your bones with a metal endoskeleton. Your Hit Points become Structure Points. All attacks with the Melee Trait your Pilot makes deal SP damage instead of HP damage.
