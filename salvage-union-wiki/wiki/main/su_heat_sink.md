**Tech 2 System**
2 Slots · Salvage Value 1 · Passive

A large slab of Scrap that acts as a passive temperature exchange harmlessly dissipating Heat from your Mech's reactor. A Heat Sink increases the Maximum Heat Capacity of a Mech by 1 for each Heat Sink installed.
