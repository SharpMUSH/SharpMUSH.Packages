Electronic warfare is a reality of the world we live in, and Hackers are at the forefront, delving into the cybersphere to discover new and ingenious ways to hack into Mech infrastructure, tap into corpo networks, and use corpo tech against their owners. Hackers are constantly experimenting with new ways to craft Mech components, and many augment their bodies with a range of modifications, finding their flesh as dull as a long system update.

Choose a Hacker if you want access to specialised hacking Abilities, to play a character with a wide range of knowledge about technology, or are interested in augmenting your character beyond the flesh.

Hackers can advance into the [[Cyborg|main:su_cyborg]] or [[Fabricator|main:su_fabricator]] Classes, or focus their talents and take Abilities from the Advanced Hacker Tree.

## Core Abilities

### Hacking Tree

1. [[Hacking Kit|main:su_hacking_kit]] - Versatile tool for allowing Mech Module hacks on foot and vice-versa.
2. [[System and Software Hacker|main:su_system_and_software_hacker]] - Attempt to destroy Systems and Modules on target Mech.
3. [[Denial of Service Attack|main:su_denial_of_service_attack]] - Attempt to shut down target Mech in Range.

### Electronics Tree

1. [[Well actually...|main:su_well_actually]] - Ask questions about Systems, Modules, or Technology.
2. [[Techno Babble|main:su_techno_babble]] - Build rapport and ask favours of other Hackers and technophiles.
3. [[Holo Companion|main:su_holo_companion]] - Construct an intelligent, holographic AI companion, projected from a device on your person.

### Augmentation Tree

1. [[Bionic Senses|main:su_bionic_senses]] - Gain integrated Thermal Optics, Zoom Optics, or IR Night Vision Optics.
2. [[Bionic Arms|main:su_bionic_arms]] - Gain a set of integrated Bionic Arms. Gives an improved base attack.
3. [[Bionic Legs|main:su_bionic_legs]] - Gain a set of integrated Bionic Legs. Gives improved base movement.

## Advanced Hacking Tree

1. [[Trojan Horse|main:su_trojan_horse]] - Gain control of a Mech in Range as if it were yours for 10 minutes.
2. [[Counter-Hacking|main:su_counter_hacking]] - Attempt to use an Ability with the Hacking Trait as a Reaction to being hacked.
3. [[Worm|main:su_worm]] - Hack multiple Mechs in Range with a complex worm virus.

## Legendary Tree

- [[Network Takeover|main:su_network_takeover]] - Hack the entire network of a settlement in range. Control it as you wish.
- [[Spyware|main:su_spyware]] - Permanent and undetectable Spyware you can install on a settlement's network.
