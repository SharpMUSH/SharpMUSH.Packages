**Tech 3 Pilot Equipment**
Medium Range · Uses 3
Traits: Silent

This weapon fires a dart loaded with a strong anaesthetic that knocks out the target. If a creature is hit with the tranquiliser rifle they fall unconscious for 10 minutes. Making any attack with this weapon reduces its Uses by 1. This does not affect Bio-Titans or Meld.
