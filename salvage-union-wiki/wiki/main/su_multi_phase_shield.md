**Tech 5 System**
6 Slots · Salvage Value 5 · 3 EP · Reaction
Traits: Shield

This multi-phase shielding array was developed as a collaboration between Aeon and Stefanus and protects a Mech against a wide array of attacks.

When you are hit by an attack you may activate your Multi-Phase Shielding to prevent the hit.

Rolls on the [[Multi-Phase Shield Table|main:su_table_multi_phase_shield]].
