**Tech 6 System**
5 Slots · Salvage Value 4 · 3 EP · Turn Action

Developed by Ascension PLC as part of their 'Matter Doesn't Matter' campaign, this allows you to teleport salvage directly from your Mech to the Crawler. This System increases your Mech's Cargo Capacity by 10.

When you activate this ability, you teleport all Scrap, Chassis, Systems, Modules, and People your Mech is carrying to the Storage Bay in your Union Crawler.

Rolls on the [[Experimental Teleportation Hold Table|main:su_table_experimental_teleportation_hold]].
