The 'V8.12.5' Aegis was developed by Aeon as a combat Mech intended to protect trade convoys or VIP corporate personnel against attack and ambush. The energy absorbers which power its Advanced Shield Projector were developed atop the great cloud spire of the corporation's home arco. Aegis units were subsequently utilised in the rebuilding of the Central Wastes following Impact Day, its shielding protecting construction units from debris, solar flares, and raider attacks.

**Integrated Advanced Shield Projector:**

**{cost=XEP} Reaction // Range: Medium // Shield**

When a target within Range is hit by an attack or something that would deal damage , you may activate the Advanced Shield Projector. The damage of the attack is reduced by 3x the amount of Energy Points you expend on this Ability. If this reduces the damage to 0 or less the attack also has no additional effects.
