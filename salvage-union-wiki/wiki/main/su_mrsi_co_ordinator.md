**Tech 4 Module**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · 4 EP · Medium Range · Turn Action
Traits: Targeter

Multiple Rounds, Simultaneous Impact. This Contour Aerospace-developed Module aims a focussed beam at a target which tracks their movement and coordinates and transmits it to other Allies, allowing you to coordinate attacks against them.

When activated, choose a target within Range. You and up to 2 Allied Mechs in Range may attack the target immediately with a single Weapons System of their choice in Range of the target. Make each attack separately.
