**Tech 1 Module**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · 2 EP · Medium Range · Reaction
Traits: Hacking

This firewall Module, made open source by the Opus Institute, is designed to prevent hacking.

If you or an Ally in Range are the target of any System, Module, or Ability with the Hacking Trait you may attempt to block the hack with your Firewall as a Reaction.

Rolls on the [[Firewall Table|main:su_table_firewall]].
