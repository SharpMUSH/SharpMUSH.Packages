**Tech 2 Pilot Equipment**
Traits: Heavy, Hover

This floating sled allows you to store cargo on it that your Pilot would not otherwise be able to carry. It ignores any Difficult Terrain or obstacles as it moves, and has a Cargo Capacity of 4 and 3 SP.
