The wasteland is a vast, hostile, and unforgiving expanse; and the Scout knows that land from end to end. Without their guidance, our Mechs and Crawlers would be lost in the wastes, prey to raider ambushes, rad storms, and mutated beast attacks. A Scout's knowledge of the wastes extends beyond the natural, allowing them to infiltrate wasteland sites and corpo settlements and bases.

Play a Scout if you want Abilities that allow you to snipe at your enemies, perform tracking and reconnaissance in the wastes, and infiltrate protected areas.

Scouts can advance into the [[Ranger|main:su_ranger]] or [[Smuggler|main:su_smuggler]] Classes or specialise and take Abilities from the Advanced Scout Tree.

## Core Abilities

### Recon Tree

1. [[Gather Intelligence|main:su_gather_intelligence]] - Ask two questions about an area and recieve truthful answers.
2. [[Tail|main:su_tail]] - Track a target without being seen.
3. [[Survey Drone|main:su_survey_drone]] - Gain a Survey Drone that can be equipped with Systems and Modules.

### Sleuth Tree

1. [[Silver Tongue|main:su_silver_tongue]] - Glean what motivates an NPC and use it to convince them to do as you wish.
2. [[Forked Tongue|main:su_forked_tongue]] - Lie with confidence and great skill to fool even the most insightful.
3. [[Persona|main:su_persona]] - Disguise yourself and create a false identity.

### Sniper Tree

1. [[You Shot First|main:su_you_shot_first]] - Activate this to act first whenever initiative would be rolled.
2. [[Spotter|main:su_spotter]] - Chooose a target in range for an ally to make a free attack against.
3. [[Custom Sniper Rifle|main:su_custom_sniper_rifle]] - Gain a custom sniper rifle that only you can use.

## Advanced Scout Tree

1. [[Flashback|main:su_flashback]] - Activate to retroactively have a solution in place for the current situation.
2. [[Camo Suit|main:su_camo_suit]] - Gain the use of an advanced Camo Suit that will make you invisible for short periods of time.
3. [[Wingsuit|main:su_wingsuit]] - Gain a Wingsuit which allows you to fly.

## Legendary Tree

- [[Wasteland Celebrity|main:su_wasteland_celebrity]] - You are known and respected everywhere and can get assistance when required.
- [[Teleport Beacon|main:su_teleport_beacon]] - Gain a beacon and controller. Activate the controller to be teleported to the beacon.
