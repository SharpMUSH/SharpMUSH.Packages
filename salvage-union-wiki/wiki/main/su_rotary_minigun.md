**Tech 3 System**
7 Slots · Salvage Value 4 · Medium Range · Damage 4 SP
Traits: Ballistic, Hot (1), Jamming, Multiattack (2), Pinning

This Evantis-developed Mech-mountable rotary ballistic weapon fires a horrifying hail of firepower at a target. This was the favoured weapon of the legendary salvager Flash, who was said to have held off an entire Evantis lance whilst defending their Union Crawler #129 during the Reclamation.
