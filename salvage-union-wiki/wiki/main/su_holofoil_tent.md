**Tech 2 Pilot Equipment**
Traits: Short Action (to set up)

A large, sturdy but light tent made out of a strong, waterproof, and insulating material. The tent can comfortably fit up to five people. Everyone inside the tent suffers no damage or effect from dangerous environmental effects such as radiation storms, cold snaps, or acid rain, and half damage from severe environ- mental effects such as tornadoes and fire - storms.
