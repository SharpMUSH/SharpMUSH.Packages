**Tech 6 System**
3 Slots · Salvage Value 3 · Passive

This System allows you to change the skin of your Mech to appear as though it is any other Tech 5 or 6 Mech or any executive corpo design of your choice. This allows them to meld seamlessly with other Mechs of a similar design.
