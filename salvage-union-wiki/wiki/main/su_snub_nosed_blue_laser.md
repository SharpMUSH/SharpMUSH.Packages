**Tech 4 System**
5 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Medium Range · Damage 3 SP
Traits: Energy, Hot (3)

An adaption of the Blue Mining Laser which fragments the energy into a myriad of small but deadly particles which blast apart a target like buck shot.

When this weapon hits a target in Close Range it deals 2x damage. Apply this after other modifiers.
