War and conflict is an ever present factor in the wastelands and whether fighting with sharpened sticks or plasma cannons, soldiers have always been at the forefront of a conflict throughout human history. Union Soldiers join from many avenues of life. Some are veterans of past wars, some are wastelanders who have earned their stripes by simply surviving out there, others defect from corpo merc life to the better one the Unions can provide. What they all have in common is a desire to fight for the Salvage Union which has provided them and their community with so much.

Play a Soldier if you want to play a combat focused character who can blow things up, engage in brutal melee combat, and survive out in the hostile wastes.

A Soldier can advance into the [[Ranger|main:su_ranger]] or [[Cyborg|main:su_cyborg]] Classes, or specialise and take Abilities from the Advanced Soldier Tree.

## Core Abilities

### Gladitorial Combat Tree

1. [[Charge|main:su_charge]] - Charge a target within Range and make a Melee attack with bonus damage.
2. [[Overpower|main:su_overpower]] - Put and hold a target in a compromised position so that they gain the Vulnerable Trait.
3. [[Duel|main:su_duel]] - Compel a target in Range to fight you in single combat.

### Survivalist Tree

1. [[Wastelander Rapport|main:su_wastelander_rapport]] - Ask questions of locals and gain assistance when required.
2. [[Resourceful|main:su_resourceful]] - Find resources in the wastes and limit the effects of environmental conditions.
3. [[Custom Missile Launcher|main:su_custom_missile_launcher]] - Gain a Custom Missile Launcher that only you can use.

### Tactical Warfare Tree

1. [[Provoke|main:su_provoke]] - Taunt a target in Range to focus its attacks on you.
2. [[Tactical Retreat|main:su_tactical_retreat]] - Allow you and your allies to retreat safely to an adjacent Area.
3. [[Counterattack|main:su_counterattack]] - Use as a reaction to make a single attack against the target that attacked you.

## Advanced Soldier Tree

1. [[Critical Strike|main:su_critical_strike]] - Gain a 'Nailed it' result on attack rolls with a result of 18 - 20.
2. [[Defy Death|main:su_defy_death]] - When reduced to 0 SP activate as a Reaction to be reduced to 1 SP instead.
3. [[Whirlwind Strike|main:su_whirlwind_strike]] - Make an attack against all targets in Range with an equipped weapon.

## Legendary Tree

- [[Omega Strike|main:su_omega_strike]] - Coordinate an attack against a single target. You and each of your allies makes a free attack.
- [[Steel Pact|main:su_steel_pact]] - Form a bond with a weapon. Gain an additional dice roll when attacking with that weapon.
