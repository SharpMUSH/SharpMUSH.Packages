Your Pilot's Callsign is the name everyone on the Union Crawler refers to them as. It is typically a nickname, but can be their actual name. Pick or roll on the Callsign Table, or have everyone else at the table choose one for your Pilot based on their impression of them. Callsigns may also change in play in this manner. Roll the die to pick a column, and roll again for your callsign.
