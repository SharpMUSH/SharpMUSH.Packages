**Tech 5 System**
3 Slots · Salvage Value 3 · Close Range · Damage 7 SP
Traits: Deadly, Energy, Melee, Wield

A honed-carbon fibre blade with a monomolecular energy field that can slice through a Mech like butter.

Nobody is sure who designed these, but they started appearing in salvage spots around the Nanite Sea following the first Meld incident.
