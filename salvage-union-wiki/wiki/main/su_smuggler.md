You have chosen the path of the Smuggler. Your cargo hides contraband goods, your comms unit whispers dark deals, and nothing is beyond your reach. What escapades will you find yourself in?

The Smuggler has access to the Smuggler Tree, [[Sleuth Tree|main:su_scout]], and [[Salvaging Tree|main:su_hauler]]. You can take Abilities from any of these trees. You can also choose one Ability from the Legendary Smuggler Tree once you have learned all Smuggler Tree Abilities.

## Core Abilities

### Sleuth Tree

See [[Soldier Class|main:su_soldier]]

### Salvaging Tree

See [[Scout Class|main:su_scout]]

## Smuggler Tree

1. [[Black Market|main:su_black_market]] - Gain access to a range of contraband Systems, Modules, and Pilot Equipment.
2. [[Pray I don't alter the deal further...|main:su_pray_i_don_t_alter_the_deal_further]] - Retroactively change the terms of a deal you've agreed.
3. [[Hidden Stash|main:su_hidden_stash]] - Smuggle contraband into even the most secure areas.

## Legendary Tree

- [[Knife Missile|main:su_knife_missile]] - Gain a Knife Missile, a small floating assassination device you control.
- [[Stealth Field Generator|main:su_stealth_field_generator]] - This makes you and everything in Close range invisible indefinitely.
