**Ranger (Hybrid Class) Tree**
3 AP · Free Action

You sharpen your senses, surpassing normal limits to pick off a target at Range. The next ranged weapon attack you make deals 2x damage if it hits. In addition, you may increase the Range Band of the attack by one, for example, Long Range becomes Far Range, or Close Range becomes Medium Range.
