**Tech 6 Module**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 1 · 1 EP · Turn Action

This Module utilises Dynamic Diode Rotarisation technology allowing a Mech to perform a series of complex movements in time with music typically meant to impress others as well as for recreation.

You may choose any dance you wish as part of activating the Module. Onlookers are highly impressed. It is not quite teleporting through walls, but trust me this, it is incredibly complicated to program a Mech to do.
