**Tech 3 System**
2 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · 2 EP · Close Range · Reaction
Traits: Shield

This Aeon-designed shield projector emits an electro-magnetic field that can stop ballistics impacts in their tracks.

When you or a target in Close Range is hit by a weapon with the Ballistic Trait, you may attempt to use your Electro-Magnetic Shield to prevent the hit.

Rolls on the [[Electro-Magnetic Shield Projector Table|main:su_table_electro_magnetic_shield_projector]].
