Engineers form an integral component of the Union, the grease that keeps our Mechs and Crawlers trundling across the great wastelands. They can take apart and put back together a Mech Chassis in the blink of an eye, and keep their salvage crew operational out in the wastes.

Choose an Engineer if you want a character with a breadth of engineering and mechanical knowledge who can repair and support you and your allies' Mechs, mount Mech parts significantly faster, and allow you to craft Mech parts in the field.

Engineers can advance into the [[Fabricator|main:su_fabricator]] or [[Union Rep|main:su_union_rep]] Class, or focus their talents and take Abilities from the Advanced Engineer Tree.

## Core Abilities

### Mech-Tech Tree

1. [[Mass Field Maintenance|main:su_mass_field_maintenance]] - Repair 4 SP on target Mechs or Vehicles in the field.
2. [[If I cut this wire...|main:su_if_i_cut_this_wire]] - Damage Systems and modules on target Mech.
3. [[Mass Field Repair|main:su_mass_field_repair]] - Repair target Systems and Modules in the field.

### Forging Tree

1. [[Jury Rig|main:su_jury_rig]] - Craft improvised equipment from everyday items.
2. [[Mech-Guyver|main:su_mech_guyver]] - Craft Mech Systems and Modules from Scrap in the field.
3. [[Auto-Turret|main:su_auto_turret]] - Construct an Immobile Auto-Turret that can be fitted with Systems and Modules.

### Mechanical Knowledge Tree

1. [[Engineering Expertise|main:su_engineering_expertise]] - Ask questions pertaining to mechanical and engineering topics.
2. [[Talk Shop|main:su_talk_shop]] - Build rapport, and ask favours of other mechanics, salvagers, and workers.
3. [[Mech Acquisition|main:su_mech_acquisition]] - Track down and acquire specific Mech parts and Chassis.

## Advanced Engineer Tree

1. [[Union Engineer|main:su_union_engineer]] - Improved repair; gain an additional 4SP when using Repair abilities or Systems.
2. [[This one goes to 11...|main:su_this_one_goes_to_11]] - Overcharge a Reactor beyond it's initial capacity to gain additional Pushes.
3. [[Mass Energy Recharge|main:su_mass_energy_recharge]] - Restore 10 Energy Points to target Mechs in the field.

## Legendary Abilities

- [[Tip Top Shape|main:su_tip_top_shape]] - Your Union Crawler no longer needs to pay upkeep and does not deteriorate.
- [[The Full Works|main:su_the_full_works]] - Repair target Systems and Modules in the field.
