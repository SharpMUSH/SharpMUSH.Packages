**Tech 6 Pilot Equipment**
Traits: Hover

This belt straps onto your Pilot and allows them to defy the laws of physics.

Whilst equipped, this allows your Pilot to levi- tate, moving over obstacles and terrain, and float onto buildings and other tall structures, gaining the Hover Trait for all of their move- ment.
