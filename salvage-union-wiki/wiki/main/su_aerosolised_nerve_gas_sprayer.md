**Tech 4 System**
5 Slots · Salvage Value 4 · 3 EP · Close Range · Turn Action

Emits a spray of caustic nerve gas that coats an area. Causes internal bleeding, vomiting, migraines, spasms, loss of bladder and bowel control and death.

On a successful hit unprotected organic targets must roll on the critical injury table. Pilots without hazard protection, radiation sealing or equivalent are affected in their chassis.

This does not affect Bio-Titans.
