**Tech 2 System**
2 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Passive

This is a separate cockpit which is mounted to the side of a Mech, and allows a Pilot to ride 'shotgun' on the Mech in support of the main Pilot, though unable to control the Mech itself.

They may fire from the Shotgun Pit with any equipped weapons and use any of their Pilot Equipment or Abilities.

When riding in the Shotgun Pit, the Pilot takes half damage from any weapons that deal HP damage and standard damage from any weapons that deal SP damage.

If the Shotgun Pit is ever damaged or destroyed, the Pilot riding inside it must roll on the Critical Injury Table.
