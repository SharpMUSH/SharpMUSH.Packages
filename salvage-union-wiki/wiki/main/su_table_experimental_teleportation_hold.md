When you activate this ability, you teleport all Scrap, Chassis, Systems, Modules, and People your Mech is carrying to the Storage Bay in your Union Crawler.

**Experimental Teleportation Hold Table**

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Everything you are attempting to teleport is destroyed or killed. |
| 2-19 | Teleport Successful! |
| 20 | Copy/Paste error. Everything you attempt to teleport appears on your Crawler. They are also still on your Mech. |
