**Tech 4 Pilot Equipment**

This specialised device scans and automatically applies layers of metallic oxide that fill and repair damaged Mechs based on their blueprints. This allows repair of some of the most advanced Mechs in production.

### {cost=2AP} Patch

**Turn Action // Range: Close**

You restore up to 5 SP to a target Mech in Range with at least 1 SP.

### {cost=2AP} System Repair

**Short Action // Range: Close**

You repair a damaged Tech 1, 2, 3, or 4 System or Module in Range to Intact Condition. It is now usable.

### {cost=4AP} Chassis Repair

**Long Action // Range: Close**

You repair a damaged Tech 1, 2, 3, or 4 Mech Chassis or Vehicle in Range to Intact Condition. It is now usable with 1 SP.
