**Tech 3 Module**
1 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Passive

This Sakura Futures-developed comms add-on encrypts all of your outgoing and ingoing communications. Anything you have installed on your Mech with the Comms Trait becomes encrypted. This means any voice or text communications you send or receive cannot be listened to, tapped into, hacked, or analysed. Anything with the Hacking or Scanner Trait does not work on them.
