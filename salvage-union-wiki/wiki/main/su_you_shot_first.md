**Sniper Tree**
2 AP · Free Action

You have a knack for being the first to act in an altercation.

When you activate this Ability you act first in the current situation. If using Group Initiative, assume you rolled a 20 on the Who Acts First Table, but you must be the first to act in the round and cannot choose to act later in the round.
