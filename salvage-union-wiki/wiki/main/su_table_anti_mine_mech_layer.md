When activated, your Mech lays a series of proximity triggered Anti-Mech cluster mines in a location or area on the map within Range such as a highway, stretch of wasteland, or city streets. The mines trigger when heavy targets such as Mechs, Vehicles, or Squads of troops enter the area.

**Anti-Mine Mech Layer Table**

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | The mines hit an unintended target such as an Ally or something innocent in the area. |
| 2-5 | The targets avoid the mines and they explode to no effect. |
| 6-10 | Deal 5 SP damage to one target in the area. The rest get clear unharmed. |
| 11-19 | Deal 5 SP damage to everything in the area. |
| 20 | Everything in the area is destroyed. |
