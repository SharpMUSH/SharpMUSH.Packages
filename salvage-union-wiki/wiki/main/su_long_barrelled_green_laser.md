**Tech 3 System**
5 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · Long Range · Damage 4 SP
Traits: Energy, Hot (1)

A Green Laser with an extended beam mount and heat sink allowing it to fire over a longer distance, whilst reducing its heat load. Salvagers adapted this tech from the original Opus Institute design and used them effectively in the Second Corpo War to take control of the great spires of the Aeon home arco in the Verdant Crescent.
