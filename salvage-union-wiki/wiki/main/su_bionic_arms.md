**Augmentation Tree**
Passive

You gain a set of bionic arms, surgically installed to any specification you desire. They provide you the following weapon attack, where X is equal to the Tech Level of your Union Crawler.

**Range: Close // Damage: X SP // Melee**

In addition, you can heal a Minor Injury during Downtime by spending 2 Tech 1 Scrap at any Crafting Bay.

You also increase your Pilot's Max HP by 2.
