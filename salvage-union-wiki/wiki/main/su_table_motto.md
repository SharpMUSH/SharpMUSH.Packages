Your Pilot's Motto is a phrase they happen to be fond of using. They may say this phrase, as a Free Action or Reaction, at a time during the game that feels appropriate, and another Pilot may re-roll the dice, accepting the second result. This Ability can be used once during a mission. A Pilot regains the use of this Ability following Downtime.
