**Tech 2 System**
6 Slots · Salvage Value 5 · Damage 6 SP · Uses 6
Traits: Explosive (2), Hot (1), Missile, Range: Close/Long

Developed as a highly destructive and versatile armament for underwater Mechs, it is not advisable to use these on land unless you have a good plan. Originally developed by Oceanic Rim, a subsidiary of Aegean Dynamics, Contour Aerospace conducted a short, highly hostile takeover of the subsidiary and solidified their power over both land and sea.

When fired in water, Torpedo Tubes are Long Range weapons. When fired out of water they are Close Range weapons.
