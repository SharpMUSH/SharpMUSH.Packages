**Tech 2 System**
2 Slots · Salvage Value 2 · 2 EP · Close Range · Reaction
Traits: Shield

Developed by Aeon as a means to combat solar flares during colony construction and asteroid mining projects, this shield projector is also highly effective against energy weapons.

When you or a target in Close Range is hit by a weapon with the Energy Trait, you may attempt to use your Refractive Shield Projector to prevent the hit.

Rolls on the [[Refractive Shield Projector Table|main:su_table_refractive_shield_projector]].
