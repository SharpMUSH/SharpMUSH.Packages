**Forging Tree**
3 AP · Short Action

Your ability to improvise extends to being able to craft mech parts from scrap in the field.

When you are in an area with Scrap at your disposal, you may craft any System or Module of your choice of a Tech Level equal or lower than your Union Crawler. You must spend 1 Scrap of the Tech Level of the System or Module or higher to use this Ability.

This Pilot Equipment gains the Unwieldy Trait, and is destroyed on any roll of 1-5 when used, in addition to any other setbacks.
