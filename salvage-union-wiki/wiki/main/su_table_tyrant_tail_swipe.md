If The Tyrant is attacked by any weapons with the Missile or Melee Trait, it may attempt to deflect the attack with its Tail.

**[Tyrant] Tail Swipe Table**

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | Tyrant fails to deflect the hit and its mammoth Tail is torn off. It gains the Vulnerable Trait until the start of its next turn. |
| 2-5 | Tyrant fails to deflect the hit and is hit as normal and takes full damage. It cannot use Tail Swat until the start of the next Turn. |
| 6-10 | Tyrant partially deflects the hit, it hits and Tyrant takes half damage rounded down from the attack. |
| 11-19 | Tyrant deflects the hit, it misses and Tyrant takes no damage. |
| 20 | Tyrant deflects the hit back. It hits the target for double damage. |
