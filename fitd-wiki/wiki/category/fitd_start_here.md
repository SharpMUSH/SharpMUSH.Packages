This game plays with a Forged in the Dark sheet: when your character acts and the outcome is in doubt, you roll
a handful of six-sided dice, one for each point of an action, and read the highest.

1. [[How to play|main:fitd_how_to_play]]: actions, positions, results, and resisting what goes wrong.
2. [[Making a character|main:fitd_making_a_character]]: sharing out your action dots.
3. [[Actions and attributes|main:fitd_actions_and_attributes]]: every action and the attribute it sits under.
4. [[Stress, harm and advancement|main:fitd_stress_harm_and_advancement]]: what you keep track of in play, and spending XP.
5. [[Encounters|main:fitd_encounters]]: scenes played from copies of the sheets.
6. [[Clocks|main:fitd_clocks]]: tracking progress toward something.

`+help sheet`, `+help check`, `+help encounter` and `+help clock` cover the commands in the game.
