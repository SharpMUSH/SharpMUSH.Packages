## Stress and trauma

You hold up to 9 stress. You mark it to push yourself or to resist a consequence.

- `+sheet/stress +2` marks 2 stress; `-2` clears 2, and `clear` clears it all.
- Stress past 9 clears, and your character takes a trauma: `+sheet/trauma cold`. Others are
  haunted, obsessed, paranoid, reckless, soft, unstable and vicious.
- At 4 traumas the character retires.

## Harm

`+sheet/harm 2=Cracked ribs` takes harm at level 1 (lesser), 2 (moderate) or 3 (severe). Levels 1 and 2 hold
two harms each, level 3 one. Harm at a full level moves up a level. `+sheet/harm 2=` clears level 2.

## Coin

`+sheet/coin -2 Bribe` spends 2 coin and notes why. Staff, or the GM of an encounter you are in, give coin.

## The ledgers

XP and coin each have a ledger of their own, apart from the ledger your action ratings are built from. Every
change is an entry: how much, the total after it, why, and who made it. `+sheet/log` shows the latest entries
of both, and `+sheet/log xp` or `coin` more of one. Changes in an encounter appear when its GM keeps them.

## Advances

Each attribute has its own XP, which staff give. Once you are approved, `+sheet/advance <action>` spends
6 XP of that action's attribute, unless your game changed that, on +1 to its rating, up to 4.

`+sheet/advance skirmish` spends 6 Prowess XP and raises Skirmish by one.

## The ledger

Your ratings are built from the ledger: every rating set and every advance is an entry. `+sheet/ledger` lists
them. Staff can take one back, so a mistake is fixed without losing the rest.
