Making a character is one step. `+sheet/chargen` shows how far you are and what comes next.

## Action dots

Share out 7 dots among the actions, at most 2 in any one, unless your game changed
those numbers.

`+sheet/set skirmish=2` puts two dots in Skirmish. `+sheet/set skirmish=` takes them out again, so you can
move them elsewhere.

Spread them with your character's past in mind: what they have done gives the actions they are good at. An
attribute's rating comes from its actions, so dots in several actions under one attribute also make that
attribute stronger when you resist.

## After approval

Once staff approve your character, they change your sheet, and you raise ratings with XP. See
[[Stress, harm and advancement|main:fitd_stress_harm_and_advancement]].
