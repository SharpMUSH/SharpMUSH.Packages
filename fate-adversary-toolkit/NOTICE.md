# Fate Adversary Toolkit SRD Notice

The fate-adversary-toolkit package's enemies (threats, hitters, bosses and fillers, and filler groups), obstacles (hazards, blocks and distractions) and constraints (countdowns, limitations and resistances), and the recipes it builds them by, are based on the Fate Adversary Toolkit system reference document. The library's Forward Scout, Baron von Darkness, its hazards, blocks and distractions, the Ticking Time Bomb, and the dragon's, the alien's and the wall's locks and keys are that document's examples, in its words. The other adversaries, the help and every message are this package's own wording.
