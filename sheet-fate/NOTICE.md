# Fate SRD Notice

The sheet-fate package's skill list, approaches, ladder words, aspects, stress, consequences, fate points and refresh, and the rules its help describes, are based on the Fate Core and Fate Accelerated system reference documents. The wording of the help and of every message is this package's own. Stress tracks of three one-shift boxes follow Fate Condensed.
