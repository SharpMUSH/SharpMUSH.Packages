Making a character takes two steps. `+sheet/chargen` shows each, what is done, and what comes next.

## 1. Abilities

Give each ability one of the starting scores: 15, 14, 13, 12, 10 and 8, unless your game changed them. Each score is
used once.

`+sheet/set str=15` gives Strength the 15. `+sheet/set str=` frees it again.

## 2. Skills

Train 4 skills, unless your game changed that.

`+sheet/set athletics=trained` trains Athletics. `+sheet/set athletics=untrained` takes it back.

A new character is level 1 and trained at most: expert training and higher levels come from advances after
approval.

## What you start with

`+sheet/gear Rope=1` lists an item, and `+sheet/coin 20` sets your coin. Your game may tell you what to take.

## After approval

Once staff approve your character, they change your sheet, and you spend XP on advances. See
[[Hit points, gear and advancement|main:d20_play_and_advancement]].
