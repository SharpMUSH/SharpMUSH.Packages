Every sheet has six abilities and a list of skills. Type a trait's key, or the start of its name, in commands.
`+sheet/traits` lists the ones your game uses, which may differ from these.

## Abilities

An ability adds its modifier: the score less 10, halved and rounded down. 10 adds +0, 14 adds +2, 9 adds -1.

| Ability | Key |
|---|---|
| Strength | `str` |
| Dexterity | `dex` |
| Constitution | `con` |
| Intelligence | `int` |
| Wisdom | `wis` |
| Charisma | `cha` |

## Skills

A skill adds its ability's modifier, plus the training bonus when you are trained in it, or twice the bonus
when you are an expert. The training bonus is +2 at level 1 and grows by 1 every
4 levels after that.

| Skill | Key | Ability |
|---|---|---|
| Acrobatics | `acrobatics` | Dexterity |
| Athletics | `athletics` | Strength |
| Deception | `deception` | Charisma |
| Endurance | `endurance` | Constitution |
| Insight | `insight` | Wisdom |
| Intimidation | `intimidation` | Charisma |
| Investigation | `investigation` | Intelligence |
| Lore | `lore` | Intelligence |
| Medicine | `medicine` | Wisdom |
| Perception | `perception` | Wisdom |
| Performance | `performance` | Charisma |
| Persuasion | `persuasion` | Charisma |
| Stealth | `stealth` | Dexterity |
| Survival | `survival` | Wisdom |
| Thievery | `thievery` | Dexterity |

## Example

With Dexterity 14 (+2) at level 1, an untrained Stealth adds +2, a trained one +4, and an expert one +6.
