## Hit points

You have 8 hit points for each level, plus your Constitution modifier for each level, at least
1 a level. A level 1 character with Constitution 14 has 10.

- `+sheet/damage 5` takes 5 hit points, down to 0.
- `+sheet/heal 3` gives 3 back, up to the most. `+sheet/heal all` heals every one.

## Coin and gear

- `+sheet/use healing potion` spends one of an item.
- `+sheet/coin -5 Rope` spends 5 coin, and notes why.

Once you are approved, staff, or the GM of an encounter you are in, add coin and gear. Staff give XP the
same way, with a reason.

## The log

`+sheet/log` lists every change to your XP, and to the coin and gear on your sheet: how much, what you had
after, why, and who made it. Changes in an encounter appear when its GM keeps them.

## Advances

Staff give XP. Once you are approved, `+sheet/advance` spends 10 XP, unless your game changed
that, on one of:

- `+sheet/advance level`: a level, so more hit points and in time a higher training bonus.
- `+sheet/advance <ability>`: +1 to its score, up to 20.
- `+sheet/advance <skill>`: untrained to trained, or trained to expert.

## The ledger

Your sheet is built from its ledger: every score, training, level and advance is an entry.
`+sheet/ledger` lists them. Staff can take one back, so a mistake is fixed without losing the rest.
