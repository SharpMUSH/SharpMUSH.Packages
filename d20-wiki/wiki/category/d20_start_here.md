This game plays with a d20 sheet: when the outcome of something your character tries is in doubt, you roll a
twenty-sided die, add what one of your abilities or skills adds, and try to meet a number the GM sets.

1. [[How to play|main:d20_how_to_play]]: checks, DCs, advantage, and natural 20s and 1s.
2. [[Making a character|main:d20_making_a_character]]: scores, skills and what you start with.
3. [[Abilities and skills|main:d20_abilities_and_skills]]: every trait and what it adds.
4. [[Hit points, gear and advancement|main:d20_play_and_advancement]]: what you keep track of in play, and spending XP.
5. [[Encounters|main:d20_encounters]]: scenes played from copies of the sheets.

`+help sheet`, `+help check` and `+help encounter` cover the commands in the game.
