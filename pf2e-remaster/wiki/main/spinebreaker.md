**Archetype Feat 10** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wrestler Dedication|main:wrestler_dedication]]

---

**Requirements** You have a creature [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] or [[Restrained|main:restrained]].

---

You squeeze your opponent in a vicious bear hug, putting intense pressure on their nerves, joints, or other pain points. Attempt an Athletics check to [[Grapple|main:grapple]] a creature you have grabbed or restrained, with the following additional effects if you succeed.

---

**Critical Success** The target is [[Clumsy 2|main:clumsy]] until the end of its next turn and [[Clumsy 1|main:clumsy]] for 1 minute.

**Success** The target is clumsy 1 until the end of its next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
