**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** Ghoran, [[Leshy|main:leshy]]

---

**Ghoran** - The warmth and light of the sun gives you life. If you rest outdoors for 10 minutes during the day, you regain Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier × half your level. You gain this benefit in addition to any healing from [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]].

**Leshy** - If you rest outdoors for 10 minutes during the day, you regain Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier × half your level. You gain this benefit in addition to any healing from [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]]. Leshies whose plant nourishment does not rely on photosynthesis require a similarly suitable environment. For example, fungus leshies need dark, damp environments and a pile of decaying plant matter.

*Source: Player Core.*
