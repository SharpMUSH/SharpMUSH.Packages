**Item 8**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Talisman  
**Price:** 100 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to a weapon

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This grisly molar hangs from a cord threaded through a hole just above its dried, exposed root. When you activate this talisman, make a melee Strike against an adjacent creature. That creature is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the Strike and until the end of your turn.

*Source: GM Core.*
