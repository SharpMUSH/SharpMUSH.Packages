**Class Feat 10** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Stance

---

The slightest distraction can provoke your wrath, and you're prepared to foil enemies' actions. As long as you are in this stance, you can use [[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike]] when a creature within your reach uses a concentrate action, in addition to manipulate and move actions. Furthermore, you disrupt a triggering concentrate or manipulate action if your Strike hits (not only if it's a critical hit).

*Source: Player Core.*
