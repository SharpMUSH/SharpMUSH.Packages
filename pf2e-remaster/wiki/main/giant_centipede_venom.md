**Item 1**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Injury, Poison  
**Price:** 4 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

Giant centipede venom causes severe muscle stiffness and general fatigue.

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 17 Fortitude

**Maximum Duration** 6 rounds

**Stage 1** 1d4 poison damage (1 round)

**Stage 2** 1d4 poison damage and [[Fatigued|main:fatigued]] (1 round)

**Stage 3** 1d4 poison damage, [[Clumsy 1|main:clumsy]], and fatigued (1 round)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
