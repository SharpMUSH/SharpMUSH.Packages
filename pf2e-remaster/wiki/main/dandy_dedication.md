**Archetype Feat 2**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Dedication  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Diplomacy|main:diplomacy]]

---

You are a consummate student of dignity, etiquette, and fashion. You become trained in Deception and Society; if you were already trained in either, you become an expert in it instead. You gain the [[Influence Rumor|main:influence_rumor]] downtime activity.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
