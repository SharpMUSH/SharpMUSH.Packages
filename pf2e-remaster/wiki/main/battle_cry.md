**Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

When you roll initiative, you can yell a mighty battle cry and [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] an observed foe as a free action. If you're legendary in Intimidation, you can use a reaction to [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] your foe when you critically succeed at an attack roll.

*Source: Player Core.*
