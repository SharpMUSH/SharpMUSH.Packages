**Class Feat 16** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Channel Smite|main:channel_smite]]

---

**Trigger** You miss with your Strike during [[Channel Smite|main:channel_smite]].

---

The energy from your smite persists for a moment, allowing you to pass it to a new target. Cast the 1-action version of the [[Harm|main:harm]] or [[Heal|main:heal]] spell you expended, targeting a creature other than the target of your Strike.

*Source: Player Core.*
