**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Kholo|main:kholo]]  
**Prerequisites:** sweetbreath kholo heritage

---

You smell of honey and savory things. Your circumstance bonus to checks to [[Make an Impression|main:make_an_impression]] if the target can smell your breath increases to +2. In addition, you can cast [[Enthrall|main:enthrall]] as a 3rd-rank occult innate spell once per day, except the spell has a range of 30 feet and the olfactory trait instead of the auditory trait. Targets don't gain any circumstance bonus for disagreeing with you.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
