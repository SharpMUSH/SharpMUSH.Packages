**Item 1**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Mechanical, Snare, Trap  
**Price:** 3 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

This basic snare consists of hidden spikes that rely on a creature's momentum to lacerate or potentially impale it as it enters the snare's square, dealing 2d8 piercing damage. The creature must attempt a basic DC 17 Reflex saving throw.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
