**Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Crafting|main:crafting]]; [[Specialty Crafting|main:specialty_crafting]]

---

You craft flawless creations with great efficiency. Whenever you roll a success at a Crafting check to make an item of the type you chose with [[Specialty Crafting|main:specialty_crafting]], you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core.*
