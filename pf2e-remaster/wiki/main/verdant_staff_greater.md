**Item 12**  
**Traits:** Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 1750 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

This oak branch grows leaves in spring that change color in autumn and shed in winter. While wielding it, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to checks to identify plants and fungi.

---

**Activate** Cast a Spell

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrips** [[Tangle Vine|main:tangle_vine]]
- **1st** [[Runic Body|main:runic_body]], [[Runic Weapon|main:runic_weapon]]
- **2nd** [[Entangling Flora|main:entangling_flora]], [[Oaken Resilience|main:oaken_resilience]], [[One with Plants|main:one_with_plants]], [[Shape Wood|main:shape_wood]]
- **3rd** [[Wall of Thorns|main:wall_of_thorns]], [[Speak with Plants|main:speak_with_plants]]
- **4th** [[Oaken Resilience|main:oaken_resilience]], [[Speak with Plants|main:speak_with_plants]]
- **5th** [[Plant Form|main:plant_form]], [[Wall of Thorns|main:wall_of_thorns]]

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
