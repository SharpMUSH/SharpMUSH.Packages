**Ritual 6**  
**Traits:** Uncommon  
**Cast:** 1 day  
**Cost:** faerie circle ingredients worth a total value of 1 gp × the spell rank × the target's level  
**Range:** 100 miles  
**Targets:** 1 animal, beast, fey, fungus, or plant  
**Duration:** see text  
**Primary check:** Nature (master)  
**Secondary casters:** 4  
**Secondary checks:** Crafting, Diplomacy, Survival

---

This functions as [[Planar Servitor|main:planar_servitor]] except you craft a faerie circle and call an animal, beast, fey, fungus, or plant from within 100 miles.

*Source: Player Core.*
