You are frozen in place.

- You are [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]].
- You can't act except to Recall Knowledge and use actions that need only your mind, as the GM decides.
- Your senses work only where you can perceive without moving, so you can't Seek.

*Source: Player Core, p. 445.*
