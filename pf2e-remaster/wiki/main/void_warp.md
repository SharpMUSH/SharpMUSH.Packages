**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Cantrip, Concentrate, Manipulate, Void  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 living creature  
**Defense:** basic Fortitude

---

You call upon the Void to harm life force. The target takes 2d4 void damage with a basic Fortitude save. On a critical failure, the target is also [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]] until the start of your next turn.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.

*Source: Player Core.*
