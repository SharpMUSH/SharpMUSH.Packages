**Key attribute:** Dexterity

Balance, agility and moving through tight or tricky places, and keeping your footing in the air.

Your Acrobatics modifier is your Dexterity modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Acrobatics (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Balance|main:balance]]
- [[Tumble Through|main:tumble_through]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Maneuver in Flight|main:maneuver_in_flight]]
- [[Squeeze|main:squeeze]]

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Acrobatics:

- **Level 1:** [[Acrobatic Performer|main:acrobatic_performer]], [[Cat Fall|main:cat_fall]], [[Quick Squeeze|main:quick_squeeze]], [[Steady Balance|main:steady_balance]]
- **Level 2:** [[Nimble Crawl|main:nimble_crawl]], [[Slippery Prey|main:slippery_prey]], [[Tumbling Teamwork|main:tumbling_teamwork]]
- **Level 4:** [[Rope Runner|main:rope_runner]]
- **Level 7:** [[Aerobatics Mastery|main:aerobatics_mastery]], [[Graceful Leaper|main:graceful_leaper]], [[Kip Up|main:kip_up]], [[Tumbling Theft|main:tumbling_theft]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
