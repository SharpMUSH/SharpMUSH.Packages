**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Dwarf|main:dwarf]]

---

Your ancestors lived and worked among the ancient stones of the mountains or the depths of the earth. This makes you solid as a rock when you plant your feet. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Fortitude or Reflex DC against attempts to [[Reposition|main:reposition]], [[Shove|main:shove]], or [[Trip|main:trip]] you. This bonus also applies to saving throws against spells or effects that attempt to force you to move or knock you [[Prone|main:prone]].

In addition, if any effect would force you to move 10 feet or more, you are moved only half the distance.

*Source: Player Core.*
