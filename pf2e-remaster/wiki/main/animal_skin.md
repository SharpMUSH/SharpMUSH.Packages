**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Morph, Primal  
**Prerequisites:** [[Animal Instinct|main:animal_instinct]]

---

Your proficiency in unarmored defense increases to expert. When you are raging and unarmored, your skin transforms into a thick hide. You gain a +2 item bonus to AC (+3 if you have the [[Greater Juggernaut|main:greater_juggernaut]] class feature). The thickness of your hide gives you a Dexterity modifier cap to your AC of +3. This item bonus to AC is cumulative with [[Armor Potency|main:armor_potency_1]] runes on your explorer's clothing, [[Mystic Armor|main:mystic_armor]], and [[Bands of Force|main:bands_of_force]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
