**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]

---

You have just the thing for the situation. You gain the [[Prescient Planner|main:prescient_planner]] and [[Prescient Consumable|main:prescient_consumable]] feats.

When using Prescient Planner, you can pull out the item as a 2-action activity to remove your backpack (or a similar container) and draw the item, rather than taking 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
