**Class Feature 9**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Class:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]

---

You always find the weak points in your foes' defenses when they're on unfavorable terrain. Enemies are [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] to you if they're in difficult terrain.
