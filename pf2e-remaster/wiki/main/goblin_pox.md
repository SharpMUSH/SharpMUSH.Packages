**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Disease, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Fortitude

---

Your touch afflicts the target with goblin pox, an irritating allergenic rash. The target must attempt a Fortitude save.

---

**Goblin Pox** (disease) **Level** 1; Creatures that have the goblin trait and goblin dogs are immune

**Stage 1** [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]] (1 round)

**Stage 2** [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]] and [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]] (1 round)

**Stage 3** [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]] and the creature can't reduce its Sickened value below 1 (1 day)

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]].

**Failure** The target is afflicted with goblin pox at stage 1.

**Critical Failure** The target is afflicted with goblin pox at stage 2.

*Source: Player Core.*
