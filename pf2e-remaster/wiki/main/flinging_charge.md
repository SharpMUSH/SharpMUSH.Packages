**Class Feat 10** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Flourish

---

You throw a weapon to distract your opponent as you close the gap to begin your assault. You Stride twice; at any point during this movement, you can make a ranged Strike with a thrown weapon. If the Strike hits, the target is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the next melee attack that you attempt against it before the end of your current turn.

*Source: Player Core.*
