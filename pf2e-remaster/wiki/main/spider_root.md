**Item 9**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Contact, Poison  
**Price:** 110 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

A paste made by mashing the fine, threadlike roots of a certain creeper vine, spider root renders a victim clumsy and maladroit.

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 28 Fortitude

**Onset** 1 minute

**Maximum Duration** 6 minutes

**Stage 1** 3d6 poison damage and [[Clumsy 1|main:clumsy]] (1 minute)

**Stage 2** 4d6 poison damage and [[Clumsy 2|main:clumsy]] (1 minute)

**Stage 3** 6d6 poison damage and [[Clumsy 3|main:clumsy]] (1 minute)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
