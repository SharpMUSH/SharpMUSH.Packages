**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Healing, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Medicine|main:medicine]]

---

You have practice combating plague, and your patients are less likely to succumb to the same disease again for a time.

When you successfully [[Treat a Disease|main:treat_disease]] on someone and they fully recover from the disease, they gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against that same disease for 1 week.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
