**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Primal, [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]  
**Prerequisites:** bloodline that grants primal spells

---

You can call upon the creatures of the wild for aid. You gain an additional spell slot of your highest rank, which you can use only to cast [[Summon Animal|main:summon_animal]] or [[Summon Plant or Fungus|main:summon_plant_or_fungus]]. You can cast either of these spells using that spell slot, even if it they aren't in your spell repertoire.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
