**Focus 5**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 hunted prey  
**Duration:** 10 minutes

---

Your target glows with a magical aura visible only to you and those who follow your lead. Your target is visible to you and others sharing your [[Hunt Prey|main:hunt_prey_class_feature]] benefits even if it wouldn't normally be due to lighting or the [[Concealed|main:concealed]] or [[Invisible|main:invisible]] conditions, though cover from opaque objects still blocks your sight. You ignore the flat check against the target due to the Concealed condition, and the target isn't automatically [[Hidden|main:hidden]] from you due to darkness or being Invisible.

*Source: Player Core.*
