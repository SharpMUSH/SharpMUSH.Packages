**Action** (free action)  
**Traits:** Incapacitation, [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

**Trigger** Your Strike hits an [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] creature and deals damage.

---

The target attempts a Fortitude save against your class DC. It then becomes temporarily immune to your Master Strike for 1 day.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Enfeebled 2|main:enfeebled]] until the end of your next turn.

**Failure** The target is [[Paralyzed|main:paralyzed]] for 4 rounds.

**Critical Failure** The target is paralyzed for 4 rounds, knocked [[Unconscious|main:unconscious]] for 2 hours, or killed (your choice).

*Source: Player Core.*
