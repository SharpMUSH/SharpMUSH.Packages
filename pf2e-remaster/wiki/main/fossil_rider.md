**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Lizardfolk|main:lizardfolk]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Bone Magic|main:bone_magic]]

---

Using your ancestors' bones, you manifest their spirits around you in a fossilized form. You can cast [[Mask of Terror|main:mask_of_terror]] on yourself as an innate spell once per day. When you do, you appear as a skeletal lizardfolk ancestor, twisted in a way that inspires the deepest fears of those who observe you.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
