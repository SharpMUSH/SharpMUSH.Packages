**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Medicine|main:medicine]]

---

Your surgery can bring a patient back from the brink of death, but might push them over the edge. When you [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]], you can deal 1d8 slashing damage to your patient just before applying the effects of [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]].

If you do, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to your Medicine check to Treat Wounds, and if you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
