**Item 6**  
**Traits:** Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 230 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** worn

---

This sprig of berry-festooned holly and mistletoe doesn't wilt or rot. It can be used as a primal locus, and it grants a +1 item bonus to Nature checks while you wear it.

---

**Activate—Anoint** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

**Effect** You squeeze juice from one of the berries and smear it onto a weapon made primarily of wood to cast [[Runic Weapon|main:runic_weapon]] on it, or onto a creature to cast [[Runic Body|main:runic_body]] on it.

---

**Activate—Bind** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

**Effect** You touch the sprig, then a tree to cast [[One with Plants|main:one_with_plants]] upon yourself, turning into a vine on the touched tree.

*Source: GM Core.*
