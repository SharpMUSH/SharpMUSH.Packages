**Class Feat 16**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]  
**Prerequisites:** unholy

---

Your life has made you a nexus for your deity's vile power. You're continuously surrounded by a [[Bane|main:bane]] spell, with a spell rank equal to half your level (rounded up). The radius is 15 feet, and you can't increase it. You can Dismiss the spell; if you do, it returns automatically after 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core.*
