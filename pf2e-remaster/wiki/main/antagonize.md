**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

Your taunts and threats earn your foes' ire. When you successfully [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] a creature, its [[Frightened|main:frightened]] condition can't decrease to less than 1 at the end of its turn until it either uses a hostile action against you or can no longer observe or sense you for at least 1 round.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
