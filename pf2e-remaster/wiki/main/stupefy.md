**Spell 2**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** varies

---

You dull the target's mind, depending on its Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Stupefied 1|main:stupefied]] until the start of your next turn.

**Failure** The target is [[Stupefied 2|main:stupefied]] for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Stupefied 3|main:stupefied]] for 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core.*
