**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

To you, a fight is a kind of performance art, and you command your foes' attention with mesmerizing motions. You are trained in Performance and gain the [[Fascinating Performance|main:fascinating_performance]] skill feat. When you [[Perform|main:perform]], the action gains the bravado trait.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
