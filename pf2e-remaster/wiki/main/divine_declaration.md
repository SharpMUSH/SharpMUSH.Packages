**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]

---

You can call forth a holy or unholy word from the realms beyond to punish your foes. Once per day, you can cast [[Divine Decree|main:divine_decree]] as a 7th-rank divine innate spell.
