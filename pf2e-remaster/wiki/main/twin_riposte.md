**Class Feat 10** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], [[Ranger|main:ranger]]

---

**Trigger** A creature within your reach critically fails to Strike you.

**Requirements** You are benefiting from [[Twin Parry|main:twin_parry]].

---

A clever parry with one weapon leaves your opponent open to an attack with the other weapon. Make a melee Strike or [[Disarm|main:disarm]] action against the triggering opponent.

*Source: Player Core.*
