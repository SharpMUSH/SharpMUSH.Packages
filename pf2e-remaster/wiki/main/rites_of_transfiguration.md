**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** [[Witch|main:witch]]

---

You can adapt your patron's power to transform other creatures into forms more befitting their behavior or your whims. Your familiar learns [[Cursed Metamorphosis|main:cursed_metamorphosis]], even if *cursed metamorphosis* isn't on your tradition's spell list. By spending 10 minutes in communion with your familiar, you can replace one spell of 6th-rank or higher you've prepared in one of your witch spell slots with a *cursed metamorphosis* spell of the same rank.

*Source: Player Core.*
