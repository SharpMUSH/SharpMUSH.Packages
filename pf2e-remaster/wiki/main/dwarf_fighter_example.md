An original level-1 example for [[Character Creation Guide|main:character_creation_guide]] and [[First Encounter|main:first_encounter]]. This is a particular selection of options, not an automatic character validator. Confirm campaign access with your GM.

## Choices and attribute ledger

Choose [[Dwarf|main:dwarf]], [[Rock Dwarf|main:rock_dwarf]], [[Warrior|main:warrior]] and [[Fighter|main:fighter]], with Strength as the class key attribute. Select [[Unburdened Iron|main:unburdened_iron]] as the ancestry feat and [[Vicious Swing|main:vicious_swing]] as the fighter feat. Warrior supplies [[Intimidating Glare|main:intimidating_glare]].

| Allocation | Str | Dex | Con | Int | Wis | Cha |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Dwarf: Con, Wis, free Str; Cha flaw | +1 | 0 | +1 | 0 | +1 | −1 |
| Warrior: restricted Str, free Con | +2 | 0 | +2 | 0 | +1 | −1 |
| Fighter: key Str | +3 | 0 | +2 | 0 | +1 | −1 |
| Four free: Str, Dex, Con, Wis | **+4** | **+1** | **+3** | **0** | **+2** | **−1** |

Each row awards its boosts to distinct attributes. The ancestry's free boost is not used on its already-boosted Constitution or Wisdom. The background uses Strength and Constitution once each. The final row uses four different attributes.

Record Medium size, Dwarf and Humanoid traits, Common and Dwarven, darkvision, and base Speed 20 feet. Intelligence +0 grants no additional languages. Darkvision sees in darkness and dim light, without color in darkness. Rock Dwarf gives a conditional +2 circumstance bonus against Reposition, Shove and Trip to the relevant Fortitude or Reflex DC; it also applies to saves against effects that force movement or knock you prone. Forced movement of at least 10 feet is halved. Keep these conditional benefits separate from the ordinary saves below.

## Training and features

Warrior trains Intimidation and Warfare Lore. Choose Athletics from Fighter's Acrobatics-or-Athletics choice. Its three additional skills (3 + Intelligence 0) are Medicine, Crafting and Survival. That gives six trained skills including the Lore subject, with no duplicates. Choosing Intimidation again would require replacing that duplicate with another skill, not raising Intimidation's rank.

Copy Fighter's initial proficiencies onto the sheet: expert Perception, Fortitude, Reflex, simple and martial weapons and unarmed attacks; trained Will, advanced weapons, all armor categories, unarmored defense and fighter class DC. Record [[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike_class_feature]] and [[Shield Block|main:shield_block_class_feature]] as well as your chosen feats. Neither reaction supplies an additional reaction to spend.

## Exact starting inventory

| Item | Paid | Bulk | Starting placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Longsword|main:longsword]] | 1 gp | 1 | One hand |
| [[Chain Mail|main:chain_mail]] | 6 gp | 2 worn | Worn |
| [[Steel Shield|main:steel_shield]] | 2 gp | 1 | Other hand, equipped |
| [[Minor Healing Potion|main:healing_potion_minor]] | 4 gp | L | Worn and readily accessible, not held |
| [[Clan Dagger|main:clan_dagger]] | Free ancestry grant | L | Worn and readily accessible, not held |
| Remaining coins | 2 gp retained | 0 for two coins | Carried |

Paid total: 1 + 6 + 2 + 4 = **13 gp** of the 15 gp budget. The dagger's listed market price is not charged against this free grant. There is no purchased backpack, travel pack, healer's toolkit or repair toolkit in this inventory. The potion and dagger are worn and readily accessible, not stowed in a backpack or container; no purchased container or container benefit is assumed. Before a journey, arrange any additional supplies with the group and record purchases or loans explicitly.

Bulk is 2 + 1 + 1 = 4, plus two light items. Those light items do not yet add a whole Bulk; the two coins add none. **Total counted Bulk 4**, with two light items recorded. Strength +4 permits 9 Bulk before becoming encumbered and at most 14 Bulk carried. [[Equipment Basics|main:equipment_basics]] explains why worn armor still counts.

## Ready-to-use numbers

At level 1, trained proficiency is +3 and expert is +5. The following numbers assume the inventory above, no conditions and no temporary effects.

| Statistic | Calculation | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum HP | Dwarf 10 + Fighter 10 + Con 3 | **23** |
| Perception | Wis 2 + expert 5 | **+7** |
| Fortitude | Con 3 + expert 5 | **+8** |
| Reflex | Dex 1 + expert 5 | **+6** |
| Will | Wis 2 + trained 3 | **+5** |
| Fighter class DC | 10 + Str 4 + trained 3 | **17** |
| Longsword Strike | Str 4 + expert 5 | **+9**, 1d8+4 slashing or piercing |
| Clan dagger Strike, if drawn | Str 4 + expert 5 | **+9**, 1d4+4 piercing or bludgeoning |
| AC, chain mail worn | 10 + Dex 1 + trained 3 + armor 4 | **18** |
| AC, shield raised | 18 + circumstance 2 | **20** |
| Speed | Dwarf 20, no remaining armor reduction | **20 feet** |

Chain mail's Strength threshold is +3; Strength +4 meets it. Its ordinary check penalty is removed and its −5-foot Speed penalty becomes zero even before Unburdened Iron. That feat also ignores armor Speed reductions. **Noisy still gives −2 to Stealth.** Flexible exempts Athletics and Acrobatics from the armor check penalty. The armor's Chain group grants no armor specialization benefit at this level.

| Crafting | Int 0 + trained 3 | +3 |

For the remaining, untrained skills: Acrobatics +1, Arcana +0, Deception −1, Diplomacy −1, Nature +2, Occultism +0, Performance −1, Religion +2, Society +0, Stealth **−1** (+1 Dexterity −2 Noisy), Thievery +1. These are modifiers, not permission to use trained-only actions.

## What the hands allow

The opening state has a sword in one hand and a shield in the other. Holding the shield alone leaves AC 18. [[Raise a Shield|main:raise_a_shield]] costs one action and makes AC 20 until your next turn begins. The shield starts at 20 HP, Hardness 5, Broken Threshold 10. Follow [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]] when blocking; track shield HP separately from your HP.

[[Vicious Swing|main:vicious_swing]] uses two actions for one melee Strike. With the longsword, an ordinary hit deals 2d8+4 at this level. It counts as two attacks for [[Multiple Attack Penalty|main:multiple_attack_penalty]], and Flourish permits only one flourish per round.

[[Trip|main:trip]] needs a free hand with this gear. Put away or release the sword before attempting it, then retrieve the sword before attacking with it again. A released sword is on the ground. The shield remains held. Read [[Equipment Basics|main:equipment_basics]] for action costs and the shield's attachment rules. A potion must likewise be retrieved into a free hand before drinking it.

Medicine training does not provide tools. This character cannot [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]] with the listed inventory alone. Borrow an explicitly available ally-owned healer's toolkit, or change the purchased loadout and money/Bulk ledger. Ten-minute treatment belongs outside the round-by-round fight.

Choose personal details, start at 0 XP and normally begin a session with 1 [[Hero Point|main:hero_points]]. This build grants no spells or Focus Points. Continue with [[First Encounter|main:first_encounter]], [[Dying and Recovery|main:dying_and_recovery]] and the fully worked [[Leveling Up|main:leveling_up]] update.
