**Focus 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Darkness, Focus, Manipulate  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

You infuse a creature's vision with darkness. It receives greater darkvision, allowing it to see even in magical darkness.
