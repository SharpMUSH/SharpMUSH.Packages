**Focus 5**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Hex, Manipulate, [[Witch|main:witch]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature

---

Your patron weaves the threads of time around the target, giving them some of the benefits of a day's passage. The target decreases the values of any [[Doomed|main:doomed]] and [[Drained|main:drained]] conditions it has by 1 and can immediately attempt one saving throw against each affliction it has with a stage length of 1 day or less. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 day.

*Source: Player Core.*
