**Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** Master in [[Arcana|main:arcana]], [[Nature|main:nature]], [[Occultism|main:occultism]] or [[Religion|main:religion]]; [[Recognize Spell|main:recognize_spell]]

You Recognize Spells swiftly. Once per round, you can [[Recognize a Spell|main:recognize_spell]] using a skill in which you're a master as a free action.
