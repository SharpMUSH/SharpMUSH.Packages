**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wizard Dedication|main:wizard_dedication]]

---

You gain your school's initial school spell. If you don't already have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point, which you can [[Refocus|main:refocus]] by studying.

*Source: Player Core.*
