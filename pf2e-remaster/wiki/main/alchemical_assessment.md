**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Crafting|main:crafting]]

---

You can identify alchemical items quickly. When using the Crafting skill to [[Identify Alchemy|main:identify_alchemy]] on an alchemical item you hold, you can do so as a single action, rather than spending 10 minutes.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
