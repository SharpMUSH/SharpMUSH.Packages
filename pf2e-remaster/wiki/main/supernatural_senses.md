**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage  
**Prerequisites:** Acute Scent or scent

---

Your scent is preternaturally sharp, and you can always rely on your sense of smell to help guide you when your vision is compromised. When you target a [[Concealed|main:concealed]] or [[Hidden|main:hidden]] opponent while you are raging, you reduce the DC of the flat check to 3 for a concealed target or to 9 for a hidden one.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
