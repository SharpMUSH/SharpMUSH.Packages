**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** 10 minutes  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** non-magical object of light Bulk or less

---

You repair the target item. You restore 5 Hit Points per spell rank to the target, potentially removing the [[Broken|main:broken]] condition if this repairs it past the item's Broken Threshold. You can't replace lost pieces or repair an object that's been completely destroyed.

---

**Heightened (2nd)** You can target a non-magical object of 1 Bulk or less.

**Heightened (3rd)** You can target a non-magical object of 2 Bulk or less, or a magical object of 1 Bulk or less.

*Source: Player Core.*
