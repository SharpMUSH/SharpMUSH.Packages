**Item 18**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Illusion, Magical, Potion  
**Price:** 4400 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

Drinking this dull-black liquid makes you undetectable to detection effects.

This grants the same effects as [[Hidden Mind|main:hidden_mind]] but without the bonus against mental effects. You also gain the effects of a 4th-rank [[Invisibility|main:invisibility]] spell, which protects against [[See the Unseen|main:see_the_unseen]] spells of 8th rank and lower and has a DC of 36 against [[Truesight|main:truesight]].

The potion's effects last for 10 minutes.

*Source: GM Core.*
