**Archetype Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Concentrate, Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** [[Familiar Master Dedication|main:familiar_master_dedication]]; able to cast spells

---

**Requirements** You have line of effect to your familiar.

---

Under your tutelage, your familiar has grown attuned to the hidden currents of the world and can serve as a conduit for your magic. If the next action you use is to [[Cast a Spell|main:cast_a_spell]] that has a range, the spell uses the familiar as its origin point.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
