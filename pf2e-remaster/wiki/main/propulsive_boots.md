**Item 13**  
**Traits:** Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 3000 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** wornshoes

---

These sleek red boots make your legs feel like they're bursting with energy. You gain a +5-foot item bonus to your land Speed and to any climb or swim Speeds you have.

**Activate—Quickening Stomp** (1 action) (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** You stomp three times and gain the [[Quickened|main:quickened]] condition for 1 minute. You can use the extra action to Stride, [[Climb|main:climb]], or [[Swim|main:swim]]. (You must still attempt an Athletics check for the Climb and Swim actions unless you have the appropriate movement type.)

*Source: GM Core.*
