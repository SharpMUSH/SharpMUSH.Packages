**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]

---

Fear makes your foes weak and more vulnerable to your attacks. You gain a circumstance bonus to damage rolls for Strikes against [[Frightened|main:frightened]] creatures. The bonus is equal to double the target's frightened value.

If you're a master in Intimidation, increase the bonus to triple the target's frightened value.

*Source: Player Core.*
