**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** mastermind racket; [[Debilitating Strike|main:debilitating_strike_class_feature]]

---

You strategically craft your debilitations to lessen or negate an enemy's tactical advantages. Add the following debilitations to the list you can choose from when you use Debilitating Strike.

- **Debilitation** The target can't flank or contribute to allies' flanking. Methodical Debilitations (Flanking)
- **Debilitation** The target doesn't gain a circumstance bonus to AC from [[Raising a Shield|main:raise_a_shield]], lesser cover, or standard cover; it gains a +2 circumstance bonus to AC only from greater cover or [[Taking Cover|main:take_cover]]. Methodical Debilitations (Cover)

*Source: Player Core.*
