**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Kholo|main:kholo]]  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

Your sinister giggle is a sound of warning and threat. You gain the [[Battle Cry|main:battle_cry]] skill feat. You don't take a penalty when you attempt to [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] a creature that doesn't understand your language.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
