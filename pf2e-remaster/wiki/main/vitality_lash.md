**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Cantrip, Concentrate, Manipulate, Vitality  
**Traditions:** [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature that is undead or otherwise has void healing  
**Defense:** basic Fortitude

---

You demolish the target's corrupted essence with the energy of life. You deal 2d6 vitality damage with a basic Fortitude save. If the creature critically fails the save, it is also [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]] until the start of your next turn.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6.

*Source: Player Core.*
