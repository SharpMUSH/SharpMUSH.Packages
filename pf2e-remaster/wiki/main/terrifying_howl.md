**Class Feat 10** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Auditory, [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage

---

You unleash a terrifying howl. Attempt Intimidation checks to [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] each enemy within 30 feet: you don't take a penalty if the creature doesn't understand your language. Regardless of the results of your checks, each target is then temporarily immune to Terrifying Howl for 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
