**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]

---

The blood of dragons flows through your veins. These beasts are both fearsome in combat and skilled at magic.

**Tradition** variable (see Draconic Exemplars below)

**Bloodline Skills** Intimidation and one other skill (see Draconic Exemplars below)

**Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip: [[Shield|main:shield]]; 1st: [[Fear|main:fear]]; 2nd: see Draconic Exemplars below; 3rd: [[Haste|main:haste]]; 4th: [[Fly|main:fly_spell]]; 5th: see Draconic Exemplars below; 6th: [[Dragon Form|main:dragon_form]] (dragon must match your draconic exemplar); 7th: [[Mask of Terror|main:mask_of_terror]]; 8th: see Draconic Exemplars below; 9th: [[Overwhelming Presence|main:overwhelming_presence]]

**Bloodline Spells** initial: [[Flurry of Claws|main:flurry_of_claws]]; advanced: [[Dragon Breath|main:dragon_breath]]; greater: [[Dragon Wings|main:dragon_wings]]

**Blood Magic—Scaly Hide** Draconic scales grow briefly on you or one target, granting a +1 status bonus to AC for 1 round.

### Draconic Exemplars

At 1st level, choose the tradition related to the dragon exemplar who influenced your bloodline. You can't change your dragon exemplar later. As noted, this affects both your bloodline's features and your sorcerous gift spells.

- **Arcane—Tradition** arcane; **Bloodline Skill** Arcana; **Sorcerous Gifts** 2nd: [[Blazing Bolt|main:blazing_bolt]]; 5th: [[Subconscious Suggestion|main:subconscious_suggestion]]; 8th: [[Quandary|main:quandary]]
- **Divine—Tradition** divine; **Bloodline Skill** Religion; **Sorcerous Gifts** 2nd: [[Augury|main:augury]]; 5th: [[Divine Immolation|main:divine_immolation]]; 8th: [[Divine Inspiration|main:divine_inspiration]]
- **Occult—Tradition** occult; **Bloodline Skill** Occultism; **Sorcerous Gifts** 2nd: [[Blood Vendetta|main:blood_vendetta]]; 5th: [[Slither|main:slither]]; 8th: [[Unrelenting Observation|main:unrelenting_observation]]
- **Primal—Tradition** primal; **Bloodline Skill** Nature; **Sorcerous Gifts** 2nd: [[Shatter|main:shatter]]; 5th: [[Howling Blizzard|main:howling_blizzard]]; 8th: [[Earthquake|main:earthquake]]

*Source: Player Core 2.*
