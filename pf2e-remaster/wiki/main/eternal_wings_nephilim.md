**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Divine Wings|main:divine_wings]]

---

Your wings are now a permanent part of your body. You gain the effects of [[Divine Wings|main:divine_wings]] at all times, rather than just once per day for 10 minutes.
