**Spell 5**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Curse, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will

---

You afflict the target with the curse of the roiling, unforgiving sea. The target must attempt a Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target becomes [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]]. Reducing its sickened condition to 0 ends the curse.

**Failure** The target becomes sickened 1 and can't reduce its sickened condition below 1 while the curse remains. The curse can be lifted by 4th-rank [[Cleanse Affliction|main:cleanse_affliction]] or similar magic. Whenever the target is sickened and on the water at least a mile from shore, it is also [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]].

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target becomes [[Sickened 2|main:sickened]].

*Source: Player Core.*
