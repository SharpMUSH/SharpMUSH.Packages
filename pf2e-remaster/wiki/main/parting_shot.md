**Class Feat 4** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]

---

**Requirements** You are wielding a loaded ranged weapon or a ranged weapon with reload 0.

---

You jump back and make a quick shot that catches your opponent by surprise. You Step and then make a ranged Strike with the required weapon. Your target is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the attack.

*Source: Player Core.*
