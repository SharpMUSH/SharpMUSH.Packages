**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Invested, Occult  
**Price:** 60 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** worn

---

This amulet is hollow and shaped in the form of an unblinking eye. Its cavity typically holds some fragment of occult text. While wearing the pendant, you gain a +1 item bonus to Occultism checks, and you can cast the [[Guidance|main:guidance]] cantrip as an occult innate spell.

*Source: GM Core.*
