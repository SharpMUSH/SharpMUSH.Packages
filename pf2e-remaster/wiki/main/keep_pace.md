**Archetype Feat 6** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** Bounty Hunter Dedication or Game Hunter Dedication

---

**Trigger** Your prey is within reach and attempts to move away from you.

---

**Bounty Hunter** Once you are upon your prey, they can't escape.

**Game Hunter** When your hunted prey tries to bolt, you follow.

[[Stride|main:stride]] up to your Speed, following the foe and keeping it in reach throughout its movement until it stops moving or you've moved your full Speed. You can use Keep Pace to [[Burrow|main:burrow]], [[Climb|main:climb]], [[Fly|main:fly]], or [[Swim|main:swim]] instead of Stride if you have the corresponding movement type.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
