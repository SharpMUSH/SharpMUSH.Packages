You are senseless and can't act. Stunned usually has a value: the total number of actions you lose, possibly over several turns.

- Each time you regain actions, regain that many fewer, then lower the stunned value by the actions you lost. Stunned 4 costs you all 3 actions on your turn and leaves stunned 1, which costs 1 action next turn.
- Stunned can have a duration instead ("stunned for 1 minute"); you lose all your actions for that time.
- Stunned overrides [[Slowed|main:slowed]]. If stunned ends while you are slowed, actions lost to stunned count toward those lost to slowed: stunned 1 and slowed 2 at the start of your turn costs 2 actions in all, leaving you 1.

*Source: Player Core, p. 446.*
