**Class Feat 2** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Emotion, Fear, [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Mental

---

Your blow not only wounds creatures but also shatters their confidence. Make a melee Strike. If you hit and deal damage, the target is [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]], or [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]] on a critical hit.

*Source: Player Core.*
