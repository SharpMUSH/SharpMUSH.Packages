**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** thief racket; [[Debilitating Strike|main:debilitating_strike_class_feature]]

You attack with great precision. Add the following debilitations to the list you can choose from when you use [[Debilitating Strike|main:debilitating_strike]].

- **Debilitation** The target takes an additional 2d6 precision damage from your attacks. Precise Debilitations
- **Debilitation** The target becomes [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]].
