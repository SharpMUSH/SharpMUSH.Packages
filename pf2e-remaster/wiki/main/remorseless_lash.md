**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

You're skilled at beating an enemy when their morale is already breaking. When you succeed at a melee weapon Strike against a [[Frightened|main:frightened]] enemy, that enemy can't reduce their frightened condition below 1 until the beginning of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
