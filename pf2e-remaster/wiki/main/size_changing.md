**Item 7**  
**Traits:** Magical  
**Price:** 350 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto armor

---

This armor can make itself and its wearer quickly change size.

**Activate—Change Size** (1 action) (concentrate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** The armor casts your choice of [[Enlarge|main:enlarge]] or [[Shrink|main:shrink]] on you.
