**Archetype Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Flourish  
**Prerequisites:** [[Medic Dedication|main:medic_dedication]]

---

You move to provide immediate care to those who need it. You [[Stride|main:stride]], then use [[Battle Medicine|main:battle_medicine]] or [[Treat Poison|main:treat_poison]]. You can spend 2 actions for Doctor's Visitation to instead Stride and then use either [[Administer First Aid|main:administer_first_aid]] or [[Treat Condition|main:treat_condition]] (if you have that feat).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
