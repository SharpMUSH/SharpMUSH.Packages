**Class Feature 15**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Class:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

Your opportunistic attacks are particularly detrimental. When you use [[Debilitating Strike|main:debilitating_strike]], you can apply two debilitations simultaneously; removing one removes both.
