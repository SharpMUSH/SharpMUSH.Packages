**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Diplomacy|main:diplomacy]]

---

First impressions are your strong suit. When you meet someone in a casual or social situation, you can immediately attempt a Diplomacy check to [[Make an Impression|main:make_an_impression]] on that creature rather than needing to converse for 1 minute. If you fail, you can engage in 1 minute of conversation and attempt a new check at the end of that time rather than accepting the failure or critical failure result.

*Source: Player Core.*
