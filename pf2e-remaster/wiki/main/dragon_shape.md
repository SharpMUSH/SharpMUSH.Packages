**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Soaring Shape|main:soaring_shape]]

---

You can take on the shape of some of the world's most fearsome creatures. Add the shapes listed in [[Dragon Form|main:dragon_form]] to your [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form]] list. Whenever you're polymorphed into another form using *untamed form*, you gain resistance 5 to your choice of bludgeoning or poison.

*Source: Player Core.*
