**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Human|main:human]]

---

You have developed a soul-deep bond with your comrades and maintain an even greater degree of cooperation with them. If you are at least an expert in the skill you are [[Aiding|main:aid]], when you roll a failure or critical failure to Aid a skill check, you get a success instead.

*Source: Player Core.*
