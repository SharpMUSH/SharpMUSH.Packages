**Item 10**  
**Traits:** Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 900 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

This ornate metal staff shines with precious inlays of gold. When you Cast a Spell from the staff, the illusory image of something you desire flashes across its surface. While wielding the staff, you gain a +2 status bonus to checks to disbelieve an illusion.

**Activate** Cast a Spell

---

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Figment|main:figment]]
- **1st** [[Illusory Disguise|main:illusory_disguise]], [[Illusory Object|main:illusory_object]]
- **2nd** [[Illusory Creature|main:illusory_creature]], [[Illusory Object|main:illusory_object]], [[Item Facade|main:item_facade]]
- **3rd** [[Illusory Disguise|main:illusory_disguise]], [[Item Facade|main:item_facade]]
- **4th** [[Illusory Creature|main:illusory_creature]], [[Illusory Scene|main:illusory_scene]], [[Illusory Disguise|main:illusory_disguise]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
