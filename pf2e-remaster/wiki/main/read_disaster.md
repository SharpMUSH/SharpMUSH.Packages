**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** Exploration, [[Oracle|main:oracle]], Prediction

---

You spend 10 minutes and open yourself to the divine mysteries of the world, peering into the most sinister portents of the future.

You gain the effects of [[Augury|main:augury]], except you learn only about the dangers ahead; any results of "good" are instead "nothing," and results of "mixed" are instead "bad." If you are legendary in Religion, you take only 1 minute to Read Disaster.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
