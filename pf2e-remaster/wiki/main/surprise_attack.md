**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Assassin Dedication|main:assassin_dedication]]

---

You act before foes can react. On the first round of combat, if you roll Deception or Stealth for initiative, creatures that haven't acted are [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] to you.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
