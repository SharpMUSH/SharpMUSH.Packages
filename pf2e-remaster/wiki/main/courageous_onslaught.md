**Class Feat 16** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Auditory, [[Bard|main:bard]], Concentrate, Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** [[Courageous Advance|main:courageous_advance]]; [[Courageous Assault|main:courageous_assault]]

---

You use your performance to orchestrate an onslaught against your enemies. If your next action is to cast the [[Courageous Anthem|main:courageous_anthem]] composition cantrip, one ally of your choice who gains a status bonus from the spell can immediately use their reaction to Stride and then make a melee Strike.

*Source: Player Core.*
