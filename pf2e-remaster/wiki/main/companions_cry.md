**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Prerequisites:** an animal companion

---

You can urge your companion to do its utmost. You can spend 2 actions to [[Command an Animal|main:command_an_animal]] instead of 1 when commanding your animal companion. If you do, your animal companion uses an additional action.

*Source: Player Core.*
