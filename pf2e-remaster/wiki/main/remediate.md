**Class Feat 16** (free action)  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Spellshape  
**Frequency:** once per hour

---

**Frequency** once per hour

---

If your next action is to use your divine font to cast a 3-action [[Heal|main:heal]] or [[Harm|main:harm]] spell, you can harness the residual energy to counterbalance opposing forces. In addition to its normal effects, you use your *heal* or *harm* spell to attempt to counteract one divine effect in the spell's area.

*Source: Player Core.*
