**Class Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

Make an unarmed melee Strike against a [[Prone|main:prone]] target. If it hits, until the end of your next turn the target is [[Stupefied 1|main:stupefied]] (or [[Stupefied 2|main:stupefied]] on a critical hit) and [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]].

*Source: Player Core.*
