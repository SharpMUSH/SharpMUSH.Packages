**Item 6**  
**Traits:** Darkness, Magical, Wand  
**Price:** 250 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

This wand is a length of wrought black iron.

---

**Activate** Cast a Spell

**Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge

**Effect** You cast [[Darkness|main:darkness]] at 2nd-rank. Each creature that ends its turn within the spell's area must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or become [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]] ([[Frightened 2|main:frightened]] on a critical failure).

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of *darkness* at 2nd-rank.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
