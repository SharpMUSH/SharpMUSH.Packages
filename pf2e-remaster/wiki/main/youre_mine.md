**Focus 5**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Emotion, Focus, Incapacitation, Manipulate, Mental, [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** 1 round

---

You manipulate the target's emotions, potentially allowing you to control it for a brief instant. The target must attempt a Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]].

**Failure** On the target's next turn, it's [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]] and you partially control it, causing it to take a single action of your choice. If it has actions left, it can act normally.

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Controlled|main:controlled]] for 1 round.

---

**Heightened (7th)** On a failure, the target is Controlled for 1 round. On a critical failure, the target is Controlled for up to 1 minute; it receives a new Will save at the end of each of its turns, and on a success, the spell ends.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
