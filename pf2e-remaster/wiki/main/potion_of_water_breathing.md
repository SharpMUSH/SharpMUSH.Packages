**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Potion  
**Price:** 11 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This filmy, gray potion smells of an old fish midden and tastes even worse. After drinking this potion, you gain the effects of a 2nd-rank [[Water Breathing|main:water_breathing]] spell for 1 hour.

*Source: GM Core.*
