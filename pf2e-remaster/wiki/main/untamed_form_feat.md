**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** untamed order

---

You are one with the wild, always changing and adapting to meet any challenge. You gain the [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form]] order spell, which lets you transform into a variety of shapes that you can expand with druid feats.

*Source: Player Core.*
