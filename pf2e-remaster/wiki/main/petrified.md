You have been turned to stone.

- You can't act or sense anything.
- You become an object with double your normal Bulk (usually 12 for a Medium creature, 6 for Small), AC 9, Hardness 8 and your current Hit Points. You have no Broken Threshold.
- When the condition ends you have the Hit Points the statue had. If the statue is destroyed, you die at once.
- Your mind and body are in stasis: you don't age or notice time passing.

*Source: Player Core, p. 445.*
