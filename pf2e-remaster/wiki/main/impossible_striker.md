**Class Feat 20**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Sly Striker|main:sly_striker]]

---

Nothing can prevent you from making a sneak attack, even if your opponent can see every blow coming. Instead of dealing the damage from [[Sly Striker|main:sly_striker]], you can deal your full sneak attack damage to a target even if the target isn't [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]].

*Source: Player Core.*
