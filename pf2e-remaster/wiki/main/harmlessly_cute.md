**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Leshy|main:leshy]]

---

Your size and demeanor make it easy for you to convince others that you mean no harm. You gain the [[Shameless Request|main:shameless_request]] skill feat. Additionally, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to initiative checks when you roll Deception for initiative.

*Source: Player Core.*
