**Class Feat 14** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Press

---

**Requirements** You are wielding a single one-handed melee weapon and hold nothing else in your hands.

---

Using your weapon as a lever, you force your opponent to end up right where you want them. Make a Strike with a one-handed melee weapon. If the Strike hits, you can [[Reposition|main:reposition]] the target up to 10 feet. You can move the target through your space during this movement. Your Strike gains the following failure effect.

**Failure** You can force the creature to move as you would on a success, but you can move the target only 5 feet.

*Source: Player Core.*
