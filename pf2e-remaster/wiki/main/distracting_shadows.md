**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Halfling|main:halfling]]

---

You have learned to remain hidden by using larger folk as a distraction to avoid drawing attention to yourself. You can use creatures that are at least one size larger than you (usually Medium or larger) as cover for the [[Hide|main:hide]] and [[Sneak|main:sneak]] actions, though you still can't use such creatures as cover for other uses, such as the [[Take Cover|main:take_cover]] action.

*Source: Player Core.*
