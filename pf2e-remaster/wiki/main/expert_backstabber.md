**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Assassin Dedication|main:assassin_dedication]]

---

You excel at quickly dispatching your foes. When you Strike an [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] foe with a weapon that has the backstabber weapon trait, you deal 2 extra precision damage instead of 1. If the weapon is a *+3 weapon*, you deal 4 extra precision damage instead of 2.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
