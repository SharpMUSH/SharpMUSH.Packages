**Item 6**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Injury, Poison  
**Price:** 40 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

The venom of a giant scorpion is excruciating and its effects are somewhat debilitating.

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 22 Fortitude

**Maximum Duration** 6 rounds

**Stage 1** 2d6 poison damage and [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]] (1 round)

**Stage 2** 2d8 poison damage and enfeebled 1 (1 round)

**Stage 3** 2d10 poison damage and [[Enfeebled 2|main:enfeebled]] (1 round)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
