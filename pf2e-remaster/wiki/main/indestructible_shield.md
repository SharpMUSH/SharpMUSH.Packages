An *indestructible shield* is a *high-grade greater reinforcing adamantine shield* (Hardness 17, HP 136) that can withstand just about any damage. It can be damaged only by a [[Disintegrate|main:disintegrate]] spell (roll damage as if against a creature that failed its save) or by an artifact tied to destruction.

---

**Craft Requirements** The raw materials must include at least 4,400 gp of adamantine.

*Source: GM Core.*
