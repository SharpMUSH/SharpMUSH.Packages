**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Bard|main:bard]]  
**Prerequisites:** polymath muse

---

You can rely on the grandeur of your performances rather than ordinary social skills. You can use Performance instead of Diplomacy to [[Make an Impression|main:make_an_impression]] and instead of Intimidation to [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]]. You can also use an acting Performance instead of Deception to [[Impersonate|main:impersonate]].

In addition, you can use your proficiency rank in Performance to meet the prerequisites of skill feats that require a particular proficiency rank in Deception, Diplomacy, or Intimidation.

*Source: Player Core.*
