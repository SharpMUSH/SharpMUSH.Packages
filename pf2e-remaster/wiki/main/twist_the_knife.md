**Class Feat 6** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

**Requirements** Your last action was a melee Strike that dealt sneak attack damage to an [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] target.

---

After stabbing your opponent in a weak spot, you tear the wound open. You deal persistent bleed damage to the target equal to your number of sneak attack damage dice.

*Source: Player Core.*
