**Archetype Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** [[Dandy Dedication|main:dandy_dedication]]; master in [[Deception|main:deception]]

---

You can lie about having taken an action so convincingly that you acquire all the tangible benefits of having successfully completed it.

You can roll Deception in place of a different skill when attempting to [[Earn Income|main:earn_income]], [[Make an Impression|main:make_an_impression]], [[Request|main:request]], or [[Subsist|main:subsist]].

You can use Fabricated Connections to [[Make an Impression|main:make_an_impression]] or [[Request|main:request]] once per day, and you can also use it once per week to Earn Income or [[Subsist|main:subsist]]

*Source: Player Core 2.*
