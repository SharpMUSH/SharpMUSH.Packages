**Spell 3**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Fortitude  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

You dilate the flow of time around the target, slowing its actions.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]] for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]] for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Slowed 2|main:slowed]] for 1 minute.

---

**Heightened (6th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
