**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** scoundrel racket; [[Debilitating Strike|main:debilitating_strike_class_feature]]

---

You learn new debilitations that grant you tactical advantages against your foes. Add the following debilitations to the list you can choose from when you use Debilitating Strike.

- **Debilitation** The target can't use reactions.
- **Debilitation** The target can't flank or contribute to allies' flanking. Tactical Debilitations (No Flanking)
