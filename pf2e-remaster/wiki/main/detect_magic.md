**Action**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Exploration

---

You cast [[Detect Magic|main:detect_magic_spell]] at regular intervals. You move at half your travel speed or slower. You have no chance of accidentally overlooking a magic aura at a travel speed up to 300 feet per minute, but must be traveling no more than 150 feet per minute to detect magic auras before the party moves into them.

*Source: Player Core.*
