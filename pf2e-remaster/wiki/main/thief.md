**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

Nothing beats the thrill of taking something that belongs to someone else. You might be a pickpocket working the streets, a cat burglar sneaking through windows, or even a consultant, testing your clients' vaults for openings.

When a fight breaks out, you prefer swift, lightweight weapons, and you strike where it hurts. When you make a melee Strike with a finesse weapon or finesse unarmed attack, you can add your Dexterity modifier to the damage roll instead of your Strength modifier.

You are trained in Thievery.

*Source: Player Core.*
