**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]

---

Your overwhelming vitality sets undead alight. When you use a [[Heal|main:heal]] spell to damage undead, each undead that takes damage also takes persistent fire damage equal to the spell's rank.

*Source: Player Core.*
