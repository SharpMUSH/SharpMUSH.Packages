**Archetype Feat 6** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Emotion, Mental, Visual  
**Prerequisites:** [[Celebrity Dedication|main:celebrity_dedication]]

---

When you meet someone's gaze, they're unable to look away from you. Choose one target creature you can see and that can see you. That creature must succeed at a Will save or be [[Fascinated|main:fascinated]] with you until the end of your next turn; the DC is the higher of your class DC or spell DC. If the creature succeeds at its save or its fascination ends due to a hostile action, it becomes temporarily immune to your Mesmerizing Gaze for 1 day.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
