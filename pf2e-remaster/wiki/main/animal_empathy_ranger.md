**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]

---

You have a connection to the creatures of the natural world that allows you to communicate with them on a rudimentary level. You can use Diplomacy to [[Make an Impression|main:make_an_impression]] on animals and to make very simple [[Requests|main:request]] of them. In most cases, wild animals will give you time to make your case.

*Source: Player Core.*
