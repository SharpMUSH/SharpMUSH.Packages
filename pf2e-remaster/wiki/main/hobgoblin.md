*Taller and stronger than their goblin kin, hobgoblins are equals in strength and size to humans, with broad shoulders and long, powerful arms.*

**Hit Points:** 8  
**Size:** Medium  
**Speed:** 25 feet  
**Attribute boosts:** Constitution, Intelligence, free  
**Attribute flaw:** Wisdom  
**Languages:** Common and Goblin. Additional languages (one per point of Intelligence modifier): Draconic, Dwarven, Halfling, Jotun, Kholo, or Orcish  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Hobgoblin, Humanoid  
**Features:** Darkvision
