**Key attribute:** Intelligence

Knowledge of people, cultures, history, law and civilization, and reading and forging documents.

Your Society modifier is your Intelligence modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Society (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]]
- [[Subsist|main:subsist]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Create Forgery|main:create_forgery]]
- [[Decipher Writing|main:decipher_writing]]
