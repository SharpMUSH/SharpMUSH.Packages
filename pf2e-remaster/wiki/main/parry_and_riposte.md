**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Opportune Riposte|main:opportune_riposte_class_feature]]

---

Your parries and finishers allow you to riposte with the slightest provocation.

You can use [[Opportune Riposte|main:opportune_riposte]] against an enemy who fails at a Strike against you (not just critically fails), provided you have both damaged that creature with a finisher on your last turn and currently have a circumstance bonus to AC from the parry weapon trait or [[Extravagant Parry|main:extravagant_parry]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
