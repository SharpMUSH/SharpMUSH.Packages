Fog or something else makes you hard to see. You can be concealed from some creatures and not others.

- You can still be [[Observed]], but you are harder to target.
- A creature you are concealed from must succeed at a DC 5 flat check to target you with an attack, spell or other effect. On a failure, the effect doesn't affect you.
- Area effects don't need the flat check.

*Source: Player Core, p. 442.*
