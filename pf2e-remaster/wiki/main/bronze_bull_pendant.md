**Item 2**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Talisman  
**Price:** 7 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to armor

---

**Activate** (1 action) (concentrate)

---

This pendant is forged from grainy steel and depicts a snorting bull's face. The pendant must be attached to the chest area or on a shoulder guard. When you activate the pendant, attempt an Athletics check to [[Shove|main:shove]] with a +1 item bonus to check. Increase the distance you Shove your target to 10 feet on a success or 20 feet on a critical success.

*Source: GM Core.*
