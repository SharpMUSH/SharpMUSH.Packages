**Item 11**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Elixir, Mutagen, Polymorph  
**Price:** 300 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

Your features become striking, and your voice becomes musical and commanding, though emotion clouds your reason.

**Benefit** You gain a +3 item bonus to Deception, Diplomacy, Intimidation and Performance checks. Your critical failures with any of these skills become failures instead.

**Drawback** You take a -2 item penalty to Arcana, Crafting, Lore, Occultism, and Society checks. Choose one skill in which you are trained; for the duration, you become untrained in that skill. All your failures to [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] become critical failures.

**Duration** 1 hour.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
