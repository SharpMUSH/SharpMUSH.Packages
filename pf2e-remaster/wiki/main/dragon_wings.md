**Focus 5**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Manipulate, Morph, [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

Leathery wings sprout from your back, giving you a fly Speed of 60 feet or your Speed, whichever is faster. If you're still flying when this spell's duration would end, you float to the ground, as with the effects of [[Gentle Landing|main:gentle_landing]].

---

**Heightened (8th)** The duration increases to 10 minutes.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
