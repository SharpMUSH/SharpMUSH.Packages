**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Oracle|main:oracle]]

---

Celestial bodies great and small exert influence on you, giving you sublime cosmic power. Perhaps you see the glittering stars as a divine blessing, or perhaps you feel drawn to the infinitely dark spaces between.

**Granted Spells**

- **cantrip:** [[Light|main:light]]
- **1st:** [[Dizzying Colors|main:dizzying_colors]]
- **2nd:** [[Darkness|main:darkness]]
- **5th:** [[Moon Frenzy|main:moon_frenzy]]

**Revelation Spells**

- **initial:** [[Spray of Stars|main:spray_of_stars]]
- **advanced:** [[Interstellar Void|main:interstellar_void]]
- **greater:** [[Moonlight Bridge|main:moonlight_bridge]]

**Related Domains** darkness, moon, nothingness, star

**Mystery Skill** Nature
