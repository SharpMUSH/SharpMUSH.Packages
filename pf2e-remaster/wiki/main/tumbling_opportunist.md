**Archetype Feat 10** (free action)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Attack  
**Prerequisites:** [[Acrobat Dedication|main:acrobat_dedication]]  
**Frequency:** once per minute

---

**Frequency** once per minute

**Requirements** Your most recent action was to [[Tumble Through|main:tumble_through]] or [[Tumbling Strike|main:tumbling_strike]], and you successfully moved through an enemy's space.

---

You use a burst of stamina to perform a breathtaking feat of Acrobatics as you speed through a foe's space, leaving your foe lying flat on their back. You attempt to [[Trip|main:trip]] the enemy whose space you moved through. You can use Acrobatics ([[Trip|main:trip]]) instead of Athletics for this check.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
