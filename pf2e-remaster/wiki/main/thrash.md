**Class Feat 8** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage

---

**Requirements** You have an enemy [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] or [[Restrained|main:restrained]].

---

You thrash your foe around. An enemy you have grabbed or restrained takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier plus your weapon specialization damage plus your [[Rage|main:rage]] damage, with a basic Fortitude save against your class DC.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
