**Spell 5**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Emotion, Manipulate, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Area:** 30-foot cone  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** 1 or more rounds

---

You inflict despair on creatures in the area. The effects for each creature are determined by its Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** For 1 round, the creature can't use reactions and must attempt another save at the start of its turn; on a failure, it is [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]] for that turn as it sobs uncontrollably.

**Failure** As success, but the duration is 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** As failure, and the creature is automatically slowed 1 for 1 minute.

---

**Heightened (7th)** The area increases to a 60-foot cone.

*Source: Player Core.*
