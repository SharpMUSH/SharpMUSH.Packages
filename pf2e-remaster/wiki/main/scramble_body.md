**Focus 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Manipulate, [[Wizard|main:wizard]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 living creature  
**Defense:** Fortitude

---

Your magic throws the creature's biology into disarray, inducing nausea, fever, and other unpleasant conditions.

---

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The target becomes [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]].

**Critical Failure** The target becomes [[Sickened 2|main:sickened]] and [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]] as long as it's sickened.

*Source: Player Core.*
