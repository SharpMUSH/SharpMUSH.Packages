**Ancestry Feat 1** (free action)  
**Traits:** [[Gnome|main:gnome]]  
**Frequency:** once per hour

---

**Frequency** once per hour

**Trigger** You blind or dazzle a creature.

---

You've spent considerable time practicing the manipulation of light, weaponizing your blade's reflection or bolstering the luminosity of magical displays to unconventional heights. Extend the duration of the [[Blinded|main:blinded]] or [[Dazzled|main:dazzled]] condition you give the target by 1 round.

*Source: Player Core.*
