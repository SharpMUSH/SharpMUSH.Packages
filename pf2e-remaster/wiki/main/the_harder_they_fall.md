**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

You make your foes fall painfully when you trip them. When you successfully [[Trip|main:trip]] an [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] foe, your target takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage. On a critical success, the target takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage plus your sneak attack damage.

*Source: Player Core.*
