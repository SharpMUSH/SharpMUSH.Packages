**Class Feat 8** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Flourish

---

**Requirements** You are wielding a two-handed melee weapon and your target is within your reach.

---

With punishing blows, you force your opponent into position. Make a Strike with the required weapon. If you hit, you [[Reposition|main:reposition]] the target 5 feet.

*Source: Player Core.*
