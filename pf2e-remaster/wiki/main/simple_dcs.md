Quick DCs by proficiency rank. Pick the rank whose training the task calls for; it is not required to attempt the task.

| Proficiency Rank | DC |
|---|---|
| Untrained | 10 |
| Trained | 15 |
| Expert | 20 |
| Master | 30 |
| Legendary | 40 |

For example, a fact only someone with master proficiency in a Lore skill would know is DC 30.

*Source: GM Core, p. 53 (Table 10-4).*
