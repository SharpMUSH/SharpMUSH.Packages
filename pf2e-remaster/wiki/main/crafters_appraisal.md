**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Crafting|main:crafting]]

---

Your knowledge of items' construction allows you to discern their magical effects as well. You can use Crafting instead of a skill associated with a magic tradition to [[Identify Magic|main:identify_magic]] on magic items, though not on any other sorts of magic.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
