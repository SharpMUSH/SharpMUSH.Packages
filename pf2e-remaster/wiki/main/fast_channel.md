**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]  
**Prerequisites:** harmful font or healing font

---

Divine power is always at your fingertips, swiftly responding to your call. When you cast [[Harm|main:harm]] or [[Heal|main:heal]] by spending 2 actions, you can get the effects of the 3-action version instead of the 2-action version.

You can do this with *harm* if you have harmful font or *heal* if you have healing font (or both if you have Versatile Font).

*Source: Player Core.*
