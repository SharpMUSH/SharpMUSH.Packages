**Key attribute:** Wisdom

Knowledge of the natural world, plants and animals, primal magic, and handling animals.

Your Nature modifier is your Wisdom modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Nature (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Command an Animal|main:command_an_animal]]
- [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Identify Magic|main:identify_magic]]
- [[Learn a Spell|main:learn_a_spell]]
