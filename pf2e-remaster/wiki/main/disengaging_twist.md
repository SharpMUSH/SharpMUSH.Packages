**Archetype Feat 4** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wrestler Dedication|main:wrestler_dedication]]

---

**Trigger** A creature gives you the [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] or [[Restrained|main:restrained]] condition.

---

Your ability to twist your opponents' bodies into painful locks and holds makes you particularly adept at escaping such predicaments. Attempt an Athletics check to [[Escape|main:escape]] the triggering condition. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to this check.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
