**Archetype Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wrestler Dedication|main:wrestler_dedication]]

---

**Requirements** You have a creature [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] or [[Restrained|main:restrained]].

---

Flexing your entire body, you heave your opponent over your head and slam them into the ground. Make an unarmed melee Strike against the creature you have grabbed or restrained; on a success, the target lands [[Prone|main:prone]], and on a critical success, the target lands prone and takes an additional 2d6 bludgeoning damage. Regardless of whether the Strike is successful, you immediately release your hold on the target.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
