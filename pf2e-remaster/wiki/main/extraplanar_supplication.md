**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]

---

You call upon divine power to place a benediction upon your allies or a malediction upon your foes. Choose either [[Bless|main:bless]] or [[Bane|main:bane]]. You can cast the chosen spell once per day as a 1st-rank divine innate spell.

*Source: Player Core.*
