**Class Feature 13**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]

---

When you use [[Quick Alchemy|main:quick_alchemy_class_feature]] to create any type of elixir of life, the creature healed by the item regains the maximum Hit Points possible, instead of rolling to determine the number of Hit Points regained.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
