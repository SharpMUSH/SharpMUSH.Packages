**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

You somehow manage to have just the thing for the situation. You gain the [[Prescient Planner|main:prescient_planner]] and [[Prescient Consumable|main:prescient_consumable]] feats. When using Prescient Planner, you can draw the item from your backpack (or a similar container) as a 2-action activity, instead of taking 1 minute to do so.

*Source: Player Core.*
