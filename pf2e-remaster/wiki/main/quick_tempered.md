**Action** (free action)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]]

---

**Trigger** You roll initiative.

**Requirements** You are not [[Encumbered|main:encumbered]] or wearing heavy armor.

---

So long as you are able to move freely, your fury is instinctive and instantaneous. You [[Rage|main:rage]].
