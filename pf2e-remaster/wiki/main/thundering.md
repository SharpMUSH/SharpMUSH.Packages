**Item 8**  
**Traits:** Magical, Sonic  
**Price:** 500 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto a weapon

---

A *thundering* weapon lets out a peal of thunder when it hits, dealing an extra 1d6 sonic damage on a successful Strike. On a critical hit, the target must succeed at a DC 24 Fortitude save or be [[Deafened|main:deafened]] for 1 minute (or 1 hour on a critical failure).

*Source: GM Core.*
