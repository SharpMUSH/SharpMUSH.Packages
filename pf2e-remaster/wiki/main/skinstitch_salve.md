**Item 7**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Healing  
**Price:** 55 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

This sticky salve stubbornly holds wounds closed and encourages swift natural healing. You can activate the salve in either of the following ways.

**Activate—Administer Stitch** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

**Effect** You [[Administer First Aid|main:administer_first_aid]] without requiring [[Healer's Toolkit|main:healers_toolkit]]. You either gain a +2 item bonus to the Medicine check, or you can use the skinstitch salve's Medicine modifier of 1d20 plus 13 instead of your own.

**Activate—Stitch Wounds** (free action) (manipulate)

---

**Trigger** You [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]] or use [[Battle Medicine|main:battle_medicine]]

**Effect** You gain a +2 item bonus to the triggering Medicine check. If you roll a success on the Medicine check, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
