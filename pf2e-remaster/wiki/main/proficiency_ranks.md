Your proficiency rank in something says how well trained you are with it. Ranks apply to Perception, saving throws, skills, weapons and unarmed attacks, armor (your Armor Class), your class DC, and spell attacks and spell DCs.

| Rank | Proficiency bonus |
|---|---|
| Untrained | +0 |
| Trained | your level + 2 |
| Expert | your level + 4 |
| Master | your level + 6 |
| Legendary | your level + 8 |

Untrained adds nothing, not even your level.

## Using the bonus

- **A check:** d20 + attribute modifier + proficiency bonus + other bonuses and penalties.
- **A DC** (such as your class DC, spell DC or Armor Class): 10 + the same modifiers.

Your class sets your starting ranks and raises them as you level up. Each skill increase (from your class) raises one skill by one rank: to trained, to expert, to master at 7th level or higher, and to legendary at 15th level or higher.

Some actions need a rank: you can't use a skill's trained actions while untrained in it. See [[Skills|category:skills]].

For rank-based DCs set by the GM, see [[Simple DCs|main:simple_dcs]].
