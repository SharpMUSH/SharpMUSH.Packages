**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Monk|main:monk]]

---

You are as light as a leaf whirling in the breeze. When you [[Leap|main:leap]] or succeed at a [[High Jump|main:high_jump]] or [[Long Jump|main:long_jump]], increase the distance you jump by 5 feet. When calculating the damage you take from falling, don't count any distance fallen while you are adjacent to a wall.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
