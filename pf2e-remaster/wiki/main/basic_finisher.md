**Action** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Finisher

---

You make a graceful, deadly attack. Make a Strike; if you hit and your weapon qualifies for [[Precise Strike|main:precise_strike]], you deal the full 1d6 damage from precise strike.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
