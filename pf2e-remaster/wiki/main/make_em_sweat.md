**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]  
**Prerequisites:** interrogation methodology

---

Your line of questioning lets a suspect know you can see right through them, shaking them to the core. When you succeed at using [[Pointed Question|main:pointed_question]], the target is also [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]] (or [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]] on a critical success). This added effect is an emotion and fear effect, but the other effects of Pointed Question still apply even if the target is immune to fear.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
