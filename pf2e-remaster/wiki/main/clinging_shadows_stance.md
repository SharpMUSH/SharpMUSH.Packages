**Focus 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Focus, Manipulate, [[Monk|main:monk]], Shadow, Stance  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Duration:** until you leave the stance

---

You adopt the stance of clinging shadows. You can make shadow grasp Strikes. These deal 1d4 void damage; are in the brawling group; and have the agile, grapple, reach, and unarmed traits. While in clinging shadows stance, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to [[Grapple|main:grapple]], and to the DC for creatures to [[Escape|main:escape]] from you.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
