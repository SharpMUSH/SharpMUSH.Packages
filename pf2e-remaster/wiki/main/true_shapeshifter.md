**Class Feat 20** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Concentrate, [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Dragon Shape|main:dragon_shape]]; [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form_feat]]

---

You transcend the limitations of form. Once per day you can cast [[Nature Incarnate|main:nature_incarnate]], even if you don't have it prepared, to transform into a kaiju. If you have [[Plant Shape|main:plant_shape]], you can choose to transform into a green man instead. You also gain the [[True Shapeshift|main:true_shapeshift]] activity.

*Source: Player Core.*
