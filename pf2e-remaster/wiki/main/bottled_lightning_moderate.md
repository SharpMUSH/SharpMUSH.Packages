**Activate** (1 action) Strike

---

Bottled lightning is packed with volatile reagents that create a blast of electricity when they are exposed to air. Bottled lightning deals 2d6 electricity damage and 2 electricity splash damage. On a hit, the target becomes [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the start of your next turn. You gain a +1 item bonus to attack rolls.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
