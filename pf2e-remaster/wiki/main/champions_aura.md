**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]

---

You're surrounded by an aura in a 15-foot emanation. It has the aura and divine traits. Any follower of your deity within the aura immediately knows you're a champion of your deity. This aura is used as the range for your champion's reaction and for various other effects. You can suppress or resume the aura as a single action, which has the concentrate trait, and it ends if you fall [[Unconscious|main:unconscious]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
