**General Feat 7**  
**Traits:** General  
**Prerequisites:** [[Prescient Planner|main:prescient_planner]]

---

You can predict which consumables you might need in advance. When using [[Prescient Planner|main:prescient_planner]], you can procure a consumable item from your backpack, instead of a piece of adventuring gear. The consumable item must still be common with a level no higher than half your level, and its Bulk must be low enough that carrying it wouldn't have made you encumbered.

*Source: Player Core.*
