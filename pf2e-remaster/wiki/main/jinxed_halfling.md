**Heritage**  
**Traits:** Uncommon  
**Ancestry:** [[Halfling|main:halfling]]

---

You were born with a strange blessing: bereft of the typical halfling luck, you can instead manipulate the fortunes of others. You can never take the [[Halfling Luck|main:halfling_luck]] feat, and you gain the [[Jinx|main:jinx]] action.

*Source: Player Core.*
