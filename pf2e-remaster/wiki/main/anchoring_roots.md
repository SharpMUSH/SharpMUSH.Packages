**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Leshy|main:leshy]]

---

Small roots sprout from your feet, steadying you as you move. You gain the [[Steady Balance|main:steady_balance]] skill feat, even if you aren't trained in Acrobatics, and you can use the [[Anchor|main:anchor]] action.

*Source: Player Core.*
