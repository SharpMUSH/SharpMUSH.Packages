**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Class:** [[Champion|main:champion]]

---

You devote yourself to a specific cause in your deity's name. Some are limited to sanctification, as noted in parentheses. Your cause adds to your edicts and anathema and grants you a special reaction called your champion's reaction, which appears after the divider, along with its upgrades if you gain the relentless reaction and exalted reaction class features at higher levels.

**[[Desecration|main:desecration]] (Unholy):** You selfishly corrupt and destroy.

**[[Grandeur|main:grandeur]] (Holy):** You exemplify the glory and splendor of the celestial realms.

**[[Iniquity|main:iniquity]] (Unholy):** You destroy, take advantage, and act with dishonor.

**[[Justice|main:justice]]:** You follow laws and mete out just punishment.

**[[Liberation|main:liberation]]:** You oppose tyranny and fight for freedom.

**[[Obedience|main:obedience]]:** You enforce hierarchies and order.

**[[Redemption|main:redemption]] (Holy):** You try to redeem those who commit wicked deeds.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
