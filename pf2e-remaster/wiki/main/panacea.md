**Item 13**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Consumable, Healing, Magical, Potion  
**Price:** 450 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This potion appears to shift colors, and no two observers describe it in the same way. When consumed, it attempts to counteract all curses and diseases affecting you, as well as the blinded and deafened conditions from spells affecting you. The potion has a counteract rank of 7 and a 1d20 plus 20 modifier for the roll.

*Source: GM Core.*
