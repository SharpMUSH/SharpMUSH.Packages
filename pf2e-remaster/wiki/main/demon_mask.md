**Item 4**  
**Traits:** Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 85 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** wornmask

---

This terrifying mask is crafted in the visage of a leering demon and grants a +1 item bonus to Intimidation checks.

**Activate—Leering Mask** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** The mask casts a [[Fear|main:fear]] spell with a DC of 20.
