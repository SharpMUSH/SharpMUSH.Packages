**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Catfolk|main:catfolk]]  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

Your powerful legs allow you to make sudden and dramatic leaps. You can [[Leap|main:leap]] as a 2-action activity to double the distance you can Leap vertically, or Leap as a 3-action activity to triple the distance you can Leap vertically. You don't automatically fail [[Long Jump|main:long_jump]] for jumping in a different direction than your Stride.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
