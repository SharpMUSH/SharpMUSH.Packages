**Archetype Feat 4** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Viking Dedication|main:viking_dedication]]

---

You make a ranged Strike with a thrown weapon you already have in your hand, Stride, and then [[Interact|main:interact]] to draw another weapon.

---

**Special** If you are [[Raging|main:rage]], and end the Stride adjacent to an enemy, that enemy is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the next Strike you make against it with the weapon you drew before the end of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
