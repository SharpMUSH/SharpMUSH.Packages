**Familiar Ability**

---

Your familiar accretes alchemical liquids in its body. Once per day, you can Interact when your familiar is adjacent to you to gain a versatile vial. You must have the [[Versatile Vials|main:versatile_vials]] ability to select this ability.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
