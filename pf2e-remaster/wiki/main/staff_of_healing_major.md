**Item 12**  
**Traits:** Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 1800 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

This white wood staff is capped at each end with a golden cross adorned with ruby cabochons. A *staff of healing* grants a +3 item bonus to the Hit Points you restore anytime you cast the *heal* spell using your own spell slots or charges from the staff.

---

**Activate** Cast a Spell

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Stabilize|main:stabilize]]
- **1st** [[Heal|main:heal]]
- **2nd** [[Clear Mind|main:clear_mind]] [[Heal|main:heal]] [[Sound Body|main:sound_body]]
- **3rd** [[Cleanse Affliction|main:cleanse_affliction]] [[Heal|main:heal]]
- **4th** [[Cleanse Affliction|main:cleanse_affliction]] [[Clear Mind|main:clear_mind]] [[Sound Body|main:sound_body]]
- **5th** [[Breath of Life|main:breath_of_life]] [[Heal|main:heal]]

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
