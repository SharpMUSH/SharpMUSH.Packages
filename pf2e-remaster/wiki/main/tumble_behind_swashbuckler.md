**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

Your tumbling puts your foes on the back foot. When you [[Tumble Through|main:tumble_through]] successfully, the foe you Tumbled Through is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the next attack you make before the end of your turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
