**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Class:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

You spring into combat faster than foes can react. On the first round of combat, if you roll Deception or Stealth for initiative, creatures that haven't acted are [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] to you.

*Source: Player Core.*
