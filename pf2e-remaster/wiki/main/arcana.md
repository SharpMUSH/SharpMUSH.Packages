**Key attribute:** Intelligence

Knowledge of arcane magic and theory, magical traditions, and creatures of arcane significance such as constructs and dragons.

Your Arcana modifier is your Intelligence modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Arcana (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Borrow an Arcane Spell|main:borrow_an_arcane_spell]]
- [[Decipher Writing|main:decipher_writing]]
- [[Identify Magic|main:identify_magic]]
- [[Learn a Spell|main:learn_a_spell]]

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Arcana:

- **Level 1:** [[Arcane Sense|main:arcane_sense]], [[Quick Identification|main:quick_identification]], [[Recognize Spell|main:recognize_spell]], [[Trick Magic Item|main:trick_magic_item]]
- **Level 2:** [[Assured Identification|main:assured_identification]], [[Magical Shorthand|main:magical_shorthand]]
- **Level 4:** [[Hidden Magic|main:hidden_magic]]
- **Level 7:** [[Quick Recognition|main:quick_recognition]]
- **Level 15:** [[Unified Theory|main:unified_theory]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
