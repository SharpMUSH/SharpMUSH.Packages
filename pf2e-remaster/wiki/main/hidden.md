A creature you are hidden from knows which space you are in but not exactly where you are. You usually become hidden by using Stealth to Hide.

- That creature is [[Off-Guard]] to you.
- It must succeed at a DC 11 flat check to target you with an attack, spell or other effect, or the effect fails to affect you. Area effects need no flat check.
- A creature Seeking you with only imprecise senses leaves you hidden rather than [[Observed]].
- A creature can Seek to try to observe you.

*Source: Player Core, p. 444.*
