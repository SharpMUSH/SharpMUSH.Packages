**Class Feat 8** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

**Requirements** You're [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] or [[Restrained|main:restrained]] by a creature.

---

When you wriggle free from an enemy's grasp, you take something of theirs with you. You attempt to [[Escape|main:escape]] from being grabbed or restrained by a creature. If you succeed, you can also attempt to [[Steal|main:steal]] from the creature, even if the creature is in combat.

*Source: Player Core.*
