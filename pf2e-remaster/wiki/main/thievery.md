**Key attribute:** Dexterity

Sleight of hand: picking pockets and locks, and disabling traps and devices.

Your Thievery modifier is your Dexterity modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Thievery (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Palm an Object|main:palm_an_object]]
- [[Steal|main:steal]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Disable a Device|main:disable_a_device]]
- [[Pick a Lock|main:pick_a_lock]]

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Thievery:

- **Level 1:** [[Concealing Legerdemain|main:concealing_legerdemain]], [[Dirty Trick|main:dirty_trick]], [[Pickpocket|main:pickpocket]], [[Subtle Theft|main:subtle_theft]]
- **Level 2:** [[Wary Disarmament|main:wary_disarmament]]
- **Level 7:** [[Quick Unlock|main:quick_unlock]], [[Tumbling Theft|main:tumbling_theft]]
- **Level 15:** [[Legendary Thief|main:legendary_thief]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
