**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Lizardfolk|main:lizardfolk]]

---

Your toes are adapted for gripping and climbing. You gain the [[Combat Climber|main:combat_climber]] feat as a bonus feat, and as long as you aren't wearing footwear, you can use the sticky pads on your feet to climb, leaving your hands free. Additionally, if you roll a success on an Athletics check to climb, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
