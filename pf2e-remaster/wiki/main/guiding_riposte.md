**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Dueling Riposte|main:dueling_riposte]]

---

By shifting your weight and angling your weapon, you guide your opponent to a more favorable position. When you use [[Dueling Riposte|main:dueling_riposte]] to Strike and you hit, you can [[Reposition|main:reposition]] up to 10 feet.

*Source: Player Core.*
