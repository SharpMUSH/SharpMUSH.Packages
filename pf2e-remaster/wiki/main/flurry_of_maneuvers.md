**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Monk|main:monk]]  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You flurry is a combination of maneuvers. You can replace one or both of your attacks during a Flurry of Blows with [[Grapples|main:grapple]], [[Repositions|main:reposition]], [[Shoves|main:shove]], or [[Trips|main:trip]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
