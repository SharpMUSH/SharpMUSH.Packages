**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Catfolk|main:catfolk]]

---

You can elude supernatural disaster. When you would gain the [[Doomed|main:doomed]] condition, you can attempt a DC 17 flat check. On a success, you don't gain the doomed condition.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
