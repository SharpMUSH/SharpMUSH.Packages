**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Kholo|main:kholo]]

---

Once per day, you can cast [[Impaling Spike|main:impaling_spike]] as a 7th-rank innate occult spell, except the spike is made of bone instead of cold iron. The bone from the spell has the effects of the *ghost touch* property rune and can [[Immobilize|main:immobilized]] incorporeal creatures as if they were corporeal.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
