**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Kobold|main:kobold]]

---

When you're in difficult terrain from an environmental feature, you can [[Take Cover|main:take_cover]], even if the feature wouldn't ordinarily be large enough for you to Take Cover.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
