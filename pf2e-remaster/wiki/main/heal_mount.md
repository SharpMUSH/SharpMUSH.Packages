**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Faithful Steed|main:faithful_steed]]; lay on hands

---

Your devotion to your mount manifests as a surge of vital energy.

When you cast [[Lay on Hands|main:lay_on_hands]] on your mount, instead of the normal amount, the spell restores 10 Hit Points, plus 10 for each heightened rank.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
