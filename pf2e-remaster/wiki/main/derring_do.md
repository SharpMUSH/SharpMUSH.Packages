**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** Fortune, [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

When you compound panache with even more derring-do, it somehow tends to work out. When you already have [[Panache|main:panache]], you can roll twice and use the higher result on checks to which the circumstance bonus from [[Stylish Combatant|main:stylish_combatant]] applies ([[Tumble Through|main:tumble_through]] and any skill actions with the bravado trait).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
