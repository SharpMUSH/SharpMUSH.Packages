**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

You've learned a flexible way to position your [[Buckler|main:buckler]] to provide more protection. When you [[Raise a Shield|main:raise_a_shield]] to gain a circumstance bonus to AC from a buckler, increase the bonus from +1 to +2.

While you have a buckler raised, if a creature critically misses you with a Strike, you gain panache until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
