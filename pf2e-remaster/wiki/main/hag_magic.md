**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Changeling|main:changeling]]

---

Through your heritage, you can replicate a hag's magic. Choose one common spell of 4th rank or lower from those available to a coven, including the spells contributed to a coven by a hag of your mother's type. You can cast this spell once per day as a 4th-rank occult innate spell.

Spells available to all covens are [[Augury|main:augury]], [[Charm|main:charm]], [[Clairaudience|main:clairaudience]], [[Clairvoyance|main:clairvoyance]], [[Dream Message|main:dream_message]], and [[Illusory Disguise|main:illusory_disguise]]. The eligible spells granted by the most prominent types of hag are:

**Cuckoo Hag** [[Nightmare|main:nightmare]], [[Spellwrack|main:spellwrack]]

**Iron Hag** [[Earthbind|main:earthbind]], [[Spellwrack|main:spellwrack]]

**Sea Hag** [[Humanoid Form|main:humanoid_form]], [[Mariner's Curse|main:mariners_curse]], [[Water Walk|main:water_walk]]

**Sweet Hag** [[Charm|main:charm]], [[Honeyed Words|main:honeyed_words]], [[Outcast's Curse|main:outcasts_curse]]

*Source: Player Core.*
