**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]

---

Fey whimsy or a tryst in a moonlit grove put the bewitching magic of the realm of the fey into your family's bloodline.

**Tradition** primal

**Bloodline Skills** Deception, Nature

**Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip [[Figment|main:figment]], 1st: [[Charm|main:charm]], 2nd: [[Laughing Fit|main:laughing_fit]], 3rd: [[Enthrall|main:enthrall]], 4th: [[Suggestion|main:suggestion]], 5th: [[Hallucination|main:hallucination]], 6th: [[Mislead|main:mislead]], 7th: [[Visions of Danger|main:visions_of_danger]], 8th: [[Uncontrollable Dance|main:uncontrollable_dance]], 9th: [[Metamorphosis|main:metamorphosis]]

**Bloodline Spells** initial: [[Faerie Dust|main:faerie_dust]], advanced: [[Fey Disappearance|main:fey_disappearance]], greater: [[Fey Glamour|main:fey_glamour]]

**Blood Magic—Cloak of Ribbons** Colorful fey ribbons dance around you. Either you gain a +2 status bonus to Performance checks for 1 round, or you can become [[Concealed|main:concealed]] for 1 round. Such obvious concealment can't be used to [[Hide|main:hide]]

*Source: Player Core 2.*
