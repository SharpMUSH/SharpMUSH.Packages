**Usage** affixed to a melee weapon

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This miniature club is typically affixed to a weapon by an iron chain. When you activate the cudgel, you use [[Brutal Finish|main:brutal_finish]], as the fighter feat. You must meet the normal requirements, including those of the press trait.

If you have the Brutal Finish feat, add an additional weapon damage die on a success or a failure to the normal results.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
