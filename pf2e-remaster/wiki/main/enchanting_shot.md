**Archetype Feat 8** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Emotion, Magical, Mental  
**Prerequisites:** [[Eldritch Archer Dedication|main:eldritch_archer_dedication]]

---

With a single whisper carried on the wind, you enchant your ammunition to make a foe more vulnerable to your attacks.

Make a bow or crossbow Strike. On a hit, the target takes an additional 2d6 mental damage. On a critical hit, the target also becomes [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]]. The mental damage increases to 3d6 if your weapon has a *greater striking rune*, or to 4d6 if your weapon has a *major striking rune*.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
