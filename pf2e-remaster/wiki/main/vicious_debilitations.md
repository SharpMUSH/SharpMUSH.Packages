**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** ruffian racket; [[Debilitating Strike|main:debilitating_strike_class_feature]]

---

The debilitations you dish out seriously impair your foes. Add the following debilitations to the list you can choose from when you use Debilitating Strike.

- **Debilitation** The target gains weakness 5 to your choice of bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage. Vicious Debilitations
- **Debilitation** The target becomes [[Clumsy 1|main:clumsy]].

*Source: Player Core.*
