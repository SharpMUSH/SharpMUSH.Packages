**Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Stealth|main:stealth]]

---

You can move your full Speed when you [[Sneak|main:sneak]]. You can use Swift Sneak while Burrowing, Climbing, Flying, or Swimming instead of Striding if you have the corresponding movement type.
