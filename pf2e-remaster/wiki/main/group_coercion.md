**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

You can strong-arm people effectively, even when you don't have them isolated. When you [[Coerce|main:coerce]], you can compare your Intimidation check result to the Will DCs of up to five targets instead of one.

It's possible to get a different degree of success for each target. The number of targets you can Coerce in a single action increases to 10 if you're an expert, 25 if you're a master, and 50 if you're legendary.

*Source: Player Core.*
