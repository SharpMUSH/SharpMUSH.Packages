**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

Unlike most of your kind, who dismiss goblins as embarrassments or expendable annoyances, you have studied the methodology behind their irresponsible and incomprehensible actions. You gain the [[Adopted Ancestry|main:adopted_ancestry]] general feat and must select goblin as the feat's chosen ancestry. You also gain one 1st-level goblin ancestry feat.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
