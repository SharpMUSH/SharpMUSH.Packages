**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]]

---

You can enter a second rage, but afterward you need to catch your breath. When you [[Rage|main:rage]], you can choose to gain temporary HP from the Rage even if 1 minute hasn't passed (or a full turn if you have the [[Revitalizing Rage|main:revitalizing_rage]] class feature). If you do, once this Rage ends you become [[Fatigued|main:fatigued]] until you rest for 10 minutes.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
