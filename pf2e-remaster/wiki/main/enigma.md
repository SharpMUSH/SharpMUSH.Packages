**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Bard|main:bard]]

---

Your muse is a mystery, driving you to uncover the hidden secrets of life and the multiverse. These muses can be people you cannot fully grasp, texts layered deeply with symbolism, or emotional paradoxes that underline a lifetime's work. Art inspired by an enigma muse could be cryptic, eerie, or laden with speculation and conspiracy. As a bard with the enigma muse, you support your allies by providing knowledge alongside inspiration and occult support.
