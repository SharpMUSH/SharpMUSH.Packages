**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Monk|main:monk]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Qi Spells|main:qi_spells]]

---

You learn a stance that lets you attack from a distance. You gain the [[Wild Winds Stance|main:wild_winds_stance]] qi spell.

While entering the stance is a qi spell, the wind crash Strikes the stance grants are not, so you can use them as often as you like while in the stance.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
