**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** leaf order

---

You can make your skin take on the woody endurance of an ancient tree and have your familiar follow suit. You can cast [[Oaken Resilience|main:oaken_resilience]] at will as an innate primal spell with a spell rank one lower than the highest rank of spells you can cast. This spell targets you, as well as your leshy familiar if it's within 30 feet of you.

*Source: Player Core.*
