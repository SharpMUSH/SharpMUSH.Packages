**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Poison  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Area:** 10-foot emanation  
**Defense:** basic Fortitude  
**Duration:** 1 round

---

You emit a cloud of toxic smoke that temporarily obscures you from sight. Each creature except you in the area when you Cast the Spell takes 1d6 poison damage (basic Fortitude save). A creature that critically fails the saving throw also becomes [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]]. All creatures in the area become [[Concealed|main:concealed]], and all creatures outside the smoke become concealed to creatures within it. This smoke can be dispersed by a strong wind.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d6

*Source: Player Core 2.*
