**Ancestry Feat 1** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Tengu|main:tengu]]

---

Assuming a peculiar stance, you make a short hop on each toe. You make a vertical [[Leap|main:leap]] without triggering reactions that can be triggered by move actions or upon leaving or entering a square.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
