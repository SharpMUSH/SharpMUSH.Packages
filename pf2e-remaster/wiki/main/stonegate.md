**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Dwarf|main:dwarf]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Stonewalker|main:stonewalker]]

---

Earthen barriers no longer impede your progress. You gain [[Magic Passage|main:magic_passage]] as a 7th-rank divine innate spell that you can cast once per day. Unlike the spell, however, this ability can be used only to open passages through barriers of earth or stone.

*Source: Player Core.*
