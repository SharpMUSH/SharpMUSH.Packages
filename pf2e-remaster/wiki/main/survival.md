**Key attribute:** Wisdom

Living in the wild: finding your way, food and shelter, and following or hiding tracks.

Your Survival modifier is your Wisdom modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Survival (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Sense Direction|main:sense_direction]]
- [[Subsist|main:subsist]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Cover Tracks|main:cover_tracks]]
- [[Track|main:track]]

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Survival:

- **Level 1:** [[Experienced Tracker|main:experienced_tracker]], [[Forager|main:forager]], [[Survey Wildlife|main:survey_wildlife]], [[Terrain Expertise|main:terrain_expertise]]
- **Level 4:** [[Steel Skin|main:steel_skin]]
- **Level 7:** [[Environmental Guide|main:environmental_guide]], [[Monster Crafting|main:monster_crafting]], [[Planar Survival|main:planar_survival]]
- **Level 15:** [[Legendary Guide|main:legendary_guide]], [[Legendary Survivalist|main:legendary_survivalist]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
