**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

During your daily preparations, you can review stratagems for the day's adventures with up to five allies. Later, you can quickly advise them on your schemes using the [[Inspired Stratagem|main:inspired_stratagem]] reaction.

*Source: Player Core.*
