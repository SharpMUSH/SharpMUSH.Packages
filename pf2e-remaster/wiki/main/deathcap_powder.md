**Item 13**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Ingested, Poison  
**Price:** 450 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

The toxic deathcap mushroom can be dried, ground, and treated to form a flavorless powder with accelerated effects.

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 33 Fortitude

**Onset** 10 minutes

**Maximum Duration** 6 minutes

**Stage 1** 7d8 poison damage (1 minute)

**Stage 2** 9d6 poison damage and [[Sickened 2|main:sickened]] (1 minute)

**Stage 3** 8d10 poison damage and [[Sickened 3|main:sickened]] (1 minute)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
