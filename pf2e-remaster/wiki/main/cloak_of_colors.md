**Spell 5**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Visual  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

A cloak of swirling colors shrouds the target. Creatures are [[Dazzled|main:dazzled]] while adjacent to it, and attacking the target causes a brilliant flash of light. A creature that hits the target with a melee attack must attempt a Will save. The creature is then temporarily immune until the end of its turn; this effect has the incapacitation trait.

---

**Success** The attacker is unaffected.

**Failure** The attacker is [[Blinded|main:blinded]] for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The attacker is [[Stunned|main:stunned]] for 1 round

*Source: Player Core 2.*
