**Focus 5**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Bard|main:bard]], Composition, Concentrate, Focus, Manipulate  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** you or 1 ally

---

Your symphony lifts listeners from their worldly concerns. Attempt a Performance check to counteract an effect applying one of the following conditions to the target: [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]], [[Immobilized|main:immobilized]], [[Paralyzed|main:paralyzed]], [[Restrained|main:restrained]], [[Slowed|main:slowed]], or [[Stunned|main:stunned]]. If you fail, you can't target the same effect on the target for 1 day. Use the condition's source to determine the counteract DC (for example, the [[Escape|main:escape]] DC for grabbed).

---

**Heightened (9th)** You can target up to four creatures.
