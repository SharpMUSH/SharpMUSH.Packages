**Spell 7**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Emotion, Incapacitation, Manipulate, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will

---

You scramble a creature's mental faculties and sensory input. The target must attempt a Will saving throw. Regardless of the result of that save, the target is then temporarily immune for 10 minutes. Warp mind's effects happen instantly, so [[Dispel Magic|main:dispel_magic]] and other effects that counteract spells can't counteract them. However, [[Wish|main:wish]] rituals and abilities that can remove non-magical effects can still counteract the effects.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target spends the first action on its next turn with the [[Confused|main:confused]] condition.

**Failure** The target is Confused for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is Confused permanently.

*Source: Player Core.*
