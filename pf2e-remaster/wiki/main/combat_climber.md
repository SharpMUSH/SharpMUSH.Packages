**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

Your techniques allow you to fight as you climb. You're not [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] while Climbing and can Climb with a hand occupied. You must still use another hand and both legs to Climb.

*Source: Player Core.*
