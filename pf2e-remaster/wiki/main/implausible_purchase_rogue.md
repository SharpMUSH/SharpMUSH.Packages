**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** Predictive Purchase

---

You can use [[Prescient Planner|main:prescient_planner]] even if you have already used it after purchasing goods, and you can Interact to draw the item as a single action instead of a 2-action activity. In addition, five times per day, you can use Prescient Planner to pull out a common consumable item up to 6 levels lower than your level.

*Source: Player Core.*
