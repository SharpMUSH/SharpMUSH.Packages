**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Poison Weapon|main:poison_weapon]]

---

You deliver poisons in ways that maximize their harmful effects. When you apply a simple poison with [[Poison Weapon|main:poison_weapon]], the poison deals 2d4 poison damage instead of 1d4 poison damage. You don't waste a poison you apply with Poison Weapon on a critically failed attack roll.

*Source: Player Core.*
