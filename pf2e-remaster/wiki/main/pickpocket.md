**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Thievery|main:thievery]]

---

You can [[Steal|main:steal]] or [[Palm an Object|main:palm_an_object]] that's closely guarded, such as in a pocket, without taking the –5 penalty. You can't steal objects that would be extremely noticeable or time consuming to remove (like worn shoes or armor or actively wielded objects). If you're a master in Thievery, you can attempt to Steal from a creature in combat or otherwise on guard. When doing so, Stealing requires 2 actions instead of 1, and you take a –5 penalty.

*Source: Player Core.*
