**Key attribute:** Charisma

Bending others to your will with threats and shows of force.

Your Intimidation modifier is your Charisma modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Intimidation (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Coerce|main:coerce]]
- [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]]

This skill has no actions that need training.

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Intimidation:

- **Level 1:** [[Group Coercion|main:group_coercion]], [[Intimidating Glare|main:intimidating_glare]], [[Quick Coercion|main:quick_coercion]]
- **Level 2:** [[Intimidating Prowess|main:intimidating_prowess]], [[Lasting Coercion|main:lasting_coercion]], [[Terrifying Resistance|main:terrifying_resistance]]
- **Level 7:** [[Battle Cry|main:battle_cry]], [[Terrified Retreat|main:terrified_retreat]]
- **Level 15:** [[Scare to Death|main:scare_to_death]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
