**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Arcane, Invested  
**Price:** 50 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** wornheadwear

---

This hat comes in many forms, such as a colorful turban or a pointy hat with a brim, and is adorned with symbols or runes. It grants you a +1 item bonus to Arcana checks and allows you to cast the [[Prestidigitation|main:prestidigitation]] cantrip as an arcane innate cantrip.

*Source: GM Core.*
