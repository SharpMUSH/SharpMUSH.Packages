**Background**  
**Attribute boosts:** Dexterity or Strength, free  
**Trained skills:** Warfare Lore ([[Lore|main:lore]])

---

You dedicated yourself to intense training and rigorous study to become a great warrior. The school you attended might have been a traditionalist monastery, an elite military academy, or the local branch of a prestigious mercenary organization.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to **Strength** or **Dexterity**, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in your choice of the Acrobatics or Athletics skill. You gain a skill feat: [[Cat Fall|main:cat_fall]] if you chose Acrobatics or [[Quick Jump|main:quick_jump]] if you chose Athletics. You're also trained in the Warfare Lore skill.

*Source: Player Core.*
