**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Acrobatics|main:acrobatics]]

---

Through nimble movements, a graceful landing, and a bit of luck, you can manage to fall from spectacular heights without a scratch. You gain the [[Cat Fall|main:cat_fall]] skill feat and the [[Plummeting Roll|main:plummeting_roll]] reaction.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
