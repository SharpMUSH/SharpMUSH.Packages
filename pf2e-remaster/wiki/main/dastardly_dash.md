**Class Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Flourish, [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

You dash past an opponent, confounding them with underhanded tactics. Stride up to half your speed. You can attempt a [[Trip|main:trip]] or a [[Dirty Trick|main:dirty_trick]] (if you have that action) against one opponent you pass within range of at any point during your movement.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
