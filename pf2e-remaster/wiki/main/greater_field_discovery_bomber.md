**Class Feature 13**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]

---

You can increase the splash on your bombs to damage creatures within 10 feet, or 15 feet if you have [[Expanded Splash|main:expanded_splash]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
