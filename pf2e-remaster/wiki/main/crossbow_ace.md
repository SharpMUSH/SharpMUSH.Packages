**Class Feat 1** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]

---

**Requirements** You are wielding a crossbow with reload 1 or higher.

---

Your deep understanding of the crossbow allows you to reload efficiently while moving yourself out of the line of return fire. Either [[Create a Diversion|main:create_a_diversion]] or [[Take Cover|main:take_cover]], then Interact to reload. As normal, you must meet the requirements to Take Cover; you must be [[Prone|main:prone]], benefiting from cover, or near a feature that allows you to Take Cover.

*Source: Player Core.*
