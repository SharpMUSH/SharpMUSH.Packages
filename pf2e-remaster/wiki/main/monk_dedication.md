**Archetype Feat 2**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Dedication, Multiclass  
**Prerequisites:** Strength +2; Dexterity +2

---

You become trained in unarmed attacks and gain the [[Powerful Fist|main:powerful_fist]] class feature. You become trained in your choice of Acrobatics or Athletics; if you are already trained in both of these skills, you become trained in a skill of your choice. You become trained in monk class DC.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
