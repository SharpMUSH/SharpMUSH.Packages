**Class Feat 10** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Incapacitation, Rage

---

A quick strike to the face or mouth silences your opponent. Make a melee Strike against an enemy. If it hits, the enemy must succeed at a Fortitude save against your class DC.

---

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The target is dazed and can barely vocalize. It's [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]] and its speech is raspy and hard to understand. It must succeed at a DC 11 flat to use linguistic actions or Cast a Spell, unless the spell has the subtle trait.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is [[Stunned 3|main:stunned]] instead of stunned 1.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
