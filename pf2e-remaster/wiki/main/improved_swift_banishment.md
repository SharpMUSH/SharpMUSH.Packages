**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Swift Banishment|main:swift_banishment]]

---

You easily banish creatures with your weapon. You can use [[Swift Banishment|main:swift_banishment]] as long as you have a spell slot of 5th rank or higher remaining, even if you don't have [[Banishment|main:banishment]] prepared. You must sacrifice a prepared spell of 5th rank or higher, and the *banishment* effect you create is heightened to the rank of that spell. The target takes a –2 circumstance penalty to its save as though you'd paid the extra cost for *banishment*.

*Source: Player Core.*
