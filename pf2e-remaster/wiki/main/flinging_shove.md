**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** Aggressive Block or Brutish Shove

---

Whether you're shoving opponents with a shield or a large weapon, you've learned to push them even further. Increase the distance you [[Shove|main:shove]] your opponent with [[Aggressive Block|main:aggressive_block]] or [[Brutish Shove|main:brutish_shove]] to 10 feet on a success or 20 feet on a critical success. When you use Aggressive Block, you can choose whether the target is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] or Shoved. When you make a Brutish Shove, you also Shove the target 5 feet on a failure.

*Source: Player Core.*
