**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Orc|main:orc]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Orc Ferocity|main:orc_ferocity]]

---

You resist death's clutches with supernatural vigor, daring death to claim you while promising that any such attempt will surely fail. When you use [[Orc Ferocity|main:orc_ferocity]], you gain temporary Hit Points equal to your level.

*Source: Player Core.*
