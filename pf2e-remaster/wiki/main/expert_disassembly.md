**Archetype Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Crafting|main:crafting]]; [[Scrounger Dedication|main:scrounger_dedication]]

You can apply the same techniques you use to reverse engineer objects to disable dangerous traps and break apart locks. You can use Crafting instead of Thievery to [[Disable a Device|main:disable_a_device]] or [[Pick a Lock|main:pick_a_lock]].
