**Key attribute:** Charisma

Entertaining an audience through acting, music, dance, oratory and similar arts.

Your Performance modifier is your Charisma modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Performance (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Perform|main:perform]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Earn Income|main:earn_income]]
