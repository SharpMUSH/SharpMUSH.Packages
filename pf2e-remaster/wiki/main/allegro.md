**Cantrip 7**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Bard|main:bard]], Cantrip, Composition, Concentrate, Emotion, Mental  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 ally  
**Duration:** 1 round

---

You perform rapidly, speeding up your ally. The ally becomes [[Quickened|main:quickened]] and can use the additional action to Strike, Stride, or Step.
