You have lost blood, life force or some other essence. Drained always has a value.

- You take a status penalty equal to the value on Constitution-based rolls and DCs, such as Fortitude saves.
- You lose Hit Points equal to your level (minimum 1) times the value, and your maximum Hit Points drop by the same amount. A 3rd-level character who becomes drained 3 loses 9 Hit Points and 9 maximum Hit Points. This loss is not damage.

**Ends:** the value drops by 1 after each full night's rest. Your maximum Hit Points go back up, but the lost Hit Points don't come back on their own.

*Source: Player Core, p. 443.*
