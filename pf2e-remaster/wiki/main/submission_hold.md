**Archetype Feat 8** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wrestler Dedication|main:wrestler_dedication]]

---

**Requirements** You have a creature [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] or [[Restrained|main:restrained]].

---

Your iron grip saps your opponent's strength. Attempt an Athletics check to [[Grapple|main:grapple]] the creature you have grabbed or restrained, with the following additional effects if you succeed.

---

**Critical Success** The target is [[Enfeebled 2|main:enfeebled]] until the end of its next turn and [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]] for 1 minute.

**Success** The target is enfeebled 1 until the end of its next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
