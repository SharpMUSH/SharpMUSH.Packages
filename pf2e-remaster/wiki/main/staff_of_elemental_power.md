**Item 6**  
**Traits:** Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 230 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

This staff is tapered at the base and carved into a gem-studded twist at the top. While wielding the staff, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to checks to identify elemental creatures.

**Activate** Cast a Spell

---

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Frostbite|main:frostbite]]
- **1st** [[Breathe Fire|main:breathe_fire]], [[Pummeling Rubble|main:pummeling_rubble]], [[Thunderstrike|main:thunderstrike]]
- **2nd** [[Blazing Bolt|main:blazing_bolt]], [[Resist Energy|main:resist_energy]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.

*Source: GM Core.*
