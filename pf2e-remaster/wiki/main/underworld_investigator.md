**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]

---

Your work as an investigator makes you familiar with the criminal element. You might be a criminal yourself, or you might have gained an understanding of how they operate from the outside. You become trained in Underworld Lore, and you gain your [[Pursue a Lead|main:pursue_a_lead]] investigation bonus to Thievery checks to investigate a case (such as checks to [[Steal|main:steal]] a clue from a suspect or [[Pick a Lock|main:pick_a_lock]] to open a safe with damning documents).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
