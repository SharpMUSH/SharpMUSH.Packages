**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Cantrip, Concentrate, Manipulate, Water  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Area:** 5-foot cube  
**Defense:** basic Reflex

---

Water blasts upward, coming out of the ground, rising from a pool, or even manifesting from thin air. Any creatures in the area take 2d4 bludgeoning damage, with a basic Reflex saving throw. A creature that critically fails its save is disoriented by the explosion of water, becoming [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the end of your next turn.

You can change this spell's area to a 5-foot burst, provided you center the burst in a body of water. This body of water can be as small as a pond or creek, but not as small as a puddle or bathtub.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d4.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
