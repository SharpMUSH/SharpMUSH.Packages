**Class Feat 16** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Light, [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]], Spellshape, [[Wizard|main:wizard]]

---

Your spells become a radiant display of light and color. If your next action is to Cast a Spell that doesn't have the darkness trait, has no duration, and requires creatures to attempt a Reflex save, the spell explodes in a spray of scintillating lights, in addition to its other effects. Each creature that failed its Reflex save against the spell is [[Dazzled|main:dazzled]] for 1 round, and those who critically failed are instead [[Blinded|main:blinded]] for 1 round.

*Source: Player Core.*
