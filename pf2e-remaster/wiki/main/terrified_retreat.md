**Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

When you critically succeed at the [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] action, if the target's level is lower than yours, the target is [[Fleeing|main:fleeing]] for 1 round.
