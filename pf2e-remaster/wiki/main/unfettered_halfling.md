**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Halfling|main:halfling]]

---

You were either pressed into indentured servitude or shackled in a prison, but you've since escaped and have trained to ensure you'll never be caught again. Whenever you roll a success on a check to [[Escape|main:escape]] or a saving throw against an effect that would impose the [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]], [[Immobilized|main:immobilized]], or [[Restrained|main:restrained]] condition on you, you get a critical success instead. Whenever a creature rolls a failure on a check to [[Grapple|main:grapple]] you, it gets a critical failure instead.

*Source: Player Core.*
