**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Tripkee|main:tripkee]]

---

Escaping your nets is no simple task. The Athletics DC to [[Force Open|main:force_open]] or [[Escape|main:escape]] your nets increases from 16 to 18. After a creature you [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] with a net Escapes or Forces Open the net, stray strands of the net cling to the creature, causing them to remain [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the beginning of their next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
