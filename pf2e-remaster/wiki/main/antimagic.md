**Item 15**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Magical  
**Price:** 6500 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto armor

---

This intricate rune displaces spell energy, granting you a +1 status bonus to saving throws against magical effects.

---

**Activate—Antimagic Armor** (reaction) (concentrate)

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** A spell targets you or includes you in its area

**Effect** The armor attempts to counteract the triggering spell with the effect of a 7th-rank [[Dispel Magic|main:dispel_magic]] spell and a counteract modifier of +26.

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of *dispel magic*.

*Source: GM Core.*
