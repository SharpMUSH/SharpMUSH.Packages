**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You easily pull yourself onto ledges. When you [[Grab an Edge|main:grab_an_edge]], you can pull yourself onto that surface and stand. You can use Athletics instead of a Reflex save to Grab an Edge. If you Climb or [[Leap|main:leap]] to within 5 feet beneath the top of an edge, you can pull yourself onto the surface and stand as part of that action.

*Source: Player Core.*
