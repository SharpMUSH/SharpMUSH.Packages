**Item 5**  
**Traits:** Magical  
**Price:** 150 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto a weapon

---

A *vitalizing* weapon pulses with vital energy, dealing an extra 1d6 persistent vitality damage to undead. On a critical hit, the undead is also [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]] until the end of your next turn.

*Source: GM Core.*
