**Item 5**  
**Traits:** Magical, Propulsive  
**Price:** 155 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Sling | 1d6 bludgeoning | 1 | 60 ft. | 1 |

---

Made of shiny brown leather, this *+1 striking sling* has a single white thread interwoven into its cord.

**Activate—Unleash Roar** (2 actions) (manipulate, sonic)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** You pull the white thread free, then whirl the sling in circles at high speed. It lets out an ear-piercing wave of sound. Each creature in a 30-foot cone takes 4d6 sonic damage (basic DC 21 Fortitude save). Any creature that fails is [[Deafened|main:deafened]] for 1 round, or 1 hour on a critical failure.

*Source: GM Core.*
