**Activate** (1 action) Strike

---

This flask is filled with a murky purple gas that briefly interferes with normal brain activity. A dread ampoule deals 1d6 mental damage and 1 mental splash damage.

On a hit, the target becomes [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]], or [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]] on a critical hit. Many types also grant an item bonus to attack rolls.

*Source: GM Core.*
