**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Arcana|main:arcana]]

---

Your study of magic allows you to instinctively sense its presence. You can cast 1st-rank [[Detect Magic|main:detect_magic_spell]] at will as an arcane innate spell. If you're a master in Arcana, the spell is heightened to 3rd rank; if you're legendary, it is heightened to 4th rank.

*Source: Player Core.*
