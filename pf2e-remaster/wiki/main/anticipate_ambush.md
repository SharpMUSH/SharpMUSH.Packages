**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** Exploration, [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Stealth|main:stealth]]

---

You keep watch for any [[Hidden|main:hidden]] creatures or dangerous sites, moving at half your travel Speed. If you're Anticipating an Ambush at the start of an encounter, you roll a Perception check for initiative, and all enemies rolling Stealth for initiative take a –2 circumstance penalty to their Stealth checks.

*Source: Player Core.*
