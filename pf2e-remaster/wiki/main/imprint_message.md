**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 object

---

You project psychic vibrations onto the target object, imprinting it with a short message or emotional theme of your design. This imprinted sensation is revealed to a creature who casts [[Object Reading|main:object_reading]] on the target object, replacing any emotional events the item was present for. If the object is in the area of a [[Retrocognition|main:retrocognition]] spell, the imprinted messages appear as major events in the timeline, but they don't interfere with any other visions.

If the object is targeted with [[Read Aura|main:read_aura]] of a higher spell rank than *imprint message*, the caster learns that the object has been magically modified. When you [[Cast this Spell|main:cast_a_spell]], any prior vibrations placed on an object by previous castings of *imprint message* fade.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
