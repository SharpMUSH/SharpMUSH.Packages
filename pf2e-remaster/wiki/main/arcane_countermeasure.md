**Focus 5**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Focus, Manipulate, [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]  
**Cast:** (reaction)  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Targets:** the spell cast by the triggering creature

---

**Trigger** A creature within range that you can see Casts a Spell.

---

You undermine the target spell, making it easier to defend against. You reduce the spell's rank by 1, and targets of the spell gain a +2 status bonus to any saving throws, skill checks, AC, or DC against it.

You can't reduce the spell's rank below its minimum. For example, a 5th-rank [[Howling Blizzard|main:howling_blizzard]] would remain 5th-rank, but a 5th-rank [[Fireball|main:fireball]] would become 4th-rank. Targets still gain all the other benefits, even if you don't reduce the spell's rank.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
