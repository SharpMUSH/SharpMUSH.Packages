**Class Feat 6** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage  
**Prerequisites:** superstition instinct

---

**Requirements** You've seen the target Cast a Spell

---

You use your hatred of magic to lash out at a known spellcaster. Make a melee Strike against the required creature. If you hit and deal damage, the target is [[Stupefied 1|main:stupefied]], or [[Stupefied 2|main:stupefied]] on a critical hit, until the beginning of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
