**Item 15**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Consumable, Magical  
**Price:** 1300 gp  
**Bulk:** —

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

The shaft of this bolt is scorched and blackened, and handling it coats your fingers with a fine black powder. When an activated *disintegration bolt* hits a target, it is subject to a [[Disintegrate|main:disintegrate]] spell requiring a DC 34 Fortitude save. As with the spell, a critical hit on the attack roll causes the target's saving throw outcome to be one degree worse.

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of *disintegrate*.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
