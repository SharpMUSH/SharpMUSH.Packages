**Class Feat 16** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Stance  
**Prerequisites:** [[Twin Parry|main:twin_parry]]

---

**Requirements** You are wielding two melee weapons, one in each hand.

---

You're always ready to use your off-hand weapon to interfere with attacks against you. While you are in this stance, you constantly gain the benefits of the [[Twin Parry|main:twin_parry]] action.

*Source: Player Core.*
