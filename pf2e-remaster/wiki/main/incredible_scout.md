**General Feat 11**  
**Traits:** Exploration, General  
**Prerequisites:** master in Perception

---

When you scout, you are particularly alert for danger, granting your allies precious moments to prepare to fight. When using the [[Scout|main:scout_action]] exploration activity, you grant your allies a +2 circumstance bonus to their initiative rolls instead of a +1 circumstance bonus.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
