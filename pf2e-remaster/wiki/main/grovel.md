**Ancestry Feat 5** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Auditory, Concentrate, Emotion, [[Kobold|main:kobold]], Mental  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Deception|main:deception]]

---

You convince your foe you're less of a threat. You attempt to [[Feint|main:feint]] against a creature. Unlike a normal Feint, the creature can be within 30 feet instead of in your melee reach, and you make your check against its Will DC instead of its Perception DC.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
