**Item 7**  
**Traits:** Invested, Occult  
**Price:** 360 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** worncloak

---

This cloak flows, covering and concealing the wearer's body. The cloak allows you to cast [[Figment|main:figment]] as an occult innate cantrip. Although naturally a dull gray, while invested the cloak picks up colors and patterns from its surroundings, granting a +1 item bonus to Stealth checks.

**Activate—Draw Hood** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** You draw the hood up and gain the effects of [[Invisibility|main:invisibility]], with the spell's normal duration or until you pull the hood back down, whichever comes first.

*Source: GM Core.*
