You have fewer actions. Slowed always has a value.

- When you regain actions at the start of your turn, you regain that many fewer.
- Becoming slowed during your turn doesn't cost you actions until your next turn.
- [[Stunned]] overrides slowed.

*Source: Player Core, p. 446.*
