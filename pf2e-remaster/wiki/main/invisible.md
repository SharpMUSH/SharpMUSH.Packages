You can't be seen.

- You are [[Undetected|main:undetected]] to everyone.
- A creature can Seek you. If its Perception check beats your Stealth DC, you become [[Hidden|main:hidden]] to it until you Sneak to become undetected again.
- If you turn invisible while someone can already see you, you start hidden to them, not undetected, until you Sneak successfully.
- You can't become [[Observed|main:observed]] while invisible except through special abilities or magic.

*Source: Player Core, p. 444.*
