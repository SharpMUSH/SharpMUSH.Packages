**Item 18**  
**Traits:** Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 22000 gp  
**Bulk:** 2

| Category | AC Bonus | Dex Cap | Strength | Check Penalty | Speed Penalty | Group |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium | +4 | +1 | +3 | -3 | -5 ft. | Plate |

---

This *+2 resilient breastplate* is made from shining bronze overlaid with reinforcing golden panels emblazoned with images of loyal soldiers. Wearing this breastplate grants you a commanding aura. You gain a +3 item bonus to Diplomacy checks, but you take a -3 item penalty to Stealth checks to [[Hide|main:hide]] and [[Sneak|main:sneak]] and Deception checks to [[Impersonate|main:impersonate]].

---

**Activate—Command Bravery** (1 action) (concentrate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** You grant allies within 100 feet a +2 status bonus to saves against fear effects for 1 minute. When you activate this ability, each affected ally who's [[Frightened|main:frightened]] reduces their frightened value by 1.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
