**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

When you successfully [[Coerce|main:coerce]] someone, the maximum time they comply increases to a week, still determined by the GM. If you're legendary, the maximum increases to a month.

*Source: Player Core.*
