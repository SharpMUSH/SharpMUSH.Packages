**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

The spells of those you have [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] are less effective on you. If you succeed in Demoralizing a creature, for the next 24 hours you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saving throws against that creature's spells.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
