**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Dhampir|main:dhampir]]

You call forth the blood of your foes to grant you vigor. You can cast [[Vampiric Exsanguination|main:vampiric_exsanguination]] as a 7th-rank divine innate spell once per day.
