**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Premonition of Avoidance|main:premonition_of_avoidance]]

---

You can project your premonitions of danger to your allies. When you use [[Premonition of Avoidance|main:premonition_of_avoidance]], allies within 20 feet of you who are rolling the same saving throw against the same hazard also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to their saving throws.

*Source: Player Core.*
