**Class Feat 16**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Extend Elixir|main:extend_elixir]]

---

Your body readily accepts and retains minor changes. When you drink one of your alchemical items that has the elixir and infused traits and a duration of 1 minute or more, you can make the elixir's duration indefinite. This can exceed the 10-minute limit of an item made with [[Quick Alchemy|main:quick_alchemy]]. You can do so only if the elixir's level is half your level or lower. If you later consume a different elixir and make it indefinite, the effect of the previous indefinite elixir ends.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
