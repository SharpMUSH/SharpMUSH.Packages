**Spell 2**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Water  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (3 actions)  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Area:** 20-foot burst  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

You call forth a cloud of mist. All creatures within the mist become [[Concealed|main:concealed]], and all creatures outside the mist become concealed to creatures within it. You can Dismiss the cloud.

*Source: Player Core.*
