**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** blessed armament

---

Your blessed armament radiates power, further enhancing your chosen weapon. When you choose the weapon for your blessed armament during your daily preparations, add the [[Astral|main:astral]] and [[Brilliant|main:brilliant]] property runes to the list of effects you can choose from. If you're holy, also add the [[Holy|main:holy]] rune, and if you're unholy, also add the [[Unholy|main:unholy]] rune.

In addition, you can change the rune you've selected for the day to a different rune from your list as a 10-minute activity that has the concentrate, divine, and exploration traits. Changing the rune doesn't restore abilities that can be used only a limited number of times, such as Holy Healing for the *holy* rune

*Source: Player Core 2.*
