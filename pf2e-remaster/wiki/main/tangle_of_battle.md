**Class Feat 10** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage

---

**Trigger** You critically hit an adjacent enemy.

---

Your vicious strike pulls your foe closer, giving you the opportunity to grab them. Attempt to [[Grapple|main:grapple]] the triggering enemy.
