**Item 10**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 900 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

The stone head piece of this smooth wooden staff is carved to look like a lidless eye. While wielding it, you gain a +1 status bonus to Perception checks made for initiative.

**Activate** Cast a Spell

---

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Detect Magic|main:detect_magic_spell]]
- **1st** [[Sure Strike|main:sure_strike]]
- **2nd** [[Darkvision|main:darkvision]], [[See the Unseen|main:see_the_unseen]], [[Translate|main:translate]]
- **3rd** [[Darkvision|main:darkvision]], [[Mind Reading|main:mind_reading]]
- **4th** [[Clairvoyance|main:clairvoyance]], [[Detect Scrying|main:detect_scrying]], [[Telepathy|main:telepathy]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
