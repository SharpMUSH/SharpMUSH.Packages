**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]

---

You have a keen sense for avoiding spurious lines of inquiry. When you roll a critical failure on a skill check to [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] related to a question about one of your active investigations, you get a failure instead.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
