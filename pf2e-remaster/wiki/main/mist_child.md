**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Changeling|main:changeling]]

---

You blend in with unnatural subtlety. When you are [[Concealed|main:concealed]] or [[Hidden|main:hidden]], increase the DC of the flat check to target you to 6 if you're concealed or 12 if you're hidden.
