**Skill Feat 15**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** legendary in [[Stealth|main:stealth]]; [[Swift Sneak|main:swift_sneak]]

---

You're always sneaking unless you choose to be seen, even when there's nowhere to hide. You can [[Hide|main:hide]] and [[Sneak|main:sneak]] even without cover or being [[Concealed|main:concealed]]. When you employ an exploration tactic other than [[Avoiding Notice|main:avoid_notice]], you also gain the benefits of Avoiding Notice unless you choose not to.

*Source: Player Core.*
