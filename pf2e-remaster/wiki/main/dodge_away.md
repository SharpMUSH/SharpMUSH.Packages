**Archetype Feat 6** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Acrobat Dedication|main:acrobat_dedication]]

---

**Trigger** You are the target of a melee attack.

**Requirements** You're aware of the attack and aren't [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]].

---

You use your acrobatic prowess to evade an attack, using momentum to keep yourself moving, if you choose.

You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to AC against the triggering attack. If the attack misses you, you can [[Step|main:step]] after the Strike.

If you're a master in Acrobatics, you can move 10 feet on this Step instead of 5 feet.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
