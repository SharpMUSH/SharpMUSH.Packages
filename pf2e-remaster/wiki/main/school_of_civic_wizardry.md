**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Wizard|main:wizard]]

---

You've learned the humble art of construction, of finding lost people and things, of moving speedily among buildings and moats—yet these same arts can be turned to demolition, and the constructs you animate to build bridges can just as easily tear them down.
