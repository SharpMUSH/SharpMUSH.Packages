**Spell 5**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Manipulate, Shadow, Teleportation  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** 1 minute  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** you and up to 10 willing creatures touched  
**Duration:** 8 hours

---

You move partially into the plane of shadow, using its warped nature to speed your travels. Each hour, you cover roughly as much ground as you normally would in 3 days. The landmarks are vague and symbolic rather than concrete, leaving you within a mile of your intended destination when you Dismiss the spell or its duration ends.

*Source: Player Core.*
