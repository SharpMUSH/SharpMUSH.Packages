**Archetype Feat 2**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Dedication  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

As a pirate, you sail the seas in search of enemy ships to plunder and great adventures to embark on. You gain the [[Additional Lore|main:additional_lore]] general feat for Sailing Lore or for a specific coastal city you have a connection to. You ignore the effects of difficult terrain or uneven ground caused by unstable ground (such as the deck of a ship). Additionally, you gain the [[Boarding Assault|main:boarding_assault]] action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
