**Item 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Consumable, Illusion, Magical, Potion  
**Price:** 20 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

An *invisibility* potion is colorless and oddly lightweight. Upon drinking it, you gain the effects of a 2nd-rank [[Invisibility|main:invisibility]] spell.
