**Familiar Ability**

---

Your plant familiar can change shape as a single action, transforming into a Tiny plant of a type roughly similar to the familiar's nature. This otherwise uses the effects of [[One with Plants|main:one_with_plants]]. You must have a familiar with the plant trait to select this ability.

*Source: Player Core.*
