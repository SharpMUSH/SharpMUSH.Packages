**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** Fortune, [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Charmed Life|main:charmed_life]]

---

You always seem to bounce back from the worst. When you use [[Charmed Life|main:charmed_life]], roll the save twice (including the +2 circumstance bonus from Charmed Life) and use the better result.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
