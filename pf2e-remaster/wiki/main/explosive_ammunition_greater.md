**Item 13**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Fire, Magical  
**Price:** 520 gp  
**Bulk:** —

---

**Ammunition** any

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This piece of ammunition is coated in gritty black soot. When activated *greater explosive ammunition* hits a target, the missile explodes in a 10-foot burst, dealing 10d6 fire damage to each creature in the area (including the target). Each creature must attempt a basic DC 30 Reflex save.

*Source: GM Core.*
