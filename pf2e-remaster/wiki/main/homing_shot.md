**Archetype Feat 14** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Magical  
**Prerequisites:** [[Eldritch Archer Dedication|main:eldritch_archer_dedication]]

---

Your shots zip around corners and fly at impossible angles to reach your target.

Make a bow or crossbow Strike against a foe you can see; the ammunition travels to your target, even going around corners. You ignore the target's [[Concealed|main:concealed]] condition and all cover.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
