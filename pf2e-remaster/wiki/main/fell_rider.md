**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]  
**Prerequisites:** animal companion

---

You have trained with your animal companion to become a terrifying juggernaut on the battlefield. Your animal companion becomes trained in Intimidation. If your animal companion uses Support while serving as your mount, it grants you the effects of the [[Aid|main:aid]] reaction on your first Intimidation check to [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] on the same turn, even though it can't take reactions.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
