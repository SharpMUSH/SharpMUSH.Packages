**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

When you hit hard enough, you leave an opening so your ally can jump in on the action. Whenever you critically hit an [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] opponent with a melee attack and deal damage, the target triggers a Reactive Strike reaction from one ally of your choice who has that reaction, as if the enemy had used a manipulate action.

*Source: Player Core.*
