**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Halfling|main:halfling]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Distracting Shadows|main:distracting_shadows]]

---

You excel at going unnoticed, especially among a crowd. You no longer need to have cover or be [[Concealed|main:concealed]] to [[Hide|main:hide]] or [[Sneak|main:sneak]]. If you would have lesser cover from a creature, you gain standard cover instead and can [[Take Cover|main:take_cover]]; if you would have standard cover from a creature, you gain greater cover instead.

*Source: Player Core.*
