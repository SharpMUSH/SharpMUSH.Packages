**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Orc|main:orc]]

---

Surviving in hostile terrain has given you a great talent for mobility. Whenever you roll a success on an Athletics check to [[Climb|main:climb]] or [[Swim|main:swim]], you get a critical success instead.
