**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Lizardfolk|main:lizardfolk]]

---

You cling to walls with a preternatural grip. You gain the [[Combat Climber|main:combat_climber]] feat as a bonus feat, and if you roll a success on an Athletics check to climb, you get a critical success instead.

---

**Special** If you have the cliffscale lizardfolk heritage, you instead gain a climb Speed of 15 feet.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
