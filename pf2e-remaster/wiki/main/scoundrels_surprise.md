**Class Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

You drop your disguise at the right moment and surprise your target. After you [[Impersonate|main:impersonate]] someone else using a disguise kit, you can remove your disguise with a dramatic gesture. Any creatures that previously failed to see through your disguise are [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the next attack you make before the end of your turn.

*Source: Player Core.*
