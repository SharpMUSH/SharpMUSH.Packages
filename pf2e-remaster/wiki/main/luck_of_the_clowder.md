**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Catfolk|main:catfolk]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Cat's Luck|main:cats_luck]]

---

When you use [[Cat's Luck|main:cats_luck]] to reroll, any creatures you choose within 10 feet who also just failed against the same effect (such as a single spell) can also reroll their saving throws and use the better result.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
