**Item 5**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Talisman  
**Price:** 22 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to armor

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

**Requirements** You are trained in Thievery

---

This small silver skeleton key can be pinned to armor or a sleeve. When you turn the key to activate it, you gain a +1 status bonus to Thievery checks to [[Pick a Lock|main:pick_a_lock]] for 1 minute. The first time you get a success or critical success on an attempt to Pick a Lock, you achieve one additional success toward opening a complex lock.

*Source: GM Core.*
