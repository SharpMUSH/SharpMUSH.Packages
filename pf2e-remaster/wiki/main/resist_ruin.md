**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Duskwalker|main:duskwalker]]

---

Your duskwalker heritage allows you to extend protection against void energy. You gain resistance 5 to void, and once per day, you can cast [[False Vitality|main:false_vitality]] as a 5th-rank divine innate spell.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
