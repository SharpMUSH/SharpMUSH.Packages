**Class Feat 2** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Finisher, [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

You attack with a flashy assault that leaves your target off balance. Make a melee Strike. If you hit and deal damage, the target is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
