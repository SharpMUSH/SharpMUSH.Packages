**Action** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Bravado, [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

**Trigger** A foe within your reach critically fails a Strike against you

---

You take advantage of an opening from your enemy's fumbled attack. You either make a melee Strike against the triggering foe or attempt to [[Disarm|main:disarm]] it of the weapon it used for the Strike.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
