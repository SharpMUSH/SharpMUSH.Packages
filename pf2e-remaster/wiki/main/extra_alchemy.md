**Familiar Ability**

---

Your familiar helps you brew items at the start of the day. You can create one additional item with [[Advanced Alchemy|main:advanced_alchemy]] during your daily preparations. You must have the Advanced Alchemy ability to select this ability.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
