**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

You move carefully, feinting and creating false openings to lead your foes into inopportune attacks. You are trained in Deception. When you [[Create a Diversion|main:create_a_diversion]] or [[Feint|main:feint]], the action gains the bravado trait.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
