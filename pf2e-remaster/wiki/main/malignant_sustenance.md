**Focus 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Focus, Manipulate, Void  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 willing undead creature  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

You embed a seed of void energy in an undead creature, restoring its unnatural vigor. The target gains fast healing 7.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The fast healing increases by 2.

*Source: Player Core.*
