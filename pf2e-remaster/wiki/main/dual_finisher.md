**Class Feat 8** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Finisher, [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

**Requirements** You wield two melee weapons, one in each hand.

---

You split your attacks. Make two melee [[Strikes|main:strike]], one with each required weapon, each against a different foe. If the second Strike is made with a non-agile weapon, it takes a -2 penalty.

Increase your multiple attack penalty only after attempting both Strikes.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
