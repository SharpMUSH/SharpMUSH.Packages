**Class Feat 20**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]

---

You are so suspicious of everyone you meet that you keep mental notes on how to defeat them all-even your own allies.

After interacting with a creature for at least 1 minute, you automatically [[Pursue a Lead|main:pursue_a_lead]] with that creature as the subject. You can have any number of such leads at any given time, and when you Pursue a Lead again, you don't give up any of these automatic subjects.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
