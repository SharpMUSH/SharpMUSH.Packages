What makes up a character, and where each part comes from. Each section links to the pages with the details.

## The big choices

- **Ancestry** ([[Ancestries|category:ancestries]]): the people you come from. Gives your starting Hit Points, size, Speed, attribute boosts and flaws, languages and special senses.
- **Heritage** ([[Heritages|category:heritages]]): a trait of your ancestry, or a versatile heritage, chosen at 1st level.
- **Background** ([[Backgrounds|category:backgrounds]]): your life before adventuring. Gives two attribute boosts, training in a skill and a Lore skill, and a skill feat.
- **Class** ([[Classes|category:classes]]): your adventuring profession. Sets your key attribute, Hit Points per level, proficiencies, class features and class feats.
- **Level**: from 1 to 20. You level up each time you gain 1,000 experience points (XP).

## Attributes

Six attributes, each given as a modifier (usually -1 to +4 at 1st level):

- **Strength**: melee damage, Athletics, how much you can carry.
- **Dexterity**: AC, Reflex saves, ranged attacks, Acrobatics, Stealth and Thievery.
- **Constitution**: Hit Points and Fortitude saves.
- **Intelligence**: extra trained skills and languages, Arcana, Crafting, Lore, Occultism and Society.
- **Wisdom**: Perception, Will saves, Medicine, Nature, Religion and Survival.
- **Charisma**: Deception, Diplomacy, Intimidation and Performance.

Every modifier starts at +0. Each boost adds 1, each flaw subtracts 1. Boosts come from your ancestry, background and class (your key attribute), plus four free boosts at 1st level. At 1st level, no modifier can be higher than +4. You gain four more boosts at 5th, 10th, 15th and 20th level; a boost to a modifier of +4 or more is a partial boost, and it takes two to raise it by 1.

## Numbers on the sheet

All of these use [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]: the proficiency bonus is your level plus 2, 4, 6 or 8 when you are trained, expert, master or legendary.

- **Hit Points**: your ancestry's HP, plus your class's HP + Constitution modifier at each level. At 0 you start dying; see [[Death and Dying|category:death_and_dying]].
- **Armor Class**: 10 + Dexterity modifier (up to your armor's Dex cap) + proficiency bonus with your armor + your armor's item bonus.
- **Saving throws**: Fortitude (Constitution), Reflex (Dexterity) and Will (Wisdom), each modifier + proficiency bonus.
- **Perception**: Wisdom modifier + proficiency bonus. You usually roll it for initiative.
- **Skills** ([[Skills|category:skills]]): attribute modifier + proficiency bonus for each of the 17 skills, Lore counting as one skill per subject.
- **Strikes**: Strength modifier (Dexterity for ranged or finesse weapons) + proficiency bonus with the weapon + item bonus. Melee and thrown weapons add Strength to damage.
- **Class DC**: 10 + key attribute modifier + proficiency bonus.
- **Spell attack modifier and spell DC**: for spellcasters, your spellcasting attribute modifier + proficiency bonus (+10 for the DC). See [[Spells|category:spells]].
- **Speed**: from your ancestry, reduced by heavy armor if you lack the Strength for it. See [[Travel Speed|main:travel_speed]] for overland travel.

Every check is a d20 plus one of these modifiers against a DC; see [[Degrees of Success|main:degrees_of_success]] and [[Difficulty Classes|category:difficulty_classes]].

## Feats

Chosen as you level up ([[Feats|category:feats]]):

- [[Ancestry Feats|category:ancestry_feats]]: at 1st level and every 4 levels after.
- [[Class Feats|category:class_feats]]: at levels your class sets, usually every even level.
- [[General Feats|category:general_feats]]: at 3rd level and every 4 levels after.
- [[Skill Feats|category:skill_feats]]: at every even level, plus one from your background.

## Everything else

- **Languages**: Common, your ancestry's languages, and one more per point of Intelligence modifier.
- **Equipment** ([[Equipment|category:equipment]]): a 1st-level character starts with 15 gp to spend. Weapons and armor list their stats; magic items have a level.
- **Bulk**: you become [[Encumbered|main:encumbered]] when carrying more than 5 + your Strength modifier Bulk, and can't carry more than 10 + your Strength modifier. See [[Bulk and Encumbered|main:bulk_and_encumbered]].
- **Hero Points**: spend them to reroll a check or to avoid dying. See [[Hero Points|main:hero_points]].
- **Focus Points**: some classes and feats give focus spells, cast with a pool of 1 to 3 Focus Points. See [[Focus Spells|category:focus_spells]].
