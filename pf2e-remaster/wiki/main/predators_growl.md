**Ancestry Feat 9** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Catfolk|main:catfolk]]  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

**Trigger** You successfully [[Seek|main:seek]] a [[Hidden|main:hidden]] or [[Undetected|main:undetected]] creature.

---

You give a throaty growl to attempt to [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] a creature you just found. You don't take the -4 penalty for not sharing a language with the creature.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
