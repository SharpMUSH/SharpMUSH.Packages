**Class Feat 14** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]], Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** dispel magic in your spell repertoire

---

You weave dispelling energy into a spell. If your next action is to cast a single-target spell against an enemy, and you either hit the enemy with the spell attack roll or the enemy fails its saving throw, you can cast [[Dispel Magic|main:dispel_magic]] on the enemy as a free action, expending a spell slot as normal and targeting one spell effect affecting the enemy.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
