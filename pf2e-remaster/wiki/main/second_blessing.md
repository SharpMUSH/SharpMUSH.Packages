**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** blessing of the devoted

---

Your continued service grants you another boon. Choose a second [[Blessing of the Devoted|main:blessing_of_the_devoted]] (different from your first one) and gain its benefits.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
