**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]

---

You're trained in light and medium armor, and you have expert proficiency in Fortitude saves. You gain the [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]] general feat, a reaction to reduce damage with a shield. If your deity's favored weapon is a simple weapon or an unarmed attack, you gain the [[Deadly Simplicity|main:deadly_simplicity]] cleric feat. At 13th level, if you gain the [[Divine Defense|main:divine_defense]] class feature, you also gain expert proficiency in light and medium armor.

*Source: Player Core.*
