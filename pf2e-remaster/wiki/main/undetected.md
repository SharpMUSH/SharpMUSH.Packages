A creature you are undetected by can't see you, doesn't know which space you are in and can't target you, though area effects still affect you.

- That creature is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] to you.
- It can guess a square and attack it. This works like targeting a [[Hidden|main:hidden]] creature (a DC 11 flat check), but the GM rolls the flat check and the attack in secret and doesn't say whether a miss came from the flat check, the attack roll or the wrong square.
- It can Seek to try to find you.

*Source: Player Core, p. 447.*
