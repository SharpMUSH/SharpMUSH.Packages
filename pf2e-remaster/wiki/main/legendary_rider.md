**Archetype Feat 20**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Cavalier Dedication|main:cavalier_dedication]]

---

You and your mount move like one being, effortlessly reading each other's signals and synchronizing even in the thick of battle. Whenever you are riding your mount, you are [[Quickened|main:quickened]]; you can use the extra action only to command your mount using the [[Command an Animal|main:command_an_animal]] skill action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
