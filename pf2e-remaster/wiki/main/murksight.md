**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Witch|main:witch]]

---

Your vision pierces through non-magical fog, mist, rain, and snow. You don't take circumstance penalties to ranged attacks or Perception checks caused by non-magical precipitation, and your targeted attacks don't require a flat check to succeed against a target [[Concealed|main:concealed]] only by such effects.

*Source: Player Core.*
