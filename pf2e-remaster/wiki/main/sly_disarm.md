**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

You can [[Disarm|main:disarm]] a creature through sleight of hand rather than brute force. You can use Thievery instead of Athletics when you attempt a Disarm. When you use Thievery to attempt a Disarm and succeed, the target is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the next attack you make before the end of your turn.

*Source: Player Core.*
