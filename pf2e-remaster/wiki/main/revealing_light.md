**Spell 2**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Light, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Area:** 10-foot burst  
**Defense:** Reflex  
**Duration:** varies

---

A wave of magical light washes over the area. You choose the appearance of the light, such as colorful, heatless flames or sparkling motes. A creature affected by *revealing light* is [[Dazzled|main:dazzled]]. If the creature was [[Invisible|main:invisible]], it becomes [[Concealed|main:concealed]] instead. If the creature was already concealed for any other reason, it is no longer concealed.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The light affects the creature for 2 rounds.

**Failure** The light affects the creature for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The light affects the creature for 10 minutes.

*Source: Player Core.*
