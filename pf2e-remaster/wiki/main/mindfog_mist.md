**Item 15**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Inhaled, Poison  
**Price:** 1000 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

Mindfog mist can be used to undermine spellcasters, as its effect on a victim's mental faculties are swift and powerful.

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 35 Fortitude

**Onset** 1 round

**Maximum Duration** 6 rounds

**Stage 1** [[Stupefied 2|main:stupefied]] (1 round)

**Stage 2** [[Confused|main:confused]] and [[Stupefied 3|main:stupefied]] (1 round)

**Stage 3** confused and [[Stupefied 4|main:stupefied]] (1 round)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
