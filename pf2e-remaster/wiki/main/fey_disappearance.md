**Focus 3**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Focus, Manipulate, [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Duration:** until the end of your next turn

---

You become [[Invisible|main:invisible]] and ignore natural difficult terrain (such as underbrush). Any hostile action you use ends this invisibility, but you still ignore natural difficult terrain.

---

**Heightened (5th)** If you use a hostile action, the invisibility doesn't end.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
