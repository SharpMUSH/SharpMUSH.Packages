**Ancestry Feat 5** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Tripkee|main:tripkee]]  
**Frequency:** once per day

---

**Trigger** You gain the [[Sickened|main:sickened]] condition or fail a saving throw against an ingested poison.

**Frequency** once per day

---

In dire circumstances you can vomit out your stomach to expel toxins. Reduce your sickened condition by 2 and immediately attempt a saving throw with a +2 circumstance bonus against any ingested poisons you have been exposed to within the last minute. You become [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] for 1 round as your exposed stomach makes you especially vulnerable to attacks.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
