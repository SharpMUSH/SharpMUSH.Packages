**Item 11**  
**Traits:** Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 1175 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** worn

---

This magic ring seems like simple tarnished brass, but it enhances your curiosity about traps and devices of all kinds.

You can use an Interact action to pull a [[Thieves' Toolkit|main:thieves_toolkit]] from the ring. This toolkit appears in your hand and any part of it folds back into the ring if they would leave your possession.

They grant you a +2 item bonus to Thievery checks to [[Disable a Device|main:disable_a_device]] and to [[Pick a Lock|main:pick_a_lock]], and the ring's insights grant you the same bonus to Crafting checks to [[Craft|main:craft]] and [[Repair|main:repair]] snares and traps.

---

**Activate—Fireball Trap** 10 minutes (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

**Effect** You create the effects of a [[Rune Trap|main:rune_trap]] trap ritual containing [[Fireball|main:fireball]]. You can have only one trapped rune from a *ring of maniacal devices* active at a time, even if you have multiple rings, and the rune disappears if you lose your investiture in the ring.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
