**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You've learned to fight underwater. You are not [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] while in water, and you don't take the usual penalties for using a bludgeoning or slashing melee weapon in water.

*Source: Player Core.*
