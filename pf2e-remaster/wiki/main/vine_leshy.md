**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Leshy|main:leshy]]

---

Your prehensile vines grant you unmatched skill at climbing. You do not need to have any hands free to [[Climb|main:climb]]. Additionally, if you roll a success on an Athletics check to Climb, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core.*
