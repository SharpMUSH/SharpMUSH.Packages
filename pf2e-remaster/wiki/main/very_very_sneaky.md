**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Goblin|main:goblin]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Very Sneaky|main:very_sneaky]]

---

You can move up to your Speed when you use the [[Sneak|main:sneak]] action, and you no longer need to have cover or greater cover or be [[Concealed|main:concealed]] to [[Hide|main:hide]] or Sneak.

*Source: Player Core.*
