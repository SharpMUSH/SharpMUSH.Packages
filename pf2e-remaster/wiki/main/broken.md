Only objects can be broken. An object is broken when damage brings its Hit Points down to its Broken Threshold or lower.

- A broken object can't be used for its normal function and grants no bonuses, except armor.
- Broken armor still grants its item bonus to AC, plus a status penalty to AC: -1 for light, -2 for medium, -3 for heavy armor.
- A broken item keeps every penalty and limit it normally has (broken armor keeps its Dexterity cap, check penalty and so on).
- An effect that breaks an item outright also lowers its Hit Points to its Broken Threshold if they were higher.

*Source: Player Core, p. 442.*
