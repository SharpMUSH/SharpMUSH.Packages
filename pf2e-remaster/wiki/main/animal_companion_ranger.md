**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]

---

You gain the service of a young animal companion that travels with you and obeys simple commands. When you [[Hunt Prey|main:hunt_prey]], your animal companion gains the action's benefits and your hunter's edge benefit if you have one.

*Source: Player Core.*
