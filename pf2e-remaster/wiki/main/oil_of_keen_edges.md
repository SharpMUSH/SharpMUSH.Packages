**Item 11**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Consumable, Magical, Oil  
**Price:** 250 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

When this silvery salve is applied to a melee weapon that deals piercing or slashing damage, the weapon grows sharper and more dangerous for 1 minute, granting it the benefits of the *[[Keen|main:keen]]* rune.

*Source: GM Core.*
