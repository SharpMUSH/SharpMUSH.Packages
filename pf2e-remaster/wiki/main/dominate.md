**Spell 6**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Incapacitation, Manipulate, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** until the next time you make your daily preparations

---

You take command of the target, forcing it to obey your orders. If you issue an obviously self-destructive order, the target doesn't act until you issue a new order. The effect depends on its Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]] as it fights off your commands.

**Failure** You control the target. It gains the [[Controlled|main:controlled]] condition, but it can attempt a Will save at the end of each of its turns. On a success, the spell ends.

**Critical Failure** As a failure, but the target receives a new save only if you give it a new order that is against its nature, such as killing its allies.

---

**Heightened (10th)** The duration is unlimited.
