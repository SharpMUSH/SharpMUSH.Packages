**Item 8**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Mechanical, Snare, Trap  
**Price:** 75 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

You affix a trip line or other trigger to a group of either stones or wooden stakes to strike a creature that enters the snare's square. The creature must attempt a basic DC 26 Reflex saving throw. If you choose stones, the snare deals 9d8 bludgeoning damage; if you choose spikes, it deals 9d8 piercing damage.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
