**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Lizardfolk|main:lizardfolk]]  
**Prerequisites:** You have a swim Speed

---

When you use the Step action, you can ignore difficult terrain caused by flooding, swamps, or quicksand. In addition, when you use the Acrobatics skill to [[Balance|main:balance]] on narrow surfaces or uneven marshy ground, you aren't [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]], and if you roll a success on the Acrobatics check, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
