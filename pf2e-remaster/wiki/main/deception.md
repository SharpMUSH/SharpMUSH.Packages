**Key attribute:** Charisma

Tricking others: lying, disguising yourself and creating distractions, and feinting in combat.

Your Deception modifier is your Charisma modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Deception (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Create a Diversion|main:create_a_diversion]]
- [[Impersonate|main:impersonate]]
- [[Lie|main:lie]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Feint|main:feint]]

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Deception:

- **Level 1:** [[Charming Liar|main:charming_liar]], [[Lengthy Diversion|main:lengthy_diversion]], [[Lie to Me|main:lie_to_me]]
- **Level 2:** [[Backup Disguise|main:backup_disguise]], [[Confabulator|main:confabulator]], [[Discreet Inquiry|main:discreet_inquiry]], [[Quick Disguise|main:quick_disguise]], [[Sow Rumor|main:sow_rumor]]
- **Level 4:** [[Distracting Flattery|main:distracting_flattery]], [[Minion Guise|main:minion_guise]]
- **Level 6:** [[Analyze Idiolect|main:analyze_idiolect]]
- **Level 7:** [[Doublespeak|main:doublespeak]], [[Fabricated Connections|main:fabricated_connections]], [[Slippery Secrets|main:slippery_secrets]], [[Subjective Truth|main:subjective_truth]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
