**Item 6**  
**Traits:** Magical, Water  
**Price:** 225 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

This small bag is filled with a fine, silvery powder that feels silky to the touch.

**Activate—Disperse** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per hour

---

**Effect** You scatter the dust into the air around you, causing it to condense into a cloud in a 20-foot burst within 10 feet, as the [[Mist|main:mist]] spell. You can Sustain the activation to make the cloud Fly 20 feet. The cloud lasts 1 minute, and you can Dismiss it.

*Source: GM Core.*
