**Archetype Feat 4** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Flourish  
**Prerequisites:** [[Cavalier Dedication|main:cavalier_dedication]]

---

**Requirements** You are riding your mount.

---

You [[Command an Animal|main:command_an_animal]] to order your mount to [[Stride|main:stride]] twice. At any point during this movement, you can [[Strike|main:strike]] one enemy within reach or within the first range increment of a ranged weapon. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your attack roll.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
