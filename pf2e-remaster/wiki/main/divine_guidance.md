**Skill Feat 15**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** legendary in [[Religion|main:religion]]

---

You're so immersed in divine scripture that you find meaning and guidance for any situation in your texts. Spend 10 minutes [[Deciphering Writing|main:decipher_writing]] on religious scriptures of your deity or philosophy while thinking about a particular problem or conundrum you face, and then attempt a Religion check (DC determined by the GM). If you succeed, you unearth a relevant passage, parable, or aphorism that can help you move forward or change your thinking to help solve your conundrum.

*Source: Player Core.*
