You have been seriously injured. Wounded has a value.

- When you lose the [[Dying]] condition, you become wounded 1, or your wounded value goes up by 1.
- When you become dying while wounded, add your wounded value to your dying value.

**Ends:** when someone restores Hit Points to you with Treat Wounds, or when you are restored to full Hit Points by any means and rest for 10 minutes.

*Source: Player Core, p. 447.*
