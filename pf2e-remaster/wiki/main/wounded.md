You have been seriously injured. Wounded has a value.

- Normally, losing [[Dying|main:dying]] gives wounded 1 or increases your wounded value by 1. [[Heroic recovery|main:hero_points]] is the specific exception: spend all remaining Hero Points (at least one), at your turn's start or when dying would increase, to stabilize at 0 HP without adding wounded. It does not remove a wounded value you already have.
- When you become dying while wounded, add your wounded value to your dying value.

**Ends:** when someone restores Hit Points to you with Treat Wounds, or when you are restored to full Hit Points by any means and rest for 10 minutes.

*Heroic-recovery exception: [Heroic Recovery](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2263) and [Hero Points](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2333), Player Core.*

*Source: Player Core, p. 447.*
