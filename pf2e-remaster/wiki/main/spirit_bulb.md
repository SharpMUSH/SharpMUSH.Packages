**Item 10**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Consumable, Magical, Plant  
**Price:** 200 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (concentrate, manipulate)

---

This magical bulb is harvested from an ancient grove rich in primal plant magic. When you activate the bulb, you either eat it to have it cast a 5th-rank [[Plant Form|main:plant_form]] spell affecting you, or plant it in the ground next to you to have it cast a 5th-rank [[Summon Plant or Fungus|main:summon_plant_or_fungus]] spell. If you choose the summoning option, the plant or fungus appears where you planted the bulb, and you can Sustain the activation to keep control of the creature.

*Source: GM Core.*
