**Class Feat 16**  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage  
**Prerequisites:** [[Thrash|main:thrash]]

---

You smack the foe you're thrashing into someone else. When you Thrash, choose another enemy adjacent to the [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] enemy. That enemy also takes your Thrash damage, with a basic Reflex save against your class DC.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
