**Item 8**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Injury, Poison  
**Price:** 80 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

Properly harvested, distilled, and preserved, the poison from a wyvern's sting is effective and direct.

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 26 Fortitude

**Maximum Duration** 6 rounds

**Stage 1** 3d6 poison damage (1 round)

**Stage 2** 3d8 poison damage (1 round)

**Stage 3** 3d10 poison damage (1 round)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
