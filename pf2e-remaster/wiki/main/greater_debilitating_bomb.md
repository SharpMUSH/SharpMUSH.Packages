**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Debilitating Bomb|main:debilitating_bomb]]

---

You have learned enhanced techniques and alchemical secrets that allow you to expand the range of effects you can impose with your bombs. When you use [[Debilitating Bomb|main:debilitating_bomb]], add the following to the list you can choose from: [[Clumsy 1|main:clumsy]], [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]], [[Stupefied 1|main:stupefied]], or –10-foot status penalty to Speeds.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
