**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Opportune Riposte|main:opportune_riposte_class_feature]]

---

You can riposte almost without a thought. At the start of each of your turns when you regain your actions, you gain an additional reaction that can be used only to perform an [[Opportune Riposte|main:opportune_riposte]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
