**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]  
**Prerequisites:** empiricism methodology

---

Sharp and piercing, your eyes see all and convey more. Your [[Point Out|main:point_out]] actions lose the auditory trait, and you don't need to be heard to convey the information to your allies. In addition, a creature you Point Out is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] to your allies until the start of your next turn.

---

**Special** If you have the [[Blind-Fight|main:blind_fight]] feat, your allies gain that feat's benefits against any creature that's off-guard due to Empiricist's Eye.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
