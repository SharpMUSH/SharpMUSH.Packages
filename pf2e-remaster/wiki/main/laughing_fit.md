**Spell 2**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Emotion, Manipulate, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 living creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** sustained

---

The target is overtaken with uncontrollable laughter. It must attempt a Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is plagued with uncontrollable laughter. It can't use reactions.

**Failure** The target is [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]] and can't use reactions.

**Critical Failure** The target falls [[Prone|main:prone]] and can't use actions or reactions for 1 round. It then takes the effects of a failure.

*Source: Player Core.*
