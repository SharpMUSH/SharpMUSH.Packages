**Class Feature 9**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]  
**Class:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]

You've refined your investigative techniques to an exceptional degree. Your circumstance bonus from [[Pursue a Lead|main:pursue_a_lead]] increases to +2. Your proficiency rank for your investigator class DC increases to expert.
