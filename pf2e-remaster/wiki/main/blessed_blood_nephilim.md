**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]

---

Your spilled blood is sanctified, with effects similar to those of [[Holy Water|main:holy_water]]. Whenever a fiend, undead, or creature with a weakness to holy drinks your blood or deals piercing or slashing damage to you with jaws, fangs, or a similar attack, that creature takes 1d6 spirit damage with the holy trait. You gain a +4 circumstance bonus to Crafting checks to [[Craft|main:craft]] *holy water* using your blood as one of the ingredients.

*Source: Player Core.*
