**Focus 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Focus, Fortune  
**Cast:** (reaction)

---

**Trigger** You attempt a Perception check to [[Seek|main:seek]], or you attempt a skill check to [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] with a skill you're trained in.

---

Speaking a short prayer as you gather your thoughts, you're blessed to find yourself pointed in the right direction. The GM rolls the triggering check twice and uses the better result.

*Source: Player Core.*
