**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Bard|main:bard]]  
**Prerequisites:** enigma muse

---

You can procure information with confidence. Whenever you [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] using any skill (including Bardic Lore), you can forgo rolling your check to instead receive a result of 10 + your proficiency bonus (don't apply any other bonuses, penalties, or modifiers).

As long as you are an expert in a skill, you meet the prerequisites for the [[Automatic Knowledge|main:automatic_knowledge]] skill feat in that skill, even if you don't have [[Assurance|main:assurance]] in that skill.

*Source: Player Core.*
