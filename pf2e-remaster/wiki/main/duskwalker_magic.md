**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Duskwalker|main:duskwalker]]

---

Your connection to psychopomps gives you the power to glimpse the near future and protect corpses from the ravages of undeath. You can cast [[Augury|main:augury]] and [[Peaceful Rest|main:peaceful_rest]] each once per day as 2nd-rank divine innate spells.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
