**Action** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]], Divine

---

**Trigger** An enemy damages your ally, and both are in your champion's aura

---

**Effect** Imperious divine light flashes out from you to surround your foe. The ally gains resistance to all damage against the triggering damage equal to 2 + your level. Until the end of your next turn, the attacker is affected by [[Revealing Light|main:revealing_light]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
