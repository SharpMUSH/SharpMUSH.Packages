**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Auditory, Cantrip, Concentrate, Manipulate, Sonic  
**Traditions:** [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Area:** 15-foot cone  
**Defense:** basic Fortitude

---

You echo a jarring hymn that only creatures in the area can hear. The hymn deals 1d8 sonic damage, with a basic Fortitude save. If a target critically fails the save, it's also [[Deafened|main:deafened]] for 1 minute.

---

**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d8.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
