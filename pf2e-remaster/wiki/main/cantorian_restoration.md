**Ancestry Feat 17** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Healing, [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]], Vitality  
**Frequency:** once per day

---

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** A living creature within 60 feet would die.

---

The energy that flows in your blood can save a life in the direst of times. You prevent the creature from dying and restore 6d8 + your Constitution modifier Hit Points to it. You can't use Cantorian Restoration if the triggering effect was a death effect or an effect that leaves no remains, such as [[Disintegrate|main:disintegrate]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
