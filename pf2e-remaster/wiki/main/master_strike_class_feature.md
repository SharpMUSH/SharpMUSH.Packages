**Class Feature 19**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Class:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

You can incapacitate an unwary foe with a single strike. Your proficiency rank for your rogue class DC increases to master. You gain the [[Master Strike|main:master_strike]] free action.

*Source: Player Core.*
