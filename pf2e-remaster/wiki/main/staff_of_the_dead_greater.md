**Item 10**  
**Traits:** Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 900 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

This twisted and grim-looking staff is adorned with hideous skull and bone motifs. Creatures summoned using this staff gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to the rank of the spell used to summon them.

**Activate** Cast a Spell

---

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Void Warp|main:void_warp]]
- **1st** [[Grim Tendrils|main:grim_tendrils]], [[Summon Undead|main:summon_undead]]
- **2nd** [[Peaceful Rest|main:peaceful_rest]], [[Summon Undead|main:summon_undead]]
- **3rd** [[Summon Undead|main:summon_undead]], [[Vampiric Feast|main:vampiric_feast]]
- **4th** [[Summon Undead|main:summon_undead]], [[Vision of Death|main:vision_of_death]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
