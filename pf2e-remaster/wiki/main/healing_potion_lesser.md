**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Healing, Magical, Potion, Vitality  
**Price:** 12 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

A *healing potion* is a vial of a ruby-red liquid that imparts a tingling sensation as the drinker's wounds heal rapidly. When you drink a *healing potion*, you regain 2d8 plus 5 Hit Points.

*Source: GM Core.*
