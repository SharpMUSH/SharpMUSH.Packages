**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Invigorating Elixir|main:invigorating_elixir]]

---

For the purposes of counteracting effects with invigorating elixirs, treat your level as though it were 2 higher. Add the listed conditions to those you can choose for an invigorating elixir you create to counteract: [[Petrified|main:petrified]], [[Stunned|main:stunned]]. You can also choose a disease regardless of what, if any, conditions it imposes.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
