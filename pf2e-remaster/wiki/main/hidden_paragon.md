**Class Feat 20** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** legendary in [[Stealth|main:stealth]]  
**Frequency:** once per hour

---

**Frequency** once per hour

**Trigger** You successfully use Stealth to [[Hide|main:hide]] and become [[Hidden|main:hidden]] from all of your current foes, or use Stealth to [[Sneak|main:sneak]] and become [[Undetected|main:undetected]] to all your current foes.

---

When you slip out of sight, you disappear. You become [[Invisible|main:invisible]] for 1 minute, even if you use a hostile action. Not even [[Revealing Light|main:revealing_light]], [[See the Unseen|main:see_the_unseen]], or similar effects can reveal you, though creatures can still use the [[Seek|main:seek]] action to locate you as normal.

*Source: Player Core.*
