**Class Feat 6** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** storm order; tempest surge order spell

---

**Trigger** An opponent adjacent to you critically hits you with a melee weapon or melee unarmed attack.

**Requirements** You have at least 1 available Focus Point.

---

You lash out, directing a burst of storming fury toward a creature that has harmed you. You cast [[Tempest Surge|main:tempest_surge]] on the triggering opponent and push that creature, moving it 5 feet away from you if it fails its Reflex save, or 10 feet if it critically fails. This movement is forced movement.

*Source: Player Core.*
