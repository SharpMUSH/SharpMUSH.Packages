**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Bard Dedication|main:bard_dedication]]

---

You gain the Basic Spellcasting Feat benefits. Each time you gain a spell slot of a new rank from the bard archetype, add a common occult spell of the appropriate rank, or another appropriate occult spell you learned or discovered, to your repertoire.

*Source: Player Core.*
