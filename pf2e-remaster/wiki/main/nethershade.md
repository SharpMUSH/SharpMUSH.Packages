**Item 10**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Injury, Poison, Void  
**Price:** 160 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

Distilled from the plane of shadow, this oily substance imposes tenebrous effects. The enfeebled condition from nethershade lasts for 24 hours.

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 29 Fortitude

**Maximum Duration** 6 rounds

**Stage 1** 2d6 void plus 2d6 poison damage (1 round)

**Stage 2** 3d6 void plus 2d6 poison damage, and [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]] (1 round)

**Stage 3** 3d6 void plus 3d6 poison damage, and [[Enfeebled 2|main:enfeebled]] (1 round)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
