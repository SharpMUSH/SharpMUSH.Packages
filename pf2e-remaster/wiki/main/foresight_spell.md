**Spell 9**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Mental, Prediction  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 1 hour

---

You gain a sixth sense that warns you of danger that might befall the target of the spell. If you choose a creature other than yourself as the target, you create a psychic link through which you can inform the target of danger. This link is a mental effect. Due to the amount of information this spell requires you to process, you can't have more than one *foresight* spell in effect at a time. Casting *foresight* again ends the previous *foresight*. While *foresight* is in effect, the target gains a +2 status bonus to initiative rolls and isn't [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against [[Undetected|main:undetected]] creatures or when flanked. In addition, you gain the following reaction: [[Foresight|main:foresight]]

*Source: Player Core.*
