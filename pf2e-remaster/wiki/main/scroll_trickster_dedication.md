**Archetype Feat 2**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Dedication  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Arcana|main:arcana]], [[Nature|main:nature]], [[Occultism|main:occultism]], or [[Religion|main:religion]]

---

You've studied scrolls in depth. This might have been a comprehensive education in formal setting, or the sort of education where you somehow obtain a number of scrolls and try not to explode anything you didn't mean to explode.

You gain the [[Trick Magic Item|main:trick_magic_item]] feat, and you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to skill checks to Trick scrolls. If you roll a critical failure to Trick a Magic Item that's a scroll, you get a failure instead.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
