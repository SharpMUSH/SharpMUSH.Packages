Preparation turns your available spell choices into the spells you can cast today. Start with your class's spellcasting feature: different classes obtain and prepare spells differently. The [[Wizard Learning Guide|main:wizard_learning_guide]] supplies a complete level-1 Wizard casting ledger.

## Rest, then prepare

After resting, make your daily preparations, which take around 1 hour. You can prepare only after resting and only once per day. Rest typically takes 8 hours; see [[Resting|main:resting]] for interruptions and recovery.

During daily preparations, spellcasters regain their spell slots and prepared spellcasters choose the spells available for the day. Focus Points and abilities with daily uses reset too. Resting alone does not perform this preparation step.

For a Wizard, study your [[spellbook|main:wizard_spellcasting]], choose legal preparations, and designate an owned item for [[Arcane Bond|main:arcane_bond]]. Record each prepared slot separately before play.

## Book entries and prepared slots

A Wizard's spellbook records spells they can choose to prepare. Having a spell in the book does not make it prepared, and casting it does not erase the book entry.

Each prepared non-cantrip spell supplies one casting. To cast the same spell twice through ordinary preparation, prepare it in two slots. For example:

| Preparation | Before casting | After casting from slot A |
|---|---|---|
| Rank-1 slot A | Force Barrage ready | Expended |
| Rank-1 slot B | Force Barrage ready | Still ready |
| Book entry | Force Barrage recorded | Still recorded |

The second preparation can still be cast. An expended preparation cannot normally be cast again before the next daily preparations; a specific ability can provide an exception. A failed attack or a successful enemy save does not refund the spell. [[Cast a Spell|main:cast_a_spell]] explains expenditure and disruption.

## Cantrips

Prepare cantrips separately from ranked spell slots. A prepared cantrip can be cast repeatedly until your next preparations without spending a slot; its own text can still impose restrictions. For example, [[Shield|main:shield]] prevents another casting for 10 minutes after you use its Shield Block reaction.

Cantrips automatically heighten to half your level rounded up. At level 1, they are rank 1. Do not put a cantrip in a ranked spell slot.

## Wizard curriculum preparations

At level 1, a Wizard with a curriculum school has five ordinary prepared cantrips and two ordinary rank-1 slots, plus one extra curriculum cantrip and one extra rank-1 curriculum slot. The extra preparations must be spells in the school's curriculum and in your book. A curriculum spell in your book can also fill an ordinary slot.

The [[Arcane School|main:arcane_school]] rules separately add curriculum spells to your book. These book additions are not additional ordinary slots. The [[School of Unified Magical Theory|main:school_of_unified_magical_theory]] has no curriculum and uses its own benefits instead; do not give it this extra preparation ledger.

For the ordinary level-1 and level-2 counts, use the local table in [[Wizard Spellcasting|main:wizard_spellcasting]]: level 2 has five ordinary cantrips and three ordinary rank-1 slots, before curriculum additions. Follow [[Leveling Up|main:leveling_up]] for the worked level-2 preparations. The [full higher-level Wizard Spells per Day table (external primary reference)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=39) remains external and excludes the school's additional curriculum preparations.

## Focus, Refocus and Arcane Bond

Track these separately from prepared slots:

| Resource or procedure | What it does | What stays spent |
|---|---|---|
| Initial school focus spell | Your school grants its initial spell and a pool of 1 Focus Point. Casting it spends 1 point, not a slot. | Prepared slots are unchanged. |
| [[Refocus|main:refocus]] | Spend 10 minutes studying your spellbook or doing arcane research to restore 1 Focus Point, up to your pool's maximum. | Expended slots and today's used Drain Bonded Item remain spent. |
| [[Drain Bonded Item|main:drain_bonded_item]] | Once per day, with your bonded item on your person, use this free action. During the current turn, cast one spell you prepared today and have already cast today without spending a slot. Still pay the spell's casting actions and meet its other requirements. | Expended slots stay expended; this permits the extra cast instead of refilling them. |
| Next daily preparations | Regain slots, select preparations, refill Focus Points and reset daily uses. | Your book still records its spells; new preparations determine today's choices. |

In the Drain Bonded Item row, **during the current turn** is the window for the extra casting after the free action; the spell can have been cast earlier today. It need not have been cast earlier in this same turn.

School focus spells automatically heighten like cantrips. You cannot cast a focus spell using a spell slot. The Wizard example starts with only its initial school spell and a maximum of 1 Focus Point; do not grant the advanced school spell automatically.

## Special preparation abilities

The [[Spell Substitution|main:spell_substitution]] thesis lets a Wizard spend 10 minutes replacing a prepared spell with another spell from their book. It changes an existing preparation; it does not restore an expended slot or count as new daily preparations. Curriculum restrictions still apply to a curriculum slot. If interrupted, the original spell remains prepared, and the substitution must be started over.

Other theses and feats have their own text. Keep their exceptions separate from the ordinary procedure above.
