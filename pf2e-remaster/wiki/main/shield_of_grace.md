**Class Feat 16**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Shield Warden|main:shield_warden]]

---

You protect an ally with both your shield and your body. Whenever you use the [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]] reaction to prevent damage to an ally, you can evenly split the remaining damage after the Shield Block between the ally and yourself.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
