**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** storm order

---

You bid the winds to aid you, carrying you through the air and allowing you passage through the strongest headwinds. You gain the [[Stormwind Flight|main:stormwind_flight]] order spell.

*Source: Player Core.*
