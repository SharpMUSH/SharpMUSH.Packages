**Spell 4**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Sanctified, Spirit  
**Traditions:** [[Divine|category:divine_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Area:** 20-foot burst  
**Defense:** Fortitude

---

You channel the fury of divinity against your foes. You deal 4d10 spirit damage to enemies in the area, depending on their Fortitude save.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]].

**Critical Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Sickened 2|main:sickened]]; while it's sickened, it's also [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]].

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d10.

*Source: Player Core.*
