**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

You can withstand blood loss startlingly well. Whenever you would gain the [[Drained|main:drained]] condition, you can attempt a DC 17 flat. On a success, you don't gain the drained condition.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
