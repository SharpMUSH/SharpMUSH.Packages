**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]  
**Frequency:** once per 10 minutes

---

**Frequency** once every 10 minutes

---

Your alchemical mastery can resuscitate the recently slain. You can administer a major or true elixir of life to a creature who has been dead for no more than 2 rounds. When you do, that creature is immediately returned to life with 1 Hit Point and becomes [[Wounded 1|main:wounded]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
