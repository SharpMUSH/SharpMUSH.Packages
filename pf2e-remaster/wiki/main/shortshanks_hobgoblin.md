**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

You have a longer torso and broader shoulders than most hobgoblins, making your legs seem short by comparison. This gives you a strong, muscular core and lowers your center of gravity—features that assist you in riding and climbing—and you've trained at riding in a saddle from an extremely early age. You gain the [[Ride|main:ride]] feat. Additionally, you are not [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] while you Climb.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
