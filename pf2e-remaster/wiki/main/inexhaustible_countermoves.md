**Class Feat 20**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

You can react whenever a foe leaves a gap in their defenses. At the start of each enemy's turn, you gain an extra reaction you can use only during that turn to perform an [[Opportune Riposte|main:opportune_riposte_class_feature]], or an [[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike_class_feature]] if you have that reaction.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
