**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Prerequisites:** hunter's edge; masterful hunter

---

You've learned to leverage every possible edge. When you use [[Hunt Prey|main:hunt_prey]], you can gain a different hunter's edge benefit instead of the one you selected at 1st level. If you do, you don't gain the additional benefit from [[Masterful Hunter|main:masterful_hunter]].

*Source: Player Core.*
