**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Dhampir|main:dhampir]], Lineage

---

You descend from one of the truly ancient vampires, a nosferatu cursed to eternal life but not eternal youth. People call you straveika, or ancient-born. The vestiges of your forebear's powers of domination have left you with an understanding of how a creature's intentions change its behaviors. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to [[Sense Motive|main:sense_motive]] and Perception DCs against attempts to [[Lie|main:lie]] to you.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
