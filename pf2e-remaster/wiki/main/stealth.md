**Key attribute:** Dexterity

Staying hidden and moving unseen, and concealing objects on your person.

Your Stealth modifier is your Dexterity modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Stealth (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Conceal an Object|main:conceal_an_object]]
- [[Hide|main:hide]]
- [[Sneak|main:sneak]]

This skill has no actions that need training.

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Stealth:

- **Level 1:** [[Experienced Smuggler|main:experienced_smuggler]], [[Terrain Stalker|main:terrain_stalker]]
- **Level 2:** [[Armored Stealth|main:armored_stealth]], [[Quiet Allies|main:quiet_allies]], [[Shadow Mark|main:shadow_mark]]
- **Level 7:** [[Foil Senses|main:foil_senses]], [[Swift Sneak|main:swift_sneak]]
- **Level 15:** [[Legendary Sneak|main:legendary_sneak]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
