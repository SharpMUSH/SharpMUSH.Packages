**Class Feat 2** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]], Concentrate

---

**Requirements** You are under the effects of a mutagen.

---

You metabolize a mutagen to heal yourself. You regain 1d6 Hit Points for every 2 levels you have, but the mutagen's duration immediately ends.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
