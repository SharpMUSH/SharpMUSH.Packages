**Key attribute:** Wisdom

Treating wounds, diseases and poisons, and knowing about anatomy and ailments.

Your Medicine modifier is your Wisdom modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Medicine (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Administer First Aid|main:administer_first_aid]]
- [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Treat Disease|main:treat_disease]]
- [[Treat Poison|main:treat_poison]]
- [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]]

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Medicine:

- **Level 1:** [[Battle Medicine|main:battle_medicine]], [[Forensic Acumen|main:forensic_acumen]], [[Inoculation|main:inoculation]], [[Risky Surgery|main:risky_surgery]]
- **Level 2:** [[Continual Recovery|main:continual_recovery]], [[Robust Recovery|main:robust_recovery]], [[Unusual Treatment|main:unusual_treatment]], [[Ward Medic|main:ward_medic]]
- **Level 7:** [[Advanced First Aid|main:advanced_first_aid]]
- **Level 15:** [[Legendary Medic|main:legendary_medic]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
