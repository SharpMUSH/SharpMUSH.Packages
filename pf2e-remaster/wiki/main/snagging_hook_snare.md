**Item 10**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Mechanical, Snare, Trap  
**Price:** 180 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

This snare snags a creature with its wicked metal hooks. The first creature to enter the square takes 5d8 piercing damage and 5d8 slashing damage, with a basic DC 29 Reflex save.

On a critical failure, the hooks piercing its flesh make the creature [[Immobilized|main:immobilized]] until it successfully Escapes.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
