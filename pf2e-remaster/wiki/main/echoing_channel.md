**Class Feat 18** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Animist, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** Embodiment of Balance or Cleric

---

**Animist** When you channel the powers of life and death, your apparition absorbs and enhances the spiritual energy, carrying it to a nearby ally.

**Cleric** When you pull forth vitality or void energy, you also create a smaller pocket of that energy.

---

If the next action you use is to cast a 2-action [[Harm|main:harm]] or [[Heal|main:heal]] to heal or damage a single creature, choose one additional creature in range. Target that creature with a 1-action version of the same spell. This spell is the same rank as the 2-action *harm* or *heal* you cast and doesn't cost another spell slot.

*Source: Player Core.*
