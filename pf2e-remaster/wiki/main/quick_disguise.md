**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Deception|main:deception]]

---

You can set up a disguise in one-tenth the usual time (generally 1 minute). If you're a master in Deception, you can create a full disguise and [[Impersonate|main:impersonate]] as a 3-action activity. If you're legendary, it takes a single action.

*Source: Player Core.*
