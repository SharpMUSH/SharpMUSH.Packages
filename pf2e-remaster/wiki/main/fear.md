**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Emotion, Fear, Manipulate, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** varies

---

You plant fear in the target; it must attempt a Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]].

**Failure** The target is [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]].

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Frightened 3|main:frightened]] and [[Fleeing|main:fleeing]] for 1 round.

---

**Heightened (3rd)** You can target up to five creatures.
