**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Confident Finisher|main:confident_finisher_class_feature]]

---

Even when your foe avoids your [[Confident Finisher|main:confident_finisher]], you can still hit a vital spot. On a failure with Confident Finisher, you apply your full precise strike damage instead of half.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
