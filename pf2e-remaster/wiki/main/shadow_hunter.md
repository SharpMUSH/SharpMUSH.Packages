**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Camouflage|main:camouflage]]

---

You blend in to your surroundings so well that others have trouble telling you apart from the terrain. While in natural terrain, you're always [[Concealed|main:concealed]] from all foes if you choose to be, except for your hunted prey.

*Source: Player Core.*
