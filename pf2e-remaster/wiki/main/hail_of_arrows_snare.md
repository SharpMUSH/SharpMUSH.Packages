**Item 16**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Mechanical, Snare, Trap  
**Price:** 1500 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

When a creature enters the snare's square, it releases hundreds upon hundreds of carefully prepared arrows, blanketing a 20-foot radius around the snare's square with massive arrow fire that deals 18d6 piercing damage. Creatures in the area must attempt a basic DC 37 Reflex save.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
