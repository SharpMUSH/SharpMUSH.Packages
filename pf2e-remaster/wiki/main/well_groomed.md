**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Catfolk|main:catfolk]]

---

You are fastidious about keeping clean. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against diseases. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a disease, you get a critical success instead. If you have a different ability that would improve the save in this way (such as the [[Battle Hardened|main:battle_hardened]] fighter class feature), if you roll a critical failure on the save you get a failure instead.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
