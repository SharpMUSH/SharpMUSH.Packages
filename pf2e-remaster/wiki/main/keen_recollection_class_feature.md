**Class Feature 3**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]  
**Class:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]

---

You can recall pertinent facts on topics that aren't your specialty. Your proficiency bonus to untrained skill checks to [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] is equal to your level instead of +0.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
