**Archetype Feat 6**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Weapon Improviser Dedication|main:weapon_improviser_dedication]]

---

Whenever you make a Strike with an improvised weapon against a creature that hasn't seen you make a Strike using an improvised weapon or that is otherwise ignorant of your skill with improvised weapons, the creature is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against you for that Strike.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
