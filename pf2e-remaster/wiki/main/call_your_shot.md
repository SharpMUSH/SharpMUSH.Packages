**Archetype Feat 8**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Play to the Crowd|main:play_to_the_crowd]]

---

When you successfully Play to the Crowd, add the following to the list of benefits you can choose from:

- Choose a creature within 30 feet that can see you to become [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]]. This effect has the emotion, fear, mental, and visual traits.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
