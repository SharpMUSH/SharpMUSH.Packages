**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Herbalist Dedication|main:herbalist_dedication]]

---

When you create an herbal elixir, you can prepare it as a poultice instead. A poultice gains the oil trait (but remains alchemical, not magical) and loses the elixir and ingested traits. You can apply a poultice by [[Interacting|main:interact]] with one hand.

When you apply a poultice, in addition to the item's normal effects, the recipient can attempt an immediate flat check to recover from a single source of persistent acid, bleed, or fire damage, with the DC reduction from appropriate assistance.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
