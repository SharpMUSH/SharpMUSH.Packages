**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Kholo|main:kholo]]

---

You're an imposing, powerful kholo, with tawny fur and brown spots on your hide. You gain 10 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 8 and gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Athletics checks to [[Reposition|main:reposition]], [[Shove|main:shove]], or [[Trip|main:trip]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
