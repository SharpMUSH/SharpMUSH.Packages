**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Slam Down|main:slam_down]]

---

You can dash your foe to the ground with a single blow. If the Strike you make with [[Slam Down|main:slam_down]] hits, you can automatically get a critical success on your [[Trip|main:trip]] instead of rolling a check. (Both the Strike and Trip still count toward your multiple attack penalty).

If you used a two-handed melee weapon for the Strike, you can use the weapon's damage die size instead of the regular die size for the damage from a critical Trip.

*Source: Player Core.*
