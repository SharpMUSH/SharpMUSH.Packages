**Archetype Feat 4** (free action)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Bastion Dedication|main:bastion_dedication]]; trained in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

**Trigger** You Shield Block a melee Strike made with a held weapon.

---

With deft and practiced movement, you block at an angle to potentially dislodge the weapon. You attempt to [[Disarm|main:disarm]] the creature whose attack you blocked of the weapon they attacked you with. You can do so even if you don't have a hand free.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
