**Item 10**  
**Traits:** Illusion, Magical  
**Price:** 1000 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto light armor

---

Light seems to partially penetrate this armor.

**Activate—Go Invisible** (1 action) (concentrate)

**Frequency** three times per day

---

**Effect** With a thought, you become invisible for 1 minute, gaining the effects of a 2nd-rank [[Invisibility|main:invisibility]] spell.

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of *invisibility*.

*Source: GM Core.*
