**Skill Feat 1** (1 action)  
**Traits:** General, Healing, Manipulate, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Medicine|main:medicine]]

---

**Requirements** You're holding or wearing a healer's toolkit.

---

You can patch up wounds, even in combat. Attempt a Medicine check with the same DC as for [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]] and restore the corresponding amount of HP; this doesn't remove the wounded condition. As with [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]], you can attempt checks against higher DCs if you have the minimum proficiency rank. The target is then immune to your Battle Medicine for 1 day. This does not make them immune to, or otherwise count as, Treat Wounds.

*Source: Player Core.*
