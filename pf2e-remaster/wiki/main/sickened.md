You feel ill. Sickened always has a value.

- You take a status penalty equal to the value to all your checks and DCs.
- You can't willingly ingest anything, including elixirs and potions.

**Ends:** you can spend a single action retching to attempt a Fortitude save against the DC of the effect that sickened you. A success lowers the value by 1, a critical success by 2.

*Source: Player Core, p. 446.*
