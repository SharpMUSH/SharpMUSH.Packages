**Class Feat 10** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]]

---

**Trigger** You fall at least 10 feet and land on a solid surface.

---

You slam into the ground, shattering it around you. Treat the fall as 10 feet shorter. You land on your feet. The ground in a 5-foot emanation becomes difficult terrain. Any creature in the emanation when you land takes 5 bludgeoning damage as the debris hits it and is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the start of its next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
