**Class Feature 11**  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]]  
**Class:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]]

---

Your rage intensifies and lets you hit harder at the start of a fight. Your proficiency rank for your barbarian class DC increases to expert. In addition, when you use Quick-Tempered, the first Strike you make during your first turn deals additional damage equal to your [[Rage|main:rage]] damage.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
