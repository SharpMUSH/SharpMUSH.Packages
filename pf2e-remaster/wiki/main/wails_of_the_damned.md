**Spell 9**  
**Traits:** Auditory, Concentrate, Death, Manipulate, Void  
**Traditions:** [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Area:** 40-foot emanation  
**Defense:** Fortitude

---

You howl a lament of damned souls. Each living enemy in the area takes 8d10 void damage and must attempt a Fortitude save.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes full damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Drained|main:drained]] 1d4.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is [[Drained 4|main:drained]].

*Source: Player Core.*
