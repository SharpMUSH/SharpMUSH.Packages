**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Crafting|main:crafting]]

---

You can jury-rig solutions when you don't have the proper tools on hand. You can attempt to [[Repair|main:repair]] damaged items without a repair kit.

If you have the raw materials available, you can [[Craft|main:craft]] a basic [[Caltrop|main:caltrops]] set, [[Candle|main:candle]], [[Compass|main:compass]], [[Crowbar|main:crowbar]], [[Fishing Tackle|main:fishing_tackle]], [[Flint and Steel|main:flint_and_steel]], [[Hammer|main:hammer]], [[Ladder|main:ladder_10_foot]], [[Piton|main:piton]], [[Rope|main:rope]], [[Ten-Foot Pole|main:ten_foot_pole]], [[Replacement Thieves Picks|main:thieves_toolkit_replacement_picks]], [[Long Tool|main:tool_long]] or [[Short Tool|main:tool_short]], or [[Torch|main:torch]] as if you had their formulas.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
