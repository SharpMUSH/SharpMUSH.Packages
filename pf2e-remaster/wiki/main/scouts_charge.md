**Archetype Feat 4** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Flourish  
**Prerequisites:** [[Scout Dedication|main:scout_dedication]]

---

You meander around unpredictably, and then ambush your opponents without warning.

Choose one enemy. Stride, [[Feint|main:feint]] against that opponent, and then make a Strike against it. For your Feint, you can attempt a Stealth check instead of the Deception check that's usually required, using the terrain around you to surprise your foe.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
