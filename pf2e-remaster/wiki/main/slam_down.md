**Class Feat 4** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Flourish  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You make an attack to knock a foe off balance, then follow up immediately with a sweep to topple them. Make a melee Strike. If it hits and deals damage, you can attempt an Athletics check to [[Trip|main:trip]] the creature you hit. If you're wielding a two-handed melee weapon, you can ignore Trip's requirement that you have a hand free. Both attacks count toward your multiple attack penalty, but the penalty doesn't increase until after you've made both of them.

*Source: Player Core.*
