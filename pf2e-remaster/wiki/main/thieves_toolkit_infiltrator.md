**Item 3**  
**Price:** 50 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** other

---

You need a thieves' toolkit to [[Pick Locks|main:pick_a_lock]] or [[Disable Devices|main:disable_a_device]] (of some types) using the Thievery skill. An infiltrator thieves' toolkit add a +1 item bonus to checks to Pick Locks and Disable Devices.

If your thieves' toolkit is broken, you can repair it by replacing the lock picks with [[Replacement Picks|main:thieves_toolkit_infiltrator_picks]] appropriate to your tools; this doesn't require using the Repair action. If you wear your thieves' toolkit, you can draw and replace it as part of the action that uses it.

*Source: Player Core.*
