**Class Feat 4** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

You make a quick attack and rob your foe in the process. Make a melee Strike against an adjacent enemy. If you hit and deal sneak attack damage, you can also attempt to [[Steal|main:steal]] from the target, even if the target is in combat.

*Source: Player Core.*
