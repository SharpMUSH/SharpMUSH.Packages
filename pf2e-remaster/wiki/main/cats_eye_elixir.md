**Item 2**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Elixir  
**Price:** 7 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

After you consume this elixir, your vision sharpens and you become sensitive to even the most minute movements.

For the next minute, you reduce the flat check to target [[Hidden|main:hidden]] creatures to DC 5 flat, and you don't need to attempt a flat check to target [[Concealed|main:concealed]] creatures. These benefits apply only against creatures within 30 feet of you.

*Source: GM Core.*
