**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Changeling|main:changeling]], Lineage

---

You are among the most common of changelings—those born to sweet hags—and a vibrant green eye signifies that parentage. Your mother's manipulative nature and versatile voice gives you an edge when deceiving others. You gain the [[Charming Liar|main:charming_liar]] skill feat. If you roll Deception for initiative, foes in that combat encounter that haven't acted yet are [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against you.

*Source: Player Core.*
