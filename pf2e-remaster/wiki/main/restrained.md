You are tied up and can barely move, or a creature has you pinned.

- You are [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] and [[Immobilized|main:immobilized]].
- You can't use attack or manipulate actions except to Escape or Force Open your bonds.
- Restrained overrides [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]].

*Source: Player Core, p. 446.*
