**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]  
**Prerequisites:** Grimspawn, Pitborn, Hellspawn, or another lineage feat associated with fiends

---

You possess fiendish magic. Choose two of the following spells: [[Disguise Magic|main:disguise_magic]], [[False Vitality|main:false_vitality]], [[Invisibility|main:invisibility]], [[See the Unseen|main:see_the_unseen]], [[Shatter|main:shatter]], or [[Paranoia|main:paranoia]]. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine innate spells.

Grimspawn typically take *false vitality* and *see the unseen*, pitborn typically take *paranoia* and *shatter*, and hellspawn typically take *invisibility* and *disguise magic*.

*Source: Player Core.*
