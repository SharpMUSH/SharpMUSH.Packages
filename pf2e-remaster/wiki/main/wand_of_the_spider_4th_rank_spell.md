**Item 11**  
**Traits:** Magical, Poison, Wand  
**Price:** 1400 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

The length of this wand is a pair of twisted giant spider legs.

---

**Activate** Cast a Spell

**Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge

**Effect** You cast [[Web|main:web]] at 4th-rank, but the strands of webbing are toxic. Any creature that fails its Athletics check or Reflex save to navigate the web takes 2d6 poison damage plus 1d6 persistent poison damage.

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of *web* at 4th-rank.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
