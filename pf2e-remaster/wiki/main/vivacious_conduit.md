**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Gnome|main:gnome]]

---

Your connection to the realm of the fey has grown, and its vital energy flows into you rapidly. If you rest for 10 minutes, you regain Hit Points equal to your Constitution modifier × half your level. This is a healing vitality effect and is cumulative with any healing you receive from [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]].

*Source: Player Core.*
