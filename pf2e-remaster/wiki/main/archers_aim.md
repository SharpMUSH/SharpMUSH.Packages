**Archetype Feat 8** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Concentrate  
**Prerequisites:** [[Archer Dedication|main:archer_dedication]]

---

You slow down, focus, and take a careful shot. Make a ranged Strike with a weapon in the bow or crossbow weapon group. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and ignore the target's [[Concealed|main:concealed]] condition. If the target is [[Hidden|main:hidden]], reduce the flat check from being hidden from 11 to 5.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
