**Class Feat 8** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Flourish, Rage  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You attack with enough force to knock the weapon out of your foe's hands. Make a melee Strike; if you hit, you can attempt an Athletics check to [[Disarm|main:disarm]] that enemy.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
