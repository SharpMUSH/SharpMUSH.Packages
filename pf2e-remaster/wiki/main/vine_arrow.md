**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical  
**Price:** 10 gp  
**Bulk:** —

---

**Activate** (1 action) (concentrate)

---

Leafy stalks protrude from the shaft of this rustic arrow. When an activated *vine arrow* hits a target, the arrow's shaft splits and grows, wrapping the target in vines.

The target takes a –10-foot circumstance penalty to its Speeds for 2d4 rounds, or until it [[Escapes|main:escape]] against a DC of 19. On a critical hit, the target is also [[Immobilized|main:immobilized]] until it Escapes.

*Source: GM Core.*
