**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Acrobatics|main:acrobatics]]

---

You can keep your balance easily, even in adverse conditions. Whenever you roll a success using the [[Balance|main:balance]] action, you get a critical success instead. You're not [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] while attempting to Balance on narrow surfaces and uneven ground.

*Source: Player Core.*
