**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Premonition of Clarity|main:premonition_of_clarity]]

---

You can project your premonitions of clarity to your allies. When you use [[Premonition of Clarity|main:premonition_of_clarity]], allies within 15 feet of you who failed the same saving throw against the same mental effect can also spend their reactions to reroll the failed saving throw with a +2 circumstance bonus.

*Source: Player Core.*
