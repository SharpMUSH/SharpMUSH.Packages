Anything in plain view is observed by you.

- A creature that tries to avoid detection, for example by using Stealth to Hide, can become [[Hidden|main:hidden]] or [[Undetected|main:undetected]] instead.
- A precise sense other than sight can let you observe a creature or object. You observe a creature only with precise senses.
- A creature you Seek with only imprecise senses stays hidden rather than observed.

*Source: Player Core, p. 444.*
