**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Stealth|main:stealth]]

---

You're skilled at moving with a group. When you are [[Avoiding Notice|main:avoid_notice]] and your allies [[Follow the Expert|main:follow_the_expert]], you and those allies can roll a single Stealth check, using the lowest modifier, instead of rolling separately. This doesn't apply for initiative rolls.

*Source: Player Core.*
