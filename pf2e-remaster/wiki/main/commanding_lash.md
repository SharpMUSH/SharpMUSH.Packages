**Focus 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Focus, Linguistic, Manipulate, Mental  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Requirements:** Your most recent action dealt damage to a target.  
**Range:** 100 feet  
**Targets:** A creature you dealt damage to on your most recent action.  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** until the end of your target's next turn

---

With the threat of more pain, you compel a creature you've recently harmed. You issue a command to the target, with the effects of the spell [[Command|main:command]].If the target is [[Frightened|main:frightened]], [[Stupefied|main:stupefied]], or taking persistent damage, it takes a –2 circumstance penalty to the save. Regardless of the result, the target is then temporarily immune for 1 hour

*Source: Player Core.*
