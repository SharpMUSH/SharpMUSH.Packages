**Item 4**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Elixir, Illusion, Visual  
**Price:** 18 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

A faint mist emanates from your skin, making you [[Concealed|main:concealed]] for 3 rounds.

As usual being concealed when your position is still obvious, you can't use this concealment to [[Hide|main:hide]] or [[Sneak|main:sneak]].

*Source: GM Core.*
