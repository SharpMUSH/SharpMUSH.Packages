**Item 16**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Illusion, Occult, Talisman  
**Price:** 1800 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to armor

---

**Activate** (1 action) (concentrate)

**Requirements** You are trained in Stealth

---

This small vial is filled with a grayish-green dust rendered from dried ectoplasm. When you activate the dust, it casts a 4th-rank [[Invisibility|main:invisibility]] spell on you. You may then Stride or Step. You can instead Burrow, Climb, Fly, or Swim if you have the corresponding Speed.

*Source: GM Core.*
