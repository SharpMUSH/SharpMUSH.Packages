**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]  
**Prerequisites:** Arcane Evolution or Primal Evolution

---

You change form readily. Once per day, you can use a sorcerer spell slot to cast any common polymorph battle form spell of the spell slot's rank as if it were a signature spell in your repertoire. You can use the extra spell slot from [[Primal Evolution|main:primal_evolution]] instead of a sorcerer spell slot.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
