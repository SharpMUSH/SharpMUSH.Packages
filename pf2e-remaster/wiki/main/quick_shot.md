**Archetype Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Archer Dedication|main:archer_dedication]]

---

You draw your bow and attack with the same action. You [[Interact|main:interact]] to draw a weapon from the bow or crossbow weapon group that is loaded or has reload 0, then Strike with that weapon.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
