**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Tengu|main:tengu]]

---

Wind and lightning have always been friends to you. You can cast the [[Electric Arc|main:electric_arc]] cantrip as a primal innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
