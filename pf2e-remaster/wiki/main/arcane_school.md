**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Wizard|main:wizard]]  
**Class:** [[Wizard|main:wizard]]

---

At 1st level, you choose your arcane school, which grants you magical abilities.

You gain additional spells and spell slots from the curriculum taught at the school you attended. Some wizards follow the school of unified magical theory, which attempts to forge a new school by studying independently and drawing information from a multitude of texts and tutors. Though a wizard with this approach lacks the focus of formal training, they have greater flexibility.

## Record the level-1 benefits

For a school with a curriculum, add one curriculum cantrip and two curriculum rank-1 spells of your choice to your spellbook, in addition to the ten cantrips and five rank-1 spells from [[Wizard Spellcasting|main:wizard_spellcasting]]. Prepare one extra curriculum cantrip and one extra curriculum spell at each rank for which you have wizard slots. These extra preparations must be curriculum spells in your book. A curriculum spell in your book can also fill an ordinary slot.

You learn the school's initial focus spell and start with a focus pool of 1 Focus Point. Casting a school focus spell spends 1 point, not a slot. Refill the pool during daily preparations, or restore 1 point with ten minutes of [[Refocus|main:refocus]] studying your spellbook or conducting arcane research, up to your pool's maximum. School focus spells automatically heighten to half your level rounded up. The advanced school spell requires the [[Advanced School Spell|main:advanced_school_spell]] feat; it is not part of the level-1 grant.

Unified Magical Theory has no curriculum and substitutes its own benefits; follow its entry rather than adding these curriculum spells and preparations. For the complete named level-1 ledger, use the [[Wizard Learning Guide|main:wizard_learning_guide]] and [[Spell Preparation|main:spell_preparation]].

*Procedure reference: Player Core p.198, [Arcane Schools](https://2e.aonprd.com/ArcaneSchools.aspx); the school entries supply their curricula and initial spells.*
