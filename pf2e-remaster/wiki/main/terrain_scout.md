**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Scout Dedication|main:scout_dedication]]

---

You have mastered movement in many different environments. You gain the Terrain Stalker skill feat twice, choosing a different terrain each time. If you [[Avoid Notice|main:avoid_notice]] while exploring and any allies use [[Follow the Expert|main:follow_the_expert]] to follow you as you do so, you can choose one of those allies to gain the benefit of any one Terrain Stalker feat you have.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
