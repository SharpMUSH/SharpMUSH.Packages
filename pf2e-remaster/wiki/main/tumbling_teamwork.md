**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Acrobatics|main:acrobatics]]

---

Your tumbling distracts a foe enough to create an advantage for one of your allies. When you successfully [[Tumble Through|main:tumble_through]] an enemy's space, an ally who is adjacent to that enemy can Step as a reaction, but they must remain adjacent to that enemy.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
