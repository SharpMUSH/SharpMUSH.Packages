**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Elf|main:elf]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Ancestral Longevity|main:ancestral_longevity]]  
**Frequency:** once per day

---

**Frequency** once per day

---

You've perfected your ability to keep up with all the skills you've learned over your long life, so you're almost never truly untrained at a skill. You reflect on your life experiences, changing the skill you selected with [[Ancestral Longevity|main:ancestral_longevity]] (and the skill you selected with [[Expert Longevity|main:expert_longevity]] if you have that feat).

*Source: Player Core.*
