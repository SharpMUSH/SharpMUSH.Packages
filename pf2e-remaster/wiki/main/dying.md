You are bleeding out or otherwise close to death. While dying you are [[Unconscious|main:unconscious]]. Dying always has a value; at dying 4 you die.

- At the start of each of your turns you attempt a recovery check to see whether you get better or worse.
- Damage while dying raises the value by 1, or by 2 if it comes from an enemy's critical hit or your critical failure on a save.
- If a recovery check removes the condition while you are still at 0 Hit Points, you stay unconscious but can wake as [[Unconscious|main:unconscious]] describes.
- You lose the condition and wake up whenever you have 1 Hit Point or more.
- Whenever you lose the condition, you become [[Wounded|main:wounded]] 1, or your wounded value goes up by 1.

*Source: Player Core, p. 443.*
