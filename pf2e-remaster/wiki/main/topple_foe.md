**Archetype Feat 10** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Marshal Dedication|main:marshal_dedication]]; trained in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

**Trigger** An ally succeeds at a melee Strike against an enemy you are both adjacent to.

---

You take advantage of the opening created by your ally to tip your foe off their feet. Attempt an Athletics check to [[Trip|main:trip]] the target of the triggering attack.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
