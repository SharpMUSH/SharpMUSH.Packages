**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Occultism|main:occultism]]

---

Members of your cult frequently pass themselves off as worshippers of other religions. You can use Occultism instead of Deception to [[Impersonate|main:impersonate]] a typical worshipper of another faith or to [[Lie|main:lie]] specifically to claim you are a member of the faith you are Impersonating. You still need to use the Deception skill to Impersonate a specific worshipper or to perform other deceptive actions, such as attempting to Lie about any other matter.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
