**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Tengu|main:tengu]]

---

You can transform yourself or the bodies of others to express your displeasure. Once per day, you can cast either [[Aerial Form|main:aerial_form]] or [[Cursed Metamorphosis|main:cursed_metamorphosis]] as a 7th-rank primal innate spell. You must take the form of a bird when casting this aerial form.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
