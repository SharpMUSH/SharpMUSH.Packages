**Class Feat 16**  
**Traits:** [[Monk|main:monk]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Qi Spells|main:qi_spells]]

---

You have achieved near-total mastery of your qi. You gain [[Medusa's Wrath|main:medusas_wrath]], [[Touch of Death|main:touch_of_death]], or another 8th-rank qi spell you have access to.

---

**Special** You can select this feat more than once, choosing a different spell each time.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
