**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** champion's reaction

---

The transgressions of your enemies fuel you to retaliate with divine speed. When an enemy triggers your champion's reaction, you are [[Quickened|main:quickened]] on your next turn. You can use the extra action to move closer to that enemy or to Strike that enemy.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
