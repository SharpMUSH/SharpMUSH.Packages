**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Injury, Poison  
**Price:** 10 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

The opaque white sap from the graveroot shrub clouds the mind.

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 19 Fortitude

**Maximum Duration** 4 rounds

**Stage 1** 1d8 poison damage (1 round)

**Stage 2** 1d10 poison damage and [[Stupefied 1|main:stupefied]] (1 round)

**Stage 3** 2d6 poison damage and [[Stupefied 2|main:stupefied]] (1 round)
