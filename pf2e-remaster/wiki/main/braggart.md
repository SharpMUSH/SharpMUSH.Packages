**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

You boast, taunt, and psychologically needle your foes. You are trained in Intimidation. When you [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]], the action gains the bravado trait.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
