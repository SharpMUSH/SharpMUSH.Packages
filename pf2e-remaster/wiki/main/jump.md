**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Manipulate, Move  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (1 action)

---

Your legs surge with strength, ready to leap high and far. You jump 30 feet in any direction without touching the ground. You must land on a space of solid ground within 30 feet of you, or else you fall after using your next action.

---

**Heightened (3rd)** The range becomes touch, the target changes to one touched creature, and the duration becomes 1 minute, allowing the target to jump as described whenever it takes the [[Leap|main:leap]] action.

*Source: Player Core.*
