**Class Feat 10** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Person of Interest|main:person_of_interest]]

---

**Trigger** A creature who isn't, to your knowledge, tied to any of your active investigations takes a hostile action against you in combat.

---

Sometimes something intrudes upon your case unexpectedly, such as an ambush sent to bring your investigation to a close. You use [[Person of Interest|main:person_of_interest]] against the triggering creature. This doesn't allow you to use Person of Interest more frequently than normal.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
