|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Martial | Bomb | 4d4 sonic | 1 | 20 ft. | - |

---

**Activate** (1 action) Strike

---

When this pebble hits a creature or a hard surface, it explodes with a deafening bang. A blasting stone deals 4d4 sonic damage and 4 sonic splash damage, and each creature within 10 feet of the space in which the stone exploded must succeed at a DC 36 Fortitude saving throw with the listed DC or be [[Deafened|main:deafened]] until the end of its next turn. You gain a +3 item bonus to attack rolls.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
