**Focus 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Emotion, Fear, Focus, Manipulate, Mental  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** varies

---

You fill the creature's mind with a terrifying vision. The target must attempt a Will save. A creature frightened by this spell takes 1 additional mental damage each time it's hit by a Strike.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]].

**Failure** The target is [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]]. If it's asleep, it wakes up and is [[Paralyzed|main:paralyzed]] for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but [[Frightened 3|main:frightened]].

---

**Heightened (+1)** The mental damage increases by 1.

*Source: Player Core.*
