**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** Ricochet Stance

---

While in [[Ricochet Stance|main:ricochet_stance_rogue]], you can bounce a thrown weapon off a foe to distract them. You can attempt a [[Feint|main:feint]] against a creature within the first range increment of a thrown weapon you are wielding, rather than only creatures within your melee reach.

*Source: Player Core.*
