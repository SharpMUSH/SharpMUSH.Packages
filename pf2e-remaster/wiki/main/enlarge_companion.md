**Focus 2**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Manipulate, Polymorph, [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** your animal companion  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

Your animal companion grows much larger, towering over its foes in battle. Your animal companion becomes Large, gaining the effects of a 2nd-rank [[Enlarge|main:enlarge]] spell.

---

**Heightened (4th)** Your animal companion instead becomes Huge, gaining the benefits of a 4th-rank *enlarge* spell

*Source: Player Core.*
