**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Oracle Dedication|main:oracle_dedication]]

---

You gain the Basic Spellcasting Feat benefits. When you gain a spell slot of a new rank from the oracle archetype, add a common divine spell or another divine spell you have learned or discovered (including the granted spells associated with your mystery) to your repertoire, of that spell rank.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
