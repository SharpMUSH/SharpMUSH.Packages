**Background**  
**Attribute boosts:** Dexterity or Strength, free  
**Trained skills:** [[Athletics|main:athletics]], Sailing Lore ([[Lore|main:lore]])

---

You heard the call of the sea from a young age. Perhaps you signed onto a merchant's vessel, joined the navy, or even fell in with a crew of pirates and scalawags.

Choose two attribute boosts. One must be to **Strength** or **Dexterity**, and one is a free attribute boost.

You're trained in the Athletics skill and the Sailing Lore skill. You gain the [[Underwater Marauder|main:underwater_marauder]] skill feat.

*Source: Player Core.*
