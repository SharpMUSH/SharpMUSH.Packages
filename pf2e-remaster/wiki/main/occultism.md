**Key attribute:** Intelligence

Knowledge of the occult: esoteric lore, mysterious philosophies, occult magic, and strange creatures.

Your Occultism modifier is your Intelligence modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Occultism (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Decipher Writing|main:decipher_writing]]
- [[Identify Magic|main:identify_magic]]
- [[Learn a Spell|main:learn_a_spell]]

Skill feats that need Occultism:

- **Level 1:** [[Deceptive Worship|main:deceptive_worship]], [[Oddity Identification|main:oddity_identification]], [[Quick Identification|main:quick_identification]], [[Recognize Spell|main:recognize_spell]], [[Root Magic|main:root_magic]], [[Schooled in Secrets|main:schooled_in_secrets]], [[Trick Magic Item|main:trick_magic_item]]
- **Level 2:** [[Assured Identification|main:assured_identification]], [[Magical Shorthand|main:magical_shorthand]]
- **Level 4:** [[Hidden Magic|main:hidden_magic]]
- **Level 7:** [[Bizarre Magic|main:bizarre_magic]], [[Break Curse|main:break_curse]], [[Consult the Spirits|main:consult_the_spirits]], [[Disturbing Knowledge|main:disturbing_knowledge]], [[Quick Recognition|main:quick_recognition]]
