**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 8 hours

---

You reinforce the target's musculoskeletal system to bear more weight. The target can carry 3 more Bulk than normal before becoming [[Encumbered|main:encumbered]] and up to a maximum of 6 more Bulk.

*Source: Player Core.*
