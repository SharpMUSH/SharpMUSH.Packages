**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Dueling Riposte|main:dueling_riposte]]

---

Your weapon whirls and darts, striking foes whenever the slightest opening or moment of weakness presents itself. You can use [[Dueling Riposte|main:dueling_riposte]] even if you aren't benefiting from [[Dueling Parry|main:dueling_parry]] (though you must be wielding a single one-handed weapon and nothing else). At the start of each of your turns, you gain an additional reaction that you can use only to make a Dueling Riposte.

*Source: Player Core.*
