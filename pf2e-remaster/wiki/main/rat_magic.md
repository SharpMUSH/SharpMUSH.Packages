**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Ratfolk|main:ratfolk]]

---

There always seems to be a little rat around to carry messages for you. You can cast [[Animal Messenger|main:animal_messenger]] once per day as a primal innate spell. When you do, the animal that responds is always a rat. If there are no rats within range, the spell is lost.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
