**Item 12**  
**Traits:** Rare, Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 1900 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

This staff of magically hardened wood is topped with a silver sculpture depicting magical runic symbols. A staff of arcane might is a *+1 striking staff*.

---

**Activate—Sunder** (2 actions) (death, force, manipulate)

**Effect** You destroy the staff, unleashing a blast of arcane power in a 30-foot cone, dealing 2d6 force damage per charge remaining in the staff with a basic DC 30 Reflex save.

---

**Activate** Cast a Spell

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Detect Magic|main:detect_magic_spell]]
- **1st** [[Phantasmal Minion|main:phantasmal_minion]]
- **2nd** [[Everlight|main:everlight]]
- **3rd** [[Force Barrage|main:force_barrage]], [[Paralyze|main:paralyze]]
- **4th** [[Dispelling Globe|main:dispelling_globe]], [[Mystic Armor|main:mystic_armor]], [[Translocate|main:translocate]]
- **5th** [[Fireball|main:fireball]], [[Force Barrage|main:force_barrage]], [[Lightning Bolt|main:lightning_bolt]]

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
