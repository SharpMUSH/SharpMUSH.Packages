**Class Feat 12** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Concentrate, [[Monk|main:monk]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Monastic Archer Stance|main:monastic_archer_stance]]

---

**Requirements** You are in [[Monastic Archer Stance|main:monastic_archer_stance]].

---

You achieve a state of perfect calm before unleashing your attack, opening your mind to the minute movements of the world around you to increase your accuracy.

Make a ranged weapon Strike against a target within your weapon's first range increment. This Strike ignores the target's [[Concealed|main:concealed]] condition and all cover.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
