**Item 0**  
**Price:** 1 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

A compass helps you [[Sense Direction|main:sense_direction]] or navigate, provided you're in a location with uniform magnetic fields. Without a compass, you take a -2 item penalty to these checks (similar to using a shoddy item).

*Source: Player Core.*
