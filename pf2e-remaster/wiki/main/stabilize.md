**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Cantrip, Concentrate, Healing, Manipulate, Vitality  
**Traditions:** [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 dying creature

---

Life energy shuts death's door. The target loses the [[Dying|main:dying]] condition, though it remains [[Unconscious|main:unconscious]] at 0 Hit Points.

*Source: Player Core.*
