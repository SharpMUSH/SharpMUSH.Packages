**Archetype Feat 8** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Scroll Trickster Dedication|main:scroll_trickster_dedication]]

---

You can activate the magic of a scroll with a cursory read as you draw it from your belt. You [[Interact|main:interact]] to draw forth a scroll, then use [[Trick Magic Item|main:trick_magic_item]] on the scroll.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
