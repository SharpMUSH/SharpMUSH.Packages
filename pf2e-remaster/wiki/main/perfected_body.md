**Focus 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Focus, Manipulate  
**Cast:** (reaction)

---

**Trigger** You fail or critically fail a saving throw against an effect that has the morph, poison, or polymorph trait, or that would make you [[Clumsy|main:clumsy]], [[Doomed|main:doomed]], [[Enfeebled|main:enfeebled]], [[Petrified|main:petrified]], or [[Sickened|main:sickened]].

---

Your body's perfection keeps you just a little bit healthier than most. You get a failure if you rolled a critical failure or a success if you rolled a failure.

*Source: Player Core.*
