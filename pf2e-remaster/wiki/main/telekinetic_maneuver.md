**Spell 2**  
**Traits:** Attack, Concentrate, Force, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature

---

With a rush of telekinetic power, you move a foe or something they carry. You can attempt to [[Disarm|main:disarm]], [[Shove|main:shove]], [[Reposition|main:reposition]], or [[Trip|main:trip]] the target using a spell attack roll instead of an Athletics check.

*Source: Player Core.*
