**Class Feat 18** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage

---

You make a vicious strike that maims your enemy. Make a melee Strike. If the Strike hits and deals damage, the target is [[Drained 1|main:drained]], or [[Drained 2|main:drained]] on a critical success.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
