**Item 2**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Ingested, Poison  
**Price:** 5 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

Sometimes called "deadly nightshade," belladonna is a widely available toxin produced from a plant similar to a tomato.

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 19 Fortitude

**Onset** 10 minutes

**Maximum Duration** 30 minutes

**Stage 1** [[Dazzled|main:dazzled]] (10 minutes)

**Stage 2** 1d6 poison damage and [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]] (10 minutes)

**Stage 3** 1d6 poison damage, [[Confused|main:confused]], and sickened 1 (1 minute)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
