**Item 1**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Mechanical, Snare, Trap  
**Price:** 3 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

This snare consists of a hidden canister of [[Caltrops|main:caltrops]] attached to a trip wire. When the snare is triggered, it flings the caltrops into either the snare's square or a square adjacent to the snare. You choose which square when you set up the snare.

If the caltrops scatter into the same square as a creature, that creature must attempt the Acrobatics check immediately.

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply a container of caltrops.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
