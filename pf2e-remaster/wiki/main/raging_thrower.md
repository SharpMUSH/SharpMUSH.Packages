**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]]

---

Thrown weapons become especially deadly in your fury. You apply the additional damage from [[Rage|main:rage]] to your thrown weapon attacks. If you have the [[Brutal Critical|main:brutal_critical]] feat or the [[Devastator|main:devastator]] class feature, apply their benefits to thrown weapon attacks.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
