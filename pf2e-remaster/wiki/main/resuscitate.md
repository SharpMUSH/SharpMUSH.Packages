**Archetype Feat 16** (3 actions)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Healing, Manipulate  
**Prerequisites:** [[Medic Dedication|main:medic_dedication]]; legendary in [[Medicine|main:medicine]]

---

**Requirements** You are holding healer's tools or are wearing them and have a hand free.

---

You resuscitate the dead. Attempt a DC 40 Medicine check to revive a dead creature who has been dead for no more than 3 rounds and whose body is mostly intact. If you succeed, the target returns to life with the effects of [[Raise Dead|main:raise_dead]], except it still has the wounded condition it had before dying, increased by 1 (or [[Wounded 1|main:wounded]] if it wasn't wounded before dying). Whether you succeed or fail, the target is temporarily immune to Resuscitate for 1 day.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
