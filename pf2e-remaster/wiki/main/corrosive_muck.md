**Spell 5**  
**Traits:** Acid, Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Defense:** basic Reflex  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

**Area** all squares in two 10-foot bursts.

---

You create two puddles of acidic slime that hinders the movement of anyone who walks through them. The area of each pool becomes greater difficult terrain. Each round that a creature starts its turn in one of the pools or enters a pool during a move action it's using, it takes 8d6 acid damage with a basic Reflex save. A creature that critically fails its saving throw also takes 1d6 persistent acid. As normal, if a Large or larger creature starts its turn in both pools, it takes the damage only once.

---

**Heightened (+2)** Create an additional 10-foot burst.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
