**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form_feat]]

---

You understand the fundamental elements of nature such that you can imbue them into your body and manifest as a living embodiment of those elements. Add the shapes in [[Elemental Form|main:elemental_form]] to your [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form]] list. Whenever you're polymorphed into another shape using *untamed form*, you gain resistance 5 to fire.

*Source: Player Core.*
