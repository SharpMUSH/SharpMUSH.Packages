**Item 12**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Illusion, Magical, Talisman  
**Price:** 320 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to armor

---

**Activate** (reaction) (concentrate)

**Trigger** An attack misses you

**Requirements** You are trained in Stealth

---

This thin, silvery wire wraps around your armor. When you activate the band, it casts a 2nd-rank [[Invisibility|main:invisibility]] spell on you.
