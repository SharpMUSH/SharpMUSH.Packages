Use this checklist with a blank sheet and the linked option pages. For numbers filled in at every stage, keep the completed [[Dwarf Fighter Example|main:dwarf_fighter_example]] or [[Dwarf Wizard Example|main:dwarf_wizard_example]] beside it. [[Character Sheet|main:character_sheet]] explains the fields.

## 1. Agree on a concept

Discuss the adventure and available options with your GM and group. Give your hero a reason to cooperate. Choose a name and a short description; return to personal details after the mechanical choices.

## 2. Start an attribute ledger

Write Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma, all at +0. Keep separate rows for ancestry, background, class and the final four boosts. A boost normally adds 1; a flaw subtracts 1. Boosts awarded together must go to different attributes. You can boost the same attribute again at a later stage. At level 1, finish between −1 and +4.

## 3. Choose ancestry, heritage and ancestry feat

Open [[Ancestries|category:ancestries]], then an ancestry's page. Record HP, size, Speed, traits, languages, senses and other granted features. Choose one eligible heritage and one eligible level-1 ancestry feat. Apply the ancestry's listed boosts and flaw, choosing any free boost without duplicating another boost in this allocation. Record granted equipment too: [[Dwarf|main:dwarf]] grants a [[Clan Dagger|main:clan_dagger]].

You may instead replace the ancestry's entire listed boost/flaw allocation with two free boosts to different attributes. This replaces it; it is not two additional boosts. Voluntary flaws are optional roleplaying choices and grant no extra boosts. The Fighter example uses the printed ancestry allocation; the Wizard example uses the alternative two boosts. Do not mix their rows.

## 4. Add a background

Choose from [[Backgrounds|category:backgrounds]]. Apply its two boosts to different attributes, obeying its restricted choice. Record its trained skill, specific Lore subject and skill feat. For [[Warrior|main:warrior]], choose Strength or Constitution for one boost, then a different attribute for the free boost; record Intimidation, Warfare Lore and [[Intimidating Glare|main:intimidating_glare]].

## 5. Add a class and finish attributes

Choose from [[Classes|category:classes]], write level 1 and apply the boost to your class's key attribute (choose when the class offers alternatives). Now apply four free boosts, each to a different attribute. Audit each allocation row before adding the columns. A free boost cannot be used four times on Strength.

## 6. Fill in class benefits

Read the class's initial proficiencies and level-1 features. Record each rank, choose the allowed additional trained skills and select any class feat. If class training duplicates a skill already trained, choose another skill instead; training twice does not become expert. Add class HP, including Constitution, to ancestry HP. Spellcasters must also complete their class's spell choices and preparation; follow that class's instructions and [[Spells|category:spells]].

## 7. Buy and place equipment

Start with 15 gp. Record purchases, their cost and remaining money. Count granted items separately. Use [[Equipment Basics|main:equipment_basics]] to interpret armor and weapons, calculate Bulk, and state what is held, worn and packed away. Choose supplies for the actual adventure with your group; an unlisted toolkit or travel pack is not included automatically.

## 8. Calculate and audit

Use [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]] and the formulas on [[Character Sheet|main:character_sheet]] to fill HP, Perception, saves, skills, attacks and damage, AC, class DC and any spell statistics. Untrained proficiency contributes zero, not your level. Apply equipment traits and persistent modifiers; note conditional bonuses separately. A shield's bonus requires raising it.

Record languages, personal details, XP and [[Hero Points|main:hero_points]]. Open every selected feat and check its level, prerequisites, access and any requirements for using its actions. An option list helps find choices; it does not check a character for you.

## Ready to play

Compare the ledger, inventory and derived numbers with the [[worked example|main:dwarf_fighter_example]], then read [[Encounter Mode|main:encounter_mode]] and [[First Encounter|main:first_encounter]]. After earning a level, use [[Leveling Up|main:leveling_up]].
