**Archetype Feat 2**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Dedication  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Society|main:society]], and [[Thievery|main:thievery]]

---

You are a student of peoples and their histories, and are in constant pursuit of knowledge and artifacts from the past. You become an expert in Society and Thievery, and you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] about ancient history, peoples, and cultures.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
