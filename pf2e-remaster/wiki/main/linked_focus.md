**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Wizard|main:wizard]]  
**Prerequisites:** arcane bond; curriculum spells  
**Frequency:** once per day

---

You have linked your bonded item to the well of energy that powers your school spells. Once per day when you [[Drain your Bonded Item|main:drain_bonded_item]] to cast one of your arcane school's curriculum spells, you also regain 1 Focus Point.

*Source: Player Core.*
