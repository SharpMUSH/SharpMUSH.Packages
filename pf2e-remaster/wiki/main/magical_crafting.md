**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Crafting|main:crafting]]

---

You can [[Craft|main:craft]] magic items, though some have other requirements. When you select this feat, you gain formulas for four common magic items of 2nd level or lower.

*Source: Player Core.*
