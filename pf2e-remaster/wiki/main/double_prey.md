**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]

---

You can focus on two foes at once, hunting both of them down. When you use the [[Hunt Prey|main:hunt_prey]] action, you can pick two creatures as your prey.

*Source: Player Core.*
