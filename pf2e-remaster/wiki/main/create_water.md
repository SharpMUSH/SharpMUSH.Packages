**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Water  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 0 feet

---

As you cup your hands, water begins to flow forth from them. You create 2 gallons of water. If no one drinks it, it evaporates after 1 day.

*Source: Player Core.*
