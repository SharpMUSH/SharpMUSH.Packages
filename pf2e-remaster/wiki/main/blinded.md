You can't see.

- All normal terrain is difficult terrain for you.
- You can't detect anything by sight. Perception checks that need sight are automatic critical failures.
- If sight is your only precise sense, you take a -4 status penalty to Perception checks.
- You are immune to visual effects.
- Blinded overrides [[Dazzled|main:dazzled]].

*Source: Player Core, p. 442.*
