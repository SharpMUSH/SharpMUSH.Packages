**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Gnome|main:gnome]]

---

You might have a flighty nature, but when a topic captures your attention, you dive into it headfirst. You gain the [[Additional Lore|main:additional_lore]] feat and the [[Assurance|main:assurance]] feat for the chosen Lore. As gnome obsessions can come and go quickly, you can retrain Gnome Obsession to a different Lore subcategory in 1 day of downtime.

*Source: Player Core.*
