**Action** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Primal, Sonic  
**Frequency:** once per hour

---

**Frequency** once per hour

---

**Effect** Your animal companion screeches and howls, empowered with natural magic. All creatures in a 30-foot cone take (half your level, rounded down)d6 sonic damage for every 2 levels your companion has, with a basic Fortitude save against your spell DC. Creatures that fail become [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]], and creatures that critically fail become [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]]. The fright is an emotion, fear, and mental effect.

*Source: Player Core.*
