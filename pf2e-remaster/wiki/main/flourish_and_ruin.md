**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Leshy|main:leshy]]

---

You can call upon the vital essence of your spirit to restore life to your allies and call forth vines to ensnare and bludgeon your foes. You can cast [[Field of Life|main:field_of_life]] and [[Tangling Creepers|main:tangling_creepers]] as 6th-rank primal innate spells once per day each.

*Source: Player Core.*
