**Item 14**  
**Traits:** Cold, Magical, Wand  
**Price:** 4500 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

This wand is a slender length of ice-blue glass.

---

**Activate** Cast a Spell

**Frequency** once per day, plus overcharge

**Effect** You cast [[Howling Blizzard|main:howling_blizzard]] at 5th-rank. Snow lingers in the spell's area, remaining as difficult terrain for 1 minute.

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of *howling blizzard* at 5th-rank.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
