**Class Feat 8** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Dueling Parry|main:dueling_parry]]

---

**Trigger** A creature within your reach critically fails a Strike against you.

**Requirements** You are benefiting from [[Dueling Parry|main:dueling_parry]].

---

You elegantly counterattack against your flailing enemy. Make a melee Strike against or attempt to [[Disarm|main:disarm]] the triggering creature.

*Source: Player Core.*
