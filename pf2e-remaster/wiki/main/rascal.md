**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

You aren't afraid to use underhanded tactics to get the edge over your opponents. You are trained in Thievery and gain the [[Dirty Trick|main:dirty_trick]] general feat. When you use Dirty Trick, the action gains the bravado trait.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
