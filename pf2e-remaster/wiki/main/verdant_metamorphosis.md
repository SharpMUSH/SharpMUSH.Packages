**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** leaf order

---

You have turned into a plant version of yourself, gaining the plant trait and losing traits inappropriate for your new form (typically humanoid). You also gain the [[Verdant Rest|main:verdant_rest]] action.

*Source: Player Core.*
