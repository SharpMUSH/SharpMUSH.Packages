**Archetype Feat 4** (3 actions)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Manipulate  
**Prerequisites:** [[Snarecrafter Dedication|main:snarecrafter_dedication]]

---

You install one of your snares prepared for quick deployment in a space that's occupied by an opponent. It must be a snare that normally takes 1 minute or less to [[Craft|main:craft]]. The snare automatically triggers.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
