**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage  
**Prerequisites:** [[Cleave|main:cleave]]

---

Your fury carries your weapon through multiple foes. When you [[Cleave|main:cleave]], if your Strike also reduces its target to 0 HP, you can continue to make melee Strikes until you make a Strike that doesn't reduce a creature to 0 HP, or until there are no creatures adjacent to the most recent creature you attacked while Cleaving, whichever comes first.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
