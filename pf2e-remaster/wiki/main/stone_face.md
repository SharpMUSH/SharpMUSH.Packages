**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

You've mastered the art of composure, even in the face of fear. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against effects with the fear trait and a +2 circumstance bonus to your Will DC against Intimidation skill actions, such as [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
