**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** thief racket

---

Interweaving attacks, you unbalance your foes. Whenever your Strike is a critical hit and deals damage, the target is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against your attacks until the end of your next turn.
