**Class Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Auditory, [[Bard|main:bard]], Concentrate, Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** warrior muse

---

With a rousing call, you exhort an ally to advance. If your next action is to cast the [[Courageous Anthem|main:courageous_anthem]] composition cantrip, one ally of your choice who gains a status bonus from the spell can immediately use their reaction to Stride.

*Source: Player Core.*
