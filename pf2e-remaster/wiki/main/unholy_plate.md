---

Crafted from black iron, this crude suit of *+2 resilient full plate* is designed to make you look like a horned demon with your face peering out of the screaming maw of the beast.

While wearing the armor, you can attack with the helmet's horns. They're a martial melee weapon with the effects of a +2 weapon potency rune. They deal 2d8 piercing damage and have the deadly d12 and unholy traits. On a critical hit with the horns, the target must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude or become [[Drained 1|main:drained]] (or [[Drained 2|main:drained]] on a critical failure). The horns can't be etched with any runes.

If you're holy, you're [[Drained 2|main:drained]] and can't recover from this condition while wearing unholy plate.

---

**Activate—Demonic Slip** (2 actions) (concentrate)

**Frequency** once per day

**Effect** You cast [[Translocate|main:translocate]].

---

**Craft Requirements** You're unholy; supply one casting of *translocate*.
