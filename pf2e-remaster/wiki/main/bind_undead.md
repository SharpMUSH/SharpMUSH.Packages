**Spell 3**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 mindless undead creature with a level no greater than bind undead's spell rank  
**Duration:** 1 day

---

With a word of power, you seize control of the target. It gains the minion trait. If you or an ally uses any hostile actions against the target, the spell ends.

*Source: Player Core.*
