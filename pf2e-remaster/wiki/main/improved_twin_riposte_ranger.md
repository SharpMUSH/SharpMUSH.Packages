**Class Feat 16**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Twin Riposte|main:twin_riposte]]

---

At the start of each of your turns, you gain an additional reaction that you can use only to perform a [[Twin Riposte|main:twin_riposte]]. You can use [[Twin Riposte|main:twin_riposte]] even if you aren't benefiting from [[Twin Parry|main:twin_parry]] (though you must be wielding two melee weapons, one in each hand).

*Source: Player Core.*
