**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 60 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** worn

---

This elegant copper ring has miniature images of songbirds engraved around its circumference. You gain a +1 item bonus to Deception checks.

**Activate—Throw Voice** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** Twisting the ring around your finger allows you to magically throw your voice, with the effects of a [[Ventriloquism|main:ventriloquism]] spell (DC 19 Perception).

*Source: GM Core.*
