**Class Feat 10** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

**Trigger** You are targeted by a ranged attack.

**Requirements** You are adjacent to an enemy and have [[Panache|main:panache]].

---

You attempt to deftly swap places with an adjacent enemy to avoid the attack. Choose an adjacent enemy and attempt to [[Reposition|main:reposition]] it. Instead of the normal results of Reposition, use the following.

---

**Critical Success** You swap places with the target enemy and the enemy becomes the target of the triggering attack.

**Success** As critical success, except the target has lesser cover against the triggering attack, and you lose your panache.

**Failure** You lose your panache.

**Critical Failure** You become [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the triggering attack, and you lose your panache.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
