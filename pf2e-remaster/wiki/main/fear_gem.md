**Item 4**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Emotion, Fear, Magical, Mental, Talisman  
**Price:** 20 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to a weapon

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (concentrate)

---

Dark smoke seems to writhe within this obsidian gem. When you activate the gem, make a melee Strike. If you hit and deal damage, the target is [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]], or [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]] on a critical hit.

If you have the [[Intimidating Strike|main:intimidating_strike]] feat, increase the frightened condition value caused by the Strike to [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]], or [[Frightened 3|main:frightened]] on a critical hit.

*Source: GM Core.*
