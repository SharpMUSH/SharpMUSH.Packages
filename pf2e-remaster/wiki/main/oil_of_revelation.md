**Item 5**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Oil  
**Price:** 25 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This bright oil, first created by humans as a tool to help them fight in darkness, holds flecks of tiny gemstones in suspension and smells like a struck matchstick.

The first time a weapon coated with this oil damages a creature, the wound glows with light for 1 minute. If the creature is [[Invisible|main:invisible]], the light's position means it's merely [[Hidden|main:hidden]] to creatures that would otherwise be unable to see it, rather than undetected. The light also negates the [[Concealed|main:concealed]] condition due to lighting conditions. If the coated weapon doesn't damage a creature within 1 hour, the oil sloughs off and loses its power.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
