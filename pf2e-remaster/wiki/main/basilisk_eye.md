**Item 9**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Talisman, Visual  
**Price:** 150 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to a shield

---

**Activate** (free action) (concentrate)

**Trigger** You [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]] a melee attack with the affixed shield

---

This slimy green stone glows with a strong light whenever the shield it adorns blocks a melee attack. When the eye is activated, the attacker must succeed at a DC 25 Fortitude save or become [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]] for 1 minute as its body slowly stiffens in partial petrification.

*Source: GM Core.*
