**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You can attempt to [[Disarm|main:disarm]], [[Grapple|main:grapple]], [[Reposition|main:reposition]], [[Shove|main:shove]], or [[Trip|main:trip]] creatures up to two sizes larger than you, or up to three sizes larger than you if you're legendary in Athletics.

*Source: Player Core.*
