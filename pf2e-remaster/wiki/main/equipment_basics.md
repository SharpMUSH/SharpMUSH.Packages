## Read money and prices

**gp** means gold pieces, **sp** silver, **cp** copper and **pp** platinum. Adjacent denominations differ by ten: **1 pp = 10 gp; 1 gp = 10 sp; 1 sp = 10 cp**. Thus the Wizard's free-book limit of 10 sp equals the ordinary blank book's 1 gp price; do not buy a second book just to satisfy a free grant. Record actual purchases and remaining coins separately from granted items, then use the Bulk rules for the coins actually carried.

*Source: [Currency, Player Core p.10](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=3295). The [[Dwarf Wizard Example|main:dwarf_wizard_example]] records the free book and retained starting money.*

## Read a weapon

Category selects your proficiency. Damage lists dice and type: B means bludgeoning, P piercing, S slashing. Hands says how many hands wield it; owning it or carrying it without holding it is not wielding it. Group matters only when an ability uses that group.

A [[Longsword|main:longsword]] uses one hand. Versatile P lets you choose piercing instead of slashing each attack; it does not add both damage types. A [[Clan Dagger|main:clan_dagger]] has Agile (reduced penalties when attacking with it: see [[Multiple Attack Penalty|main:multiple_attack_penalty]]), Parry (spend one action while wielding it with at least trained proficiency for +1 circumstance AC until your next turn), and Versatile B (bludgeoning instead of piercing). The Dwarf trait does not itself grant weapon training. Uncommon concerns access, not an attack bonus. The dwarf ancestry explicitly grants its starting dagger.

## Read armor columns

Use your proficiency for the listed armor category. Add its AC bonus and Dexterity up to its Dex cap. The check penalty normally applies to Strength/Dexterity skill checks except checks with the attack trait. Meeting the Strength threshold removes that check penalty and reduces the Speed penalty by 5 feet. Speed cannot fall below 5 feet. Strength is a threshold for mitigating penalties, not permission to wear armor.

Read traits after those general rules. [[Chain Mail|main:chain_mail]] is Flexible, exempting Acrobatics and Athletics from its check penalty, and Noisy, retaining that penalty to Stealth even when Strength meets the threshold. [[Unburdened Iron|main:unburdened_iron]] ignores armor Speed reduction; it does not remove Noisy. The example's Stealth is therefore −1.

Armor Bulk assumes it is worn. Carrying an unworn suit usually adds 1 Bulk (light-Bulk armor becomes 1 Bulk). Wearing armor never makes its listed Bulk disappear. Putting on medium or heavy armor takes 5 minutes; light armor takes 1 minute, and removing any armor takes 1 minute.

## Shield state and hands

A [[Steel Shield|main:steel_shield]] occupies a hand and is normally strapped to that arm. [[Raise a Shield|main:raise_a_shield]] costs one action; only then does its circumstance bonus apply, through the start of your next turn. Losing the usable shield hand loses the benefits. Hardness, HP and Broken Threshold matter when it takes damage; [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]] is a separate reaction with its own trigger, not automatic damage reduction on every hit.

To equip a shield, attaching it takes one Interact with both hands. Detaching takes one Interact with a free hand and leaves the shield held; you can then put it away or drop it. Merely releasing your grip does not unequip it. Plan these changes before spending the turn's actions. [[First Encounter|main:first_encounter]] uses a released sword to free a hand while the shield stays equipped.

The example's potion and dagger are **worn and readily accessible**, neither held nor stowed in a container. With a free hand, drawing either costs one [[Interact|main:interact]]. Stowed gear is different: retrieve it from its actual container and account for any Interact needed to open or close that container; do not assume the worn-item draw cost covers unpacking. [[Release|main:release]] is a free action to drop an ordinary held item. Dropped gear stays where it fell. Draw the potion before its separate activation; check its [[item entry|main:healing_potion_minor]] and [[Dying and Recovery|main:dying_and_recovery]] for drinking or helping an ally.

## Count carried Bulk

Use [[Bulk and Encumbered|main:bulk_and_encumbered]] for ordinary light-item accumulation, coin rounding, limits and size conversions. Keep every item on your ledger even when its current rounded contribution is zero.

The example's worn chain mail, held sword and held shield total 4 Bulk. Its dagger and potion are two light items, still below the next whole Bulk. Two remaining gold coins add zero. No container reduction is assumed.

## Tools and practical supplies

Check requirements before buying. Training in Medicine alone cannot replace a [[Healer's Toolkit|main:healers_toolkit]] for [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]]. That toolkit requires one free hand when worn, or two hands when held. The example owns none. Borrowing one changes who has it and who counts its Bulk; returning it ends the loan. A potion also leaves the inventory when consumed. Record changes rather than silently restoring supplies between encounters.
