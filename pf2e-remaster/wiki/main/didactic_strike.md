**Class Feat 16**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Shared Stratagem|main:shared_stratagem]]

---

When you find a glaring weakness, you can set your allies up to annihilate your foe.

When you use [[Shared Stratagem|main:shared_stratagem]], you can designate up to 10 allies instead of just one. The foe is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the first attack from each designated ally before your next turn, and each ally's first attack deals an extra 2d6 precision damage to the target if it hits.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
