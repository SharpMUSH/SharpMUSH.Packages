**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Tengu|main:tengu]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Tengu Feather Fan|main:tengu_feather_fan]]

---

Your fan can stir up even more powerful winds. You can use the Wave Fan activation one additional time per day. Add a 3rd-rank [[Wall of Wind|main:wall_of_wind]] spell to the spells contained within your fan.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
