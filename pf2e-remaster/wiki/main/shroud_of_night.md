**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Cantrip, Darkness, Hex, Manipulate, [[Witch|main:witch]]  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Your patron blankets the target's eyes in darkness. If you cast this hex on a willing ally (for instance, one with light blindness), the ally can choose which result it gets without rolling.

---

**Success** The target is unaffected.

**Failure** The target is shrouded in murky darkness. It treats bright light as dim light, and unless it has greater darkvision, all creatures are [[Concealed|main:concealed]] to it.

*Source: Player Core.*
