**Class Feat 6** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Auditory, [[Bard|main:bard]], Concentrate, Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** warrior muse; [[Rallying Anthem|main:rallying_anthem_feat]]

---

Like the storied heroes who persist in the face of overwhelming odds, you and your allies will hold the line. If your next action is to cast the [[Rallying Anthem|main:rallying_anthem]] composition cantrip, you can Raise a Shield, and one ally of your choice who gains a status bonus from the spell can immediately use their reaction to Raise a Shield.

*Source: Player Core.*
