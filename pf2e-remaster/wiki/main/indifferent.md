An attitude: how a creature feels about one particular character. Only supernatural effects, such as spells, can give this condition to a player character.

- An indifferent creature doesn't care one way or the other about the character.
- A creature is indifferent to a character unless something says otherwise.

*Source: Player Core, p. 444.*
