**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Call of the Wild|main:call_of_the_wild]]

---

Whenever you summon an ally, you can empower it with the elemental power of air, earth, fire, or water. You gain the [[Primal Summons|main:primal_summons]] order spell.

*Source: Player Core.*
