**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Cantrip, Hex, Manipulate, [[Witch|main:witch]]  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Your patron deigns to whisper a few secrets. The target can [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]], [[Seek|main:seek]], or [[Sense Motive|main:sense_motive]] as a free action. The target gains a +1 status bonus to the statistic used for the roll (a skill or Perception), as long as you Sustain the spell. The target is temporarily immune to *discern secrets* for 1 minute.

---

**Heightened (5th)** You can target two creatures instead of one.

*Source: Player Core.*
