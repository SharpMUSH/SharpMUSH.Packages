**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Deception|main:deception]]

---

Even when caught in falsehoods, you pile lie upon lie. Reduce the circumstance bonus a target gains for your previous attempts to [[Create a Diversion|main:create_a_diversion]] or [[Lie|main:lie]] to it from +4 to +2. If you're a master in Deception, reduce the bonus to +1, and if you're legendary, your targets don't get these bonuses at all.

*Source: Player Core.*
