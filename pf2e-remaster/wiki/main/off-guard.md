You are distracted or can't give your full attention to defense.

- You take a -2 circumstance penalty to AC.
- Some effects make you off-guard only to certain creatures or attacks. Others, especially conditions, make you off-guard against everything. When a rule doesn't limit it ("The target is off-guard"), it applies against everything.

*Source: Player Core, p. 445.*
