**Class Feat 1** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

**Requirements** You are wielding two melee weapons, each in a different hand.

---

You use an attack with one weapon to distract your foe from a second attack coming at a different angle. Make one Strike with each of your two melee weapons, both against the same target. The target is automatically [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the second attack. Apply your multiple attack penalty to the Strikes normally.

*Source: Player Core.*
