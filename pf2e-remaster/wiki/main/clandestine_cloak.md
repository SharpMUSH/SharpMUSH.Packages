**Item 6**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 230 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** worncloak

---

When you pull up the hood of this nondescript gray cloak (an Interact action), you become drab and uninteresting, gaining a +1 item bonus to Stealth checks and to Deception checks to [[Impersonate|main:impersonate]] a forgettable background character, such as a servant, but also taking a -1 item penalty to Diplomacy and Intimidation checks.

---

**Activate—Cloak Identity** (2 actions) (concentrate, manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** You pull the cloak's hood up and gain the benefits of [[Veil of Privacy|main:veil_of_privacy]] for 1 hour or until you pull the hood back down, whichever comes first.

*Source: GM Core.*
