**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Acrobatics|main:acrobatics]]

---

You're an incredible acrobat, evoking wonder and enrapturing audiences with your prowess. You can roll an Acrobatics check instead of a Performance check when using the [[Perform|main:perform]] action. If you are trained in both Acrobatics and Performance, you gain a +1 circumstances bonus on Acrobatics checks made to Perform.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
