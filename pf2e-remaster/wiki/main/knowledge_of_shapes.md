**Class Feat 4** (free action)  
**Traits:** Cursebound, [[Oracle|main:oracle]], Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** Reach Spell or Widen Spell

---

Inspiration lets you surpass your preconceptions of your spells' limits. You use [[Reach Spell|main:reach_spell]] or [[Widen Spell|main:widen_spell]] as a free action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
