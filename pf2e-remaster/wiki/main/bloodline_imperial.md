**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]

---

One of your ancestors was a mortal who mastered magic.

**Tradition** arcane

**Bloodline Skills** Arcana, Society

**Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip [[Detect Magic|main:detect_magic_spell]], 1st: [[Force Barrage|main:force_barrage]], 2nd: [[Dispel Magic|main:dispel_magic]], 3rd: [[Haste|main:haste]], 4th: [[Translocate|main:translocate]], 5th: [[Scouting Eye|main:scouting_eye]], 6th: [[Disintegrate|main:disintegrate]], 7th: [[Retrocognition|main:retrocognition]], 8th: [[Quandary|main:quandary]], 9th: [[Implosion|main:implosion]]

**Bloodline Spells** initial: [[Ancestral Memories|main:ancestral_memories]], advanced: [[Extend Blood Magic|main:extend_blood_magic]], greater: [[Arcane Countermeasure|main:arcane_countermeasure]]

**Blood Magic—Imperious Defense** Raw magic emanates from every cell in your body, protecting you. Until the start of your next turn, you gain your choice of either a +1 status bonus to AC or a +1 status bonus to saving throws.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
