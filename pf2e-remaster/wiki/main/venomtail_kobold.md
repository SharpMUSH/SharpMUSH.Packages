**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Kobold|main:kobold]]

---

Thanks to the proximity of a venomous creature to your warren, you were born with a vestigial spur in your tail that secretes deadly venom. You gain the [[Tail Toxin|main:tail_toxin]] action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
