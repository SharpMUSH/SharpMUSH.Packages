**Class Feat 8** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** [[Monk|main:monk]]  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You leverage your knowledge of martial arts to mix different maneuvers together into a single flowing combination. Choose any two of [[Grapple|main:grapple]], [[Reposition|main:reposition]], [[Shove|main:shove]], and [[Trip|main:trip]]. Attempt both of the attacks you chose against the same or different creatures, but don't apply the multiple attack penalty until after resolving both attacks.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
