**Focus 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Emotion, Focus, Incapacitation, Manipulate, Mental  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** varies

---

Merging minds with the target, you swap distressing visions. One of you will become [[Confused|main:confused]], based on the target's Will save.

---

**Critical Success** You are Confused for 1 round.

**Success** At the start of your next turn, you spend your first action with the Confused condition, then act normally.

**Failure** The target spends the first action of each of its turns Confused. The duration is 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is Confused. The duration is 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core.*
