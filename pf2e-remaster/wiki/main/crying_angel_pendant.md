**Item 2**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Divine, Talisman  
**Price:** 7 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to armor

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (concentrate)

---

When you activate this alabaster pendant, attempt to [[Administer First Aid|main:administer_first_aid]] using Medicine with a +1 item bonus to the check. If you succeed, and you were trying to stabilize, the target regains 1 Hit Point, losing the dying condition and becoming conscious as normal. If you succeed, and you were trying to stop bleeding, the bleeding ends.

*Source: GM Core.*
