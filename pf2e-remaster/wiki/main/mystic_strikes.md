**Class Feature 3**  
**Traits:** [[Monk|main:monk]]  
**Class:** [[Monk|main:monk]]

---

Focusing your will into your physical attacks imbues them with mystical energy. Your unarmed attacks become magical, allowing them to get past resistances to non-magical attacks. However, you still need an item such as [[Handwraps of Mighty Blows (+1)|main:handwraps_of_mighty_blows]] to gain an item bonus to attack rolls or increase your attacks' weapon damage dice.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
