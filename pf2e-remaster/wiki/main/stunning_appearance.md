**Archetype Feat 16**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Vigilante  
**Prerequisites:** Startling Appearance

---

Your sudden appearance leaves your foe unable to respond. When you use Startling Appearance, if your foe's level is equal to or lower than yours, they are also [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]] on a hit, or [[Stunned 2|main:stunned]] on a critical hit.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
