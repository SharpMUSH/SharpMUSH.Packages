**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Lizardfolk|main:lizardfolk]]

---

You gain [[Unfettered Movement|main:unfettered_movement]] and [[Mountain Resilience|main:mountain_resilience]] as 4th-rank primal innate spells that you can cast once per day. As a 3-action activity, you can Cast a Spell twice to cast both of these innate spells, as long as they are both still available for the day.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
