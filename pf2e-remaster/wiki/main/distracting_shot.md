**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]

---

The sheer power of your attacks, or the overwhelming number of them, leaves an enemy flustered. If you critically hit your hunted prey with a ranged weapon, or hit it at least twice on the same turn with a ranged weapon, it's [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core.*
