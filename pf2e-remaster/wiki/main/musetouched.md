**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** Lineage, [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]

---

Your blood sings with the liberating power of the azatas, living embodiments of freedom from a wild celestial realm. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [[Escape|main:escape]]. When you roll a critical failure on a check to Escape, you get a failure instead, and when you roll a success, you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core.*
