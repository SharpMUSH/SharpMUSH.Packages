**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Ratfolk|main:ratfolk]]

---

You can easily compress your body and squeeze through gaps. You gain the [[Quick Squeeze|main:quick_squeeze]] feat as a bonus feat, even if you aren't trained in Acrobatics. Tight spaces not tight enough to require the [[Squeeze|main:squeeze]] action aren't difficult terrain for you.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
