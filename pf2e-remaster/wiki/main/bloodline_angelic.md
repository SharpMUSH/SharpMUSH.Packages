**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]

---

One of your forebears hailed from a celestial realm, or your ancestors' devotion led to their lineage being blessed.

**Spell List** divine

**Bloodline Skills** Diplomacy, Religion

**Granted Spells** cantrip [[Light|main:light]], 1st: [[Heal|main:heal]], 2nd: [[Spiritual Armament|main:spiritual_armament]], 3rd: [[Holy Light|main:holy_light]], 4th: [[Divine Wrath|main:divine_wrath]], 5th: [[Divine Immolation|main:divine_immolation]], 6th: [[Blessed Boundary|main:blessed_boundary]], 7th: [[Divine Decree|main:divine_decree]], 8th: [[Moment of Renewal|main:moment_of_renewal]], 9th: [[Foresight|main:foresight_spell]]

**Bloodline Spells** initial: [[Angelic Halo|main:angelic_halo]], advanced: [[Angelic Wings|main:angelic_wings]], greater: [[Celestial Brand|main:celestial_brand]]

**Blood Magic—Divine Aura** You project a warming aura that protects you or one target, granting a +1 status bonus to saving throws for 1 round

*Source: Player Core 2.*
