**Class Feat 8** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Concentrate, [[Fighter|main:fighter]]

---

By spending a moment to focus, you can ensure your attack strikes true. Make a ranged weapon Strike. On this Strike, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and ignore the target's [[Concealed|main:concealed]] condition.

*Source: Player Core.*
