**Spell 5**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Shadow  
**Traditions:** [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** varies

---

**Area** varies

**Defense** basic Reflex or basic Will (target's choice)

---

You shape the shadow substance of the plane of shadow into a blast. Choose 6d8 acid, 6d8 bludgeoning, 6d8 cold, 6d8 electricity, 6d8 fire, 6d8 force, 6d8 piercing, 6d8 slashing, 6d8 sonic, or 6d8 spirit damage, and choose a 30-foot cone, a 15-foot burst within 120 feet, or a 50-foot line. The blast deals 6d8 damage of the type you chose to each creature in the area.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 1d8.

*Source: Player Core.*
