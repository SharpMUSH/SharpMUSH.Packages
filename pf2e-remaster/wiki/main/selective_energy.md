**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]

---

As you call down divine power, you can prevent some enemies from benefiting or some allies from being hurt. When you cast a version of [[Harm|main:harm]] or [[Heal|main:heal]] that has an area, you can designate up to 5 creatures in the area. They are not targeted by the spell.

*Source: Player Core.*
