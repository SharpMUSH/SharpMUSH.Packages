**Item 17**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Ingested, Poison  
**Price:** 2250 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

Concentrated hemlock is a particularly deadly toxin that halts muscle action—including that of the victim's heart.

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 38 Fortitude

**Onset** 30 minutes

**Maximum Duration** 60 minutes

**Stage 1** 16d6 poison damage and [[Enfeebled 2|main:enfeebled]] (10 minutes)

**Stage 2** 17d6 poison damage and [[Enfeebled 3|main:enfeebled]] (10 minutes)

**Stage 3** 18d6 poison damage and [[Enfeebled 4|main:enfeebled]] (10 minutes)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
