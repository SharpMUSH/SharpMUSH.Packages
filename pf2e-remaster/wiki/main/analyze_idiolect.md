**Archetype Skill Feat 6**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** [[Linguist Dedication|main:linguist_dedication]]; expert in [[Deception|main:deception]]; expert in [[Society|main:society]]

---

You break down a specific individual's idiolect, memorizing their speech mannerisms and habits.

If you interact with someone for at least 10 minutes, when you later attempt to [[Impersonate|main:impersonate]] that individual, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus to your Deception checks and DCs. Due to the intense character study required, you can't remember more than one idiolect at a time.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
