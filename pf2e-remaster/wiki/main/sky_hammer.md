**Traits:** Rare, Magical, Shove  
**Price:** 70000 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Martial | Hammer | 1d8 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

The sturdy head of this *+3 major striking [[Flaming|main:flaming]] [[Shock|main:shock]] orichalcum warhammer* is shaped like a blazing comet.

**Activate—Comet Fall** (reaction) (concentration)

**Trigger** Your attack roll with the *sky hammer* is a critical success

---

**Effect** A 6th-rank arcane [[Fireball|main:fireball]] spell explodes, centered on the *sky hammer*. The spell DC is 45. You are immune to the *fireball's* effect, though your allies are not.

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of *fireball* (6th rank), and the initial raw materials must include 12,375 gp of orichalcum.
