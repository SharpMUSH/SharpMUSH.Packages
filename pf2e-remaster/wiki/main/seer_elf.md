**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Elf|main:elf]]

---

You have an inborn ability to detect and understand magical phenomena. You can cast the [[Detect Magic|main:detect_magic_spell]] cantrip as an arcane innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up.

In addition, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Identify Magic|main:identify_magic]] and to [[Decipher Writing|main:decipher_writing]] of a magical nature. These skill actions typically use the Arcana, Nature, Occultism, or Religion skill.

*Source: Player Core.*
