**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]  
**Prerequisites:** Angelkin, Lawbringer, Musetouched, or another lineage feat associated with celestials

---

You possess celestial magic. Choose two of the following spells: [[Clear Mind|main:clear_mind]], [[Everlight|main:everlight]], [[Humanoid Form|main:humanoid_form]], [[Revealing Light|main:revealing_light]], [[Share Life|main:share_life]], or [[Sure Footing|main:sure_footing]]. You can use each of the chosen spells once per day as 2nd-rank divine innate spells.

Angelkin typically take *clear mind* and *humanoid form*, lawbringers typically have *everlight* and *share life*, and musetouched typically have *revealing light* and *sure footing*.

*Source: Player Core.*
