**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

You come from a line of wandering mercenaries constantly on the march and scavenging food on the trail. If you fail, but don't critically fail, to [[Subsist|main:subsist]] in the wilderness, you can still keep yourself fed with poor meals. When exploring, you can [[Hustle|main:hustle]] twice as long before you have to stop.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
