**Focus 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Focus, Manipulate  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Duration:** until the end of the current turn

---

The blessings of your god make your feet faster and your movements more fluid. You gain a +5-foot status bonus to your Speed and ignore difficult terrain. As part of casting agile feet, you can Stride, Step, or [[Tumble Through|main:tumble_through]]; you can instead Burrow, Climb, Fly, or Swim if you have the appropriate Speed.

*Source: Player Core.*
