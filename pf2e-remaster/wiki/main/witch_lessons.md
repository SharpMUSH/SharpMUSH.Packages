**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Witch|main:witch]]

---

A witch's knowledge from their patron comes in the form of lessons, which you can learn by selecting feats like Basic Lesson. Each lesson grants you a hex and teaches your familiar a new spell, adding it to the spells you can prepare using your witch spellcasting. You gain this spell even if it's not on your tradition's spell list.

#### BASIC LESSONS

You can select from these lessons when a feat or another effect grants you a basic lesson.

**Lesson of Dreams** You gain the [[Veil of Dreams|main:veil_of_dreams]] hex, and your familiar learns [[Sleep|main:sleep]].

**Lesson of the Elements** You gain the [[Elemental Betrayal|main:elemental_betrayal]] hex. Your familiar learns your choice of [[Breathe Fire|main:breathe_fire]], [[Air Bubble|main:air_bubble]], [[Hydraulic Push|main:hydraulic_push]], or [[Pummeling Rubble|main:pummeling_rubble]].

**Lesson of Life** You gain the [[Life Boost|main:life_boost]] hex, and your familiar learns [[Spirit Link|main:spirit_link]].

**Lesson of Protection** You gain the [[Blood Ward|main:blood_ward]] hex; your familiar learns [[Mystic Armor|main:mystic_armor]].

**Lesson of Vengeance** You gain the [[Needle of Vengeance|main:needle_of_vengeance]] hex, and your familiar learns [[Phantom Pain|main:phantom_pain]].

- Uncommon

**Lesson of Calamity:** You gain the Stumbling Curse hex, and your familiar learns [[Ill Omen|main:ill_omen]].

#### GREATER LESSONS

You can select from these lessons when a feat or other effect grants you a greater lesson.

**Lesson of Mischief** You gain the [[Deceiver's Cloak|main:deceivers_cloak]] hex, and your familiar learns [[Mad Monkeys|main:mad_monkeys]].

**Lesson of Shadow** You gain the [[Malicious Shadow|main:malicious_shadow]] hex, and your familiar learns [[Chilling Darkness|main:chilling_darkness]].

**Lesson of Snow** You gain the [[Personal Blizzard|main:personal_blizzard]] hex, and your familiar learns [[Wall of Wind|main:wall_of_wind]].

**Lesson of Favors** You gain the Return the Favor hex, and your familiar learns Mind of Menace.

**Lesson of Decay** All living things eventually decay, feeding fungus and insect. You gain the Mycological Malady hex, and your familiar learns [[Insect Form|main:insect_form]].

**Lesson of the Flock** Power flows when many work as one. You gain the Sheltering Wings hex, and your familiar learns [[Levitate|main:levitate]].

**Lesson of the Shark** Learn the ways of this deadly oceanic predator. You gain the Blood in the Water hex, and your familiar learns [[Feet to Fins|main:feet_to_fins]].

#### MAJOR LESSONS

You can select from these lessons when a feat or other effect grants you a major lesson.

**Lesson of Bargains** You gain the Over the Coals hex, and your familiar learns [[Subconscious Suggestion|main:subconscious_suggestion]].

**Lesson of Death** You gain the [[Curse of Death|main:curse_of_death]] hex, and your familiar learns [[Raise Dead|main:raise_dead]].

**Lesson of Renewal** You gain the [[Restorative Moment|main:restorative_moment]] hex, and your familiar learns [[Field of Life|main:field_of_life]].

- Rare

**Lesson of Frozen Queen** You gain the Glacial Heart hex and your familiar learns [[Wall of Ice|main:wall_of_ice]].

*Source: Player Core.*
