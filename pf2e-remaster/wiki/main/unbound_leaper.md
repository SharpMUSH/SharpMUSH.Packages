**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Tripkee|main:tripkee]]

---

Your jumping prowess is unmatched. You can [[Leap|main:leap]] up to 30 feet in any direction without touching the ground and without requiring an Athletics check; when doing so, you must land on a space of solid ground within 30 feet of you, or else you fall after using your next action. You can exceed your normal Speed while Leaping.

---

**Special** If you have the Fantastic Leaps feat, the total distance you can Leap horizontally is 40 feet, and the total distance you can Leap vertically is 35 feet.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
