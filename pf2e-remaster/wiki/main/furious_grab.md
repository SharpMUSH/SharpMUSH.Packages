**Class Feat 12** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage

---

**Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike, and either you have a hand free or your Strike used a grapple weapon.

---

You grab your foe while it's distracted by your attack. The foe you hit becomes [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]], as if you had succeeded at an Athletics check to [[Grapple|main:grapple]] the foe.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
