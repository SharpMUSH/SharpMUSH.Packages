**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

You reposition, maneuver, and bewilder your foes with daring feats of physical prowess. You are trained in Athletics. When you [[Grapple|main:grapple]], [[Reposition|main:reposition]], [[Shove|main:shove]], or [[Trip|main:trip]], the action gains the bravado trait.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
