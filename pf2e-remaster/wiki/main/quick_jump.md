**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You can use [[High Jump|main:high_jump]] and [[Long Jump|main:long_jump]] as a single action instead of 2 actions. If you do, you don't perform the initial Stride (nor do you fail if you don't Stride 10 feet).

*Source: Player Core.*
