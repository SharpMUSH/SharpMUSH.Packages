**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Cantrip, Curse, Hex, Manipulate, [[Witch|main:witch]]  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** sustained up to 1 minute

---

Your patron's resentment manifests in a baleful, envious gaze. The target becomes [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]] if it fails a Will save (or sickened 2 on a critical failure). This condition value can't be reduced below 1 while the spell is active and you can see the target.

*Source: Player Core.*
