**Item 11**  
**Traits:** Magical  
**Price:** 1200 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto armor

---

A *ready* rune draws component pieces of a suit of armor toward one another, making it extremely fast to get into. You can don any armor with this rune as a single action.

*Source: GM Core.*
