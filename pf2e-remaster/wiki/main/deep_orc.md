**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Orc|main:orc]]

---

Your calloused hands and red eyes speak to a life spent in the deep darkness of mountain caverns, where you learned to battle on rocky cliffs and survive with minimal resources. You gain the [[Terrain Expertise|main:terrain_expertise]] skill feat for underground terrain and the [[Combat Climber|main:combat_climber]] skill feat.

*Source: Player Core.*
