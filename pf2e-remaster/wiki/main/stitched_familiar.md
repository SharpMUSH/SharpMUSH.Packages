**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Witch|main:witch]]  
**Prerequisites:** arcane or primal patron

---

Your familiar can shed its material form, becoming a mass of animated magic. Your familiar gains the [[Stitching Strike|main:stitching_strike]] activity.
