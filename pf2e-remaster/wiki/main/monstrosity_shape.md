**Class Feat 16**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form_feat]]

---

You can transform into a powerful magical creature. Add the cave worm and sea serpent shapes listed in [[Monstrosity Form|main:monstrosity_form]] to your [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form]] list. If you have [[Soaring Shape|main:soaring_shape]], also add the phoenix shape to your *untamed form* list.

*Source: Player Core.*
