**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Cantrip, Concentrate, Manipulate, Mental, Nonlethal  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** basic Will

---

You push into the target's mind and daze it with a mental jolt. The jolt deals 1d6 mental damage, with a basic Will save. If the target critically fails the save, it is also [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]].

---

**Heightened (+2)** The damage increases by 1d6.

*Source: Player Core.*
