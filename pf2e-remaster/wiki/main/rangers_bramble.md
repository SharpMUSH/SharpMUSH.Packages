**Focus 3**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Manipulate, Plant, [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 100 feet  
**Area:** 5-foot burst  
**Defense:** Reflex  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

You cause plants in the area to entangle your foes, with the effects of [[Entangling Flora|main:entangling_flora]]. A creature that critically fails the save takes 2d4 bleed damage in addition to being [[Immobilized|main:immobilized]]. [[Escaping|main:escape]] your bramble doesn't end the bleed damage.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The bleed damage on a critical failure increases by 1d4.

*Source: Player Core.*
