**Archetype Feat 12**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]; Startling Appearance

---

Your dramatic appearances can frighten bystanders.

When you make a Startling Appearance, you can also attempt to [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] each opponent within 10 feet to whom you were unnoticed before your Strike.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
