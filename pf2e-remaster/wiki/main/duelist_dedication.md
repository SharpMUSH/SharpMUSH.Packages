**Archetype Feat 2**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Dedication  
**Prerequisites:** trained in light armor and simple weapons

---

You are always ready to draw your weapon and begin a duel, no matter the circumstances. You gain the [[Quick Draw|main:quick_draw]] feat, enabling you to both draw and attack with a weapon as 1 action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
