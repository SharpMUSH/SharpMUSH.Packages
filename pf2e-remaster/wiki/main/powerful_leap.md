**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You can jump 5 feet up with a vertical [[Leap|main:leap]] without making a [[High Jump|main:high_jump]]. You also increase the horizontal distance when you Leap, including as part of a High Jump or [[Long Jump|main:long_jump]], by 5 feet.

*Source: Player Core.*
