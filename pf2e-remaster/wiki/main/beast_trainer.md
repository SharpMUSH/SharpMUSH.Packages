**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Orc|main:orc]]

---

You have an impressive innate ability to tame and command ferocious beasts. You become trained in the Nature skill and gain your choice of the [[Pet|main:pet]] general feat or the [[Train Animal|main:train_animal]] skill feat.

*Source: Player Core.*
