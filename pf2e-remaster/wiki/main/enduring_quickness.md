**Class Feat 20**  
**Traits:** [[Monk|main:monk]]

---

You move as fast and as high as the wind itself. You're permanently [[Quickened|main:quickened]].

You can use your extra action to Stride or [[Leap|main:leap]], to provide one of the actions needed for a [[High Jump|main:high_jump]] or [[Long Jump|main:long_jump]]

*Source: Player Core 2.*
