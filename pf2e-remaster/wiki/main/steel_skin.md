**Archetype Skill Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Survival|main:survival]]; [[Sentinel Dedication|main:sentinel_dedication]]

---

You wear your armor like a second skin, taking comfort in the protection it offers. You don't become [[Fatigued|main:fatigued]] if you sleep while wearing armor.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
