**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Lizardfolk|main:lizardfolk]]

---

Non-lizardfolk creatures in difficult terrain are [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] to you. If you have a swim Speed, non-lizardfolk creatures that are in water and lack a swim Speed are also off-guard to you.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
