**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]

---

You can shape the energy you channel in a single direction. When you cast a version of [[Harm|main:harm]] or [[Heal|main:heal]] that has an area, you can make its area a 60-foot cone instead of a 30-foot emanation.

*Source: Player Core.*
