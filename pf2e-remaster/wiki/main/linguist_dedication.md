**Archetype Feat 2**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Dedication  
**Prerequisites:** You speak at least three languages.

---

You have studied languages and their development. You become trained in Society; if you were already trained in Society, you instead become an expert in Society. You gain the [[Multilingual|main:multilingual]] skill feat twice.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
