**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]

---

You have used alchemy to create life, a simple creature formed from alchemical materials, reagents, and a bit of your own blood. You gain a familiar, which has the [[Construct|main:construct]] familiar ability; this is permanent, doesn't require the familiar to have the [[Tough|main:tough]] familiar ability, and doesn't count against your usual limit of familiar abilities (typically 2). The familiar uses your Intelligence modifier to determine its Perception, Acrobatics, and Stealth modifiers.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
