**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** Divine, [[Oracle|main:oracle]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Water Walker|main:water_walker]]

---

Your mysterious steps become even lighter, transcending the mortal world altogether. When you have the Cursebound condition, you gain the effects of [[Fly|main:fly_spell]]. If you are cursebound 3 or greater, you gain a +10-foot status bonus to your fly Speed.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
