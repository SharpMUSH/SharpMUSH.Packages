**Item 2**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Talisman  
**Price:** 7 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to armor

---

**Activate** (1 action) (concentrate)

---

This silver-bound opal pendant is afire with iridescence. When you activate it, attempt a Deception check to [[Feint|main:feint]]. If the outcome is a success, you get a critical success instead. If the outcome is a critical failure, you get a failure instead.

*Source: GM Core.*
