**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** Strength +3; expert in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

In situations where you can physically menace the target when you [[Coerce|main:coerce]] or [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]], you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your Intimidation check and you ignore the penalty for not sharing a language. If your Strength modifier is +5 or higher and you are a master in Intimidation, this bonus increases to +2.

*Source: Player Core.*
