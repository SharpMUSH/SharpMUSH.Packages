**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Leshy|main:leshy]]

---

Your command over your vital essence allows you or an ally to recover from grievous wounds. You can cast [[Regenerate|main:regenerate]] as a 7th-rank primal innate spell once per day.

*Source: Player Core.*
