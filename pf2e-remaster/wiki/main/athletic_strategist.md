**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

You know how to calculatedly manipulate joints and body weight. In addition to using an attack stratagem's roll substitution for a Strike, you can use it to modify a [[Disarm|main:disarm]], [[Grapple|main:grapple]], [[Reposition|main:reposition]], [[Shove|main:shove]], or [[Trip|main:trip]] attempt, substituting your [[Devise a Stratagem|main:devise_a_stratagem]] roll for the Athletics check. You must apply the substitution to the first eligible attack you make, whether it's a Strike or one of the Athletics actions.

You can also use your Intelligence modifier instead of Strength for the Athletics check when you substitute your Devise a Stratagem roll, unless you're using a weapon for the maneuver and the weapon doesn't fit the restrictions for using Intelligence with a stratagem.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
