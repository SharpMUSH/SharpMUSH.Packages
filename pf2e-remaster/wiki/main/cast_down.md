**Class Feat 6** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** harmful font or healing font

---

The sheer force of your faith can bring a foe crashing down. If the next action you use is to cast [[Harm|main:harm]] or [[Heal|main:heal]] to damage one creature, the target is knocked [[Prone|main:prone]] if it takes any damage from the spell. If the target critically fails its save against the spell, it also takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed for 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core.*
