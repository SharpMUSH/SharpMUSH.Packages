**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Witch|main:witch]]

---

Your patron first contacted you at the witching hour, as your body lay paralyzed by sleep while your mind had yet to escape the waking world. Your patron might be a creature of the plane of shadow or a long-forgotten spirit of twilight—all you remember of them are haunting eyes of moonlight, offering you power from the darkness.

**Spell List** occult

**Patron Skill** Occultism

**Lesson of Night's Terrors** Your patron taught you that the darkness can hide all manner of things best left unseen. You gain the [[Shroud of Night|main:shroud_of_night]] hex cantrip, and your familiar learns [[Fear|main:fear]].

**Familiar of Stalking Night** Your familiar is dark of fur or feather, and light seems to disappear into it. When you Cast or Sustain a hex, and your familiar is adjacent to an enemy to which it's [[Concealed|main:concealed]], [[Hidden|main:hidden]], or undetected, the enemy becomes [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]].

*Source: Player Core.*
