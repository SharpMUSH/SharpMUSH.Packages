**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

Your ancestors lived underground. Your ears are larger than those of other hobgoblins and sensitive to echoes. While you're underground, when you target an opponent that is [[Concealed|main:concealed]] from you or [[Hidden|main:hidden]] from you, reduce the DC of the flat check to 3 for a concealed target or 9 for a hidden one. In addition, if you roll a success on an Acrobatics check to [[Squeeze|main:squeeze]], you get a critical success instead.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
