**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Oracle|main:oracle]]

---

Knowledge and information come freely to you. You might use this lore to gain power or perhaps to understand the divine mysteries of the multiverse.

**Granted Spells**

- **cantrip:** [[Read Aura|main:read_aura]]
- **1st:** [[Mindlink|main:mindlink]]
- **3rd:** [[Hypercognition|main:hypercognition]]
- **6th:** [[Never Mind|main:never_mind]]

**Revelation Spells**

- **initial:** [[Brain Drain|main:brain_drain]]
- **advanced:** [[Access Lore|main:access_lore]]
- **greater:** [[Dread Secret|main:dread_secret]]

**Related Domains** knowledge, magic, secrecy, truth

**Mystery Skill** Occultism and one Lore skill of your choice
