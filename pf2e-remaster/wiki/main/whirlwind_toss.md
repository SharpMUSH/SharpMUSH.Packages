**Class Feat 18** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage  
**Prerequisites:** [[Collateral Thrash|main:collateral_thrash]]

---

You whirl a foe to smash into all nearby creatures before throwing them far away. You [[Thrash|main:thrash]]. During this Thrash, your [[Collateral Thrash|main:collateral_thrash]] feat applies to all other enemies adjacent to you. You can then throw the [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] creature 10 feet, where they fall [[Prone|main:prone]].

If the enemy you choose for Collateral Thrash is also adjacent to you, it attempts only one save and takes the damage only once.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
