**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Catfolk|main:catfolk]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Evade Doom|main:evade_doom]]

---

When your character would die, you can attempt a DC 17 flat. On a success, you're instead reduced to 0 HP and a [[Dying|main:dying]] value 1 less than what would normally kill you (typically dying 3). This doesn't change the result for any other creature affected.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
