**Item 6**  
**Traits:** Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 230 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

This ash staff is decorated with animals, elementals, and dragons. Creatures summoned using this staff gain a number of temporary Hit Points equal to the rank of the spell used to summon them.

**Activate** Cast a Spell

---

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Tangle Vine|main:tangle_vine]]
- **1st** [[Summon Animal|main:summon_animal]]
- **2nd** [[Marvelous Mount|main:marvelous_mount]], [[Mist|main:mist]], [[Summon Animal|main:summon_animal]], [[Summon Elemental|main:summon_elemental]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.
