**Skill Feat 2**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Acrobatics|main:acrobatics]]

---

You can [[Crawl|main:crawl]] incredibly swiftly—up to half your Speed, rather than 5 feet. If you're a master in Acrobatics, you can Crawl at full Speed, and if you're legendary, you aren't [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] while [[Prone|main:prone]].

*Source: Player Core.*
