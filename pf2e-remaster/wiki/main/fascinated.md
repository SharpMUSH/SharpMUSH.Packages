Something holds your attention and distracts you from everything else.

- You take a -2 status penalty to Perception and skill checks.
- You can't use concentrate actions unless they, or what they aim at, concern the subject of your fascination, as the GM decides. You might Seek or Recall Knowledge about it, but probably not cast a spell at another creature.

**Ends:** when a creature uses a hostile action against you or any of your allies.

*Source: Player Core, p. 443.*
