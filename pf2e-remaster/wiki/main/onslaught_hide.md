This *+1 resilient hide armor* is decorated with the horns of many slain beasts. It has an armor check penalty of –1 instead of –2. When you use the [[Sudden Charge|main:sudden_charge]] class feat while wearing this armor, your Strike deals an additional 1d8 untyped damage.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
