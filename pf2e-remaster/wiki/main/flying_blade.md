**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]  
**Prerequisites:** precise strike

---

You apply your flashy techniques to thrown weapons as easily as melee attacks. You apply your [[Precise Strike|main:precise_strike]] damage on ranged Strikes you make with a thrown weapon within that weapon's first range increment. The thrown weapon must be an agile or finesse weapon. This also allows you to make a thrown weapon ranged Strike for [[Confident Finisher|main:confident_finisher]] and any other finisher that includes a Strike that can benefit from your precise strike.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
