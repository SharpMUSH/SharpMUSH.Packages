**Item 12**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Mechanical, Snare, Trap  
**Price:** 320 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

This snare sends a powerful series of scything blades to slice through a creature entering the snare's square, dealing 10d8 slashing damage (basic DC 32 Reflex save).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
