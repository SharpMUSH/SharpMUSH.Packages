**Archetype Skill Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** [[Cavalier Dedication|main:cavalier_dedication]]; expert in [[Nature|main:nature]]

---

**Requirements** You are adjacent to a creature that is at least one size larger than you and is willing to be your mount.

---

You and your mount can spring into action at a moment's notice. You [[Mount|main:mount]] the creature and [[Command an Animal|main:command_an_animal]] to issue it an order of your choice.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
