**Activate** (1 action) Strike

---

Bottled lightning is packed with volatile reagents that create a blast of electricity when they are exposed to air. Bottled lightning deals 1d6 electricity damage and 1 electricity splash damage. On a hit, the target becomes [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.
