**Class Feat 2** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Press

---

**Requirements** You have one hand free, and your target is within reach of that hand.

---

Using your prior attack to shift your opponent's guard, you take another swing and grab them. Make a melee Strike while keeping one hand free. If the Strike hits, you grab the target using your free hand. The creature remains [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] until the end of your next turn or until it [[Escapes|main:escape]], whichever comes first.

*Source: Player Core.*
