**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Mercy|main:mercy]]

---

The divine grace that flows through you grants reprieve from an affliction. Add mercy of recuperation to the types of [[Mercy|main:mercy]] you can provide, allowing you to counteract an affliction instead of an effect causing a condition (typically an affliction is a curse, disease, or poison).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
