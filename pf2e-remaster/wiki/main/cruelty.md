**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** touch of the void

---

The harm you deal with your corrupting touch saps the strength from your enemy's body. You can cast [[Touch of the Void|main:touch_of_the_void]] targeting a living creature using 2 actions instead of 1. If you do, the target is also [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]] for 1 minute if it fails its save ([[Enfeebled 2|main:enfeebled]] if it critically fails).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
