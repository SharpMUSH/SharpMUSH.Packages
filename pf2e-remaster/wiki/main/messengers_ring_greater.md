**Item 17**  
**Traits:** Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 13500 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** worn

---

This silver signet ring changes to match the insignia of a lord or organization you serve (or your own face, if you serve no one else). It grants you a +3 item bonus to Diplomacy checks and lets you cast [[Message|main:message]] as an arcane innate spell at will.

---

**Activate—Dispatch Messenger** 1 minute (concentrate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** The ring casts [[Animal Messenger|main:animal_messenger]] to your specification. The animal is a magical creature that springs from the ring, and its appearance suits the iconography or heraldry of the lord or organization represented by the ring.

---

**Activate—Sending** (3 actions) command

**Frequency** once per hour

---

**Effect** The ring casts [[Sending|main:sending]] to your specifications.
