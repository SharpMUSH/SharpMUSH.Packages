**Archetype Feat 8** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Mauler Dedication|main:mauler_dedication]]; expert in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

**Trigger** An enemy within your reach leaves a square during a move action it's using.

**Requirements** You are wielding a melee weapon in two hands.

---

You swing your weapon at your foe, rebuffing them back. You attempt to [[Shove|main:shove]] the triggering creature, ignoring the requirement that you have a hand free. Unless you critically succeed at your check, the creature continues its movement after the Shove.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
