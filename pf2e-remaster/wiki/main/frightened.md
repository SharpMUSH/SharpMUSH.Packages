Fear grips you and you struggle to keep your nerve. Frightened always has a value.

- You take a status penalty equal to the value to all your checks and DCs.

**Ends:** unless something says otherwise, the value drops by 1 at the end of each of your turns.

*Source: Player Core, p. 444.*
