You can't hear.

- Perception checks that need hearing are automatic critical failures.
- You take a -2 status penalty to Perception checks for initiative and to checks that involve sound but also use other senses.
- An action with the auditory trait needs a DC 5 flat check, rolled after you spend the action and before its effects; on a failure the action is lost.
- You are immune to auditory effects.

*Source: Player Core, p. 443.*
