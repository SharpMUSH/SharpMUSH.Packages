**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]

---

You have learned some of the tricks the fey use to bend primal magic toward illusions and trickery. Add [[Illusory Disguise|main:illusory_disguise]], [[Illusory Object|main:illusory_object]], and [[Illusory Scene|main:illusory_scene]] to your spell list, which you cast as primal spells.

*Source: Player Core.*
