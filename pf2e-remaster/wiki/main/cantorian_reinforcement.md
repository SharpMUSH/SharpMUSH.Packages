**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

The life energy that helped create the first hobgoblins is particularly potent in you, protecting you from ongoing maladies. If you roll a success on a saving throw against a disease or poison, you get a critical success instead. If you have a different ability that would improve the save in this way (such as the [[Battle Hardened|main:battle_hardened]] fighter class feature), if you roll a critical failure on the save you get a failure instead.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
