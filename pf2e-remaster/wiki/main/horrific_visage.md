**Focus 3**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Emotion, Fear, Focus, Illusion, Manipulate, Mental, [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]], Visual  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Area:** 30-foot emanation  
**Defense:** Will

---

You briefly transform your features into the horrific visage of a hag, striking fear into your enemies. Foes in the area must attempt a Will save.

---

**Success** The foe is unaffected.

**Failure** The foe is [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]].

**Critical Failure** The foe is [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]].

---

**Heightened (5th)** Foes in the area are [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]] on a success, [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]] on a failure, and [[Frightened 3|main:frightened]] and [[Fleeing|main:fleeing]] for 1 round on a critical failure. They are still unaffected on a critical success.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
