**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]

---

An elemental influence has imbued your blood with primal fury. You are granted different sorcerous gift spells and damage type from blood magic depending on which element affected your bloodline (see Elemental Influences below).

**Tradition** primal

**Bloodline Skills** Intimidation, Nature

**Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip: see Elemental Influences below; 1st: see Elemental Influences below; 2nd: [[Resist Energy|main:resist_energy]]; 3rd: see Elemental Influences below; 4th: [[Unfettered Movement|main:unfettered_movement]]; 5th: [[Elemental Form|main:elemental_form]] (elemental influence only); 6th :see Elemental Influences below; 7th: [[Energy Aegis|main:energy_aegis]]; 8th: [[Earthquake|main:earthquake]]; 9th: [[Wrathful Storm|main:wrathful_storm]]

**Bloodline Spells** initial: [[Elemental Toss|main:elemental_toss]]; advanced: [[Elemental Motion|main:elemental_motion]]; greater: [[Elemental Blast|main:elemental_blast]]

**Blood Magic—Elemental Fury** Elemental energy wreathes your form and infuses your magic. Either you gain a +2 status bonus to Intimidation checks for 1 round, or a target takes 1 damage per spell rank; this damage type is listed under your elemental influence. If the spell already deals that type of damage, combine it with the spell's initial damage before determining weaknesses and resistances.

### Elemental Influences

At 1st level, choose the type of elemental that influenced your bloodline: air, earth, fire, metal, water, or wood. You can't change this later. You are granted different spells at certain ranks according to this influence.

- **Air—Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip: [[Gale Blast|main:gale_blast]]; 1st: [[Tailwind|main:tailwind]]; 3rd: [[Wall of Wind|main:wall_of_wind]]; 6th: [[Chain Lightning|main:chain_lightning]]; **Blood Magic Damage** slashing
- **Earth—Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip: [[Scatter Scree|main:scatter_scree]]; 1st: [[Pummeling Rubble|main:pummeling_rubble]]; 3rd: [[Earthbind|main:earthbind]]; 6th: [[Petrify|main:petrify]]; **Blood Magic Damage** bludgeoning
- **Fire—Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip: [[Ignition|main:ignition]], 1st: [[Breathe Fire|main:breathe_fire]]; 3rd: [[Fireball|main:fireball]]; 6th: [[Tree of Seasons|main:tree_of_seasons]] (all pods are summer); **Blood Magic Damage** fire
- **Metal—Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip [[Electric Arc|main:electric_arc]]; 1st: [[Thunderstrike|main:thunderstrike]]; 3rd: [[Lightning Bolt|main:lightning_bolt]]; 6th: [[Chain Lightning|main:chain_lightning]]; **Blood Magic Damage** piercing
- **Water—Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip: [[Frostbite|main:frostbite]]; 1st: [[Hydraulic Push|main:hydraulic_push]]; 3rd: [[Aqueous Orb|main:aqueous_orb]]; 6th: [[Scintillating Safeguard|main:scintillating_safeguard]]; **Blood Magic Damage** bludgeoning
- **Wood—Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip: [[Tangle Vine|main:tangle_vine]]; 1st: [[Cleanse Cuisine|main:cleanse_cuisine]]; 3rd: [[Wall of Thorns|main:wall_of_thorns]]; 6th: [[Tangling Creepers|main:tangling_creepers]]; **Blood Magic Damage** bludgeoning

*Source: Player Core 2.*
