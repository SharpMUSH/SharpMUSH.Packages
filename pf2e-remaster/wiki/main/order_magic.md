**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Order Explorer|main:order_explorer]]

---

You have delved deeper into the teaching of a new order, gaining access to a coveted order spell. Choose an order you have selected with [[Order Explorer|main:order_explorer]]. You gain the initial order spell from that order.

---

**Special** You can take this feat multiple times. Each time you do, you must choose a different order you have selected with Order Explorer.

*Source: Player Core.*
