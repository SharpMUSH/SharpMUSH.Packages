**Class Feat 12**  
**Traits:** [[Bard|main:bard]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Courageous Assault|main:courageous_assault]]

---

In the triumph of battle, you can share the glory with another ally. When the ally you chose for [[Courageous Assault|main:courageous_assault]] critically succeeds at the Strike granted by that action, another ally affected by your [[Courageous Anthem|main:courageous_anthem]] composition spell can immediately use a reaction to make a melee Strike. This effect doesn't continue to a third ally, even if the second ally also critically succeeds.

*Source: Player Core.*
