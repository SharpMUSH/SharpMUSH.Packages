**Item 19**  
**Traits:** Auditory, Invested, Magical, Void  
**Price:** 35000 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** wornmask

---

This ice-blue half-mask is adorned with a wicked silver grin that covers the wearer's mouth, leaving the rest of the face uncovered. You gain a +3 item bonus to Intimidation checks.

**Activate—Hideous Wail** (2 actions) (concentrate, manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** The mask casts a 9th-rank [[Wails of the Damned|main:wails_of_the_damned]]

Each living creature in a 40-foot emanation takes 8d10 void damage and must attempt a DC 41 Fortitude save.

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes full damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and is [[Drained|main:drained]] 1d4.

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and is [[Drained 4|main:drained]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
