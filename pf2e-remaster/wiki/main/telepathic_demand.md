**Spell 9**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Incapacitation, Linguistic, Manipulate, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (3 actions)  
**Range:** planetary  
**Targets:** 1 creature you've telepathically contacted before  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** varies

---

You send the target a message of 25 words or fewer, and it can respond immediately with its own message of 25 words or fewer. Your message is insidious and has the effect of a [[Suggestion|main:suggestion]] spell, with the message substituting for the spoken suggestion. On a successful save, the target is temporarily immune for 1 day, and on a critical success, the target is temporarily immune for 1 month. You can target a creature only if you've previously been in telepathic contact with it before, such as via the telepathy spell.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
