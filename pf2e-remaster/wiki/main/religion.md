**Key attribute:** Wisdom

Knowledge of faiths, divine magic, scripture, and creatures tied to the divine such as celestials, fiends and undead.

Your Religion modifier is your Wisdom modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Religion (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Decipher Writing|main:decipher_writing]]
- [[Identify Magic|main:identify_magic]]
- [[Learn a Spell|main:learn_a_spell]]
