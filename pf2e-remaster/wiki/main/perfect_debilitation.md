**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]

---

You have perfected the formulas for bombs that impede your enemies. When you use [[Debilitating Bomb|main:debilitating_bomb]], your target avoids the condition the bomb imposes only if it critically succeeds at its saving throw.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
