**Action** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]], Divine

---

**Trigger** An enemy in your champion's aura damages you

---

**Effect** Bloodshed begets bloodshed as you drag your enemy toward oblivion. You increase the amount of damage you take by 1d6 (2d6 at level 5 or higher; 3d6 at level 9 or higher; 4d6 at level 12 or higher; 5d6 at level 16 or higher; 6d6 at level 19 or higher), and you deal 1d6 (2d6 at level 5 or higher; 3d6 at level 9 or higher; 4d6 at level 12 or higher; 5d6 at level 16 or higher; 6d6 at level 19 or higher) spirit damage to the triggering enemy. The damage you take and deal when you use this reaction increases to 2d6 at 5th level, 3d6 at 9th level, 4d6 at 12th level, 5d6 at 16th level, and 6d6 at 19th level.

In addition, until the end of your next turn, your Strikes against the triggering creature deal 2 extra spirit damage. This extra damage increases to 4 at 9th level and 6 at 16th level.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
