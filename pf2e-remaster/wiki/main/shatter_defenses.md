**Class Feat 6** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Press

---

**Requirements** A [[Frightened|main:frightened]] creature is in your melee reach.

---

Your offense exploits your enemy's fear. Make a melee Strike against a frightened creature. If you hit and deal damage, the target becomes [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until its frightened condition ends. If the target was already off-guard to you when you damaged it with this Strike, it can't reduce its frightened value below 1 until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core.*
