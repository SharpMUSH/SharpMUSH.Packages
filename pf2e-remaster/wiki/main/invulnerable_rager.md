**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]]

---

Even the heaviest armors serve only to enhance your unbridled fury. You are trained in heavy armor. Whenever you gain a barbarian class feature that grants you expert or greater proficiency in medium armor, you also gain that proficiency in heavy armor. If you have the [[Quick-Tempered|main:quick_tempered]] action, you can use it while wearing heavy armor.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
