**Item 5**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Injury, Poison  
**Price:** 25 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

This spider venom erodes a target's defenses.

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 22 Fortitude

**Maximum Duration** 6 rounds

**Stage 1** 1d10 poison damage and [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]] (1 round)

**Stage 2** 1d12 poison damage, [[Clumsy 1|main:clumsy]], and [[Sickened 2|main:sickened]] (1 round)

**Stage 3** 2d6 poison damage, [[Clumsy 2|main:clumsy]], and [[Sickened 3|main:sickened]] (1 round)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
