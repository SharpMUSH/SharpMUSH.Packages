Your movements are clumsy and inexact. Clumsy always has a value.

- You take a status penalty equal to the value on Dexterity-based rolls and DCs: AC, Reflex saves, ranged attack rolls, and Acrobatics, Stealth and Thievery checks.

*Source: Player Core, p. 442.*
