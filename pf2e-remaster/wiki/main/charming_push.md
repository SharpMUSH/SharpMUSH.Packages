**Focus 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Incapacitation, Mental, [[Wizard|main:wizard]]  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** until the start of your next turn

---

You push at the target's mind to deflect their ire. The target must attempt a Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes a –1 circumstance penalty to attack rolls and damage rolls against you.

**Failure** The target can't use hostile actions against you.

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]] and can't use hostile actions against you.

*Source: Player Core.*
