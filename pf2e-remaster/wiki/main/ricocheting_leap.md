**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Tripkee|main:tripkee]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wall Jump|main:wall_jump]]

---

You quickly use your momentum to topple and spring off of foes. You can use [[Wall Jump|main:wall_jump]] to make additional jumps off of creatures larger than you as if they were walls. Once per turn when you make an additional jump off of a creature in this way, you can also attempt to [[Shove|main:shove]] or [[Trip|main:trip]] that creature as a free action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
