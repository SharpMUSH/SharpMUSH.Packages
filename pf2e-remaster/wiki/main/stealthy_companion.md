**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Animal Companion|main:animal_companion]]; [[Camouflage|main:camouflage]]

---

You've trained your animal companion to blend in to its surroundings. Your animal companion gains the benefit of the [[Camouflage|main:camouflage]] feat. If your companion is a specialized ambusher, its proficiency rank for Stealth increases to master (or legendary if it was already master).

*Source: Player Core.*
