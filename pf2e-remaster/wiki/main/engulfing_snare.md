**Item 14**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Mechanical, Snare, Trap  
**Price:** 900 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

You arrange a spiky cage of bones, particularly tough vegetation, or other material to spring up when disturbed. The snare deals 10d8 piercing damage to the first creature to enter this square; that creature must attempt a DC 34 Reflex save.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is knocked aside by the snare's deployment, takes half damage, and is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the end of its next turn.

**Failure** The creature is captured by the cage, taking full damage and falling [[Prone|main:prone]]. It's [[Immobilized|main:immobilized]] while it remains within the cage. It can get free by Escaping or by destroying the cage (AC 30, Fort +18, Ref +24, Hardness 5, HP 30, object immunities).

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature takes double damage.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
