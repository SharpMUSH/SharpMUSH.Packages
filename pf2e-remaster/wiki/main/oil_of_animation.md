**Item 12**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Consumable, Magical, Oil  
**Price:** 330 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

You can rub this bronze-colored oil onto a melee weapon to grant it the *[[Animated|main:animated]]* rune. Once you fail a flat check for the weapon, causing it to fall, this effect ends.

*Source: GM Core.*
