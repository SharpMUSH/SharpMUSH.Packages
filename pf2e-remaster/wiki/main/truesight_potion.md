**Item 16**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Potion  
**Price:** 1500 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

Upon drinking this clear, refreshing potion, you can see things as they actually are. You gain the benefits of a 7th-rank [[Truesight|main:truesight]] spell that has a counteract modifier of 1d20 plus 25.

*Source: GM Core.*
