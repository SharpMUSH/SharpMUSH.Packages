You can't move.

- You can't use actions with the move trait.
- If something holds you in place and an outside force would move you, the force must succeed at a check against the DC of the effect holding you, or against the relevant defense (usually Fortitude DC) of the creature holding you.

*Source: Player Core, p. 444.*
