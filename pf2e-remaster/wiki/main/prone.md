You are lying on the ground.

- You are [[Off-Guard]] and take a -2 circumstance penalty to attack rolls.
- The only move actions you can use are Crawl and Stand. Standing up ends the condition.
- You can Take Cover while prone, even with nothing to hide behind, for a +4 circumstance bonus to AC against ranged attacks (you stay off-guard).
- If you would be knocked prone while Climbing or Flying, you fall. You can't be knocked prone while Swimming.

*Source: Player Core, p. 445.*
