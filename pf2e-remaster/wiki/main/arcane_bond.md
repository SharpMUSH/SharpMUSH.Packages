**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Wizard|main:wizard]]  
**Class:** [[Wizard|main:wizard]]

---

You place some of your magical power in a bonded item. Each day when you prepare your spells, you can designate a single item you own as your bonded item. This is typically an item associated with spellcasting, such as a wand, ring, or staff, but you are free to designate a weapon or other item. You gain the [[Drain Bonded Item|main:drain_bonded_item]] free action.

*Source: Player Core.*
