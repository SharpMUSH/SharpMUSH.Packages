**Class Feat 12** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage

---

**Trigger** A creature within your reach damages you with a melee attack.

---

Ignoring your pain, you reach out and grab the creature or weapon that caused you harm. Attempt an Athletics check to [[Grapple|main:grapple]] the triggering creature or to [[Disarm|main:disarm]] the creature of the weapon it damaged you with.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
