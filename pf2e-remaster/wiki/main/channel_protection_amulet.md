**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 56 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** worn

---

This nugget of polished tektite is trapped in a cage of braided wire and hangs from a silken cord. When wearing this amulet, you gain resistance 5 against damage from [[Harm|main:harm]] spells if you're living, or against [[Heal|main:heal]] spells if you're undead.

*Source: GM Core.*
