**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

Sometimes, you need to follow the leads as they come in the middle of an investigation. Choose a creature that you can see who isn't, to your knowledge, tied to any of your current investigations. For the next minute, you can [[Devise a Stratagem|main:devise_a_stratagem]] against that creature as a free action.
