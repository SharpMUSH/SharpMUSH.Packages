**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Kobold|main:kobold]]

---

Thanks to your warren's association with a dragon, your scales are sturdier than other kobolds'. You gain 10 Hit Points from your ancestry instead of 6. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to saves against dragon breath, effects with the sleep trait, and effects that would make you [[Paralyzed|main:paralyzed]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
