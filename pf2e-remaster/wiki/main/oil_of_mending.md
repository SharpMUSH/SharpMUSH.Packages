**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Oil  
**Price:** 9 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

**Activate** 1 minute (manipulate)

---

A vial of oil of mending appears to have countless translucent threads swirling within. Applying this oil to an item casts a 2nd-rank [[Mending|main:mending]] spell to repair the item.

*Source: GM Core.*
