Though referred to as a breastplate, this type of armor consists of several pieces of plate or [[Half Plate|main:half_plate]] armor that protect the torso, chest, neck, and sometimes the hips and lower legs. It strategically grants some of the protection of plate while allowing greater flexibility and speed.

*Source: Player Core.*
