**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Tengu|main:tengu]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Long-Nosed Form|main:long_nosed_form]]

---

You take on the imposing form of a large, winged spirit. Once per day, as part of using [[Long-Nosed Form|main:long_nosed_form]], you also gain the benefits of 4th-rank [[Enlarge|main:enlarge]] and [[Fly|main:fly_spell]]. This lasts for 5 minutes or until you shift out of your Long-Nosed Form, whichever happens first.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
