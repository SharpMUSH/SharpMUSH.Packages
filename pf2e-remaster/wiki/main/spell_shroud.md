**Class Feat 14** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Concentrate, [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]], Spellshape

---

Your spell shrouds you in a billowing cloud. If your next action is to Cast a Spell that targets you, you surround yourself in a 15-foot emanation of dense magical mist that lasts until the start of your next turn. All creatures within the cloud become [[Concealed|main:concealed]], and all creatures outside the cloud are concealed to creatures within it.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
