**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]

---

You call upon the creatures of nature to come to your aid. You can spend 10 minutes in concert with nature to replace a spell you've prepared in one of your druid spell slots with a [[Summon Animal|main:summon_animal]] or [[Summon Plant or Fungus|main:summon_plant_or_fungus]] spell of the same rank.

*Source: Player Core.*
