**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Athletics|main:athletics]]

---

Your panache allows you to perform incredible feats: climbing, swimming, and leaping far beyond your normal capacity. While you have [[Panache|main:panache]], you gain the following benefits.

- You gain climb and swim Speeds equal to half your land Speed.
- The DCs of your [[High Jumps|main:high_jump]] and [[Long Jumps|main:long_jump]] decrease by 10. This doesn't combine with other abilities that reduce those DCs.
- The distance you can move with a vertical [[Leap|main:leap]] increases to 5 feet. Your distance for a horizontal Leap increases to 15 feet if your Speed is at least 15 feet, or to 20 feet if your Speed is at least 30 feet.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
