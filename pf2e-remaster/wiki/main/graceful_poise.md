**Class Feat 16** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Stance  
**Prerequisites:** [[Double Slice|main:double_slice]]

---

With the right positioning, your off-hand weapon can strike like a scorpion's stinger. While you are in this stance, if you make your second Strike from [[Double Slice|main:double_slice]] with an agile weapon, Double Slice counts as one attack when calculating your multiple attack penalty.

*Source: Player Core.*
