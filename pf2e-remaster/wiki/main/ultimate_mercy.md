**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Mercy|main:mercy]]

---

Your mercy transcends the bounds of life and death. Add ultimate mercy to the types of mercy you can provide. When you use [[Mercy|main:mercy]], you can target a creature that died since your last turn to return it to life. The target returns to life with 1 Hit Point and becomes [[Wounded 1|main:wounded]]. You can't return the target to life if it died from [[Disintegrate|main:disintegrate]] or a death effect. The creature gains the other benefits of [[Lay on Hands|main:lay_on_hands]] after it returns to life.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
