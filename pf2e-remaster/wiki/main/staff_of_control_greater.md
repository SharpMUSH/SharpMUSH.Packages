**Item 10**  
**Traits:** Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 900 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

An array of dazzling gemstones lines the twisting head of the golden staff. While wielding the staff, you gain a +1 status bonus to Diplomacy checks to make a [[Request|main:request]].

**Activate** Cast a Spell

---

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Daze|main:daze]]
- **1st** [[Charm|main:charm]], [[Command|main:command]]
- **2nd** [[Laughing Fit|main:laughing_fit]], [[Stupefy|main:stupefy]]
- **3rd** [[Enthrall|main:enthrall]]
- **4th** [[Charm|main:charm]], [[Confusion|main:confusion]], [[Suggestion|main:suggestion]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.

*Source: GM Core.*
