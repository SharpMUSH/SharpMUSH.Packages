Every check has one of four outcomes, found by comparing its total with the DC:

| Result | Total |
|---|---|
| Critical success | DC + 10 or more |
| Success | DC or more |
| Failure | below the DC |
| Critical failure | DC - 10 or less |

Then the die itself can shift the result one step:

- A natural 20 (the d20 shows 20) makes the result one step better.
- A natural 1 makes it one step worse.

If an effect lists no critical success, a critical success does what a success does. Likewise, with no critical failure listed, a critical failure does what a failure does.

*Source: Player Core.*
