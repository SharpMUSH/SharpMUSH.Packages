Every check has one of four outcomes, found by comparing its total with the DC:

| Result | Total |
|---|---|
| Critical success | DC + 10 or more |
| Success | DC through DC + 9 |
| Failure | DC - 9 through DC - 1 |
| Critical failure | DC - 10 or less |

For example, a total of 28 against DC 18 is a critical success; 27 is a success. A total of 9 is a failure; 8 is a critical failure. These are the degrees **before** adjusting for the die itself.

Then the die itself can shift the result one step:

- A natural 20 (the d20 shows 20) makes the result one step better.
- A natural 1 makes it one step worse.

Critical success is the best degree and critical failure is the worst; the die cannot shift beyond them. Apply a natural 20 or natural 1 before any ability that changes the degree.

If an effect lists no critical success, a critical success does what a success does. Likewise, with no critical failure listed, a critical failure does what a failure does.

*Source: Player Core, p. 401; [Degree of Success](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2286).*
