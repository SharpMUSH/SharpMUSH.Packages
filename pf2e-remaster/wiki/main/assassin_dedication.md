**Archetype Feat 2**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Dedication  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Deception|main:deception]]; trained in [[Stealth|main:stealth]]

---

You've trained to assassinate your foes, and you do so with tenacity and precision. You gain the [[Mark for Death|main:mark_for_death]] activity.
