**Item 15**  
**Traits:** Magical, Spirit  
**Price:** 6000 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto a weapon

---

*Astral* weapons command powerful spiritual energy from the Astral Plane. This rune has the same effects as a *[[Ghost Touch|main:ghost_touch]]* rune, plus Strikes with it deal an extra 1d6 spirit damage. If used to attack a creature that's possessing another creature, this weapon deals no damage to the possessed creature. On a critical hit against a creature possessing another creature, the possessing creature must succeed at a DC 36 Will save or be expelled and unable to possess a creature for 1d4 rounds.

Spirit damage dealt by this weapon ignores the target's spirit resistance.

*Source: GM Core.*
