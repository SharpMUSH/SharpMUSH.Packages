**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Summon  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (3 actions)  
**Range:** 60 feet  
**Duration:** sustained

---

You summon a Phantasmal Minion. The minion is roughly the shape of a humanoid. You can choose to have it be [[Invisible|main:invisible]] or have an ephemeral appearance, but it's obviously a magical effect, not a real creature.

*Source: Player Core.*
