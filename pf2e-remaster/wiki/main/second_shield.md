**Archetype Feat 6** (free action)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Viking Dedication|main:viking_dedication]]

---

**Trigger** Your [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]] causes your shield to break or be destroyed.

---

You're used to your shield breaking in the middle of battle, and you're prepared to use a backup or any convenient nearby object to defend yourself.

You can [[Interact|main:interact]] to draw a shield on your person or an unattended shield within your reach. If there is an object within your reach that could serve as an improvised shield-for example, a table or chair-you can Interact to draw it with this feat. The GM determines if something can be used as an improvised shield.

Your new shield isn't raised until you use the [[Raise a Shield|main:raise_a_shield]] action, as normal.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
