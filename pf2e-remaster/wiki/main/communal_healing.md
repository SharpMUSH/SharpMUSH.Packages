**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Healing, Vitality

---

You're an exceptional conduit for vitality and, as you channel it through you, you can divert some to heal yourself or another creature. When you cast the [[Heal|main:heal]] spell to heal a single creature, choose another willing living creature within range of your *heal* to regain Hit Points equal to the *heal* spell's rank.

*Source: Player Core.*
