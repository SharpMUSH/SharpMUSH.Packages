**Item 6**  
**Traits:** Magical, Staff, Two-Hand d8  
**Price:** 230 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Simple | Club | 1d4 bludgeoning | 1 | — | — |

---

This wooden staff is remarkably sturdy and unyielding. While wielding the staff, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to your DC to avoid being shoved or tripped.

**Activate** Cast a Spell

---

**Effect** You expend a number of charges from the staff to cast a spell from its list.

- **Cantrip** [[Shield|main:shield]]
- **1st** [[Alarm|main:alarm]], [[Mystic Armor|main:mystic_armor]]
- **2nd** [[Dispel Magic|main:dispel_magic]], [[Environmental Endurance|main:environmental_endurance]], [[Resist Energy|main:resist_energy]]

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of all listed ranks of all listed spells.

*Source: GM Core.*
