**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Kholo|main:kholo]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Laughing Kholo|main:laughing_kholo]]

---

Your laugher echoes in the minds of your enemies. You can [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] creatures up to 60 feet away. Additionally, whenever you successfully Demoralize a creature, it takes 3d8 mental damage (or 6d8 mental damage on a critical success).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
