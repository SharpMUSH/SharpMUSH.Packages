**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

While exploring, when you are leading and allies are [[Following the Expert|main:follow_the_expert]], instead of granting a +2 circumstance bonus in the applicable skill, you grant a +3 circumstance bonus if you're an expert and a +4 circumstance bonus if you're a master.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
