**Ancestry Feat 1** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Tripkee|main:tripkee]]  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

You release a haunting croak that reminds those around you of their fears. Attempt an Intimidation check to [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] a creature within 30 feet; you do not take a penalty to this check for not speaking a language. If the check is successful, the target cannot reduce their frightened condition below 1 for 1 round.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
