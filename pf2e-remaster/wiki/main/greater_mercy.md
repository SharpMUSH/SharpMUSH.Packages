**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Mercy|main:mercy]]

---

Your faith enhances your ability to remove conditions. Add the following options to the list of conditions you can counteract for any type of mercy you can grant.

- **Mercy of the Body** [[Drained|main:drained]], [[Slowed|main:slowed]]; if you're 16th level, add [[Stunned|main:stunned]]
- **Mercy of Grace** [[Immobilized|main:immobilized]], [[Restrained|main:restrained]], [[Slowed|main:slowed]]; if you're 12th level, add [[Petrified|main:petrified]]; if you're 16th level, add [[Stunned|main:stunned]]
- **Mercy of the Mind** [[Confused|main:confused]], [[Controlled|main:controlled]], [[Slowed|main:slowed]]; if you're 16th level, add [[Doomed|main:doomed]] and [[Stunned|main:stunned]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
