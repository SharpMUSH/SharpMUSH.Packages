**Archetype Skill Feat 6**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Diplomacy|main:diplomacy]]; [[Treat Condition|main:treat_condition]]

---

You provide emotional and spiritual care. Add [[Frightened|main:frightened]], [[Stupefied|main:stupefied]], and [[Stunned|main:stunned]] to the list of conditions you can reduce with [[Treat Condition|main:treat_condition]]. If the stunned condition has a duration instead of a value, you can't use Treat Condition to reduce it.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
