Use your class's advancement table whenever you gain a level. A list of feats is a selection aid; read the selected entry's prerequisites and access rules.

## Ordinary advancement checklist

At 1,000 XP, gain a level and subtract 1,000; carry the remainder forward. Add the class's HP for the new level. Apply its advancement row, choose granted feats and skill increases, update spells if applicable, then recalculate the sheet. Trained-or-better proficiency includes level, so its bonus rises by 1 even if its rank stays unchanged. Untrained proficiency remains zero. Recheck level-dependent abilities and any attribute changes, including their effects on previous levels' HP when Constitution rises.

## Worked update: the dwarf fighter reaches level 2

Start with the exact [[Dwarf Fighter Example|main:dwarf_fighter_example]]. Suppose it reaches **1,080 XP**. Write **level 2 and 80 XP**. Add 10 + Constitution 3 = 13 maximum HP: **23 → 36**. This updates maximum HP; it does not say every current injury or condition disappears.

The [[Fighter|main:fighter]] level-2 row grants a fighter feat and a skill feat. Select [[Dueling Parry|main:dueling_parry]] (Fighter feat 2) and [[Hefty Hauler|main:hefty_hauler]] (skill feat 1). Hefty Hauler requires trained Athletics, already present. Its General and Skill traits allow it in the skill-feat slot. Dueling Parry has a use requirement, not a prerequisite preventing this fighter from selecting it: wield one single-handed melee weapon and hold nothing else. It cannot be used with the steel shield held. Put the shield away, accounting for detaching it first, before using this option. See [[Equipment Basics|main:equipment_basics]].

Using Dueling Parry costs one action, grants +2 circumstance AC until the next turn begins, and lasts only while its hand requirements remain met. At level 2 that makes AC **21** while applicable. It does not grant Shield Block without an eligible raised shield.

No new ancestry feat, general-feat slot, skill increase or attribute boosts arrive at Fighter level 2. Do not promote Athletics to expert. Keep all six attributes, earlier feats, Reactive Strike and Shield Block. Trained bonuses change from +3 to +4; expert bonuses from +5 to +6.

| Sheet field | Level 1 | Level 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum HP | 23 | 36 |
| AC in chain mail | 18 | 19 |
| AC with raised steel shield | 20 | 21 |
| Longsword and drawn clan dagger attacks | +9 | +10 |
| Perception | +7 | +8 |
| Fortitude / Reflex / Will | +8 / +6 / +5 | +9 / +7 / +6 |
| Fighter class DC | 17 | 18 |
| Athletics | +7 | +8 |
| Crafting / Warfare Lore | +3 / +3 | +4 / +4 |
| Intimidation | +2 | +3 |
| Medicine / Survival | +5 / +5 | +6 / +6 |

Untrained skills, including noisy-chain-mail Stealth −1, do not increase. Speed stays 20 feet. Longsword damage remains 1d8+4, or 2d8+4 with Vicious Swing; clan dagger damage stays 1d4+4. No item bonus or striking rune appears from leveling alone.

Hefty Hauler raises the encumbered threshold **9 → 11 Bulk** and maximum **14 → 16 Bulk**. The starting load still counts as 4 Bulk with two light items, if nothing was spent, consumed or acquired. Copy the actual current inventory, coins and shield HP from play; level 2 does not replenish them. There is no spellcasting progression for this selection.

## Worked update: the dwarf wizard reaches level 2

Start with the exact [[Dwarf Wizard Example|main:dwarf_wizard_example]] and the book in [[Wizard Learning Guide|main:wizard_learning_guide]]. Suppose it reaches **1,080 XP**: write **level 2 and 80 XP**. Add Wizard 6 + Constitution 1 = 7 maximum HP: **17 → 24**. Current injuries, conditions, coins and supplies carry forward; leveling is not healing or daily preparation.

Wizard's level-2 row grants one Wizard feat and one skill feat. Choose [[Conceal Spell|main:conceal_spell]] (class feat 2) and [[Assurance|main:assurance]] in Arcana (skill feat 1, requires training in the chosen skill). Arcana is already trained. Conceal Spell costs **one action** and affects the next Cast a Spell action; it is not a passive bonus to attacks or defenses. Read its subtle-trait limits: visible spell effects can remain apparent. A one-action Conceal Spell followed immediately by a two-action spell uses all three actions.

At level 2, Arcana's trained proficiency is +4. Assurance can replace an Arcana roll with **10 + 4 = 14**; it excludes Intelligence and all other bonuses, penalties and modifiers. The ordinary rolled Arcana modifier is still Intelligence 4 + proficiency 4 = **+8**. Assurance is not a +14 modifier or a bonus to the ordinary roll.

No new ancestry feat, general-feat slot, skill increase or attribute boosts arrive at Wizard level 2. Keep the six attributes and existing ranks. Trained bonuses become +4; expert becomes +6.

| Sheet field | Level 1 | Level 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum HP | 17 | 24 |
| AC, unarmored before temporary spells | 15 | 16 |
| Perception | +4 | +5 |
| Fortitude / Reflex / Will | +4 / +5 / +6 | +5 / +6 / +7 |
| Wizard class DC / spell DC | 17 / 17 | 18 / 18 |
| Spell attack | +7 | +8 |
| Drawn clan dagger attack | +4 | +5 |
| Arcana / Crafting / Occultism / Society / Warfare Lore | +7 each | +8 each |
| Medicine | +4 | +5 |
| Intimidation | +3 | +4 |
| Stealth / Thievery | +5 / +5 | +6 / +6 |

Untrained skills do not increase. Dagger damage remains 1d4+1, Speed 20 feet and Bulk limits 6/11. Ordinary Fortitude/Reflex DCs become 15/16, or **17/18** against Rock Dwarf's listed maneuvers. Conceal Spell and Assurance add no passive AC, damage, attribute or proficiency-rank increase.

### Update the book, then prepare a level-2 day

The [[Wizard Spellcasting|main:wizard_spellcasting]] table gives **five ordinary cantrips and three ordinary rank-1 slots** at level 2. Battle Magic adds its separate curriculum cantrip and rank-1 slot. No rank-2 slots arrive yet; cantrips and the initial school focus spell remain rank 1.

Add **two arcane rank-1 book spells**, [[Breathe Fire|main:breathe_fire]] and [[Sure Strike|main:sure_strike]]. Their entries list arcane and no uncommon trait. Neither was in the level-1 book. This makes **11 book cantrips and nine rank-1 book spells**. Breathe Fire happens to be in the curriculum, but is added here using one of the ordinary two-spell level-up choices. There is no additional new-rank curriculum book grant at this level.

After resting and making the next allowed daily preparations, choose this complete day:

| Resource | Level-2 preparation | Starting state |
|---|---|---|
| Five ordinary cantrips | Ignition, Electric Arc, Detect Magic, Light, Telekinetic Hand | Prepared and reusable |
| Extra curriculum cantrip | Shield | Prepared; follow its own restrictions |
| Ordinary rank-1 slot A | Force Barrage | Ready |
| Ordinary rank-1 slot B | Sure Strike | Ready |
| Ordinary rank-1 slot C | Fear | Ready |
| Separate curriculum rank-1 slot D | Mystic Armor | Ready |
| Focus pool | Force Bolt | 1/1 point |
| Arcane Bond | Owned spellbook on your person | Drain Bonded Item unused |

Breathe Fire is in the book but unprepared today. Slot D cannot hold Fear or Sure Strike: neither is in this school's curriculum. Preparing Mystic Armor does not activate it; AC is **16** until an actual effect changes it. Spell Substitution can replace a still-ready preparation under its own ten-minute procedure, with curriculum restrictions preserved; it grants no fifth slot. Use [[Spell Preparation|main:spell_preparation]] to track this level-2 day's resources.

The scripted casts in [[Wizard Learning Guide|main:wizard_learning_guide]] are a **separate level-1 exercise**. To replay them, use a separate practice sheet reset to that guide's original level-1 statistics and preparations: spell attack +7, DC17, ordinary slots A and B both Force Barrage, curriculum slot C Mystic Armor, focus 1/1 and unused Drain Bonded Item. Its supplied dice and printed outcomes apply to that setup. Do not carry slots or spent resources from the level-2 day above into that exercise, or substitute DC18 while keeping its level-1 save outcomes. This practice reset does not change your actual level-2 character or replenish its resources.

This local worked progression stops at level 2. For later levels, follow the [full Wizard advancement and Spells per Day table (external primary reference)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=39), which excludes curriculum additions.
