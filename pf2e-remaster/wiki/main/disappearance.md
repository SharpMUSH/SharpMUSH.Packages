**Spell 8**  
**Traits:** Illusion, Manipulate, Subtle  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 10 minutes

---

You shroud a creature from others' senses. The target becomes [[Invisible|main:invisible]], but not merely to vision. The invisibility granted by *disappearance* applies to all precise senses an observer might have. It's still possible for a creature to find the target by Seeking using various senses, looking for disturbed dust, hearing gaps in the sound spectrum, or finding some other way to discover the presence of a creature that is otherwise undetectable.

*Source: Player Core.*
