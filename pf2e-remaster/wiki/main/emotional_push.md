**Class Feat 2** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Bard|main:bard]], Concentrate

---

**Trigger** An enemy you're aware of fails its save against an emotion spell.

---

You prepare to take advantage of your enemy's sudden change in emotions. The target is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against your next attack before the end of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core.*
