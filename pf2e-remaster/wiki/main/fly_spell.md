**Spell 4**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 5 minutes

---

The target can soar through the air, gaining a fly Speed equal to its Speed or 20 feet, whichever is greater.

---

**Heightened (7th)** The duration increases to 1 hour.
