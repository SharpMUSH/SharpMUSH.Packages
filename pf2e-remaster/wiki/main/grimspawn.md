**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** Lineage, [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]

---

Your lineage traces back to a daemon, one of the manifestations of horrific forms of death that devour souls within their foul home of Abaddon. As a result, you cling tenaciously to the last shreds of your own life force. You gain the [[Diehard|main:diehard]] general feat.

*Source: Player Core.*
