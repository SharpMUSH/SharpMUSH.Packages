**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Oracle Dedication|main:oracle_dedication]]

---

You gain your mystery's initial revelation spell. If you don't have one, you gain a focus pool of 1 Focus Point. You can Refocus by reconciling the conflicting nature of your mystery, which also reduces your Cursebound value by 1.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
