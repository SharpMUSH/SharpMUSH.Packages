**Class Feat 10** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Rage

---

**Requirements** Your last action was a successful Strike

---

The weight of your swing drives your enemy back. You push the enemy back 5 feet, with the effects of a successful [[Shove|main:shove]]. You can follow the enemy as normal for a successful Shove.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
