**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Form Control|main:form_control]]; Strength +4

---

Thanks to magic and muscle memory, you can stay in your alternate shapes indefinitely. When you use [[Form Control|main:form_control]], instead of lasting 1 hour, [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form]] duration is unlimited (you can still Dismiss it).

*Source: Player Core.*
