**Focus 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Focus, Manipulate  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 living creature  
**Defense:** Fortitude

---

Huge amounts of food and drink fill the target. It receives a full meal's worth of nourishment and must attempt a Fortitude save. A target sickened by this spell takes a –10-foot status penalty to its Speed until it's no longer sickened.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]], but if it spends an action to end the condition, it succeeds automatically.

**Failure** The target is sickened 1.

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Sickened 2|main:sickened]].

*Source: Player Core.*
