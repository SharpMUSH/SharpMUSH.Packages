**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Guardian

---

You can use your shield to fend off explosions and the like. When you [[Raise your Shield|main:raise_a_shield]], you gain your shield's circumstance bonus to Reflex saves.

---

**Special** If you have the [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]] reaction, damage you take as a result of a Reflex save can trigger that reaction, even if the damage isn't physical damage.

*Source: Player Core.*
