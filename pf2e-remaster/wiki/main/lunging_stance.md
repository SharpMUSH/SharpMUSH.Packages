**Class Feat 12** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Stance  
**Prerequisites:** [[Lunge|main:lunge]]; [[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike_feat]]

---

**Requirements** You are wielding a melee weapon.

---

Your body coiled to strike, you can lash out at distant enemies. While you are in this stance, you can use [[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike]] against a creature that is outside your reach but within the reach you would have with a [[Lunge|main:lunge]]. If you do, you increase your range with the Strike by 5 feet.

*Source: Player Core.*
