**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Catfolk|main:catfolk]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Cat's Luck|main:cats_luck]]

---

You are luckier even than most catfolk. You can trigger [[Cat's Luck|main:cats_luck]] when you fail or critically fail a Fortitude saving throw, Will saving throw, Acrobatics check, or Athletics check in addition to Reflex saving throws. You still can use Cat's Luck only once per day.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
