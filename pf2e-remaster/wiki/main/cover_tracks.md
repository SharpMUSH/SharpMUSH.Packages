**Action**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Exploration, Move

---

You cover your tracks, moving up to half your travel Speed, using the Travel Speed rules. You don't need to attempt a Survival check to cover your tracks, but anyone tracking you must succeed at a Survival check against your Survival DC if it is higher than the normal DC to [[Track|main:track]].

In some cases, you might Cover Tracks in an encounter. In this case, Cover Tracks is a single action and doesn't have the exploration trait.

*Source: Player Core.*
