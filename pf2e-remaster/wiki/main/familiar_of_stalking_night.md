**Familiar Ability**

---

Your familiar is dark of fur or feather, and light seems to disappear into it. When you Cast or Sustain a hex, and your familiar is adjacent to an enemy to which it's [[Concealed|main:concealed]], [[Hidden|main:hidden]], or undetected, the enemy becomes [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]].

*Source: Player Core.*
