**Item 17**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Talisman  
**Price:** 2400 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** affixed to a weapon

---

**Activate** (free action) (concentrate)

**Trigger** Your Strike damages a target

---

Made from a treated sliver of cold iron, this talisman allows you to counteract magical effects. When you activate the *dispelling sliver*, it attempts to counteract a single spell active on the target (counteract modifier +29), with the effects of an 8th-rank [[Dispel Magic|main:dispel_magic]] spell. If you activate the talisman on a successful [[Dispelling Slice|main:dispelling_slice]], the talisman attempts to counteract all spells active on the target, in addition to your attempt from Dispelling Slice.

*Source: GM Core.*
