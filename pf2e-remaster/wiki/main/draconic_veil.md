**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Dragonblood|main:dragonblood]]

---

Like some dragons, your forebear had the supernatural ability to change their shape to walk among humanoids. You have learned how to mimic this ability. You can cast [[Humanoid Form|main:humanoid_form]] as a 5th-rank innate spell once per day, except that the duration is increased to 1 hour. The spell's tradition matches your draconic exemplar's.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
