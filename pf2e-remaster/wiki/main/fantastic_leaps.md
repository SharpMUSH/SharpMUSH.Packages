**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Tripkee|main:tripkee]]

---

Your leaps seem to defy gravity as you soar through the air in a single bound. When Leaping, increase the maximum distance you can [[Leap|main:leap]] horizontally by 10 feet, and the maximum distance you can Leap vertically by 5 feet.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
