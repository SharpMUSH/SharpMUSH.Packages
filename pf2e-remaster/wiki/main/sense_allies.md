**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Human|main:human]]

---

Like many humans raised in a close-knit community, you have always been strongly attuned to the presence of others. Willing allies that you are aware of within 60 feet that would otherwise be [[Undetected|main:undetected]] by you are instead [[Hidden|main:hidden]] from you. The flat check for you to target willing allies within 60 feet that are hidden from you is DC 5 flat instead of 11.

*Source: Player Core.*
