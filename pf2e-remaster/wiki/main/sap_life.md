**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Healing

---

You draw the life force out of your enemies. When you cast a [[Harm|main:harm]] spell and damage at least one living creature, you regain Hit Points equal to the spell rank of your *harm* spell. If you aren't a living creature, you gain no benefit from this feat.

*Source: Player Core.*
