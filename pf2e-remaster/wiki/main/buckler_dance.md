**Class Feat 10** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Stance, [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

**Requirements** You are wielding a [[Buckler|main:buckler]].

---

You spin your buckler defensively toward every attack. You constantly have your buckler raised as if you'd used [[Raise a Shield|main:raise_a_shield]], as long as you meet that action's requirements.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
