**Archetype Feat 8** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Archetype, Concentrate, Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** [[Blessed One Dedication|main:blessed_one_dedication]]; ability to cast spells from spell slots; [[Mercy|main:mercy]]  
**Frequency:** once per 10 minutes

---

**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

---

When you focus your magic on an ally, you can remove harmful conditions.

If your next action is to [[Cast a Spell|main:cast_a_spell]] from a spell slot, and that spell targets only a single ally, you can also attempt to remove a harmful condition from that ally. The condition must be one that could be removed by your Mercy feat, including those granted by later feats such as [[Greater Mercy|main:greater_mercy]].

Attempt a counteract check based on the spell's DC and level. This effect is in addition to the normal effects of your spell.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
