**Item 4**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Plant, Potion, Primal, Wood  
**Price:** 15 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

After you drink this bitter draft, your skin thickens like bark. You gain the effects of a 2nd-rank [[Oaken Resilience|main:oaken_resilience]] for 10 minutes.
