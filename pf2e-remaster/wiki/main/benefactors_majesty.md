**Ancestry Feat 17** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Healing, [[Kobold|main:kobold]], Visual  
**Frequency:** once per day

---

**Frequency** once per day

---

You tap into the grandeur of your warren's benefactor. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to your level, which last for 1 minute. In addition, you can immediately attempt a flat check to remove each type of persistent damage you have. Finally, until the start of your next turn, any creature targeting you with a harmful attack, spell, or ability much first succeed at a DC 11 flat or the action is disrupted as the creature avoids setting eyes upon your majesty.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
