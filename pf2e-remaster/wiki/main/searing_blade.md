**Traits:** Fire, Magical, Versatile P  
**Price:** 2800 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Martial | Sword | 1d8 slashing | 1 | — | — |

---

This *+2 greater striking [[Flaming|main:flaming]] longsword* has an ornate brass hilt and a blade shaped like stylized flames. When wielded, the blade projects illumination resembling shimmering firelight, emitting dim light in a 10-foot radius.

**Activate—Shoot Fire** (2 actions) (concentrate, manipulate)

---

**Effect** You cast the [[Ignition|main:ignition]] cantrip from the sword as a 7th-rank arcane spell, using your melee attack modifier with *searing blade* as your spell attack modifier.

*Source: GM Core.*
