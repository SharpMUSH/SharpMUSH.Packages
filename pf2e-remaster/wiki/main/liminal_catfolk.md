**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Catfolk|main:catfolk]]

---

You've inherited a closeness to the far corners of the world, where the boundaries between dimensions grow thin. You can cast the [[Detect Magic|main:detect_magic_spell]] cantrip as an occult innate spell at will. A cantrip is heightened to half your level rounded up. You also gain a +1 circumstance bonus to Occultism checks to [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] about creatures that originated on planes other than the Universe.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
