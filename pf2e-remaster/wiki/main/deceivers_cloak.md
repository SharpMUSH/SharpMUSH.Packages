**Focus 3**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Hex, Illusion, Manipulate, [[Witch|main:witch]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Duration:** 1 hour

---

Your patron drapes a cloak of illusion about you, making you appear as another creature of the same body shape with roughly similar height and weight as yourself. This has the effects of a 3rd-rank [[Illusory Disguise|main:illusory_disguise]].

---

**Heightened (6th)** You can appear as any creature of the same size, even with a completely different body shape.

*Source: Player Core.*
