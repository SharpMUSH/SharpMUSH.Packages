**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Illusion, Manipulate, Mental, Nonlethal  
**Traditions:** [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

Illusory pain wracks the target, dealing 2d4 mental damage and 1d4 persistent mental damage. The target must attempt a Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target takes full initial damage but no persistent damage, and the spell ends immediately.

**Failure** The target takes full initial and persistent damage, and the target is [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]]. If the target recovers from being Sickened, the persistent damage ends and the spell ends.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the target is [[Sickened 2|main:sickened]].

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d4 and the persistent damage by 1d4.
