**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Barreling Charge|main:barreling_charge]]

---

You trample your foes as you charge past them. When you use [[Barreling Charge|main:barreling_charge]] and successfully move through a creature's space, that creature takes bludgeoning damage equal to your Strength modifier. If you critically succeed, the creature takes double this amount of damage and becomes [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the end of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
