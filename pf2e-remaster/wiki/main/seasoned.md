**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in Alcohol Lore, Cooking Lore, or [[Crafting|main:crafting]]

---

You've mastered the preparation of many types of food and drink. You gain a +1 circumstance bonus to checks to [[Craft|main:craft]] food and drink, including elixirs if you have [[Alchemical Crafting|main:alchemical_crafting]] and potions if you have [[Magical Crafting|main:magical_crafting]]. If you are a master in one of the prerequisite skills, this bonus increases to +2.

*Source: Player Core.*
