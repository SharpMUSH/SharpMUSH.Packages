**Class Feat 10** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Concentrate, [[Monk|main:monk]]

---

You focus your strength into a blow powerful enough to push an enemy away from you. Make an unarmed Strike. If you hit, attempt an Athletics check to [[Shove|main:shove]] the target. This attack uses the same multiple attack penalty as your Strike, and doesn't count toward your multiple attack penalty.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
