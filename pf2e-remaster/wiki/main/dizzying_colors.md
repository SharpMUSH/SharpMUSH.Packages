**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Illusion, Incapacitation, Manipulate, Visual  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Area:** 15-foot cone  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** 1 or more rounds (see below)

---

You unleash a swirling multitude of colors that overwhelms creatures based on their Will saves.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is [[Dazzled|main:dazzled]] for 1 round.

**Failure** The creature is [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]], [[Blinded|main:blinded]] for 1 round, and dazzled for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The creature is stunned for 1 round and blinded for 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core.*
