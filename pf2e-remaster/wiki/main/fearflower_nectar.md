**Item 4**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Injury, Poison  
**Price:** 16 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

The nectar of a night-blooming desert flower attacks a victim's central nervous system and causes feelings of panic.

---

**Activate** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Saving Throw** DC 21 Fortitude

**Maximum Duration** 6 rounds

**Stage 1** 1d6 poison damage and [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]] (1 round)

**Stage 2** 1d6 poison damage and [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]] (1 round)

**Stage 3** 1d6 poison damage and [[Frightened 3|main:frightened]] (1 round)

*Source: Player Core 2.*
