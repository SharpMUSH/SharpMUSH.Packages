**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Lizardfolk|main:lizardfolk]]

---

Your iruxi unarmed attacks channel the spiritual energy of your ancestors. Any unarmed attacks you acquire from the lizardfolk ancestry, lizardfolk heritages, or lizardfolk ancestry feats gain the effects of a *ghost touch* property rune.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
