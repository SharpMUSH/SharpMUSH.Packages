**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Ratfolk|main:ratfolk]]

---

You gain the [[Prescient Consumable|main:prescient_consumable]] and [[Prescient Planner|main:prescient_planner]] feats, except that you always withdraw the objects from your cheek pouches. This means the object you retrieve must fit in your cheek pouches; this is generally an item of negligible Bulk unless you also have the [[Cheek Pouches|main:cheek_pouches]] feat.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
