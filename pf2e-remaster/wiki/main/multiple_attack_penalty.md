Use this with [[Encounter Mode|main:encounter_mode]] and [[Degrees of Success|main:degrees_of_success]]. Count attacks made during your turn, including failed attacks, skill actions with the Attack trait (such as [[Trip|main:trip]]), spell attacks, and [[Strike|main:strike]]. Spending an action that has no Attack trait does not increase the count.

## Apply the penalty to the current attack

| Attack this turn | Ordinary penalty | Agile weapon penalty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | 0 | 0 |
| Second | −5 | −4 |
| Third or later | −10 | −8 |

Choose the column for the weapon used now. A [[Longsword|main:longsword]] is not agile. Start a fresh count each turn. The penalty normally applies only on your turn.

*Source: [Multiple Attack Penalty](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2289), Player Core, p. 402.*

## Read exceptions literally

[[Vicious Swing|main:vicious_swing]] costs two actions, makes one Strike, and counts as two attacks. As your opening attack, its Strike has no penalty; a subsequent longsword Strike takes −10. At level 1 its hit deals 2d8+4 for the [[Dwarf Fighter Example|main:dwarf_fighter_example]].

*Source: [Vicious Swing](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=4775), Player Core, p. 141.*

[[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike]] explicitly neither suffers nor increases this penalty. [[Ready|main:ready]] is different: a readied attack retains the penalty you had when you Readied it, even off your turn.

*Sources: [Reactive Strike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2256), Player Core, p. 138; [Ready](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2299), Player Core, p. 417.*

Practice Trip followed by a longsword attack in [[First Encounter|main:first_encounter]].
