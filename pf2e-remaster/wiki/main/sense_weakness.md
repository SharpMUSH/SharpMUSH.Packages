**Action** (reaction)

---

**Trigger** You attempt a melee Strike against your opponent, but haven't rolled yet.

**Requirements** You're in a duel, you're trained in Perception, and you rolled a Perception check for initiative this round.

---

You pick a precise moment to attack, giving you an edge. Your opponent is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the attack. If your opponent is using Deception for initiative when this ability is used, they are instead off-guard until the start of their next turn.

*Source: GM Core.*
