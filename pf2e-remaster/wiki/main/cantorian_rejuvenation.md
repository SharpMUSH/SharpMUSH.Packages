**Ancestry Feat 9** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Healing, [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]], Vitality  
**Frequency:** once per day

---

**Frequency** once per day

---

The life-giving energy that flows in your blood revitalizes you. You recover 4d6 (6d6 at level 15 or higher) Hit Points and gain 10 temporary Hit Points for 1 minute. At 15th level, you instead recover 6d6 HP and gain 15 temporary HP.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
