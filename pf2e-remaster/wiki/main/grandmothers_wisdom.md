**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Kholo|main:kholo]]

---

You carry the bones of your ancestors with you, and you can ask them for counsel. You can cast [[Augury|main:augury]] twice per day as a 2nd-rank occult innate spell.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
