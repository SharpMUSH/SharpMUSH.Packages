**Archetype Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Deception|main:deception]]; [[Vigilante Dedication|main:vigilante_dedication]]

---

Your disparate identities allow you to defeat magic that detects lies. As long as what you say is true from the point of view of your current identity, you can deceive effects like [[Ring of Truth|main:ring_of_truth]] that reveal lies.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
