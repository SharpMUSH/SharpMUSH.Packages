**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Dhampir|main:dhampir]]

---

You can tap into the magic that runs through your blood. You gain [[Animal Form|main:animal_form]] (wolf only, using the statistics for a canine) and [[Mist|main:mist]] as 2nd-rank divine innate spells. You can cast each of these spells once per day.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
