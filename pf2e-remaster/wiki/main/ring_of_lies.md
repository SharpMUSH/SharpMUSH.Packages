**Item 10**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 850 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** worn

---

This plain silver ring has an almost oily sheen. While wearing the ring, you gain a +2 item bonus to Deception checks.

**Activate—Sweeten Lies** (2 actions) (manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** Snapping your fingers on the hand that wears the ring causes the ring to cast [[Honeyed Words|main:honeyed_words]] on you with no visual manifestations of a spell being cast.

*Source: GM Core.*
