**Spell 2**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Detection, Manipulate, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Area:** 30-foot emanation  
**Duration:** 10 minutes

---

You open your mind to the metaphysical, enabling you to sense nearby spirits. Even if you aren't [[Searching|main:search]], you get a check to find haunts and spirits in the area. You gain a +1 status bonus to the following checks regarding haunts or spirits: Perception checks to [[Seek|main:seek]], attempts to Recall Knowledge, skill checks to determine the reason for their existence, and skill checks to disable a haunt. You also gain a +1 status bonus to AC and saving throws against haunts and spirits.

---

**Heightened (6th)** The spell's duration lasts until the next time you make your daily preparations.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
