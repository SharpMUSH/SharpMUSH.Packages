**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in Lore

---

You carefully safeguard your professional endeavors to prevent disaster. When you use Lore to [[Earn Income|main:earn_income]], if you roll a critical failure, you instead get a failure. If you're an expert in Lore, you gain twice as much income from a failed check to Earn Income, unless it was originally a critical failure.

*Source: Player Core.*
