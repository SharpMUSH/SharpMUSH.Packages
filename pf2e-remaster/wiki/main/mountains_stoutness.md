**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Dwarf|main:dwarf]]

---

Your hardiness lets you withstand more punishment than most. Increase your maximum Hit Points by your level. When you have the [[Dying 1|main:dying]] condition, the DC of your recovery checks is equal to 9 + your dying value (instead of 10 + your dying value).

If you also have the [[Toughness|main:toughness]] feat, the Hit Points gained from it and this feat are cumulative, and the DC of your recovery checks is equal to 6 + your dying value.

*Source: Player Core.*
