**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Dwarf|main:dwarf]]

---

You understand the intricacies of stonework. You become trained in Crafting. If you're already trained in Crafting, you instead gain the [[Specialty Crafting|main:specialty_crafting]] skill feat for stonemasonry.

You also gain a +2 circumstance bonus to Perception checks to notice unusual stonework. This bonus applies to checks to discover mechanical traps made of stone or [[Hidden|main:hidden]] within stone.

If you aren't using the [[Seek|main:seek]] action or searching, the GM automatically rolls a secret check for you to notice unusual stonework anyway. This check doesn't gain your normal circumstance bonus.

*Source: Player Core.*
