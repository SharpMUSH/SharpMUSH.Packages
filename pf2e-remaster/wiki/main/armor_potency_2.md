**Item 11**  
**Traits:** Magical  
**Price:** 1060 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto armor

---

Magic wards deflect attacks. Increase the armor's item bonus to AC by 2. The armor can be etched with two property runes.

You can upgrade the *armor potency* rune already etched on a suit of armor using the normal process for upgrading items and runes.

**Craft Requirements** You are an master in Crafting.

*Source: GM Core.*
