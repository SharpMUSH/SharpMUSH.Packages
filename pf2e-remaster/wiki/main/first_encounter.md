This original exercise uses the [[Dwarf Fighter Example|main:dwarf_fighter_example]]. Read [[Encounter Mode|main:encounter_mode]], [[Multiple Attack Penalty|main:multiple_attack_penalty]] and [[Degrees of Success|main:degrees_of_success]] first.

## Set the table

You have **23 HP, AC 18, Perception +7, Athletics +7, longsword +9 (1d8+4 slashing)**. Your chain mail is worn; sword and steel shield occupy one hand each. Your single minor healing potion and granted clan dagger are worn and readily accessible, neither held nor stowed in a backpack; you retain 2 gp. No container Bulk discount or storage benefit is assumed. No toolkit or other supplies appear for free.

The illustrative opponent is a Medium creature, already adjacent within your melee reach: **30 HP, AC 18, Reflex DC 17, melee attack +7 dealing a fixed 6 slashing damage on a normal hit**. These are exercise inputs, not a published monster or a complete adventure. Assume ordinary level ground, no resistances, other modifiers, or enemy reactions. Both creatures stay adjacent.

Initiative: your die 12 + Perception 7 = **19**; the GM supplies the opponent's **14**. You act first.

## Round 1: free a hand, Trip, Strike

At your turn's start, gain 3 actions and 1 reaction.

1. **Release the sword (free action).** It falls at your feet. Your shield remains held and unraised; your sword hand is now free.
2. **Trip (action 1).** Roll 12 + Athletics 7 = **19 vs Reflex DC 17: success**. The opponent becomes [[Prone|main:prone]], hence [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]]: its AC against your melee attack is now **16**. This was your first attack, with no penalty; ordinary Trip success deals no damage.
3. **Interact (action 2)** to pick up your sword with the free hand.
4. **Strike (action 3).** This is attack number two: 14 + 9 − 5 = **18 vs AC 16: success**. Damage die 4 + 4 = **8**; the opponent has **22 HP**.

You finish holding sword and shield, with AC 18: no action remained to Raise a Shield.

The opponent spends one action to [[Stand|main:stand]], ending prone and restoring its AC to 18. Standing can trigger your [[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike]]; you choose to decline it in this exercise. Its next action is its first Strike: 15 + 7 = **22 vs your AC 18: success**, dealing 6 damage. You have **17 HP**. It ends its turn without using its third action. You cannot Shield Block: your shield was not raised.

## Round 2: Vicious Swing and shield defense

Your new turn refreshes your three actions and one reaction; attack counting starts again.

1. **Vicious Swing (actions 1–2).** Roll 11 + 9 = **20 vs AC 18: success**. Roll weapon dice 3 and 5, then add Strength 4: **12 damage**, leaving the opponent at **10 HP**. The activity counts as two attacks. A later longsword Strike this turn would take −10.
2. **Raise a Shield (action 3).** Your held steel shield raises your AC to **20** until your next turn starts.

The opponent's first Strike is 14 + 7 = **21 vs AC 20: success**, for 6 slashing damage. Spend your available reaction on [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]]: Hardness 5 prevents 5; **you and the shield each take 1**. You have **16 HP**, and the shield has **19 of 20 HP** (above its broken threshold 10). The opponent ends its turn early.

Your reaction is now spent: no Reactive Strike until it refreshes. At your next turn's start, the raised-shield AC bonus expires; refresh your actions and reaction. To regain AC 20, spend another action Raising the still-held shield. Continue taking turns if the fight continues; these two rounds do not defeat the opponent.

## If you fall later

Use [[Dying and Recovery|main:dying_and_recovery]] for defeat, an ally feeding your purchased potion, and the increased danger of a second defeat. An ally's healer's toolkit remains their equipment.
