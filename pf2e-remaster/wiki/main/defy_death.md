**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Orc|main:orc]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Orc Ferocity|main:orc_ferocity]]

---

You're exceptionally difficult to kill. The DC of your recovery checks is equal to 9 + your dying value, or 8 + your dying value if you have the [[Toughness|main:toughness]] general feat. In addition, whenever someone returns you to life using magic that would normally leave you debilitated for a week (such as [[Raise Dead|main:raise_dead]] or the resurrect ritual), you don't suffer that condition.

*Source: Player Core.*
