**Action** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Finisher

---

You make an incredibly graceful attack, piercing your foe's defenses. Make a Strike with the following failure effect.

---

**Failure** You deal half your [[Precise Strike|main:precise_strike]] damage to the target. This damage type is that of the weapon or unarmed attack you used for the Strike.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
