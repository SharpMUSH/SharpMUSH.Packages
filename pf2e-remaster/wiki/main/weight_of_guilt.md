**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** redemption cause

---

Guilt clouds the minds of those who ignore your [[Glimpse of Redemption|main:glimpse_of_redemption]]. Instead of making the triggering creature enfeebled 2, you can make it [[Stupefied 2|main:stupefied]] for the same duration.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
