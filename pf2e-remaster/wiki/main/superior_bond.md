**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** [[Wizard|main:wizard]]  
**Prerequisites:** arcane bond

---

When you draw upon your bonded item, you can leave a bit of energy within it for later use. You can use [[Drain Bonded Item|main:drain_bonded_item]] one additional time per day, but only to cast a spell 2 or more ranks lower than your highest-rank wizard spell slot.

*Source: Player Core.*
