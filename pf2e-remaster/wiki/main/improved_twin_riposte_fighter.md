**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Twin Riposte|main:twin_riposte]]

---

Your weapons are a blur, blocking and biting at your foes. You can use [[Twin Riposte|main:twin_riposte]] even if you aren't benefiting from [[Twin Parry|main:twin_parry]]. At the start of each of your turns, you gain an additional reaction that you can use only to make a Twin Riposte.

*Source: Player Core.*
