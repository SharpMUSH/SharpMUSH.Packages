**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Vicious Swing|main:vicious_swing]]

---

You've learned to maintain your balance even when swinging furiously. When you make a [[Vicious Swing|main:vicious_swing]] with a melee weapon you're wielding in two hands, it counts as one attack toward your multiple attack penalty instead of two.

*Source: Player Core.*
