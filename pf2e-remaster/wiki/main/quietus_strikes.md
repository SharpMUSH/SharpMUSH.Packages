**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Duskwalker|main:duskwalker]]

---

Your connection to the realm of the dead empowers you to bring death to all forms of life and unlife, exploiting their weaknesses. All your weapon and unarmed Strikes are magical and deal 1 additional void damage to living creatures and 1 additional vitality damage to undead. The extra damage increases to 2 if the Strike has a *+3 potency rune*.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
