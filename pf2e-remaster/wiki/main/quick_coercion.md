**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

You can bully others with just a few choice implications. You can [[Coerce|main:coerce]] a creature after 1 round of conversation instead of 1 minute. You still can't Coerce a creature in the midst of combat, or without engaging in a conversation.

*Source: Player Core.*
