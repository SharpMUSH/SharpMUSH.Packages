**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]

---

You can call the elements to you. You can spend 10 minutes in concert with nature to replace a spell you've prepared in one of your druid spell slots with a [[Summon Elemental|main:summon_elemental]] spell of the same rank.

*Source: Player Core.*
