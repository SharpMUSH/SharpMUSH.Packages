**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

You capitalize on your enemies' fear to slip past their defenses. Any creature that has the [[Frightened|main:frightened]] condition is also [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against your attacks.
