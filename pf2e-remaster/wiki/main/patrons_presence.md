**Action** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Aura  
**Frequency:** once per hour

---

**Frequency** once per hour

---

**Effect** A palpable weight extends from your familiar in a 15-foot emanation. Enemies who enter or start their turn within the aura must succeed at a Will save against your spell DC or become [[Stupefied 2|main:stupefied]] as long as they remain within the aura, or [[Stupefied 3|main:stupefied]] on a critical failure. The aura lasts until the end of your next turn, but the familiar can Sustain it up to 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core.*
