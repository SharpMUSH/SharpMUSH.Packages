**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]], [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** shield block

---

You can bring your shield into place with hardly a thought. At the start of each of your turns, you gain an additional reaction that you can use only to [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]].

*Source: Player Core.*
