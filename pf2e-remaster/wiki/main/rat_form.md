**Ancestry Feat 9** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Polymorph, Primal, [[Ratfolk|main:ratfolk]]

---

You can transform into an innocuous-looking rat. You gain the effects of a 1st-rank [[Pest Form|main:pest_form]] spell, but you must assume the battle form of a Tiny rat.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
