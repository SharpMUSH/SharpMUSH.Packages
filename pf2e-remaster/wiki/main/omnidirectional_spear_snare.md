**Item 16**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Mechanical, Snare, Trap  
**Price:** 1500 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

As soon as a creature enters the snare's square, the snare unleashes wickedly powerful spears at the creature from all directions, dealing 15d8 piercing damage (basic DC 37 Reflex save).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
