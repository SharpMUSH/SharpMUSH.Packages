**Focus 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Focus, Manipulate, Water  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Area:** 30-foot burst  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

You call forth a torrential downpour, which extinguishes non-magical flames. Creatures in the area are [[Concealed|main:concealed]] and gain fire resistance 10. Creatures outside the area are Concealed to those inside the area. If a creature with weakness to water ends its turn in the area, the downpour triggers its weakness.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The fire resistance increases by 2.

*Source: Player Core.*
