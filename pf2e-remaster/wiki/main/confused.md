You have lost your wits and attack wildly.

- You are [[Off-Guard]], you treat no one as your ally (they may still treat you as theirs), and you can't Delay, Ready or use reactions.
- You spend all your actions on Strikes or offensive cantrips. The GM may have you use other actions that serve an attack, such as drawing a weapon or moving into reach.
- The GM picks your targets at random. With no other target, you attack yourself: an automatic hit, never a critical hit.
- If you can't attack or cast, you babble and waste your actions.

**Ends:** each time an attack or spell damages you, you may attempt a DC 11 flat check to end the condition.

*Source: Player Core, p. 442.*
