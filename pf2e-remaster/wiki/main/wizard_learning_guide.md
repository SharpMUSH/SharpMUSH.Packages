Use this route to learn a level-1 Wizard's spell choices, daily preparations and casting. Start with [[Wizard|main:wizard]], [[Wizard Spellcasting|main:wizard_spellcasting]], [[Arcane School|main:arcane_school]], [[Arcane Thesis|main:arcane_thesis]] and [[Arcane Bond|main:arcane_bond]]. Then read [[Spell Preparation|main:spell_preparation]], [[Cast a Spell|main:cast_a_spell]] and [[Basic Saving Throws|main:basic_saving_throws]].

Use the completed [[Dwarf Wizard Example|main:dwarf_wizard_example]] alongside this casting lesson. It supplies the ancestry/background/class choices, boost ledger, languages, skills, inventory, HP and defenses. The [[Character Creation Guide|main:character_creation_guide]] explains how to make other choices. The [[Dwarf Fighter Example|main:dwarf_fighter_example]] is a separate build; its statistics and purchases do not belong to this Wizard.

## Set the example's inputs

The [[Dwarf Wizard Example|main:dwarf_wizard_example]] derives Intelligence +4 and trained spellcasting at level 1. It chooses the [[School of Battle Magic|main:school_of_battle_magic]] and the [[Spell Substitution|main:spell_substitution]] thesis. Use that sheet here. No extra spell grants, scrolls, staff charges or magic items are added for the exercise.

Trained [[Proficiency|main:proficiency_ranks]] at level 1 contributes 1 + 2 = +3. With no other modifiers:

- Spell attack modifier: Intelligence +4 + proficiency +3 = **+7**.
- Spell DC: 10 + Intelligence +4 + proficiency +3 = **17**.

These values come from the completed character's attribute and proficiency ledger. Its ordinary AC is 15 before any spell is cast; preparing Mystic Armor in slot C does not activate it. Do not add Intelligence to the damage of the spells used below.

## Record the complete spellbook

Wizard Spellcasting gives a free spellbook worth 10 sp or less, ten arcane cantrips and five rank-1 arcane spells. Arcane School additionally gives one curriculum cantrip and two curriculum rank-1 spells. This example chooses distinct entries for every grant: **11 book cantrips and seven book rank-1 spells**.

Every entry below is common and includes the arcane tradition, so these choices require no special uncommon-spell access. All are rank 1 at this level. Open their full entries for their other traditions and requirements.

| Book grant | All chosen entries |
|---|---|
| Ten ordinary cantrips | [[Ignition|main:ignition]], [[Electric Arc|main:electric_arc]], [[Detect Magic|main:detect_magic_spell]], [[Figment|main:figment]], [[Light|main:light]], [[Message|main:message]], [[Prestidigitation|main:prestidigitation]], [[Read Aura|main:read_aura]], [[Telekinetic Hand|main:telekinetic_hand]], [[Telekinetic Projectile|main:telekinetic_projectile]] |
| One additional curriculum cantrip | [[Shield|main:shield]] |
| Five ordinary rank-1 spells | [[Fear|main:fear]], [[Fleet Step|main:fleet_step]], [[Grease|main:grease]], [[Runic Weapon|main:runic_weapon]], [[Jump|main:jump]] |
| Two additional curriculum rank-1 spells | [[Force Barrage|main:force_barrage]], [[Mystic Armor|main:mystic_armor]] |

Battle Magic's other rank-1 curriculum option, [[Breathe Fire|main:breathe_fire]], has not been selected for this book. Curriculum membership does not place every listed spell in your book.

Separately, the school grants its initial focus spell, [[Force Bolt|main:force_bolt]]. It is uncommon, but the school directly grants it; it is not one of the ordinary common book choices. It is an arcane focus spell for this Wizard. Record **one known focus spell, a focus pool maximum of 1, and 1/1 Focus Point** after daily preparations. Do not add the advanced school spell [[Energy Absorption|main:energy_absorption]] at level 1.

## Make today's preparations

After resting, spend the roughly one-hour daily preparation studying the book. This can happen only after rest and once per day. See [[Spell Preparation|main:spell_preparation]] for the full sequence.

| Resource | Today's choice | Starting state |
|---|---|---|
| Five ordinary prepared cantrips | Ignition, Electric Arc, Detect Magic, Light, Telekinetic Hand | All prepared and reusable |
| Extra curriculum cantrip | Shield | Prepared; follow its own restrictions if used |
| Ordinary rank-1 slot A | Force Barrage | Ready: one casting |
| Ordinary rank-1 slot B | Force Barrage | Ready: a separate casting |
| Extra curriculum rank-1 slot C | Mystic Armor | Ready: one casting |
| Focus pool | Force Bolt | 1/1 point |
| Arcane Bond | The owned free spellbook, designated as bonded item and kept on your person | Drain Bonded Item unused: once per day |

The two ordinary slots deliberately repeat the same spell. Force Barrage can fill them even though it was added through the curriculum. Slot C must hold a curriculum spell; it cannot hold Fear. Today's six cantrips are preparations, not six expendable ranked slots.

The unprepared book cantrips are Figment, Message, Prestidigitation, Read Aura and Telekinetic Projectile. The unprepared rank-1 spells are Fear, Fleet Step, Grease, Runic Weapon and Jump. They remain in the book but cannot be cast through today's preparations. Spell Substitution is selected but is not used in the sequence below.

## Work the casts

Read [[Encounter Mode|main:encounter_mode]] for three actions per turn and [[Degrees of Success|main:degrees_of_success]] for checks. These are practice turns within one preparation day, with no intervening resource changes. Each turn starts with three actions. Targets and dice are supplied teaching inputs, not monster statistics or a full combat simulation.

For every cast below, assume a visible creature 20 feet away, clear line of effect, available speech and suitable gestures. Assume no cover, concealment, conditions, hostile reactions, additional modifiers, immunities, resistances or weaknesses. The bonded book stays on your person. No unlisted supplies or spell costs are required by these casts.

### Turn 1: spell attack

Spend **two actions** to cast prepared [[Ignition|main:ignition]] at a target with supplied AC18. It is your first attack this turn, so no [[Multiple Attack Penalty|main:multiple_attack_penalty]] applies.

Roll the supplied d20 of 12: 12 + 7 = **19 against AC18**, a success. Supplied damage dice of 2 and 3 on 2d4 deal **5 fire damage**. This is the ranged version, not its optional melee version. You have one action left, unused in this exercise.

Ignition stays prepared. No slot or Focus Point was spent.

### Turn 2: basic save, then focus spell

Spend **two actions** to cast [[Electric Arc|main:electric_arc]] at just one creature. Supply its Reflex modifier as +7 and its d20 as 10: 10 + 7 = **17 against your DC17**, a target success. Supplied 2d4 damage rolls of 2 and 3 total 5; a successful [[basic save|main:basic_saving_throws]] halves that to **2 electricity damage**, rounding down. You do not roll a spell attack.

Use the remaining **one action** to cast [[Force Bolt|main:force_bolt]] at a visible creature in range. Spend **1 Focus Point**. It automatically hits; a supplied d4 of 3 gives 3 + 1 = **4 force damage**. No actions remain. Focus is now **0/1**; ordinary and curriculum slots are unchanged.

### Turn 3: spend slot A

Cast [[Force Barrage|main:force_barrage]] from ordinary slot A using **two actions**, producing two shards. Send both to one visible creature in range. Supplied d4 rolls of 2 and 3 give (2 + 1) + (3 + 1) = **7 force damage**. The shards automatically hit; combine their damage for that target before damage defenses.

Mark **A expended**. B and C stay ready. One action remains unused. Force Barrage is still in the book.

### Turn 4: spend the repeated preparation

Cast Force Barrage from **slot B** using two actions. Supplied d4 rolls of 1 and 4 give (1 + 1) + (4 + 1) = **7 force damage** to one visible target. Mark **B expended**. One action remains unused.

Both ordinary slots are now spent. C still holds Mystic Armor; it cannot provide Force Barrage without changing that preparation through an applicable rule.

### Turn 5: use Arcane Bond

Your bonded book is on your person. Use [[Drain Bonded Item|main:drain_bonded_item]] as a **free action**, then cast Force Barrage **during this same turn**. It qualifies because you prepared it today and already cast it. Use three actions for three shards to one visible target: supplied d4s of 1, 2 and 3 give (1 + 1) + (2 + 1) + (3 + 1) = **9 force damage**.

No actions remain. This extra cast spends no slot. A and B remain expended, C still holds Mystic Armor, focus remains 0/1, and today's Drain Bonded Item use is spent. Arcane Bond did not refill a slot or remove the casting action cost.

## Check the remaining resources

“Ready” means that numbered preparation can still be cast. The cantrips remain prepared and the whole book remains unchanged throughout this table.

| After | Slot A | Slot B | Curriculum slot C | Focus | Drain Bonded Item |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily preparations | Ready | Ready | Mystic Armor ready | 1/1 | Unused |
| Ignition | Ready | Ready | Mystic Armor ready | 1/1 | Unused |
| Electric Arc and Force Bolt | Ready | Ready | Mystic Armor ready | 0/1 | Unused |
| First ordinary Force Barrage | Expended | Ready | Mystic Armor ready | 0/1 | Unused |
| Second ordinary Force Barrage | Expended | Expended | Mystic Armor ready | 0/1 | Unused |
| Bond's extra Force Barrage | Expended | Expended | Mystic Armor ready | 0/1 | Used |
| Ten-minute Refocus | Expended | Expended | Mystic Armor ready | 1/1 | Used |
| Next allowed daily preparations, choosing the same spells | Ready | Ready | Mystic Armor ready | 1/1 | Unused |

After the encounter, [[Refocus|main:refocus]] for ten minutes studying the book or conducting arcane research. This restores the one Focus Point; it does not restore A, B or Drain Bonded Item. You still have Mystic Armor ready, your six prepared cantrips, and the refilled focus point.

Spell Substitution could separately take ten minutes to replace the still-ready C's Mystic Armor with Force Barrage: both are curriculum spells in the book. **That optional swap was not performed.** It would not refill A or B, reset daily abilities or count as daily preparations. If interrupted, the original preparation remains.

After resting and making the next allowed daily preparations, regain slots, choose the same legal preparations or different ones, refill focus and reset the daily bond use. Designate the owned book again. You still have the same 11 cantrips and seven rank-1 spells recorded in it.
