**Spell 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Illusion, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** 10 minutes  
**Range:** 500 feet  
**Area:** 50-foot burst  
**Duration:** until your next daily preparations

---

You create an illusion that causes natural terrain to look, sound, feel, and smell like a different kind of terrain. This doesn't disguise any structures or creatures in the area.

Any creature that touches the illusion or uses the [[Seek|main:seek]] action to examine it can attempt to disbelieve your illusion.

---

**Heightened (5th)** Your image can also disguise structures or create illusory structures (but still doesn't disguise creatures).
