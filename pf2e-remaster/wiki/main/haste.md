**Spell 3**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

Magic empowers the target to act faster. It gains the [[Quickened|main:quickened]] condition and can use the extra action each round only for Strike and Stride actions.

---

**Heightened (7th)** You can target up to 6 creatures.
