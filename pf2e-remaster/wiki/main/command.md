**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Auditory, Concentrate, Linguistic, Manipulate, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** until the end of the target's next turn

---

You shout a command that's hard to ignore. You can command the target to approach you, run away (as if it had the [[Fleeing|main:fleeing]] condition), release what it's holding, drop [[Prone|main:prone]], or stand in place. It can't [[Delay|main:delay]] or take any reactions until it has obeyed your command. The effects depend on the target's Will save.

---

**Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Failure** For the first action on its next turn, the creature must use a single action to do as you command.

**Critical Failure** The target must use all its actions on its next turn to obey your command.

---

**Heightened (5th)** You can target up to 10 creatures.
