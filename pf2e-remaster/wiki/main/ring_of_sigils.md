**Item 1**  
**Traits:** Arcane, Invested  
**Price:** 20 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** worn

---

This silver band is carved with the personal sigils of different individuals, adding one to represent you when you invest it. The ring allows you to cast [[Sigil|main:sigil]] as an arcane innate cantrip.

---

**Activate—Track Sigil** (1 action) (concentrate, detection)

**Frequency** once per 10 minutes

**Effect** You detect the general direction toward the most recent *sigil* you created using the ring. This activation fails if the *sigil* is more than 5 miles away or if there's lead or running water between you and the *sigil*.

*Source: GM Core.*
