**Spell 2**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]], [[Primal|category:primal_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Fortitude

---

The target loses hearing; it must attempt a Fortitude save. The target is then temporarily immune for 1 minute.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Deafened|main:deafened]] for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is Deafened for 10 minutes.

**Critical Failure** The target is Deafened permanently.

*Source: Player Core.*
