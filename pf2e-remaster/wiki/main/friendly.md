An attitude: how a creature feels about one particular character. Only supernatural effects, such as spells, can give this condition to a player character.

- A friendly creature likes the character and is likely to agree to Requests that are simple, safe and cheap to fulfil.
- If the character or an ally uses a hostile action against it, its attitude worsens by a step the GM chooses to fit the action.

*Source: Player Core, p. 444.*
