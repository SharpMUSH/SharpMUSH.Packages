**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Tengu|main:tengu]]

---

You're always on the lookout for supplies and valuables. You gain a +2 circumstance bonus to locate objects (including secret doors and hazards) you search for within 30 feet with a [[Seek|main:seek]] action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
