**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Monk|main:monk]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Qi Spells|main:qi_spells]]

---

You learn a mystical stance that transforms your qi into sticky smoke that shrouds your limbs. You gain the [[Clinging Shadows Stance|main:clinging_shadows_stance]] qi spell. While entering the stance is a qi spell, the shadow grasp attacks it grants are not, so you can use them as often as you like while in the stance.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
