**Item 5**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Potion  
**Price:** 21 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

For 1 minute after you drink this fizzy potion, whenever you Leap, you gain the effect of the 1st-rank [[Jump|main:jump]] spell.
