**Class Feat 20**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]

---

Your skill with weapons bends the laws of reality, allowing you to continuously attack with speed that would normally require magical assistance. You're permanently [[Quickened|main:quickened]]. You can use your extra action only to Strike.

*Source: Player Core.*
