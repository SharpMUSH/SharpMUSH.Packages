**Spell 6**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate, Spirit  
**Traditions:** [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** basic Fortitude

---

You concentrate ethereal energy and attack a creature's spirit, dealing 16d6 spirit damage with a basic Fortitude save.

---

**Heightened (+1)** The damage increases by 2d6.
