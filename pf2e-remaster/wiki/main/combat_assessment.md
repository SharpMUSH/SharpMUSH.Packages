**Class Feat 1** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Commander, [[Fighter|main:fighter]]

---

You make a telegraphed attack to learn about your foe. Make a melee Strike. On a hit, you can immediately attempt a check to [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] about the target. On a critical hit, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the check to Recall Knowledge. The target is temporarily immune to Combat Assessment for 1 day.

*Source: Player Core.*
