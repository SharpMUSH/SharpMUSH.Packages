**Item 3**  
**Price:** 50 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** other

---

This kit of bandages, herbs, and suturing tools is necessary for Medicine checks to [[Administer First Aid|main:administer_first_aid]], [[Treat Disease|main:treat_disease]], [[Treat Poison|main:treat_poison]], or [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]]. Expanded healer's toolkits provide a +1 item bonus to such checks. If you wear your healer's toolkit, you can draw and replace its tools as part of the action that uses them.

*Source: Player Core.*
