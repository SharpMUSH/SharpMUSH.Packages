**Archetype Feat 10**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Archaeologist Dedication|main:archaeologist_dedication]]; [[Magical Scholastics|main:magical_scholastics]]

---

You broaden your magical studies, allowing you to find the right path, detect hidden objects, and conceal those objects from unscrupulous rivals.

You can cast [[Augury|main:augury]], [[Locate|main:locate]], and [[Veil of Privacy|main:veil_of_privacy]] as occult innate spells, each once per day. You can cast this *veil of privacy* spell only on an object, and it's automatically heightened to half your level rounded up.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
