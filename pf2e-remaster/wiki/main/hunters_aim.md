**Class Feat 2** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Concentrate, [[Ranger|main:ranger]]

---

When you focus on aiming, your attack becomes particularly accurate. Make a ranged weapon Strike against your hunted prey. On this Strike, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus to the attack roll and ignore your prey's [[Concealed|main:concealed]] condition and any lesser cover.

*Source: Player Core.*
