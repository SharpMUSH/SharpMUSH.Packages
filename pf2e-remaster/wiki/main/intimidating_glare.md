**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

You can [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] with a mere glare. When you do, Demoralize loses the auditory trait and gains the visual trait, and you don't take a penalty if the creature doesn't understand your language.

*Source: Player Core.*
