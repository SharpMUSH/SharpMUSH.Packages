**Class Feat 20**  
**Traits:** [[Witch|main:witch]]

---

You embody the link to your patron so completely that you can cast your hexes in rapid succession. You are no longer limited to casting only a single hex each turn. In addition, when you cast [[Cackle|main:cackle]] to sustain a hex, you gain the effects of Sustain for all your active hexes that have a duration of sustained.

*Source: Player Core.*
