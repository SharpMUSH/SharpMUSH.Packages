**Item 8**  
**Traits:** Cold, Magical  
**Price:** 500 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto a weapon

---

This weapon is empowered with freezing ice. It deals an additional 1d6 cold damage on a successful Strike. On a critical hit, the target is also [[Slowed 1|main:slowed]] until the end of your next turn unless it succeeds at a DC 24 Fortitude save.

*Source: GM Core.*
