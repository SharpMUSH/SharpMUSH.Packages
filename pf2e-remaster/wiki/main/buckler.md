This very small shield is a favorite of duelists and quick, lightly armored warriors. It's typically made of steel and strapped to your forearm. You can [[Raise a Shield|main:raise_a_shield]] with your buckler as long as you have that hand free or are holding a light object that's not a weapon in that hand.

*Source: Player Core.*
