**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Halfling|main:halfling]]

---

Whether keeping your balance or climbing, your feet easily find purchase. If you roll a success on an Acrobatics check to [[Balance|main:balance]] or an Athletics check to [[Climb|main:climb]], you get a critical success instead. You're not [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] when you attempt to Balance or Climb.

*Source: Player Core.*
