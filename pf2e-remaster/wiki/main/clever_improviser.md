**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Human|main:human]]

---

You've learned how to handle situations when you're out of your depth. You gain the [[Untrained Improvisation|main:untrained_improvisation]] general feat. In addition, you can attempt skill actions that normally require you to be trained, even if you are untrained.

*Source: Player Core.*
