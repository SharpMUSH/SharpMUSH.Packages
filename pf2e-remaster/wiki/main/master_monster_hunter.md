**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Nature|main:nature]]; [[Monster Hunter|main:monster_hunter]]

---

You have a nearly encyclopedic knowledge of all creatures of the world. You can use Nature to [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] to identify any creature. In addition, you gain the benefits of [[Monster Hunter|main:monster_hunter]] (and [[Monster Warden|main:monster_warden]], if you have it) on a success as well as a critical success.

*Source: Player Core.*
