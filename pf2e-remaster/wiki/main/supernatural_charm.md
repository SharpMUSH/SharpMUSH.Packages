**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Aiuvarin|main:aiuvarin]]

---

The elven magic in your blood manifests as a force you can use to become more appealing or alluring. You can cast 1st-rank [[Charm|main:charm]] as an arcane innate spell once per day.
