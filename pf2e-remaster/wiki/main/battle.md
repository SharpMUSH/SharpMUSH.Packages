**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Oracle|main:oracle]]

---

Warlike forces fill you with physical might and tactical knowledge, aiming to have you uphold the glory of combat, fight to improve the world, prepare against the necessity of conflict, or endure the inevitability of war.

**Granted Spells**

- **cantrip:** [[Shield|main:shield]]
- **1st:** [[Sure Strike|main:sure_strike]]
- **2nd:** [[Telekinetic Maneuver|main:telekinetic_maneuver]]
- **4th:** [[Weapon Storm|main:weapon_storm]]

**Revelation Spells**

- **initial:** [[Weapon Trance|main:weapon_trance]]
- **advanced:** [[Battlefield Persistence|main:battlefield_persistence]]
- **greater:** [[Revel in Retribution|main:revel_in_retribution]]

**Related Domains** destruction, might, protection, zeal

**Mystery Skill** Athletics
