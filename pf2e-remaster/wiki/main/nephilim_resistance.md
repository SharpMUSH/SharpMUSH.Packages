**Ancestry Feat 5**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]

---

You resist energy like your extraplanar predecessors. Choose one of the following energy damage types: acid, cold, electricity, fire, or sonic. You gain resistance 5 to that damage type.

The damage type typically matches an extraplanar entity associated with your bloodline. For instance, an [[Angelkin|main:angelkin]] might choose resistance to cold, while a [[Hellspawn|main:hellspawn]] might choose resistance to fire.

*Source: Player Core.*
