Another creature holds you in place.

- You are [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] and [[Immobilized|main:immobilized]].
- A manipulate action needs a DC 5 flat check, rolled after you spend the action and before its effects; on a failure the action is lost.
- [[Restrained|main:restrained]] overrides grabbed.

*Source: Player Core, p. 444.*
