**Class Feat 18**  
**Traits:** [[Bard|main:bard]], Sonic  
**Prerequisites:** courageous anthem

---

Your [[Courageous Anthem|main:courageous_anthem]] lets loose a discordant shriek that imbues your allies' attacks with powerful sonic reverberations. While your allies are affected by your *courageous anthem*, their weapon Strikes and unarmed attacks deal an additional 1d6 sonic damage.

*Source: Player Core.*
