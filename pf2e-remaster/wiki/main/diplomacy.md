**Key attribute:** Charisma

Persuading and negotiating, gathering rumors and changing how people feel about you.

Your Diplomacy modifier is your Charisma modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Diplomacy (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Gather Information|main:gather_information]]
- [[Make an Impression|main:make_an_impression]]
- [[Request|main:request]]

This skill has no actions that need training.

Skill feats that need Diplomacy:

- **Level 1:** [[Bargain Hunter|main:bargain_hunter]], [[Bon Mot|main:bon_mot]], [[Group Impression|main:group_impression]], [[Hobnobber|main:hobnobber]], [[No Cause for Alarm|main:no_cause_for_alarm]]
- **Level 2:** [[Discreet Inquiry|main:discreet_inquiry]], [[Eyes of the City|main:eyes_of_the_city]], [[Glad-Hand|main:glad_hand]]
- **Level 6:** [[Holistic Care|main:holistic_care]]
- **Level 7:** [[Evangelize|main:evangelize]], [[Shameless Request|main:shameless_request]]
- **Level 15:** [[Legendary Negotiation|main:legendary_negotiation]]
