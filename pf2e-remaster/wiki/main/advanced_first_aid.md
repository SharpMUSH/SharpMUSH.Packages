**Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** General, Healing, Manipulate, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Medicine|main:medicine]]

---

You use your medical training to ameliorate sickness or assuage fears. When you use Medicine to [[Administer First Aid|main:administer_first_aid]], instead of Stabilizing a character or Stopping Bleeding, you can reduce an ally's [[Frightened|main:frightened]] or [[Sickened|main:sickened]] condition by 2, or remove either of those conditions entirely on a critical success. You can remove only one condition at a time. The DC for the Medicine check is usually the DC of the effect that caused the condition.

*Source: Player Core.*
