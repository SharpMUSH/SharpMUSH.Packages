**Class Feat 16** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** legendary in [[Deception|main:deception]]

---

You use clever tactics to mislead your foes as you sneak away. You [[Sneak|main:sneak]] while leaving a decoy behind. The decoy acts as the spell [[Mislead|main:mislead]], though you aren't [[Invisible|main:invisible]], just [[Undetected|main:undetected]]. You can continue to concentrate to move your decoy, as with the spell, whether or not you remain [[Hidden|main:hidden]] throughout the duration. Once you use Perfect Distraction, you need to spend 10 minutes to set up another decoy before you can use it again.

*Source: Player Core.*
