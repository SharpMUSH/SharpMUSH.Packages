**Archetype Feat 14**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Dual-Weapon Warrior Dedication|main:dual_weapon_warrior_dedication]]

---

When you lash out with both weapons, you leave no room for the target to escape your attack.

When you use [[Double Slice|main:double_slice]], if you miss with both [[Strikes|main:strike]], choose one of the two weapons and apply the effects of a hit with that weapon.

You can't choose a weapon if your attack roll with that weapon was a critical failure, meaning you still miss entirely if both attack rolls were critical failures.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
