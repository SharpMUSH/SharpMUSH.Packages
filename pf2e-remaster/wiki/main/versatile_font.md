**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]  
**Prerequisites:** harmful font or healing font; deity that allows clerics to have both fonts

---

As you explore your deity's aspects, you move beyond restrictions on healing or harming. You can prepare either [[Harm|main:harm]] or [[Heal|main:heal]] in the spell slots gained from the harmful font or healing font.

*Source: Player Core.*
