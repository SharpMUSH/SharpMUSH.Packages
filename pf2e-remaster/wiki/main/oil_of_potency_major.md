**Item 19**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Oil  
**Price:** 8000 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

When you apply this thick, viscous oil to a weapon or suit of armor, that item immediately becomes magically potent. If the item is a weapon, it temporarily becomes a *+3 major striking weapon*, or, if it's armor, it temporarily becomes *+3 major resilient armor*. This lasts for 1 minute.

*Source: GM Core.*
