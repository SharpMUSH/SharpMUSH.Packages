**Action**  
**Traits:** Exploration

---

You attempt a Stealth check to avoid notice while traveling at half speed. If you're Avoiding Notice at the start of an encounter, you usually roll a Stealth check instead of a Perception check both to determine your initiative and to see if the enemies notice you (based on their Perception DCs, as normal for [[Sneak|main:sneak]], regardless of their initiative check results).

*Source: Player Core.*
