**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], [[Investigator|main:investigator]], [[Ranger|main:ranger]], [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** master in Perception

---

**Fighter, Rogue, Investigator** Your battle instincts make you more aware of [[Concealed|main:concealed]] and [[Invisible|main:invisible]] opponents.

**Ranger** Your heightened senses allow you to instinctively detect unseen opponents.

---

You don't need to succeed at a flat check to target concealed creatures. You're not [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] to creatures that are [[Hidden|main:hidden]] from you (unless you're off-guard to them for reasons other than the hidden condition), and you need only a successful DC 5 flat to target a hidden creature.

While you're adjacent to an [[Undetected|main:undetected]] creature of your level or lower, it is instead only hidden from you.

*Source: Player Core.*
