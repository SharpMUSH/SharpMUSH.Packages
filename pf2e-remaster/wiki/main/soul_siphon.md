**Focus 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Focus, Manipulate, [[Oracle|main:oracle]], Void  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 living creature  
**Defense:** Fortitude

---

You siphon off a portion of the target's soul to bolster your own vitality. The target must attempt a Fortitude save or take 1d4 void damage. You gain temporary Hit Points equal to the total amount of HP the target loses, including the HP lost from becoming [[Drained|main:drained]].

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature takes half damage.

**Failure** The creature takes full damage and becomes [[Drained 1|main:drained]].

**Critical Failure** The creature takes double damage and becomes [[Drained 2|main:drained]].

---

**Heightened (+1)** Increase the damage by 1d4.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
