**Class Feat 4** (free action)  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]], [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

**Trigger** You are about to roll a Perception, Stealth, or Survival check for initiative.

---

You visually or audibly warn your allies of danger, granting them each a +1 circumstance bonus to their initiative rolls, or a +2 circumstance bonus if you're using the [[Scout|main:scout_action]] exploration activity. Depending on whether you use gestures or call out, this action gains either the visual or the auditory trait, respectively.

*Source: Player Core.*
