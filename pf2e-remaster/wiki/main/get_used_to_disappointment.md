**Class Feat 12** (free action)  
**Traits:** Bravado, [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]  
**Prerequisites:** expert in [[Intimidation|main:intimidation]]

---

**Requirements** Your turn begins, and one target within 30 feet failed an attack roll or skill check against you on its last turn.

---

You capitalize on an opponent's failure with smug attitude and swagger, reminding them of the gap in skill between you and your opponent. You attempt to [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] the opponent. Regardless of the result, the target becomes temporarily immune for 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
