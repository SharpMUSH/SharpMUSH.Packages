**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Champion|main:champion]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Cruelty|main:cruelty]]

---

The extent of your corruption devastates your foes' minds and reflexes. When you use [[Cruelty|main:cruelty]], you can choose to make the target [[Clumsy|main:clumsy]] or [[Stupefied|main:stupefied]] instead, with the same condition value. Make the choice when you Cast the Spell (before you know the result of the target's save).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
