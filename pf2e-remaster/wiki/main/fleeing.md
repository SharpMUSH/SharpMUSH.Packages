Fear or some other compulsion makes you run.

- On your turn, you spend every action escaping the source of the condition as quickly as you can: moving away, opening doors in your way, and so on.
- The source is usually the effect or creature that gave you the condition, unless the effect names another.
- You can't Delay or Ready.

*Source: Player Core, p. 444.*
