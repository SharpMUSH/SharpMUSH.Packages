**Archetype Feat 12**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wrestler Dedication|main:wrestler_dedication]]

---

Your grasp has a supernatural quality to it, preventing your foes from easily escaping—with or without magical assistance. If a creature you have [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] attempts to use a teleportation spell or effect, it must succeed at a DC 15 flat or the spell fails. If a creature you have grabbed attempts to [[Escape|main:escape]] while under the effect of [[Unfettered Movement|main:unfettered_movement]] or a similar effect, it must succeed at a DC 15 flat or be forced to roll the Escape attempt normally, rather than automatically succeeding.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
