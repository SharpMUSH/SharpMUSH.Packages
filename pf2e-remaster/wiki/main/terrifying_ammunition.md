**Item 6**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Emotion, Fear, Magical, Mental  
**Price:** 50 gp  
**Bulk:** —

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This black-and-gray ammunition is etched with occult symbols and tiny, grinning skulls. When activated terrifying ammunition damages a creature, it fills the creature's mind with visions of their own failures, tragedies, and eventually, their own death. The creature must attempt a DC 20 Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]].

**Failure** The creature is [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]] and can't reduce its frightened value below 1 until it spends an action, which has the concentrate trait, to calm itself down.

**Critical Failure** As failure, but the creature is [[Frightened 2|main:frightened]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
