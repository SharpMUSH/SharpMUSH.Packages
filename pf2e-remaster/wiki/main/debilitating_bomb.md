**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** Additive, [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]

---

You can add a crystalline additive to an alchemical bomb to have it undermine your enemy. Choose [[Dazzled|main:dazzled]], [[Deafened|main:deafened]], [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]], or a –5-foot status penalty to Speeds. If the attack with the bomb hits, in addition to the bomb's normal effects, the target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or take that effect until the start of your next turn. Use your class DC for this saving throw, even if someone else throws the bomb.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
