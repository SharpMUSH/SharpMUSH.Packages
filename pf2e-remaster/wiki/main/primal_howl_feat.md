**Class Feat 10**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** Incredible Companion

---

Your companion can let out a howl laced with your primal magic. It gains the [[Primal Howl|main:primal_howl]] advanced maneuver, in addition to any advanced maneuvers it already knows.

*Source: Player Core.*
