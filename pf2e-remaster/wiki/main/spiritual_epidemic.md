**Spell 8**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Curse, Manipulate, Spirit  
**Traditions:** [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Will  
**Duration:** varies

---

You curse the target, sapping its spirit and leaving a contagious trap in its essence. The target must attempt a Will save. Any creature that casts a divine or occult spell on the target while it's affected is targeted by spiritual epidemic and must also attempt a Will save. The curse continues to spread in this way.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Enfeebled 2|main:enfeebled]] and [[Stupefied 2|main:stupefied]] for 1 round.

**Failure** The target is enfeebled 2 and stupefied 2 for 1 minute and [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]] and [[Stupefied 1|main:stupefied]] permanently.

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Enfeebled 3|main:enfeebled]] and [[Stupefied 3|main:stupefied]] for 1 minute and enfeebled 2 and stupefied 2 permanently.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
