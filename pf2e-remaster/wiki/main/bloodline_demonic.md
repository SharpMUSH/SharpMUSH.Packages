**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]

---

Demons debase all they touch. One of your ancestors fell victim to their corruption, and you're burdened by that sin.

**Tradition** divine

**Bloodline Skills** Intimidation, Religion

**Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip: [[Caustic Blast|main:caustic_blast]]; 1st: [[Fear|main:fear]]; 2nd: [[Enlarge|main:enlarge]]; 3rd: [[Slow|main:slow]]; 4th: [[Divine Wrath|main:divine_wrath]]; 5th: [[Blister|main:blister]]; 6th: [[Disintegrate|main:disintegrate]]; 7th: [[Divine Decree|main:divine_decree]]; 8th: [[Canticle of Everlasting Grief|main:canticle_of_everlasting_grief]]; 9th: [[Implosion|main:implosion]]

**Bloodline Spells** initial: [[Glutton's Jaws|main:gluttons_jaws]]; advanced: [[Swamp of Sloth|main:swamp_of_sloth]]; greater: [[Chthonian Wrath|main:chthonian_wrath]]

**Blood Magic—Corruption of Sin** You mentally broadcast brief glimpses into the darkest desires of the mind. Either a target takes a –1 status penalty to AC for 1 round, or you gain a +2 status bonus to Intimidation checks for 1 round.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
