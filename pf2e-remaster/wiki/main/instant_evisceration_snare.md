**Item 20**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Mechanical, Snare, Trap  
**Price:** 10000 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

When a creature enters the snare's square, the snare releases an unbelievable arsenal of blades at the creature, dealing 18d8 piercing damage (basic DC 42 Reflex save).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
