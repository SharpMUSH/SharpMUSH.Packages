**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]  
**Prerequisites:** scoundrel racket

---

Your [[Feints|main:feint]] are far more distracting than normal. While a creature is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] by your [[Feint|main:feint]], it also takes a –2 circumstance penalty to Perception checks and Reflex saves.
