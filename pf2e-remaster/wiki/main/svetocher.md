**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Dhampir|main:dhampir]], Lineage

---

You were born to a moroi, the most common vampires, and you share their supernatural fortitude and power of persuasion. When you have the [[Drained|main:drained]] condition, calculate the penalty to your Fortitude saves and your Hit Point reduction as though the condition value were 1 lower. You're trained in Diplomacy. If you would automatically be trained in it, you instead become trained in a skill of your choice.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
