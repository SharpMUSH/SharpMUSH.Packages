**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Tengu|main:tengu]]

---

Whether due to a divine blessing or hatching from your egg during a squall, you are resistant to storms. You gain electricity resistance equal to half your level (minimum 1). You automatically succeed at the flat check to target a [[Concealed|main:concealed]] creature if that creature is concealed only by rain or fog.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
