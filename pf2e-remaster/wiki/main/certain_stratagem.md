**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]

---

You know exactly where to strike a foe to target their weak spots. When you make a Strike that you're adding your Intelligence modifier to due to [[Devising a Stratagem|main:devise_a_stratagem]] and miss, you deal half of your [[Strategic Strike|main:strategic_strike]] damage to the target. This damage type is that of the weapon or unarmed attack you used for the Strike.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
