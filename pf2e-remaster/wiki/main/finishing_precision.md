**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Swashbuckler Dedication|main:swashbuckler_dedication]]

---

You've learned how to land daring blows when you have Panache.

You gain the [[Precise Strike|main:precise_strike]] class feature but you deal 1 addition damage on a hit and 1d6 damage on a finisher. This damage doesn't increase as you gain levels. In addition, you gain the [[Basic Finisher|main:basic_finisher]] action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
