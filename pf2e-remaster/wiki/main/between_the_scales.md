**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Kobold|main:kobold]]

---

You've learned to take advantage of slips in your foes' defenses. When you Strike an [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] creature using a melee weapon or unarmed attack that has the agile and finesse traits, it gains the backstabber trait.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
