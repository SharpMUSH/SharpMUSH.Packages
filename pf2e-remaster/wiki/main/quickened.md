You can act faster.

- You gain 1 extra action at the start of each of your turns.
- Many effects limit what that action can be used for. If you are quickened by several effects, the one extra action can be used for anything any of them allows.
- Becoming quickened during your turn doesn't give you the action until your next turn starts.

*Source: Player Core, p. 446.*
