**Spell 2**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Illusion, Manipulate, Visual  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** touch  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Duration:** 1 minute

---

The target's form appears blurry. It becomes [[Concealed|main:concealed]]. As the nature of this effect still leaves the target's location obvious, the target can't use this concealment to Hide or Sneak.

*Source: Player Core.*
