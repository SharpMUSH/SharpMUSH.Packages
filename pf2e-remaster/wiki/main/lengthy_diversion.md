**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Deception|main:deception]]

---

When you critically succeed to [[Create a Diversion|main:create_a_diversion]], you continue to remain [[Hidden|main:hidden]] after the end of your turn. This effect lasts for an amount of time that depends on the diversion and situation, as determined by the GM (minimum 1 additional round).

*Source: Player Core.*
