**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike_feat]]

---

You use your foes' openings to expose even greater weaknesses. When you hit a creature as part of a Reactive Strike, that creature becomes [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Player Core.*
