**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]  
**Class:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]

---

As an investigator, you think of your adventures as cases waiting to be solved. You gain one activity and one reaction you can use to investigate cases: [[Pursue a Lead|main:pursue_a_lead]] and [[Clue In|main:clue_in]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
