**Archetype Feat 8** (2 actions)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wrestler Dedication|main:wrestler_dedication]]

---

You charge, throwing your body at your foe in a vicious tackle. Stride twice or make a [[High Jump|main:high_jump]] or [[Long Jump|main:long_jump]], then attempt to [[Grapple|main:grapple]] or [[Shove|main:shove]]. If you made a High Jump or Long Jump, you can make the [[Grapple|main:grapple]] or [[Shove|main:shove]] at any point in the jump, but you can't travel any farther on the jump after you do.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
