**Class Feat 14**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]]

---

You pour divine energy into a desperate block. When you Shield Block, you can expend a [[Harm|main:harm]] or [[Heal|main:heal]] spell. Roll 1d8 for each rank of the spell, and increase the shield's Hardness by the total for this block.

*Source: Player Core.*
