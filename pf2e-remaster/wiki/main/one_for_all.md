**Class Feat 1** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Auditory, Concentrate, Emotion, Linguistic, Mental, [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Diplomacy|main:diplomacy]]

---

With precisely the right words of encouragement, you bolster an ally's efforts. Designate an ally within 30 feet; this action counts as sufficient preparation to [[Aid|main:aid]] that ally. When you use the Aid reaction to help that ally, you can roll Diplomacy in place of the usual check and the action gains the bravado trait

*Source: Player Core 2.*
