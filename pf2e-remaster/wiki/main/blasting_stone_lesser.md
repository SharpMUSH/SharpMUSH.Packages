**Item 1**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Bomb, Consumable, Sonic, Splash  
**Price:** 3 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

| Category | Group | Damage | Hands | Range | Reload |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Martial | Bomb | 1d4 sonic | 1 | 20 ft. | - |

---

**Activate** (1 action) Strike

---

When this pebble hits a creature or a hard surface, it explodes with a deafening bang. A blasting stone deals 1d4 sonic damage and 1 sonic splash damage, and each creature within 10 feet of the space in which the stone exploded must succeed at a DC 17 Fortitude saving throw with the listed DC or be [[Deafened|main:deafened]] until the end of its next turn.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
