**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Emotion, Fear, Mental

---

Vitality strikes terror in the undead. When you use a [[Heal|main:heal]] spell to damage undead, any undead that fails its saving throw is also [[Frightened 1|main:frightened]]. If it critically failed, the creature also gains the [[Fleeing|main:fleeing]] condition until the start of your next turn. Mindless undead are not immune to this effect due to being mindless.

*Source: Player Core.*
