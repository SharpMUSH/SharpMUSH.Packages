**Archetype Feat 12** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Blessed One Dedication|main:blessed_one_dedication]]

---

**Trigger** An ally within 30 feet would become [[Frightened|main:frightened]], [[Drained|main:drained]], [[Enfeebled|main:enfeebled]], [[Sickened|main:sickened]], or [[Stupefied|main:stupefied]].

---

You exude peace and protection, lessening a harmful condition as it falls upon an ally. Reduce the value of the condition your ally would take by 1 (to a minimum of 0). If the ally takes more than one condition at the same time, choose one to reduce.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
