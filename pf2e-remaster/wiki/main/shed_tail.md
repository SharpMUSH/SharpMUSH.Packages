**Ancestry Feat 5** (reaction)  
**Traits:** [[Lizardfolk|main:lizardfolk]]  
**Prerequisites:** Iruxi Armaments (Tail)

---

**Trigger** You become [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]].

**Requirements** You have a fully grown tail.

---

You can shed a portion of your tail to escape. You cease being grabbed, then Stride without triggering any reactions from the creature that grabbed you. It takes 1 day for your tail to fully grow back. Until it does, you can't use your tail unarmed attack, and you take a –2 circumstance penalty on checks to [[Balance|main:balance]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
