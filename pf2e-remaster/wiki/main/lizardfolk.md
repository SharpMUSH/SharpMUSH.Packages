*Lizardfolk are consummate survivors, heirs to empires considered ancient even by the elves.*

**Hit Points:** 8  
**Size:** Medium  
**Speed:** 25 feet  
**Attribute boosts:** Strength, Wisdom, free  
**Attribute flaw:** Intelligence  
**Languages:** Common and Iruxi. Additional languages (one per point of Intelligence modifier): Amurrun, Boggard, Draconic, Elven, Fey, Jotun, or Thalassic  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Humanoid, Lizardfolk  
**Features:** Aquatic Adaptation, Claws
