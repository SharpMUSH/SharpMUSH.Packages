**Item 15**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Elixir, Healing  
**Price:** 1300 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

Elixirs of life accelerate a living creature's natural healing processes and immune system. Upon drinking this elixir, you regain 8d6 plus 21 Hit Points and gain +3 item bonus to saving throws against diseases and poisons for 10 minutes.

*Source: GM Core.*
