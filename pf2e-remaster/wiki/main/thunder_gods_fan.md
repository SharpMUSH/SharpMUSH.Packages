**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Tengu|main:tengu]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Tengu Feather Fan|main:tengu_feather_fan]]

---

Your fan unleashes the fury of the storm. You can use the Wave Fan activation one additional time per day. Add a 5th-rank [[Lightning Bolt|main:lightning_bolt]] spell to the spells contained within your fan.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
