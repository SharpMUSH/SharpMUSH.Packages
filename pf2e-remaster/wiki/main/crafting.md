**Key attribute:** Intelligence

Making and repairing items, and knowing about how they are made, including alchemy and constructs.

Your Crafting modifier is your Intelligence modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Crafting (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]]
- [[Repair|main:repair]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Craft|main:craft]]
- [[Earn Income|main:earn_income]]
- [[Identify Alchemy|main:identify_alchemy]]

Skill feats that need Crafting:

- **Level 1:** [[Alchemical Crafting|main:alchemical_crafting]], [[Crafter's Appraisal|main:crafters_appraisal]], [[Improvise Tool|main:improvise_tool]], [[Quick Repair|main:quick_repair]], [[Seasoned|main:seasoned]], [[Snare Crafting|main:snare_crafting]], [[Specialty Crafting|main:specialty_crafting]]
- **Level 2:** [[Communal Crafting|main:communal_crafting]], [[Inventor|main:inventor]], [[Magical Crafting|main:magical_crafting]]
- **Level 4:** [[Reverse Engineering|main:reverse_engineering]]
- **Level 7:** [[Expert Disassembly|main:expert_disassembly]], [[Impeccable Crafting|main:impeccable_crafting]], [[Rapid Affixture|main:rapid_affixture]], [[Signature Crafting|main:signature_crafting]]
- **Level 15:** [[Craft Anything|main:craft_anything]]
