**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]  
**Prerequisites:** Grimspawn, Pitborn, or Hellspawn, or another lineage feat associated with fiends or the plane of chaos

---

Like many fiends, you have the supernatural ability to teleport yourself to safety. Once per day, you can cast [[Translocate|main:translocate]] as a 5th-rank divine innate spell.

*Source: Player Core.*
