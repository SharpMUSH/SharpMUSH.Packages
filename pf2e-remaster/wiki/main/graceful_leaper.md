**Archetype Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** [[Acrobat Dedication|main:acrobat_dedication]]; Master in [[Acrobatics|main:acrobatics]]

---

Mass and muscle are meaningless when you propel yourself through the air; only grace and balance matter.

You can roll an Acrobatics check instead of an Athletics check when making a [[High Jump|main:high_jump]] or [[Long Jump|main:long_jump]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
