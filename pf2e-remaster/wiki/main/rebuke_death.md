**Focus 4**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Focus, Healing, Manipulate, Vitality  
**Cast:** (1 to 3 actions)  
**Area:** 20-foot emanation  
**Targets:** 1 living creature per action spent to Cast this Spell

---

You snatch creatures from the jaws of death, restoring them without the straing of a typical close call. You can spend 1 to 3 actions Casting this Spell, and you can target a number of creatures equal to the actions spent. Each target regains 3d6 Hit Points. If the target had the [[Dying|main:dying]] condition, coming back from Dying due to this healing doesn't increase its [[Wounded|main:wounded]] condition.

---

**Heightened (+1)** Increase the healing by 1d6.

*Source: Player Core.*
