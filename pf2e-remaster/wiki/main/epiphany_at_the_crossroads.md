**Class Feat 12** (free action)  
**Traits:** Divine, [[Oracle|main:oracle]]  
**Frequency:** once per day

---

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** Your turn begins.

**Requirements** You are [[Unconscious|main:unconscious]] and have the [[Dying|main:dying]] condition.

---

The crossroads between life and death is a place that can reveal many secrets. You gain the effects of an [[Augury|main:augury]] spell in a strange, near-death vision. Empowered by the realization, you then lose the dying condition (becoming [[Wounded 1|main:wounded]] or increasing your wounded value by 1, as normal), regain 2 times your level Hit Points, and can Stand.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
