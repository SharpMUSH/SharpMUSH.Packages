**Archetype Feat 2**  
**Traits:** Archetype, Dedication  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Acrobatics|main:acrobatics]]

---

You become an expert in Acrobatics. At 7th level, you become a master in Acrobatics, and at 15th level, you become legendary in Acrobatics. Whenever you critically succeed at an Acrobatics check to [[Tumble Through|main:tumble_through]] an enemy's space, you don't treat the enemy's space as difficult terrain.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
