**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Archaeologist Dedication|main:archaeologist_dedication]]

---

While study is key to your success, a bit of magic can be a helpful aid in discovering the secrets of the past. You can cast [[Detect Magic|main:detect_magic_spell]], [[Guidance|main:guidance]], and [[Read Aura|main:read_aura]] as occult innate cantrips.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
