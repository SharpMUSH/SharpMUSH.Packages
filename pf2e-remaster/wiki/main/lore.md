**Key attribute:** Intelligence

Specialized knowledge of one narrow subject, such as a profession, a kind of creature or a place. Each Lore is a separate skill; you can be trained in several. The GM decides whether a Lore applies.

Your Lore modifier is your Intelligence modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Lore (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Earn Income|main:earn_income]]

A background gives training in one Lore. You can also gain a Lore when you would become trained in a skill, or from feats such as [[Additional Lore|main:additional_lore]].

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Lore:

- **Level 1:** [[Armor Assist|main:armor_assist]], [[Experienced Professional|main:experienced_professional]], [[Seasoned|main:seasoned]]
- **Level 2:** [[Battle Planner|main:battle_planner]], [[Unmistakable Lore|main:unmistakable_lore]]
- **Level 15:** [[Legendary Professional|main:legendary_professional]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
