**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]  
**Prerequisites:** any nephilim lineage feat

---

You have a connection to the Outer Planes, allowing you to summon a divine ally. Choose either [[Summon Celestial|main:summon_celestial]], [[Summon Fiend|main:summon_fiend]], or another 5th-rank spell capable of summoning an extraplanar creature appropriate to your lineage. Once per day, you can cast the chosen spell as a 5th-rank divine innate spell, but the creature summoned must be from the same category as your own lineage, such as a creature with the angel trait if you are an angelkin.

*Source: Player Core.*
