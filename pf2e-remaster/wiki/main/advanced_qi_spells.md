**Class Feat 6**  
**Traits:** [[Monk|main:monk]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Qi Spells|main:qi_spells]]

---

Your talent at controlling your qi has reached new heights. You gain [[Qi Blast|main:qi_blast]], [[Shrink the Span|main:shrink_the_span]], or another 3rd-rank qi spell you have access to.

---

**Special** You can select this feat more than once, choosing a different spell each time.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
