**Class Feat 6** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Barbarian|main:barbarian]], Polymorph, Primal, Rage  
**Prerequisites:** giant instinct

---

**Requirements** You are Medium or smaller.

---

You grow to incredible size. You become Large, increasing your reach by 5 feet and gaining the [[Clumsy 1|main:clumsy]] condition until you stop raging. Your equipment grows with you.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
