**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** Intelligence +1

---

You become trained in the skill of your choice.

---

**Special** You can select this feat multiple times, choosing a new skill to become trained in each time.

*Source: Player Core.*
