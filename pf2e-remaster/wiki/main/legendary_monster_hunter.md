**Class Feat 16**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Prerequisites:** legendary in [[Nature|main:nature]]; [[Master Monster Hunter|main:master_monster_hunter]]

---

Your knowledge of monsters is so incredible that it reveals glaring flaws in your prey. Your bonus from [[Monster Hunter|main:monster_hunter]] (and the bonus from [[Monster Warden|main:monster_warden]] if you have it) increases from +1 to +2 for you and any allies who benefit.

*Source: Player Core.*
