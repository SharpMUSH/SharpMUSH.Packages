**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Tengu|main:tengu]]

---

You have near-supernatural poise that lets you move swiftly across the most unsteady surfaces. You gain the [[Steady Balance|main:steady_balance]] skill feat. You can Step into difficult terrain caused by uneven ground, such as a rocky field, hilly incline, or rocking ship.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
