**Focus 5**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Illusion, Manipulate, [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Area:** 30-foot burst or Target  
**Targets:** up to 10 creatures  
**Duration:** 10 minutes

---

You call upon fey glamours to cloak an area or the targets in illusion. This has the effect of either [[Illusory Scene|main:illusory_scene]] on the area or [[Illusory Disguise|main:illusory_disguise]] on the creatures, as if heightened to a rank 1 rank lower than *fey glamour*, using *fey glamour*'s range and duration.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
