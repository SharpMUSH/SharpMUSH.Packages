**Ancestry Feat 13** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Auditory, [[Catfolk|main:catfolk]], Concentrate, Emotion, Mental  
**Frequency:** once per day

---

**Frequency** once per day

**Trigger** An ally within 30 feet would be reduced to 0 HP but not immediately killed.

---

You give a daring yowl, calling your companion back from the brink of unconsciousness. Your ally isn't knocked out and remains at 1 Hit Point. Their [[Wounded|main:wounded]] condition still increases by 1 as though they had been [[Dying|main:dying]] and recovered.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
