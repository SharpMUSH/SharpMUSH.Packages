**Class Feat 12** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Concentrate, [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Press  
**Prerequisites:** [[Incredible Aim|main:incredible_aim]]

---

After your first shot singles out your opponent's position, you direct another that ricochets around obstacles and strikes unerringly. Make a ranged weapon Strike against a creature you previously attacked this turn. You ignore the target's [[Concealed|main:concealed]] condition and all cover.

*Source: Player Core.*
