**Class Feat 8** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Stance

---

Your shots become nimble and deadly. While you're in this stance, your ranged Strikes don't trigger Reactive Strike or other reactions that are triggered by a ranged attack.

---

**Special** If you have [[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike]], you can use it with a loaded ranged weapon you're wielding. The triggering creature must be within 5 feet of you for you to do so.

*Source: Player Core.*
