**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Manipulate  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (2 actions)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** 1 creature  
**Defense:** Fortitude  
**Duration:** varies

---

You sap the target's strength, depending on its Fortitude save.

---

**Critical Success** The target is unaffected.

**Success** The target is [[Enfeebled 1|main:enfeebled]] until the start of your next turn.

**Failure** The target is [[Enfeebled 2|main:enfeebled]] for 1 minute.

**Critical Failure** The target is [[Enfeebled 3|main:enfeebled]] for 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core.*
