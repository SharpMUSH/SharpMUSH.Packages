**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Gnome|main:gnome]]

---

You build a rapport with an animal, which becomes magically bonded to you. You gain a familiar. The type of animal is up to you, but most gnomes choose animals with a burrow Speed.

*Source: Player Core.*
