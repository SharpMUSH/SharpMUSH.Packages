**Ancestry Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Nephilim|main:nephilim]]

---

You have a halo of light and goodness that sheds light with the effects of a divine [[Light|main:light]] cantrip. A cantrip is heightened to a spell rank equal to half your level rounded up. You can Sustain to suppress or reestablish the halo.

*Source: Player Core.*
