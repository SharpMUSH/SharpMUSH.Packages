**Skill Feat 7**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** master in [[Stealth|main:stealth]]

---

You are adept at foiling creatures' special senses and cautious enough to safeguard against them at all times. Whenever you use the [[Avoid Notice|main:avoid_notice]], [[Hide|main:hide]], or [[Sneak|main:sneak]] actions, you are always considered to be taking precautions against special senses.

*Source: Player Core.*
