**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Leshy|main:leshy]]

---

You can call forth nearby nature spirits to augment your body. Once per day, you can cast [[Plant Form|main:plant_form]] as a 5th-rank primal innate spell. When you reach 17th level, you can heighten the spell to 6th rank when you cast it.

*Source: Player Core.*
