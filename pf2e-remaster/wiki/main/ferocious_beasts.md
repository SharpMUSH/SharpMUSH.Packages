**Ancestry Feat 13**  
**Traits:** [[Orc|main:orc]]  
**Prerequisites:** animal companion, Pet, or Bonded Animal; [[Orc Ferocity|main:orc_ferocity]]

---

Since ancient times, the mightiest orc beast tamers would draw out the true fighting spirit of their companion beasts by feeding the creatures a draft incorporating the orc's own blood. All animal companions, pets, familiars, and bonded animals you have gain the [[Orc Ferocity|main:orc_ferocity]] feat, and gain one reaction per round they can use only for Orc Ferocity. If you have the [[Undying Ferocity|main:undying_ferocity]] ancestry feat, all animal companions, pets, familiars, and bonded animals you have also gain the benefits of that feat when using the Orc Ferocity reaction.

*Source: Player Core.*
