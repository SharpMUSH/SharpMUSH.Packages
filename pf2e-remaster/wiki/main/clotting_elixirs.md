**Class Feat 2**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]

---

Your restorative concoctions have a clotting effect on the drinker's blood. When a living creature drinks an infused elixir with the healing trait that you have created, it can immediately attempt a DC 10 flat to remove any persistent bleed damage.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
