**Class Feature 5**  
**Traits:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Class:** [[Ranger|main:ranger]]

---

When you move through natural terrains, you are hard to track. You always gain the benefits of the [[Cover Tracks|main:cover_tracks]] action in such terrains, without moving at half your Speed.

*Source: Player Core.*
