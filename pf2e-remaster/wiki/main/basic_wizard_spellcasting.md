**Archetype Feat 4**  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wizard Dedication|main:wizard_dedication]]

---

You gain the Basic Spellcasting Feat benefits. Each time you gain a spell slot of a new rank from the wizard archetype, add two common spells of that rank to your spellbook.

*Source: Player Core.*
