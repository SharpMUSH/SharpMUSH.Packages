**Class Feat 10** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Auditory, [[Bard|main:bard]], Concentrate, Spellshape  
**Prerequisites:** warrior muse

---

With a mighty shout, you can stir an ally to attack. If your next action is to cast the [[Courageous Anthem|main:courageous_anthem]] composition cantrip, one ally of your choice who gains a status bonus from the spell can immediately use their reaction to make a melee Strike.

*Source: Player Core.*
