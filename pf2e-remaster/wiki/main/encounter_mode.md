## Enter initiative

When the GM calls for initiative, roll the check they specify, usually Perception. Rank totals highest first; enemies win ties with PCs, while tied PCs choose their order. Each participant takes one turn per round (about 6 seconds). Repeat that order without rerolling until the encounter ends; then return to exploration.

*Source: [Encounter Mode](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2421), Player Core, p. 435.*

## Run your turn

1. **Start:** resolve start-of-turn effects, including a [[Dying and Recovery|main:dying_and_recovery]] check if dying. Decrease durations you created that count down at this point. Finally regain 3 actions and 1 reaction, subject to conditions; unused resources do not accumulate.
2. **Act:** pay each action's cost and finish an activity before beginning another. A two-action activity needs two actions remaining this turn. Track [[Multiple Attack Penalty|main:multiple_attack_penalty]]. You can end early.
3. **End:** resolve expiring effects; apply [[Persistent Damage|main:persistent_damage]], its recovery checks, then affliction saves and other end-of-turn condition changes. Resolve any remaining end triggers. Pass to the next participant.

*Source: [Turns](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2427), Player Core, pp. 435–436.*

## Move into reach

Before a melee attack, check [[Size and Reach|main:size_and_reach]] and the actual distance. This route's Medium dwarf has 5-foot reach: an enemy 15 feet away is not already within it. Spend one action to [[Stride|main:stride]] up to your Speed; with Speed 20, a clear 10-foot approach can finish within 5 feet of that enemy. Moving is not included for free in a Strike.

[[Step|main:step]] instead costs one action to move just 5 feet and avoids reactions triggered by that movement. It requires land Speed at least 10 feet and cannot enter difficult terrain. A Step does not cover that whole 10-foot approach in one action. Ordinary movement can trigger an opponent's applicable reaction; having a reaction available does not give every creature Reactive Strike.

On the standard square grid, each square is 5 feet across. Moving one straight adjacent square costs 5 feet before terrain adjustments. Diagonal squares alternate **5, 10, 5, 10 feet** across all your movement in the same turn. A second Stride continues the count; it does not restart it. Reset the count for the next turn. For example, four consecutive diagonal squares cost 30 feet. See [[Terrain and Cover|main:terrain_and_cover]] for costs and defenses that the simple straight-line practice leaves out.

The first, separate exercise in [[First Encounter|main:first_encounter]] pays for an actual approach, attack and defense. Reset before its adjacent Trip lesson; those are not consecutive fights on one HP ledger.

*Sources: linked Stride/Step entries; [Grid Movement](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2356) and [Diagonal Movement](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2357), Player Core p.421.*

## Reactions and hands

A reaction needs its trigger and an available reaction. You can react on another creature's turn. The GM decides availability before your first turn. Thereafter, refresh happens at your turn's start, not at the round boundary. [[Reactive Strike|main:reactive_strike]] and [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]] normally compete for your one reaction.

*Source: [Reactions in Encounters](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2432), Player Core, p. 436.*

[[Stand|main:stand]] is a Move action and can trigger an eligible creature's Reactive Strike even without traveling to another square. For a Move action that stays in its square, the trigger happens at the action's end: Stand has finished and the target is upright when the reaction attack resolves. Do not retain prone's off-guard effect from before it stood; other independent effects can still affect AC. The worked Trip lesson declines this reaction, so it adds no reaction attack to that ledger.

*Sources: [Stand](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2303), p.418; [Reactions to Movement](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2355), p.421; [Reactive Strike](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2256), p.138, Player Core.*

[[Raise a Shield|main:raise_a_shield]] costs one action and requires a wielded shield; its AC benefit lasts until your next turn starts. Shield Block additionally needs a raised shield and its physical-attack-damage trigger. A sword and steel shield fill both hands: [[Trip|main:trip]] needs a hand freed first. See [[Equipment Basics|main:equipment_basics]] and the worked encounter for paid hand changes.

*Sources: [Raise a Shield](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2316), Player Core, p. 419; [Shield Block](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=5212), p. 262; [Trip](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2382), p. 236.*
