**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Druid|main:druid]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form_feat]]

---

You've mastered the shape of dinosaurs. Add the shapes listed in [[Dinosaur Form|main:dinosaur_form]] to your untamed form list. Whenever you use [[Untamed Form|main:untamed_form]] to take a shape that grants you a specific Athletics modifier, you gain a +1 status bonus to your Athletics checks.

*Source: Player Core.*
