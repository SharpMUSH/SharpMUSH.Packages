**Focus 2**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, Concentrate, Focus, Fortune, [[Ranger|main:ranger]]  
**Cast:** (free action)

---

**Trigger** You attempt a check to [[Recall Knowledge|main:recall_knowledge]] about a creature, but you haven't rolled yet.

---

You have a preternatural ability to remember details about your foes. Roll the triggering check twice and use the better result.
