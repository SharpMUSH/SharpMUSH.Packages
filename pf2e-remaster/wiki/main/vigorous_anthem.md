**Class Feat 14** (1 action)  
**Traits:** Auditory, [[Bard|main:bard]], Concentrate, Spellshape

---

You instill magical vigor in your allies when you inspire them to attack. If your next action is to cast the [[Courageous Anthem|main:courageous_anthem]] composition cantrip, you and all affected allies gain temporary HP equal to 3 + your Charisma modifier that last for 1 minute.

*Source: Player Core.*
