**Key attribute:** Strength

Physical feats: climbing, swimming, jumping, and wrestling or shoving foes in combat.

Your Athletics modifier is your Strength modifier plus your proficiency bonus in Athletics (see [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]]), plus any bonuses and penalties.

## Actions

Anyone can use these, trained or not:

- [[Climb|main:climb]]
- [[Force Open|main:force_open]]
- [[Grapple|main:grapple]]
- [[High Jump|main:high_jump]]
- [[Long Jump|main:long_jump]]
- [[Reposition|main:reposition]]
- [[Shove|main:shove]]
- [[Swim|main:swim]]
- [[Trip|main:trip]]

You must be trained in the skill to use these:

- [[Disarm|main:disarm]]

## Skill feats

Skill feats that need Athletics:

- **Level 1:** [[Armor Assist|main:armor_assist]], [[Combat Climber|main:combat_climber]], [[Hefty Hauler|main:hefty_hauler]], [[Quick Jump|main:quick_jump]], [[Titan Wrestler|main:titan_wrestler]], [[Underwater Marauder|main:underwater_marauder]]
- **Level 2:** [[Lead Climber|main:lead_climber]], [[Powerful Leap|main:powerful_leap]], [[Rapid Mantel|main:rapid_mantel]], [[Slippery Prey|main:slippery_prey]]
- **Level 4:** [[Rope Runner|main:rope_runner]]
- **Level 7:** [[Quick Climb|main:quick_climb]], [[Quick Swim|main:quick_swim]], [[Wall Jump|main:wall_jump]], [[Water Sprint|main:water_sprint]]
- **Level 15:** [[Cloud Jump|main:cloud_jump]]

See also [[Skills|category:skills]] and [[Skill Actions|category:skill_actions]].
