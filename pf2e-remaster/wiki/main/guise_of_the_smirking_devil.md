**Item 9**  
**Traits:** Auditory, Invested, Magical, Void  
**Price:** 700 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** wornmask

---

This ice-blue half-mask is adorned with a wicked silver grin that covers the wearer's mouth, leaving the rest of the face uncovered. You gain a +2 item bonus to Intimidation checks.

**Activate—Hideous Wail** (2 actions) (concentrate, manipulate)

**Frequency** once per day

---

**Effect** The mask emits a soul-chilling scream that deals 6d10 void damage to each living creature in a 20-foot emanation (basic DC 25 Fortitude save).

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply a casting of [[Wails of the Damned|main:wails_of_the_damned]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
