**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]]  
**Prerequisites:** [[Invigorating Elixir|main:invigorating_elixir]]

---

Choose physical invigoration or mental invigoration, and add the listed conditions to those you can choose for an invigorating elixir you create to counteract:

**Physical Invigoration** [[Blinded|main:blinded]], [[Deafened|main:deafened]], [[Drained|main:drained]], [[Paralyzed|main:paralyzed]], [[Slowed|main:slowed]]

**Mental Invigoration** [[Confused|main:confused]], controlled, [[Fleeing|main:fleeing]], [[Frightened|main:frightened]], [[Paralyzed|main:paralyzed]], [[Slowed|main:slowed]].

---

**Special** You can select this feat a second time to choose a different type of invigoration and add its options to those you can choose.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
