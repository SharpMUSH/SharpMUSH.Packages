**Class Feat 16** (free action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], [[Monk|main:monk]]  
**Prerequisites:** Opening Stance (Fighter); Reflexive Stance (Monk)

---

**Trigger** Your turn begins.

---
