**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Thievery|main:thievery]]

---

When you successfully [[Steal|main:steal]] something, observers (creatures other than the creature you stole from) take a –2 circumstance penalty to their Perception DCs to detect your theft. Additionally, if you first [[Create a Diversion|main:create_a_diversion]] using Deception, taking a single [[Palm an Object|main:palm_an_object]] or Steal action doesn't end your [[Undetected|main:undetected]] condition.

*Source: Player Core.*
