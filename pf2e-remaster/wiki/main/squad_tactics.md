**Ancestry Feat 9**  
**Traits:** [[Hobgoblin|main:hobgoblin]]

---

You are adept at working with your allies to surround an enemy. If an enemy is within reach of you and at least two of your allies, that enemy is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] to you.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
