**Ancestry Feat 17**  
**Traits:** [[Ratfolk|main:ratfolk]]

---

You call upon the familial bonds you share with your allies and other ratfolk to become an overwhelming force in battle. You can cast [[Enlarge|main:enlarge]] as a 6th-rank primal innate spell once per day.
