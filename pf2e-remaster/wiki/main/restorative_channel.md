**Class Feat 8**  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]]  
**Prerequisites:** healing font

---

You can remove conditions with divine grace. You can sacrifice one [[Heal|main:heal]] spell you've prepared in your extra slots from your healing font to cast one of the following spells instead: [[Cleanse Affliction|main:cleanse_affliction]], [[Clear Mind|main:clear_mind]], [[Sound Body|main:sound_body]], or [[Sure Footing|main:sure_footing]]. The spell is heightened to the same rank as the spell you sacrificed.

*Source: Player Core.*
