**Spell 1**  
**Traits:** Concentrate, Emotion, Mental  
**Traditions:** [[Arcane|category:arcane_spells]], [[Divine|category:divine_spells]], [[Occult|category:occult_spells]]  
**Cast:** (reaction)  
**Range:** 30 feet  
**Targets:** the triggering foe  
**Defense:** Will

---

**Trigger** You critically fail a saving throw against a foe's effect.

---

You distract your enemy with their feeling of smug pleasure when you fail catastrophically. They must attempt a Will save.

---

**Critical Success** The creature is unaffected.

**Success** The creature is distracted by its amusement and takes a -1 status penalty on Perception checks and Will saves for 1 round. Schadenfreude (Success)

**Failure** The creature is overcome by its amusement and is [[Stupefied 1|main:stupefied]] for 1 round.

**Critical Failure** The creature is lost in its amusement and is [[Stupefied 2|main:stupefied]] for 1 round and [[Stunned 1|main:stunned]].

*Source: Player Core 2.*
