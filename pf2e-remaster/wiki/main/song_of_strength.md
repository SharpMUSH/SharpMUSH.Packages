**Cantrip 1**  
**Traits:** Uncommon, [[Bard|main:bard]], Cantrip, Composition, Concentrate, Emotion, Mental  
**Cast:** (1 action)  
**Area:** 60-foot emanation  
**Duration:** 1 round

---

You bolster your allies' physical strength with a hearty exhortation. You and your allies gain a +1 status bonus to Athletics checks and to their DCs against Athletics skill actions such as [[Disarm|main:disarm]], [[Reposition|main:reposition]], [[Shove|main:shove]], and [[Trip|main:trip]].

*Source: Player Core.*
