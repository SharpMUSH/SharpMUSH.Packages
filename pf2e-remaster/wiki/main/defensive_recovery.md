**Class Feat 12** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Cleric|main:cleric]], Concentrate, Spellshape

---

Your faith provides temporary protection in addition to healing. If the next action you use is to cast [[Harm|main:harm]] or [[Heal|main:heal]] on a single target and the target regains Hit Points from the spell, it also gains a +2 status bonus to AC and saving throws for 1 round.

*Source: Player Core.*
