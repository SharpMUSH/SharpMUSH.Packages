**Class Feat 20**  
**Traits:** [[Swashbuckler|main:swashbuckler]]

---

You find opportunities to perform stylish feats in the moments between others' heartbeats and eye blinks. You are permanently [[Quickened|main:quickened]]. You can use the extra action only to perform a bravado action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
