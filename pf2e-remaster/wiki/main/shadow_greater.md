**Item 9**  
**Traits:** Magical  
**Price:** 650 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** etched onto lm nonmetal armor

---

Armor etched with this rune takes on a hazy black appearance. You gain a +2 item bonus to Stealth checks while wearing the armor.
