Your thoughts and instincts are clouded. Stupefied always has a value.

- You take a status penalty equal to the value on Intelligence-, Wisdom- and Charisma-based rolls and DCs: Will saves, spell attack modifiers, spell DCs, and skill checks using those attributes.
- When you Cast a Spell, it is disrupted unless you succeed at a flat check with a DC of 5 + your stupefied value.

*Source: Player Core, p. 446.*
