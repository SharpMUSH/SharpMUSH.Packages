Use this procedure for a player character. Keep current HP, [[Dying|main:dying]] and [[Wounded|main:wounded]] separate. See [[First Encounter|main:first_encounter]] for the character's starting equipment.

## When damage drops you to 0 HP

HP stops at 0. Move your initiative immediately before the turn during which you fell. Ordinary lethal defeat gives dying 1; an attacker's critical success or your critical failure gives dying 2. Add your existing wounded value. Nonlethal defeat instead leaves you unconscious at 0 without dying.

Apply [[Unconscious|main:unconscious]]: you cannot act, fall [[Prone|main:prone]] and normally drop held items. Waking does not pick them up or stand you up.

*Source: [Getting Knocked Out](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2324), Player Core, pp. 410–411; unconscious details on the linked condition page.*

## At each dying turn's start

Roll a **flat check**: d20 with no skill, attribute or proficiency modifier, against **DC 10 + current dying value**. Use [[Degrees of Success|main:degrees_of_success]], including natural 20/1 adjustments.

| Outcome | Change to dying |
| --- | --- |
| Critical success | Decrease by 2 |
| Success | Decrease by 1 |
| Failure | Increase by 1 |
| Critical failure | Increase by 2 |

For dying 2 (DC 12), alternative rolls illustrate all four: natural 20 → dying 0; 12 → dying 1; 5 → dying 3; natural 1 → dying 4 and death. These are separate possibilities, not four consecutive rolls.

*Sources: [Recovery Checks](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2326), Player Core, p. 411; [Checks (Flat Checks)](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2278), p. 400.*

## Damage, stabilization and healing

Damage while dying adds 1 to dying, or 2 for an attacker's critical hit or your critical failure. Do not add wounded again to each increase.

At dying 0, remove dying; at 0 HP you remain unconscious. Healing to at least 1 HP removes dying and unconscious, allowing normal action on your next turn. Normally, losing dying gives wounded 1, or increases existing wounded by 1. Death normally occurs at dying 4; [[Doomed|main:doomed]] lowers that threshold. Death effects and massive damage can bypass this sequence; consult the external full rules below.

*Sources: [Taking Damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2327) and [Losing the Dying Condition](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2328), Player Core, p. 411; [Instant Death](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2330), Player Core, p. 411.*

[[Hero Points|main:hero_points]] provide a specific exception: spend all remaining points (at least one), at your turn’s start or when dying would increase, for heroic recovery. This removes dying and stabilizes at 0 HP without adding wounded; it does not erase existing wounded or supply positive HP. Ordinary stabilization does add wounded.

*Specific exception: [Heroic Recovery](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2263) and [Hero Points](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2333), Player Core.*

## An ally administers the potion

An unconscious character cannot spend actions to drink. Your [[Minor Healing Potion|main:healing_potion_minor]] is worn and readily accessible, neither held nor stowed in a backpack. An ally with a free hand can retrieve it with one [[Interact|main:interact]], then spend another Interact to feed it to you within reach. An actually stowed item may also require opening its container; account for that access rather than assuming every retrieval has the same total cost. See [Carrying Items](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2148) and [Drawing and Stowing Items](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2150), Player Core, pp. 267–268. Potion rules permit a willing or helpless recipient. Movement to reach you costs its own actions. The potion is consumed.

Example: you are at 0 HP, dying 1, not wounded. An adjacent ally starts with a hand free, draws your potion (one action) and administers it (one action). Its d8 rolls 5: you have 5 HP, no dying or unconscious, and wounded 1. You remain prone with dropped gear. If ordinary lethal damage defeats you again before wounded ends, you start at dying 2; critical defeat starts at dying 3.

*Source: [Potions](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=3196), GM Core, p. 259; item healing and Interact on the linked package pages.*

## Recover after the danger

[[Administer First Aid|main:administer_first_aid]] can stabilize using a healer's toolkit; [[Treat Wounds|main:treat_wounds]] takes 10 minutes and is not an ordinary encounter rescue action. This fighter owns no toolkit. An ally who owns one can provide treatment using their own Medicine check.

Successful Treat Wounds healing removes wounded. Alternatively, recover to full HP and rest 10 minutes to remove it. Ending combat alone removes neither dying nor wounded. If stabilized at 0 without healing, the GM determines when you wake (at least 10 minutes, possibly hours); see [[Unconscious|main:unconscious]].

*Sources: linked condition and treatment pages; [Dying and related conditions](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2325), Player Core, pp. 411–412.*
