**Archetype Feat 6** (reaction)  
**Traits:** Archetype  
**Prerequisites:** [[Wrestler Dedication|main:wrestler_dedication]]

---

**Trigger** A creature you had [[Grabbed|main:grabbed]] or [[Restrained|main:restrained]] successfully Escapes.

---

Your opponents can't slip your grasp without receiving further punishment. Make an unarmed melee Strike against the triggering creature.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
