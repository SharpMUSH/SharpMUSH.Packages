**Item 15**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical  
**Price:** 1300 gp  
**Bulk:** —

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This sling bullet looks like a petrified serpent's eye. A creature hit by an activated *stone bullet* is subject to the effects of a 6th-rank [[Petrify|main:petrify]] spell (DC 34 Fortitude).

---

**Craft Requirements** Supply one casting of [[Petrify|main:petrify]].
