*Guardians and emissaries of the environment, leshies are immortal spirits of nature temporarily granted a physical form.*

**Hit Points:** 8  
**Size:** Small  
**Speed:** 25 feet  
**Attribute boosts:** Constitution, Wisdom, free  
**Attribute flaw:** Intelligence  
**Languages:** Common and Fey. Additional languages (one per point of Intelligence modifier): Draconic, Elven, Gnomish, Goblin, Halfling, or Sakvroth  
**Traits:** Leshy, Plant  
**Features:** Low-light vision, Plant Nourishment
