**Heritage**  
**Ancestry:** [[Lizardfolk|main:lizardfolk]]

---

You can flare your neck frill and flex your dorsal spines, Demoralizing your foes. When you do, [[Demoralize|main:demoralize]] loses the auditory trait and gains the visual trait, and you don't take a penalty when you attempt to Demoralize a creature that doesn't understand your language. You also gain the [[Threatening Approach|main:threatening_approach]] action.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
