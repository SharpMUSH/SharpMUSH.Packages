**Class Feature 1**  
**Traits:** [[Sorcerer|main:sorcerer]]

---

The touch of undeath runs through your blood. Your family tree might contain powerful undead, like a vampire.

**Tradition** divine

**Bloodline Skills** Intimidation, Religion

**Sorcerous Gifts** cantrip [[Void Warp|main:void_warp]], 1st: [[Harm|main:harm]], 2nd: [[See the Unseen|main:see_the_unseen]], 3rd: [[Bind Undead|main:bind_undead]], 4th: [[Talking Corpse|main:talking_corpse]], 5th: [[Invoke Spirits|main:invoke_spirits]], 6th: [[Vampiric Exsanguination|main:vampiric_exsanguination]], 7th: [[Execute|main:execute]], 8th: [[Canticle of Everlasting Grief|main:canticle_of_everlasting_grief]], 9th: [[Wails of the Damned|main:wails_of_the_damned]]

**Bloodline Spells** initial: [[Undeath's Blessing|main:undeaths_blessing]], advanced: [[Drain Life|main:drain_life]], greater: [[Grasping Grave|main:grasping_grave]]

**Blood Magic—Stolen Life** You can channel the necromantic energy in your blood. Either you gain temporary Hit Points equal to the spell's rank that last until the start of your next turn, or a target takes 1 void damage per spell rank (if the spell already deals initial void damage, combine this with the spell's initial damage before determining weaknesses and resistances).

*Source: Player Core 2.*
