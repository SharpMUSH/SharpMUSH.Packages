**Class Feat 4** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Alchemist|main:alchemist]], Manipulate

---

**Requirements** You are under the effects of a mutagen.

---

You redirect a mutagen within your body to spit a stream of stomach acid at a foe. A creature within 30 feet takes 1d6 acid damage for every 2 levels you have ((half your level, rounded down)d6 acid), with a basic Reflex save against your class DC. On a failure, the creature is also [[Sickened 1|main:sickened]] (or [[Sickened 2|main:sickened]] on a critical failure). The mutagen's duration immediately ends.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
