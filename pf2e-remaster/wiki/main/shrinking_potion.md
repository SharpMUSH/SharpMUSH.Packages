**Item 4**  
**Traits:** Consumable, Magical, Potion  
**Price:** 15 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This fungus-flavored potion conveys the effects of the [[Shrink|main:shrink]] spell to make you and all your gear smaller. After the onset, you remain small for 10 minutes.

**Onset** 1 minute
