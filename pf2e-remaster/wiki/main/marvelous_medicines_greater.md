**Item 18**  
**Traits:** Magical  
**Price:** 19000 gp  
**Bulk:** 1  
**Usage:** held in two hands

---

This healer's toolkit contains a seemingly endless supply of bandages, herbs, and healing items of impeccable quality, granting you a +3 item bonus to Medicine checks.

If you use the *marvelous medicines* when you [[Treat Poison|main:treat_poison]] or [[Treat Disease|main:treat_disease]], before you roll your check, the medicines attempt to counteract the poison or disease you're treating, with a counteract rank of 8 and a counteract modifier of +30. This is a healing effect.

The medicines can't be used to treat the same affliction for that patient again

*Source: GM Core.*
