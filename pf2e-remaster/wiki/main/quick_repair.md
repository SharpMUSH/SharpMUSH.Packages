**Skill Feat 1**  
**Traits:** General, Skill  
**Prerequisites:** trained in [[Crafting|main:crafting]]

---

For you, the [[Repair|main:repair]] activity loses the exploration trait and takes 1 minute instead of 10 minutes. If you're a master in Crafting, it takes 3 actions. If you're legendary, it takes 1 action.

*Source: Player Core.*
