**Item 13**  
**Traits:** Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 2700 gp  
**Bulk:** —  
**Usage:** worneyepiece

---

Adherents of a god of the hunt create these magical eye patches. An *eye of fortune* has a jeweled eye symbol on its front, allowing you to magically see through the eye patch as though it were transparent.

**Activate—Luck Beyond Sight** (free action) (concentrate, fortune)

**Trigger** You attack a concealed or hidden creature and haven't attempted the flat check yet

---

**Effect** You can roll the flat check for the concealed or hidden condition twice and use the higher result.

*Source: GM Core.*
