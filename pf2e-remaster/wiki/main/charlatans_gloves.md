**Item 3**  
**Traits:** Invested, Magical  
**Price:** 50 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** worngloves

---

Tiny silver hooks decorate these fine silk gloves. They grant a +1 item bonus to Thievery and allow you to cast [[Telekinetic Hand|main:telekinetic_hand]] as an innate occult spell.

*Source: GM Core.*
