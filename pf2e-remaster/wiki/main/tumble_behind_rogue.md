**Class Feat 1**  
**Traits:** [[Rogue|main:rogue]]

---

You tumble under and behind your foe, your rapid movement letting you catch them off guard. When you successfully [[Tumble Through|main:tumble_through]], the foe whose space you passed through is [[Off-Guard|main:off-guard]] against the next attack you make before the end of your turn.

*Source: Player Core.*
