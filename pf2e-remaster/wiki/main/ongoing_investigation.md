**Class Feat 4**  
**Traits:** [[Investigator|main:investigator]]

---

You're always investigating what's around you, even as you perform other activities. You can move at full Speed while using the [[Investigate|main:investigate]] exploration activity, and you can use another exploration activity while Investigating.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
