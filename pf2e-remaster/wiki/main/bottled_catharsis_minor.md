**Item 2**  
**Traits:** Alchemical, Consumable, Elixir, Healing  
**Price:** 5 gp  
**Bulk:** L  
**Usage:** held in one hand

---

**Activate** (1 action) (manipulate)

---

This drink unlocks a flood of emotions that helps reset your mental state. When you drink this elixir, the elixir attempts to counteract at 1st-rank and has a +6 counteract modifier each effect on you that has the emotion trait or is inflicting the [[Stupefied|main:stupefied]] condition on you, using the listed counteract rank and modifier.

*Source: Player Core 2.*
