**Class Feat 12** (1 action)  
**Traits:** [[Fighter|main:fighter]], Stance  
**Prerequisites:** [[Dueling Parry|main:dueling_parry]]

---

**Requirements** You are wielding a single one-handed melee weapon and hold nothing else in your hands.

---

Using a free hand as pivot and balance, you both attack and defend with your weapon. While you are in this stance, you constantly have the benefits of [[Dueling Parry|main:dueling_parry]].

*Source: Player Core.*
