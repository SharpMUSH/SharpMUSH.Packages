General feats can be taken by any character; you gain one at 3rd level and every 4 levels after. Every [[skill feat|category:skill_feats]] is also a general feat.

- **Level 1:** [[Adopted Ancestry|main:adopted_ancestry]], [[Armor Proficiency|main:armor_proficiency]], [[Breath Control|main:breath_control]], [[Canny Acumen|main:canny_acumen]], [[Diehard|main:diehard]], [[Fast Recovery|main:fast_recovery]], [[Feather Step|main:feather_step]], [[Fleet|main:fleet]], [[Incredible Initiative|main:incredible_initiative]], [[Pet|main:pet]], [[Ride|main:ride]], [[Shield Block|main:shield_block]], [[Toughness|main:toughness]], [[Weapon Proficiency|main:weapon_proficiency]]
- **Level 3:** [[Ancestral Paragon|main:ancestral_paragon]], [[Improvised Repair|main:improvised_repair]], [[Keen Follower|main:keen_follower]], [[Pick up the Pace|main:pick_up_the_pace]], [[Prescient Planner|main:prescient_planner]], [[Robust Health|main:robust_health]], [[Thorough Search|main:thorough_search]], [[Untrained Improvisation|main:untrained_improvisation]]
- **Level 7:** [[Expeditious Search|main:expeditious_search]], [[Numb to Death|main:numb_to_death]], [[Prescient Consumable|main:prescient_consumable]], [[Supertaster|main:supertaster]]
- **Level 11:** [[A Home in Every Port|main:a_home_in_every_port]], [[Caravan Leader|main:caravan_leader]], [[Incredible Investiture|main:incredible_investiture]], [[Incredible Scout|main:incredible_scout]]
- **Level 19:** [[True Perception|main:true_perception]]
