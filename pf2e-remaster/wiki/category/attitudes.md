How a creature feels about one particular character, from best to worst: helpful, friendly, indifferent, unfriendly, hostile. Only supernatural effects can give an attitude to a player character.

Conditions in this group: [[Friendly]], [[Helpful]], [[Hostile]], [[Indifferent]], [[Unfriendly]].

*Source: Player Core, pp. 442-447.*
