Conditions are lasting effects on a creature or object, such as being frightened, prone or invisible. Each one has a page here, filed in this category.

## How conditions work

- **Values.** Some conditions have a number, as in frightened 2. The number is how severe it is, and usually the size of the bonus or penalty it gives. When the value drops to 0, the condition ends.
- **The same condition twice.** Values don't add up. Only the highest value applies.
- **Overriding.** Some conditions override others; the overriding condition's page says so. The overridden condition does nothing until the overriding one ends, but its duration keeps running.
- **Ending.** A condition lasts as long as the effect that gave it says, or until its own rules end it.
