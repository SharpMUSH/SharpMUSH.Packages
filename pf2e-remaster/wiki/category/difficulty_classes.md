A check succeeds when its total (a d20 roll plus modifiers) meets or beats a difficulty class (DC). See [[Degrees of Success|main:degrees_of_success]] for how far above or below the DC matters.

When no rule sets a DC, the GM picks one:

- **[[Simple DCs|main:simple_dcs]]**: quick DCs by proficiency rank, for skill checks with no level attached. Pick the rank that best fits the task; the character doesn't need that rank to try.
- **[[DCs by Level|main:dcs_by_level]]**: for anything with a level, such as Recalling Knowledge about a creature, Earning Income, or a wall built by a 15th-level villain.
- **[[Spell DCs by Rank|main:spell_dcs_by_rank]]**: the same for spells, which use ranks 1 to 10.
- **[[DC Adjustments|main:dc_adjustments]]**: make a DC easier or harder, or account for an uncommon, rare or unique subject.

Simple DCs change little from rank to rank, so for hazards and fights, where lives are at stake, level-based DCs work better.

*Source: GM Core, pp. 52-53.*
