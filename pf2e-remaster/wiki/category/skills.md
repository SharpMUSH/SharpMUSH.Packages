Skills are what your character knows and can do outside of fighting. Each uses one attribute, and your proficiency rank in it (untrained, trained, expert, master or legendary) adds to your checks. See [[Proficiency Ranks|main:proficiency_ranks]].

Your class and background make you trained in some skills, plus a number of extra skills equal to your class's amount plus your Intelligence modifier. Your class gives skill increases as you level up.
