An **obstacle** makes a scene harder, but the PCs cannot simply fight it: they must get past it, put up with it, or take it out of play. Obstacles accent the enemies in a scene; use them now and then, not in every fight. They go into an encounter and end with it.

## The three kinds

- A **hazard** can attack the PCs: fire jets, rolling boulders, a sniper too far away to reach. It has a rating, usually at least the PCs' best skill, and a Weapon of 1 to 4. It acts each exchange, rolling its rating, and adds its Weapon when it hits. Nobody can attack it.
- A **block** stops the PCs doing something: a fence, a vat of acid, a statue that guards a door. It takes no turn. Getting past it is an overcome roll against its rating, which should be no more than one above the PCs' best skill. Failing against a harmful one hits by the margin plus its Weapon. Forced into it, a character takes an attack with half its Weapon (at least 1); behind it, they may get Armor at half its rating (at least 1).
- A **distraction** makes the PCs choose: a ritual to stop, a bus to save. It has a choice, one or more repercussions if they leave it, and sometimes an opposition to roll against.

Taking one out of play means taking a risk and rolling: against a hazard's rating, or a block's rating plus two.

## At the table

Players in the encounter use `+obstacle` to list the obstacles, `+obstacle/overcome <obstacle>=<skill>` to get past one, and `+obstacle/disable <obstacle>=<skill>` to take it out of play. The GM puts them in with `+obstacle/add` from the library, or `/hazard`, `/block` and `/distraction`, and takes a hazard's turn with `+obstacle/roll`.

For example:

`+obstacle/overcome acid=athletics`

Output: `[OBSTACLE] Pat tries to get past Acid Vat: Athletics: 4dF[1, 1, -1, 0] + 0 = 1 (Average) against Good (+3): fail, 2 shifts short (roll 7) Acid Vat hits them for 6 shifts: the margin and its Weapon:4.`

## Examples from the toolkit

- Hazards: Great (+4) *Machine-Gun Turret*, Weapon:3; Fantastic (+6) *Whirling Spike Apparatus*, Weapon:2; Superb (+5) *Distant Sniper*, Weapon:4.
- Blocks: Fair (+2) *Chain Link Fence*; Good (+3) *Vat of Acid*, Weapon:4; Great (+4) *Animate Statue*, Weapon:1.
- Distractions: *Sinister Ritual* (Do you deal with the enemies attacking you, or do you stop the ritual?), *Bus Full of Civilians* (Will the bus plunge off the bridge?), *The Glittering Gem* (Will you take the gem from the pedestal?).
