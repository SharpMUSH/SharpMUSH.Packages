Opposition for Fate games: who the PCs fight, what stands in their way, and what makes it urgent. New to Fate itself? Read [[Learn Fate|main:learn_fate]] first.

An **adversary** is anything that exists to hinder, challenge or oppose the PCs. There are three kinds:

1. [[Enemies|main:fate_adversary_enemies]]: threats, hitters, bosses and fillers, which can be fought and taken out.
2. [[Obstacles|main:fate_adversary_obstacles]]: hazards, blocks and distractions, which cannot be fought, only got past, put up with or taken out of play.
3. [[Constraints|main:fate_adversary_constraints]]: countdowns, limitations and resistances, which change how the PCs must deal with an enemy or an obstacle.

Enemies are NPCs on the Fate sheet's `+npc`, and play in an encounter like any other: see [[Encounters|main:fate_encounters]].
