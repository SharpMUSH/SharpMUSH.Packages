# Fate System Toolkit Notice

The fate-system-toolkit package's conditions (fleeting, sticky and lasting, their stress soaking and their treatment), Wealth as a stress track (Extra Effort bonuses, starting boxes, aspects about wealth and parcels of Wealth), and Stormcallers (the five storms, their aspects' passive effects, invokes and compels, barriers, and each storm's rules for overcoming, creating advantages, attacking and defending), supers (origin stories, super skills that double for a fate point and step back, and fate-point bids between powers), gadgets (function and flaw aspects, stunts and additional flaws) and cyberware (prostheses, the cyber-heart, minor and major augs, their refresh and surgery costs) are based on the Fate System Toolkit system reference document. The six conditions, the five storms, the example gadgets and the example augs are that document's examples. The wording of the help and of every message is this package's own.
