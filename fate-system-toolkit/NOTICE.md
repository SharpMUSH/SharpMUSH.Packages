# Fate System Toolkit Notice

The fate-system-toolkit package's conditions (fleeting, sticky and lasting, their stress soaking and their treatment), Wealth as a stress track (Extra Effort bonuses, starting boxes, aspects about wealth and parcels of Wealth), and Stormcallers (the five storms, their aspects' passive effects, invokes and compels, barriers, and each storm's rules for overcoming, creating advantages, attacking and defending) are based on the Fate System Toolkit system reference document. The six conditions and the five storms are that document's examples. The wording of the help and of every message is this package's own.
