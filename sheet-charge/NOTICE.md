# Charge SRD Notice

The sheet-charge package's details, actions and their descriptions, momentum, the stress clock and condition, milestones and talents, risk and effect, assists, pushes and tension, determination and fortune rolls, clocks, and the dice rules its help describes are based on the Charge system reference document, which is itself based on Blades in the Dark. The action descriptions on the Charge Sheet Store are the SRD's; the help and every message are this package's own wording.
